The schedule books an operatory and a provider for each appointment and shows production against the day's goal. Schedule templates define the blocks each chair offers. A hold reserves a time while the front desk or a patient finishes booking. The database refuses a double-booked chair or patient, even under many bookings at once.

Reminders check consent and quiet hours again at send time and honour C and STOP replies. The ASAP list tracks patients who want an earlier time.

| List | Statuses |

## Commands

Named mutations for this area. Each checks permissions on the server, takes an idempotency key and writes an audit event.

`holdSlot`, `releaseHold`, `bookAppointment`, `confirmAppointment`, `markArrived`, `seatPatient`, `markReadyForCheckout`, `completeAppointment`, `cancelAppointment`, `markBroken`, `rescheduleAppointment`, `addAsapEntry`, `closeAsapEntry`, `publishScheduleTemplate`, `reviewBookingRequest`.
