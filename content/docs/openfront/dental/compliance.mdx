Compliance tracks deadlines, incidents and their notification decision, patient rights requests (access, amendment and accounting of disclosures), retention policies and legal holds. A record under hold is never disposed of.

**Privacy** shows emergency access (break-glass) events and who read which patient record. **Import and export** previews a patient list import before a second person applies it, and exports records and the ledger.

## Pages

| Page | Route | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Compliance | `/dashboard/platform/compliance` | Deadlines, incidents, patient requests, legal holds and retention. |
| Import and export | `/dashboard/platform/data` | Bring in a patient list, or take records and the ledger out. |
| Privacy | `/dashboard/platform/privacy` | Patient privacy requests, disclosures, records exports, emergency access and who read records. |

## Lifecycles

| List | Statuses |
| --- | --- |
| `ComplianceTask` | `open`, `done`, `dismissed` |
| `IncidentReport` | `reported`, `assessing`, `notifiable`, `not_notifiable`, `notified`, `closed` |
| `PatientRightsRequest` | `open`, `extended`, `fulfilled`, `denied` |
| `RetentionDisposition` | `eligible`, `approval_requested`, `approved`, `executed`, `blocked` |
| `ImportBatch` | `previewed`, `approval_requested`, `applying`, `applied`, `rejected` |

## Commands

Named mutations for this area. Each checks permissions on the server, takes an idempotency key and writes an audit event.

`addComplianceTask`, `completeComplianceTask`, `reportIncident`, `advanceIncident`, `openRightsRequest`, `extendRightsRequest`, `resolveRightsRequest`, `recordDisclosure`, `saveRetentionPolicy`, `placeRecordHold`, `releaseRecordHold`, `scanRetention`, `requestDisposition`, `previewImport`, `requestImportApply`, `applyImportChunk`, `exportEhiBundle`.
