The storefront lives in `features/storefront` and mounts at the site root.

| Route | Page |
|---|---|
| `/` | Practice home ("Cleanings, without the wait.") |
| `/book` | Book a visit: pick a visit type, see open times, hold one and submit |
| `/account/sign-in` | Patient portal sign-in with a one-time code |
| `/pay/[code]` | Pay a statement by card, and download it as a PDF |

## Online booking

Booking uses four public operations: `publicSite`, `publicAvailability`, `publicHoldSlot` and `publicSubmitBooking`. A hold lasts `holdMinutes` (10 by default) and returns a hold token. Only the token's hash is stored.

On submit, the contact is matched against existing patients. It may match an existing record, create a new prospect, or create a prospect flagged as a possible duplicate. The answer is the same in every case: a booking reference, the time and the location name. It never says whether someone is already a patient and never returns a name. Staff review online bookings under **Booking requests** (`reviewBookingRequest`).

Rate limits are counted in Postgres and keyed by hashes. With `TURNSTILE_SECRET_KEY` set, a Turnstile token is required.

## Patient portal

`portalRequestCode` emails a six-digit code (10 minutes, 5 tries). `portalSession` exchanges it for a portal token sent back as the `x-portal-token` header. `portalMe`, `portalAppointments`, `portalConfirm`, `portalCancel`, `portalReschedule` and `portalPreferences` all name a patient and pass the `PortalScope` check. A guardian scope ends when the patient reaches the age of majority under the location's jurisdiction pack. Every portal read is written to `PhiReadAudit`.
