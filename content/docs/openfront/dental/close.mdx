title: Day and month close
description: Close the day, bank the deposit, close and reopen the month, and run pending work.
icon: CalendarCheck
---

Closing a day freezes its takings by tender, and the cash and checks are banked as a deposit batch. Closing a month locks the period. Reopening it needs a second person, and changes after reopening are posted as a restatement before the period is closed again.

**Run pending work** runs the scheduled steps (hold expiry, no-show sweep, stale eligibility checks and claims, remittance fetch and payment jobs) on request, each under a lease.

## Commands

Named mutations for this area. Each checks permissions on the server, takes an idempotency key and writes an audit event.

`closeDay`, `bankDeposit`, `closePeriod`, `requestPeriodReopen`, `postRestatement`, `reclosePeriod`, `exportJournalCsv`, `runPendingWork`.
