A practice can sell its own membership plans instead of insurance. Plans are defined and retired, and a patient membership starts once the agreement is signed. Where a state requires a licence for in-house plans, it is recorded with `recordLicence`.

## Pages

| Page | Route | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Memberships | `/dashboard/platform/memberships` | In-house plans, members and the licence the state asks for. |

## Lifecycles

| List | Statuses |
| --- | --- |
| `MembershipPlan` | `draft`, `active`, `retired` |
| `PatientMembership` | `pending_agreement`, `active`, `past_due`, `frozen`, `cancelled`, `expired` |

## Commands

Named mutations for this area. Each checks permissions on the server, takes an idempotency key and writes an audit event.

`recordLicence`, `defineMembershipPlan`, `retireMembershipPlan`, `enrolMembership`, `signMembershipAgreement`, `freezeMembership`, `cancelMembership`.
