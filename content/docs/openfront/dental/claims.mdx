title: Claims and insurance payments
description: Build, scrub and release claims, post remittances, and work denials and appeals.
icon: FileCheck
---

Staff build a claim from posted procedures and scrub it against the scrub rules. A person releases it, never the system. Release freezes a `ClaimVersion`, and after that the lines do not change; a correction is a new version. Pending work then sends released claims through the connected clearinghouse. With the manual clearinghouse, the claim is exported or printed and sent through the payer portal.

Remittances arrive from the clearinghouse or are entered by hand from the explanation of benefits (`enterManualEob`). Each line is matched to a claim line and posted once. Denied lines open denial work, which can end in a correction, an appeal, a write-off or a patient bill.

## Commands

Named mutations for this area. Each checks permissions on the server, takes an idempotency key and writes an audit event.

`buildClaim`, `scrubClaim`, `overrideScrub`, `releaseClaim`, `exportClaimCsv`, `correctClaim`, `voidClaim`, `addClaimAttachment`, `saveClaimNarrative`, `requestClaimStatus`, `enterManualEob`, `matchRemittance`, `startDenialWork`, `resolveDenial`, `fileAppeal`, `recordAppealOutcome`.
