A patient belongs to a family group and has one or more guarantor links. Duplicate detection flags likely matches. A merge is proposed by one person and approved by a second, and it can be undone for 30 days (`revertMerge`). Staff grant and revoke portal access from the patient overview.

## Pages

| Page | Route | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Patients | `/dashboard/platform/patients` | Patients, families, guarantors and alerts. |

## Lifecycles

| List | Statuses |
| --- | --- |

| `ConsentGrant` | `granted`, `revoked`, `expired` |

## Commands

Named mutations for this area. Each checks permissions on the server, takes an idempotency key and writes an audit event.

`registerPatient`, `updatePatientDemographics`, `addContactPoint`, `deactivateContactPoint`, `recordConsent`, `addPatientAlert`, `endPatientAlert`, `linkGuarantor`, `setPatientStatus`, `proposeMerge`, `revertMerge`, `grantPortalAccess`, `revokePortalScope`, `suspendPortalAccount`.
