---
title: Clinical chart
description: Tooth and perio charting, clinical notes, medical history, vitals, imaging and prescriptions.
icon: Stethoscope
---

The chart records procedures and conditions by tooth and surface. A chart entry moves from diagnosed or planned to completed and posted, and a completed procedure posts its fee to the ledger. Clinical notes are drafted, then signed; a signed note can only be marked entered in error. Perio exams record site measurements and a suggested classification that the clinician confirms or overrides.

Prescriptions are written, checked against the state PDMP for controlled drugs (`recordPdmpCheck`), signed and printed. The chart is opened from the patient record; prescriptions have their own page.

## Pages
