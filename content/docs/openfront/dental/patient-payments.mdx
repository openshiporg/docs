description: Ledger, payments, refunds, statements, payment plans and credits.
icon: Wallet

Each guarantor account has an append-only ledger. Payments are allocated to charges, and a mistake is fixed with a reversal or an approved adjustment, never an edit. Refunds over the approval threshold need a second person. Statements are generated and hashed, then sent or held, and a patient can pay one online at `/pay/[code]`.

## Commands

Named mutations for this area. Each checks permissions on the server, takes an idempotency key and writes an audit event.

`requestAdjustment`, `reverseLedgerEntry`, `takePayment`, `requestRefund`, `recordRefundPaid`, `correctLedgerNote`, `generateStatements`, `sendStatement`, `holdStatements`, `proposePaymentPlan`, `activatePaymentPlan`, `cancelPaymentPlan`, `rebuildAccountBalance`.
