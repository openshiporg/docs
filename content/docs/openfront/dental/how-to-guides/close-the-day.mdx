---
title: Close the day
description: Freeze the day's takings and bank the deposit.
icon: Lock
---

import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

<Steps>
<Step>

### Check the day

Open **Day and month close** and review takings by tender.

</Step>
<Step>

### Close

Close the day (`closeDay`). A day cannot be closed before it has happened, or twice.

</Step>
<Step>

### Bank

Record the deposit for the cash and checks (`bankDeposit`).

</Step>
<Step>

### Run pending work

Press **Run pending work** to expire holds, sweep no-shows and fetch remittances.

</Step>
</Steps>
