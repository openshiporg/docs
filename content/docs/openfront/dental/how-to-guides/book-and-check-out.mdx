---
title: Book and check out a visit
description: Book a chair, seat the patient, complete procedures and take payment.
icon: CalendarPlus
---

import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

<Steps>
<Step>

### Book

In **Schedule**, pick an operatory, provider and time. Booking holds the slot, then `bookAppointment` confirms it.

</Step>
<Step>

### Confirm and arrive

Mark the visit confirmed, then arrived when the patient checks in (`confirmAppointment`, `markArrived`).

</Step>
<Step>

### Seat and chart

Seat the patient (`seatPatient`) and complete the planned procedures in the chart (`completeProcedure`). Each completed procedure posts its fee.

</Step>
<Step>

### Check out

Mark the visit ready for checkout, then complete it (`markReadyForCheckout`, `completeAppointment`).

</Step>
<Step>

### Take payment

Record the patient's payment in **Patient payments** (`takePayment`).

</Step>
</Steps>
