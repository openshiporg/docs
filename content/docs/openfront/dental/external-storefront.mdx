To build a custom Dental storefront, copy the skill and give it to your LLM. The skill explains that `features/storefront` holds the built-in site, booking panel and portal sign-in, and that booking and the portal are public GraphQL operations a separate site can call.

Skill file: [`/skills/openfront-dental-custom-storefront/SKILL.md`](/skills/openfront-dental-custom-storefront/SKILL.md)

## Operations a separate site uses

| Need | Operation |
|---|---|
| Practice, locations and bookable visit types | `publicSite` |
| Open times | `publicAvailability` |
| Hold and book | `publicHoldSlot`, `publicSubmitBooking` |
| Portal sign-in | `portalRequestCode`, `portalSession` |
| Portal reads and actions | `portalMe`, `portalAppointments`, `portalConfirm`, `portalCancel`, `portalReschedule`, `portalPreferences` |

A separate site must not reveal whether a visitor is a patient, and must keep the portal token server-side or in a secure cookie.
