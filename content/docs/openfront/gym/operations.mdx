`/dashboard/platform/operations` has tabs (`operationsWorkspace`, `participationWorkspace`). Most actions go through `runOperationsCommand`.

- **Member notices:** `OperationalNotice`
- **Cases:** incident, inspection, facility closure, financial reconciliation, integration exception, privacy review (`OperationsCase`)
- **Policy and waivers:** `ParticipationPolicy` and `ParticipationEvidence`. Publish from [Store settings](/docs/openfront/gym/store-settings). "Explicit acceptance or staff verification is required".
- **Facility resources:** `GymResource`, with `updateGymResourceAllocation`
- **Partner credentials:** `IntegrationCredential`, `gym_`-prefixed, stored hashed, scopes `classes:read` and `bookings:create`
- **Exports:** `exportOperatingData` for payments, members, or cases
- **Payment events:** `replayPaymentEvent`
- **Entitlements:** `reconcileGymEntitlements`
