## Invite and manage

<Steps>
<Step>
### Invite
`inviteMember` creates the member and account invitation.
</Step>
<Step>
### Set account status
`setMemberAccountStatus`: active, suspended, or cancelled. "Member account status must be active, suspended, or cancelled".
</Step>
</Steps>

## Import contacts

`importMemberContacts` takes 1 to 100 rows with a dry-run preview, then applies. Each row is kept in `MemberImportRecord`.

Members can also sign up themselves on `/account` when `PUBLIC_MEMBER_SIGNUPS_ALLOWED=true`.
