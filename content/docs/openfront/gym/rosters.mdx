`/dashboard/platform/rosters` lists sessions by state (`rosterSessions`). `/rosters/[id]` shows confirmed, waitlist, and other bookings (`rosterDetail`).

<Steps>
<Step>
### Promote from the waitlist
`promoteFromWaitlist`. Cancelling a confirmed booking can also promote the next member.
</Step>
<Step>
### Mark attendance
`markClassAttendance`. "Attendance can only be marked for a confirmed booking". Rows go to `AttendanceRecord`.
</Step>
</Steps>

Booking itself is by the member (`bookClass`, `cancelClassBooking`). See [Storefront](/docs/openfront/gym/external-storefront).
