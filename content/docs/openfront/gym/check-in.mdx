## Front desk

`/dashboard/platform/check-in` (`frontDeskWorkspace`) validates the membership, then `recordMemberCheckIn` and `checkOutMember`. Methods: qr_code, rfid, manual, app.

## Kiosk

<Steps>
<Step>
### Configure
Set `KIOSK_API_TOKEN` and `KIOSK_ORGANIZATION_ID`.
</Step>
<Step>
### Unlock
Staff enter the token at `/kiosk`; `authorizeKioskSession` sets a 12-hour HttpOnly cookie.
</Step>
<Step>
### Check in
`kioskSearchMembers` (2 to 100 characters), `kioskRecordMemberCheckIn`, or `kioskRecordGuestCheckIn` with an idempotency key.
</Step>
</Steps>

Members show a QR code from `/member/check-in-code` that rotates every 30 seconds. It needs `QR_CODE_SECRET`. The QR code and kiosk record membership-based entry. They do not control doors.
