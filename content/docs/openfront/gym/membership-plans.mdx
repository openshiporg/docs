---
title: Membership plans
description: Tiers, prices, class credits, freezes, and Stripe price IDs.
icon: IdCard
---

`/dashboard/platform/membership-plans` edits `MembershipTier` with generated CRUD: price per cycle (`monthly`, `annual`), class credits, freeze rules, access hours, and Stripe product and price IDs. Online checkout needs a Stripe product ID and a price above zero on at least one plan. The IDs are format-checked only.

Members buy plans on `/join`. Credits are granted per service period as a `MembershipCreditGrant`, and every change is a `MembershipCreditEntry`.
