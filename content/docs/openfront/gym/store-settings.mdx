`/dashboard/platform/store-settings` (`gymSettingsEditor`, `upsertGymSettings`).

<Steps>

### Identity and locale
Name, contact, timezone, hours, facility copy, storefront hero.
</Step>
<Step>
### Publish participation terms
`publishGymParticipationTerms`. Members accept them before checkout on `/join` and at `/account/participation`. Setup never publishes terms for you.
</Step>
</Steps>

Locations (`/dashboard/platform/locations`) hold site names, contact details, and active state.
