## Team roles

`Team` is the tenant root. Reads are scoped to teams where the user has an active `Member` row.

| Role | Access |
|---|---|
| `owner` | everything in the team, ownership transfer |
| `admin` | manage the team's records |
| `host` | read, and confirm or decline bookings they are assigned to |
| `viewer` | read |

Writes need `owner` or `admin`. A record cannot move between teams. Command-only lists (`Booking`, `Member`, `TeamInvitation`, `Resource`, `EventResourceRequirement`, `PaymentBooking`, `ConferenceConnection`, `ApiKey`) refuse raw create, update, and delete.

## Global role flags

`canAccessDashboard`, `canManageScheduling`, `canManageOnboarding`, `canSeeOtherPeople`, `canEditOtherPeople`, `canManagePeople`, `canManageRoles`. They gate dashboard areas and do not bypass team scoping. Accepting an invitation grants dashboard access.

## Machine scopes

API keys and OAuth tokens carry scopes checked by `requireMachineScope`.
