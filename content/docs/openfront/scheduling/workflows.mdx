## Workflows

`WorkflowDefinition` triggers: `booking_created`, `booking_confirmed`, `booking_rescheduled`, `booking_cancelled`, `booking_completed`, `booking_no_show`, `before_start`, `after_end`. Steps are `WorkflowAction` rows of type `email` or `webhook`. Activate, pause, or archive a workflow. Runs appear as `WorkflowExecution` and `WorkflowActionExecution`; retry a failed step with `retryWorkflowAction`.

## Reminders

`WorkflowReminder` sends an email, SMS, or webhook reminder per event type. `ReminderDelivery` tracks each send. SMS posts to your own gateway (`SCHEDULING_SMS_ENDPOINT`, `SCHEDULING_SMS_TOKEN`).

Outbound HTTP goes through `safeEgress.ts`, which blocks private and loopback addresses.
