---
title: Store settings
description: Public booking identity, copy, logo, and theme.
icon: Settings
---

`/dashboard/platform/store` edits the team the storefront shows: name, slug, time zone, `publicBookingEnabled`, storefront headline and copy, logo (sanitized inline SVG), and brand hue (`updateSchedulingStorefrontBrandHue`). The storefront serves the team named by `STOREFRONT_TEAM_SLUG`, or the oldest active team with public booking turned on. Team status is `active`, `paused`, or `archived`.
