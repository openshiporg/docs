`/dashboard/platform/resources`.

- `upsertSchedulingResource` creates or edits a resource (room, equipment, or capacity pool) with a unique slug and capacity.
- `transitionSchedulingResource` moves it between statuses, for example into maintenance.
- `ResourceAllocation` rows are `held`, `reserved`, `released`, or `expired`. Holds reserve capacity while a customer fills the booking form.
- Event types require units with `configureEventResourceRequirement`.
- **Managed templates** define event settings once and apply them to many events.
