---
title: Estimates and authorizations
description: Frozen estimate versions, approval links, and recording what the customer said.
icon: FileCheck
---

Sending an estimate freezes a version of the selected jobs. Staff then text or email an approval link (`requestCustomerAuthorization`), and the customer approves or declines each job on `/authorizations/[token]` with a signature. Staff can also record what the customer said in person, on the phone or on a written form. An assistant can never record an authorization.

The charge for an authorized job may never pass the authorized amount plus the state's tolerance. California allows nothing over, so more work needs a new authorization. Estimates are opened from the repair order.

## Lifecycles

| List | Statuses |
| --- | --- |
| `EstimateVersion` | `draft`, `sent`, `superseded` |
| `Authorization` | `recorded`, `superseded`, `revoked` |
| `AuthorizationRequest` | `created`, `delivered`, `viewed`, `answered`, `expired`, `withdrawn` |

## Commands

Named mutations for this area. Each checks permissions on the server, takes an idempotency key and writes an audit event.

`sendRepairEstimate`, `requestCustomerAuthorization`, `recordCustomerAuthorization`.
