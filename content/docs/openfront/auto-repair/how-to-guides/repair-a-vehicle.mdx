import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

<Steps>
<Step>

### Check in

On **Check-in**, record the customer, vehicle, odometer and concerns, and take the customer's signature (`checkInShopVehicle`).

</Step>
<Step>

### Open the repair order

Open it and add jobs and lines (`openShopRepairOrder`, `addRepairJob`, `saveJobLines`).

</Step>
<Step>

### Inspect

Run the inspection and send it to the customer (`startShopInspection`, `completeShopInspection`, `sendShopInspection`).

</Step>
<Step>

### Get approval

Send the estimate and an approval link (`sendRepairEstimate`, `requestCustomerAuthorization`), or record the customer's answer (`recordCustomerAuthorization`).

</Step>
<Step>

### Repair

Technicians clock onto the job and move it through its stages (`startTechJobClock`, `transitionRepairJob`).

</Step>
<Step>

### Invoice and release

Finalize the invoice, take payment and release the vehicle (`finalizeShopInvoice`, `takeShopPayment`, `releaseShopVehicle`).

</Step>
</Steps>
