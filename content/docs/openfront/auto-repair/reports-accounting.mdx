**Reports** shows car count, average repair order, effective labor rate, gross profit, technician productivity, and approval and comeback rates, each with its definition and a tie-out to the books. **Accounting export** stages daily journal files for the books. Nothing counts as posted until a person records that the books accepted it.

## Pages

| Page | Route | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reports | `/dashboard/platform/reports` | Car count, ARO, ELR, gross profit, technician productivity, approval and comeback rates, with definitions and books tie-outs. |
| Accounting export | `/dashboard/platform/accounting` | Daily journal files staged for the books. Nothing posts until a person records the books accepted it. |

## Lifecycles

| List | Statuses |
| --- | --- |
| `AccountingBatch` | `staged`, `exported`, `confirmed`, `rejected`, `voided` |

## Commands

Named mutations for this area. Each checks permissions on the server, takes an idempotency key and writes an audit event.

`saveAccountingMappings`, `stageAccountingBatch`, `exportAccountingBatch`, `recordAccountingResponse`, `saveReportSnapshot`.
