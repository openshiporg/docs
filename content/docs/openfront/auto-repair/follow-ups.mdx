**Follow-ups** lists work customers put off and services coming due, with who to call. **Messages** is the record of calls, texts and emails with customers, a thread per repair order, and the wording staff copy. Nothing on that screen sends. **Campaigns** builds audience lists to export or copy from, and includes only customers who agreed to marketing in writing.

## Pages

| Page | Route | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Follow-ups | `/dashboard/platform/follow-ups` | Work customers put off and services coming due, with who to call. |
| Messages | `/dashboard/platform/messages` | The record of calls, texts and emails with customers, and the wording staff copy. |
| Campaigns | `/dashboard/platform/campaigns` | Audience lists to export or copy from. Only customers who agreed to marketing in writing. |

## Lifecycles

| List | Statuses |
| --- | --- |
| `ServiceReminder` | `contacted` |
| `Campaign` | `draft`, `active`, `ended` |

## Commands

Named mutations for this area. Each checks permissions on the server, takes an idempotency key and writes an audit event.

`logCustomerMessage`, `logCustomerCall`, `readMessageThread`, `saveMessageTemplate`, `markFollowUpContacted`, `markServiceReminderContacted`, `saveMarketingCampaign`, `exportMarketingCampaign`, `copyMarketingMessage`, `recordReviewRequest`.
