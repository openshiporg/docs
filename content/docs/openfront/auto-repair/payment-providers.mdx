## Card payments (Stripe)

`features/keystone/lib/stripeGateway.ts` takes card payments through Stripe. The key decides test or live, and it accepts `sk_` or `rk_` keys.

There is no webhook endpoint. Every payment and refund is settled by asking Stripe, from the polling the pay page does or from **Resolve payments** (`resolveShopPayments`). A call that fails without a clear answer leaves the payment `unknown`, and it is retried with the same idempotency key, so Stripe answers with the original result.

Customers pay at `/pay/[token]` from a link staff create with `createInvoicePayLink`, or from the invoice page.

## Manual tenders

Cash, check and other tenders are recorded with `takeShopPayment`. Day close counts the drawer and freezes the day.

## Other adapters

| Adapter | What it does |
|---|---|
| Labor guide | The shop's own library of labor times. Licensed guides (MOTOR, ALLDATA, Mitchell 1) plug in once licensed. |
| Parts supplier | `manual` by default: staff phone, walk up or use the supplier's website and record what they were told. PartsTech and Nexpart plug in once licensed. |
| Vehicle data | NHTSA vPIC for VIN decoding and the NHTSA recall lookup, called live. |

**Integrations** lists each provider connection and what it reported (`saveIntegrationConnection`, `recordIntegrationHealth`, `recordProviderObservation`).
