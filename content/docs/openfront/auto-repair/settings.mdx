**Shops** holds shops, bays, hours and staff memberships. **Canned jobs** are job templates with lines, labor-guide codes and wording, drafted then published. **Policies** holds jurisdiction rules, labor rates, parts matrices, fees, sales tax and warranty, each drafted and published as a version. **Imports** brings customers and vehicles in from Tekmetric, Shopmonkey or any CSV, with a preview first. **Integrations** lists provider connections. **Audit log** shows the hash-chained events and checks the chain. **Approvals** lists requests waiting for a second person.

## Pages

| Page | Route | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Approvals | `/dashboard/platform/approvals` | Maker-checker requests waiting for a second person. |
| Imports | `/dashboard/platform/imports` | Bring customers and vehicles in from Tekmetric, Shopmonkey or any CSV, with a preview first. |
| Integrations | `/dashboard/platform/integrations` | Provider connections and what they told us. |
| Shops | `/dashboard/platform/shops` | Shops, bays, hours and staff memberships. |
| Canned jobs | `/dashboard/platform/canned-jobs` | Job templates: lines, labor-guide codes and wording, drafted then published. |
| Policies | `/dashboard/platform/policies` | Jurisdiction rules, labor rates, matrices, fees, tax and warranty: draft and publish. |
| Price list & website | `/dashboard/platform/website` | Services and prices on the public site (`PublishedService`: name, what the customer gets, price from, time, **Customers can book it online**, **Show on the website**, copied from a canned job). Per shop: tagline, about, the state registration number shown to customers, and **Show this shop on the website**. Without published prices, customers see canned jobs with "Request an estimate". |
| Audit log | `/dashboard/platform/audit` | Hash-chained audit events and chain verification. |

## Commands

Named mutations for this area. Each checks permissions on the server, takes an idempotency key and writes an audit event.

`createPolicyDraft`, `publishPolicyVersion`, `saveCannedJob`, `publishCannedJobVersion`, `setCannedJobStatus`, `previewDataImport`, `applyDataImport`, `discardDataImport`, `saveIntegrationConnection`, `recordIntegrationHealth`, `revokeIntegrationConnection`, `recordProviderObservation`.
