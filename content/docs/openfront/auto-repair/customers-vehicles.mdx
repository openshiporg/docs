A customer has contacts with per-channel texting consent, and STOP is honoured. Contact values are stored as keyed hashes for search. A vehicle has a VIN checked by its check digit, decoded through NHTSA, checked for open recalls, and an odometer history. Staff create a customer portal link from the customer record.

## Pages

| Page | Route | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Customers | `/dashboard/platform/customers` | Customers, contacts and consent history. |
| Vehicles | `/dashboard/platform/vehicles` | VIN decode, recalls, ownership and odometer history. |

## Commands

Named mutations for this area. Each checks permissions on the server, takes an idempotency key and writes an audit event.

`saveCustomer`, `addCustomerContact`, `recordContactConsent`, `saveVehicle`, `linkVehicleOwner`, `recordOdometerReading`, `decodeVehicleVin`, `checkVehicleRecalls`, `createCustomerPortalLink`.
