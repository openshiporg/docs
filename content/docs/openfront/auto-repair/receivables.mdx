Finalizing an invoice fixes its lines and taxes. Payments are recorded by tender or taken by card, and a refund over policy needs a second person. **Receivables** lists invoices still owing, with the overdue ones first. **Day close** shows takings by tender, takes the drawer count and freezes the day. Reopening a closed day goes through approval.

## Pages

| Page | Route | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Receivables | `/dashboard/platform/receivables` | Invoices still owing, with the overdue ones first. |
| Day close | `/dashboard/platform/day-close` | Takings by tender, the drawer count and the frozen day. |

## Lifecycles

| List | Statuses |
| --- | --- |
| `Invoice` | `finalized`, `sent`, `partially_paid`, `paid`, `credited` |
| `Payment` | `initiated`, `pending`, `succeeded`, `failed`, `unknown`, `partially_refunded`, `refunded` |
| `Refund` | `requested`, `pending`, `succeeded`, `failed`, `unknown` |
| `CreditMemo` | `draft`, `issued` |
| `BusinessDayClose` | `open`, `counting`, `closed`, `reopened` |

## Commands

Named mutations for this area. Each checks permissions on the server, takes an idempotency key and writes an audit event.

`finalizeShopInvoice`, `createInvoicePayLink`, `takeShopPayment`, `checkShopPayment`, `resolveShopPayments`, `refundShopPayment`, `issueShopCreditMemo`, `closeShopDay`, `reopenShopDay`.
