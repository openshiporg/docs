Appointments book against each shop's capacity: drop-offs, waiters per hour and bay hours. Online bookings arrive as requests. Check-in on a tablet records the customer, vehicle, odometer and concerns, and the customer signs the work order with the state's wording.

## Pages

| Page | Route | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Check-in | `/dashboard/platform/check-in` | Customer, vehicle, odometer, concerns and work-order signature. |
| Schedule | `/dashboard/platform/schedule` | Appointments by day and bay, capacity and no-shows. |

## Lifecycles

| List | Statuses |
| --- | --- |
| `Appointment` | `requested`, `confirmed`, `arrived`, `no_show`, `cancelled` |
| `BookingHold` | `held`, `consumed`, `expired`, `released` |

## Commands

Named mutations for this area. Each checks permissions on the server, takes an idempotency key and writes an audit event.

`holdAppointmentSlot`, `bookShopAppointment`, `transitionAppointment`, `attachAppointmentLead`, `checkInShopVehicle`.
