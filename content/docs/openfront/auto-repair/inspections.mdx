An inspection follows a published template. Technicians record each item as red, yellow or green, with measurements, photos and notes. Photos are stored in S3. Sending the inspection creates a customer link to `/inspections/[token]`.

## Pages

| Page | Route | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inspections | `/dashboard/platform/inspections` | Digital vehicle inspections: measurements, photos and findings. |

## Lifecycles

| List | Statuses |
| --- | --- |
| `Inspection` | `assigned`, `in_progress`, `completed`, `sent`, `reviewed` |
| `InspectionTemplateVersion` | `draft`, `published`, `retired` |

## Commands

Named mutations for this area. Each checks permissions on the server, takes an idempotency key and writes an audit event.

`startShopInspection`, `recordInspectionItem`, `attachInspectionMedia`, `completeShopInspection`, `sendShopInspection`.
