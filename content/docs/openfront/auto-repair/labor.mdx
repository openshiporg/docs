---
title: Labor, time and comebacks
description: Time clock, technicians and certificates, flagged hours, pay periods and comebacks.
icon: Timer
---

Technicians clock in for the shift and start and stop a clock on each job. Time corrections go through approval. Flagged hours come from the labor time on each job and can be adjusted. Pay periods are closed and exported. Technician certificates are kept with their expiry.

A comeback is a vehicle that returns for the same problem. It is diagnosed, resolved with or without goodwill, and can open a parts warranty claim against the supplier.

## Pages

| Page | Route | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Labor | `/dashboard/platform/time` | Time clock, technicians and certificates, flagged hours, pay periods and comebacks. |
| Technicians | `/dashboard/platform/technicians` | Technicians and their certificates. |
| Pay periods | `/dashboard/platform/pay-periods` | Hours by technician and flagged hours. A period can be closed once its last day has passed; **Close this period** needs a second person to approve. The first period opens when a job is completed. |
| Comebacks | `/dashboard/platform/comebacks` | Work that came back for the same problem. **Open a comeback** against a recently finished job, **Make it free (needs approval)**, or **Cancel this comeback**. Also lists supplier warranty claims. |

## Commands

Named mutations for this area. Each checks permissions on the server, takes an idempotency key and writes an audit event.

`clockInShift`, `clockOutShift`, `startShiftBreak`, `endShiftBreak`, `startTechJobClock`, `stopTechJobClock`, `logJobWaiting`, `requestTimeCorrection`, `saveTechCredential`, `assignShopJobTechnicians`, `adjustTechFlaggedHours`, `closeShopPayPeriod`, `exportShopPayPeriod`, `openShopComeback`, `diagnoseShopComeback`, `requestComebackGoodwill`, `resolveShopComeback`, `createShopPartWarrantyClaim`, `attachShopComebackJob`, `cancelShopComeback`, `withdrawShopPartWarrantyClaim`.
