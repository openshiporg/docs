Parts are quoted from suppliers, ordered and received against jobs. With the manual supplier adapter, staff record a phone or website order. An order that may or may not have reached the supplier is marked unknown and resolved, never sent again. Stock is reserved, issued and returned per job, and counted in cycle counts.

Core charges are tracked until the core is returned or written off. Supplier invoices are matched to receipts, and discrepancies are resolved before approval. Tires sold are registered with the manufacturer, and an overdue registration is computed on read.

## Pages

| Page | Route | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Parts | `/dashboard/platform/parts` | Quotes, supplier orders, stock, cores, supplier invoices and tire registrations. |
| Supplier orders | `/dashboard/platform/purchase-orders` | Open and closed orders. **Place the order**; **Settle: ask the supplier**. Orders for a job are placed only after the customer has approved it. |
| Stock | `/dashboard/platform/stock` | Stocked parts on hand. |
| Cores | `/dashboard/platform/cores` | Cores owed back to suppliers, returned and waiting for the credit, and closed. **Mark returned** or **Write off**. |
| Supplier invoices | `/dashboard/platform/supplier-invoices` | **Match to orders** and **Export for the books**. |
| Tire registrations | `/dashboard/platform/tires` | To register, sent and waiting, and done. **Confirm it was registered**. |
