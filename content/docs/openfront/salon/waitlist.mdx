## Entries

A `WaitlistEntry` has a client or guest name and phone, a `service`, `preferredStylists`, `preferredDays`, `preferredTimeRange` (morning 9am to 12pm, afternoon 12pm to 5pm, evening 5pm to 8pm, or anytime), a date range, notes, and a `status`: `active`, `notified`, `converted`, or `removed`.

## Add an entry

Staff call `joinWaitlist` with a service, optional stylists, preferences, and an idempotency key. Signed-in clients on the storefront call `joinWaitlistAsClient` when `/book` shows no open times for a day. The date range cannot end before it starts or run more than a year ahead.

## Work the queue

`transitionWaitlistEntry` takes an `action`:

| Action | Result |
| --- | --- |
| `notify` | `active` to `notified` |
| `remove` | to `removed` |
| `reactivate` | `removed` or `notified` back to `active` |
| `convert` | to `converted`; needs the `appointmentId` you booked |

A converted entry cannot change again. The appointment must match the entry's client and service.
