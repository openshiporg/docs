`/dashboard/platform/sales` lists `SalonTransaction` rows with status tabs and search. Open a sale at `/dashboard/platform/sales/[id]`. The pages need `canManagePayments`, `canIssueRefunds`, or `canViewReports`. Reversals need `canIssueRefunds`.

## Sale fields

- `type`: `appointment`, `walk_in`, or `retail`
- `status`: `open`, `completed`, `voided`
- `paymentStatus`: `pending`, `paid`, `partially_paid`, `refunded`
- `paymentMethod`: `cash`, `card`, `split`, `gift_card`, `package`
- line items, tip, and linked appointment

## Reverse a sale

`reverseSalonTransaction` takes the transaction, a `type` (`void`, `refund`, `partial_refund`), the lines and quantities to reverse, `tipCents` to recover, `restorePackageCents`, a reason, and an idempotency key.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Choose the type
Void and refund must cover the full remaining sale. A partial refund must not equal the full remaining amount.
</Step>
<Step>
### Pick lines and quantities
Quantity cannot exceed what is still unreversed.
</Step>
<Step>
### Set retail stock handling
Each retail line needs an inventory disposition: `return_to_stock`, `damaged`, or `consumed`. Services use `not_applicable`.
</Step>
<Step>
### Recover tip and package value
Tip recovery cannot exceed the unreversed gratuity. Package value restored cannot exceed what checkout used.
</Step>
<Step>
### Submit
A `TransactionReversal` and `TransactionReversalLine` rows are written. Payment refunds go to the provider when the sale was paid through one, and the reversal is `posted` or `provider_pending`.
</Step>
</Steps>

Reversals are append-only. Commission and tip effects are written as `CommissionAdjustment` and `GratuityLedgerEntry` rows.

## Correct compensation

`adjustSaleCompensation` changes commission or moves tip between practitioners after a sale without refunding the client. Fields: `commissionId`, `commissionDelta`, `sourceGratuityEntryId`, `targetPractitionerId`, `gratuityTransferCents`, and a reason. It needs `canManageCommissions`. A commission cannot go below zero.

## Related mutations

`reverseSalonTransaction`, `adjustSaleCompensation`, `refundPayment`. See [Refund a sale](/docs/openfront/salon/how-to-guides/refund-a-sale).
