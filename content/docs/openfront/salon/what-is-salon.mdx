Openfront Salon runs one salon business with one or more locations. It has a client storefront, an operations dashboard, and a GraphQL API.

## Who uses what

| User | Surface | Typical jobs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Client | Storefront and `/account` | Browse services, book, cancel or reschedule, sign consultation forms, leave reviews, see points and gift cards |
| Reception | Appointments, checkout, waitlist | Book, check in, take payment |
| Stylist | Schedules, staff profile | Set own hours, see upcoming work and earnings |
| Manager | Everything | Catalog, staff, refunds, reports |
| Finance | Sales, commissions, reports | Refunds, payouts |

## A visit

1. Client books online or at the desk. The service price, duration, steps, and stylist are copied onto the appointment.
2. Consultation form is signed and reviewed where required.
3. The appointment is confirmed, started, and finished.
4. Checkout writes the sale, payment, stock movements, commissions, tip allocations, and points.
5. Refunds and corrections add new rows against the original sale.
