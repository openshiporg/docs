## Where amounts come from

- A `Commission` is created per service line at checkout: `saleAmount`, `commissionRate`, `commissionAmount`, `isPaid`, `paidAt`.
- Tips are `GratuityLedgerEntry` rows of type `allocation` or `recovery`, with `payoutStatus` `payable`, `paid`, or `recovery_due`.

`calculateCommission(stylistId, startDate, endDate)` returns totals for a period.

## Pay out

<Steps>
<Step>
### Pick a stylist
The page lists unpaid commissions per stylist (`UnpaidStylistCommissions`).
</Step>
<Step>
### Pay commissions
`payoutCommissions` takes `commissionIds` and a `reference`. All commissions in one batch must belong to one stylist.
</Step>
<Step>
### Pay card tips
`payoutGratuities` takes `stylistId` and a `reference` and marks payable tips as paid.
</Step>
</Steps>

Refunds create `CommissionAdjustment` rows and tip recoveries, so a later payout reflects them. See [Sales and refunds](/docs/openfront/salon/sales-refunds).
