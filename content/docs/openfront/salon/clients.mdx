Clients live at `/dashboard/platform/clients` and need `canManageClients`. A `Client` is either linked to a `User` account or a guest with `guestName`, `guestEmail`, and `guestPhone`.

## Client profile

`/dashboard/platform/clients/[id]` shows visit history, lifetime value, and care details:

- `allergies`, `skinSensitivities`, `notes`
- `skinType`, `hairType`, `hairTexture`, `scalpCondition`
- `preferredStylist`, `homeLocation`
- `photoConsent` and `marketingConsent`
- `firstVisit`, `lastVisit`, `totalVisits`, `lifetimeValue`

Edit care details with `updateClientCareProfile`. Add notes (`ClientNote`) and color formulas (`ColorFormula`: formula name, stylist, appointment) from the same page.

## Creating and editing clients

Staff create and edit clients through `saveSalonRecord` with `kind: "client"`: name, phone, email, and home location. Changing the email of an existing account is refused: "Account email changes require the separate verified identity workflow". A new client whose email matches an existing account is refused so identities are not merged.

## Self-service

Clients sign up at `/account/signup` (`registerSalonCustomer`) and see their own visits, receipts, benefits, points, and gift cards at `/account`.
