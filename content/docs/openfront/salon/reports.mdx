`/dashboard/platform/reports` needs `canViewReports`. Pick a range with `?range=`: `today`, `week` (last 7 days), `month` (last 30 days), or `all`. Days are counted in the first active location's timezone.

## Metrics

- Net payments (including tax and tips)
- Tips
- Refunds
- Unpaid commissions
- Completed appointments, and cancelled plus no-show appointments

## Close-out tables

**By payment method**: services, retail, tax, tips, refunded, and net.

**By stylist**: services, retail, and tips (tips split by service value).

Source data is completed, partially refunded, and refunded payments in the range. The data comes from `salonOperationsWorkspace(section: "reports", range: ...)`.

## Other workspace sections

The same query backs the list pages for `clients`, `catalog`, `benefits`, `inventory`, `engagement`, `administration`, and `integrations`. Each is guarded by the matching role flag.
