Openfront Salon is a self-hostable salon app. Clients browse services and stylists, book appointments, and manage their visits in an account. Salon staff use the dashboard to run the day: bookings, check-in, consultations, checkout, refunds, schedules, resources, retail stock, memberships, packages, gift cards, loyalty, reviews, commissions, and reports.

It is built on Next.js 16, React 19, Keystone 6 (GraphQL), Prisma, and PostgreSQL. Start with [Getting started](/docs/openfront/salon/getting-started).

<Callout type="warn">
The app runs one salon business. It has no organization or tenant model. Role flags apply to every location. Test permissions, money, and booking races on your own database before you use real client data.
</Callout>

## Surfaces

- **Storefront** at `/`: home, services, team, booking, account, memberships, gift cards, gallery, reviews, and contact. See [External storefronts](/docs/openfront/salon/external-storefront).
- **Dashboard** at `/dashboard`: salon pages under `/dashboard/platform/*` plus the generic Keystone list screens at `/dashboard/[listKey]`. See [External dashboards](/docs/openfront/salon/external-dashboard).
- **GraphQL** at `/api/graphql`.
- **Payment webhooks** at `/api/payments/webhooks/[provider]`.
- **Dashboard assistant** at `/api/completion`, backed by an MCP endpoint at `/api/mcp-transport/[transport]`.

## Models

The schema has 58 lists in `features/keystone/models`. Most business lists block generated create, update, and delete. They change only through custom mutations that check the role, run in a serializable transaction, and take an idempotency key. Money is stored as integer cents. Times are instants; the salon's local time comes from `Location.timezone`.

| Area | Lists |
| --- | --- |
| Identity | `User`, `Role` |
| Catalog | `ServiceCategory`, `Service`, `ServiceVariant`, `ServiceSegment`, `SalonImage`, `Product`, `InventoryMovement`, `ProductSale` (legacy) |
| People and capacity | `Location`, `StoreSettings`, `Stylist`, `StylistSchedule`, `SalonResource` |
| Clients and care | `Client`, `ClientNote`, `ColorFormula`, `FormTemplate`, `ConsultationTemplateVersion`, `ConsultationSubmission`, `ConsultationReview`, `Review`, `IntakeForm` and `FormSubmission` (legacy) |
| Booking | `Appointment`, `AppointmentLineItem`, `AppointmentScheduleRevision`, `AppointmentSegment`, `SegmentTransition`, `WaitlistEntry` |

Revisions, transitions, consultation reviews, ledger entries, reversals, and adjustments are append-only. A correction is a new row. The source repo keeps a fuller map in `docs/schema-map.md`.

## Tenancy and roles

There is one salon per deployment. `Location` is a site of that salon, not a tenant. `StoreSettings` is a single row for storefront identity. There is no organization list and no per-location staff membership.

Access comes from boolean flags on `Role`: `canAccessDashboard`, `canManageSalon`, `canManageAppointments`, `canManageClients`, `canManageCareRecords`, `canManageCatalog`, `canManageSchedules`, `canManageOwnSchedule`, `canManageResources`, `canManageInventory`, `canManagePayments`, `canIssueRefunds`, `canManageCommissions`, `canViewReports`, `canManageIntegrations`, `canManageOnboarding`, and the people/role flags.

- `canManageSalon` acts as a manager override for most flags.
- `canManageCareRecords` and `canManageOnboarding` must be set on their own. The manager flag does not grant them.
- Signed-in clients read their own clients, appointments, payments, benefits, points, and gift cards through owner filters.
- Anyone can read active locations. Managers also see inactive ones.
- With more than one active location, booking needs an explicit `locationId`.

The first user created at `/dashboard/init` gets an Admin role with every flag.

## Custom GraphQL operations

Registered in `features/keystone/mutations/index.ts`.

**Queries:** `checkAvailability`, `calculateCommission`, `storefrontStylists`, `storefrontStylist`, `salonOperationsWorkspace`, `salonManagementWorkspace`, `salonLocationStock`, `clientPortal`, `publicStorefrontSettings`, `storefrontBenefits`, `giftCardBalance`, `redirectToInit`.

**Mutations:**

- Accounts: `registerSalonCustomer`.
- Booking: `bookAppointment`, `transitionAppointment`, `scheduleAppointmentSegments`, `transitionAppointmentSegment`, `processAppointmentDeposit`.
- Waitlist: `joinWaitlist`, `joinWaitlistAsClient`, `transitionWaitlistEntry`.
- Schedules and resources: `upsertStylistSchedule`, `setStylistScheduleAvailability`, `setSalonResourceAvailability`.
- Checkout and payments: `completeAppointmentCheckout`, `processPayment`, `confirmPayment`, `refundPayment`, `distributeTip`.
- Refunds and corrections: `reverseSalonTransaction`, `adjustSaleCompensation`.
- Payouts: `payoutCommissions`, `payoutGratuities`.
- Benefits: `sellClientBenefit`, `setClientBenefitStatus`, `redeemClientPackage`.
- Gift cards: `purchaseGiftCard`, `confirmGiftCardPurchase`, `applyGiftCardBalance`.
- Loyalty: `adjustClientPoints`, `completeReferral`.
- Care and consultations: `updateClientCareProfile`, `submitAppointmentReview`, `publishConsultationTemplateVersion`, `submitConsultation`, `reviewConsultation`.
- Stock: `adjustSalonInventory`.
- Setup: `upsertSalonStoreSettings`, `saveSalonRecord` (locations, resources, staff, categories, services, products), `runSalonOnboarding`.

Some dashboard pages still use generated CRUD for simpler lists: membership and package plans, loyalty programs, referrals, review moderation, client notes, color formulas, form templates, and image uploads.

## Main workflow

1. Set up the salon: storefront identity, locations and hours, resources, staff and roles.
2. Build the menu: categories, services (price, duration, deposit, consultation and patch-test flags, specialists, resources).
3. Set weekly hours and time off.
4. Take a booking online or at the desk. The booking stores a service snapshot, segments, and a schedule revision.
5. Publish consultation forms. Clients sign. Staff review before service.
6. Check in, work each step, then complete or mark no-show.
7. Check out: services, retail, discount, tip, package or membership credit. This writes the sale, payment, stock movements, commissions, tip allocations, and loyalty points.
8. Refund, void, or partly refund a sale. Correct commission and tips.
9. Pay out commissions and card tips.

## Onboarding

New dashboard users see onboarding cards in the sidebar. The dialog offers three templates: **Complete Salon Setup**, **Essential Booking Setup**, and **Custom Setup** (edit the seed JSON and paste it back). All three call `runSalonOnboarding` with a seed built from `features/platform/onboarding/lib/seed.json`.

The seed creates a sample salon, "Morrow & Mane": store settings, a location, resources, categories, services, stylists and schedules, two clients, a consultation form, membership plans, a package, a product, a message template, two appointments, gallery images, and two payment providers (manual, installed and default; Stripe, not installed).

The mutation needs `canManageOnboarding`. It runs in one transaction and reports how many records it created and reused, so a second run reuses existing rows. The data is sample data. Remove or replace it before real use.

## Payments and integrations

- **Payment adapters:** `manual` and `stripe`, in `features/integrations/payment`. Desk checkout, recorded deposits, and gift-card sales use the installed manual provider. A manual payment is a record of tender taken outside the app, not a card capture.
- **Stripe:** reads `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY` and `STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET`. The webhook route takes a provider code like `pp_stripe`, checks the signature, logs each event once, rejects amount or currency mismatches, and handles `payment_intent.succeeded`, `payment_intent.payment_failed`, `payment_intent.canceled`, `charge.refunded`, and `refund.updated`. Online deposit and online gift-card collection are not available in the current flows.
- **Email:** booking confirmations, gift-card emails, and password resets are sent with SMTP only when `SMTP_HOST` and `SMTP_FROM` are set. Without SMTP, nothing is sent and the dashboard says reset email is unavailable.
- **File storage:** uploads use S3-compatible storage (`S3_*` variables). Bundled storefront images under `/images` do not need it.
- **Dashboard assistant:** `/api/completion` uses OpenRouter. It needs a signed-in user with `canAccessDashboard`, plus `OPENROUTER_API_KEY` and `OPENROUTER_MODEL`, or a key the user enters locally. Its MCP endpoint exposes generic list tools (`listModels`, `queryData`, `searchModels`, `getFieldsForType`, `lookupInputType`, `createData`, `updateData`, `deleteData`, `modelSpecificSearch`) that run as the caller's session.

## Limitations

- One business per deployment. No organization, tenant, or per-location staff scoping.
- No message dispatcher. `MessageTemplate` and `MessageLog` are not used to send anything. SMS is not implemented.
- `DomainEvent` rows are written on tracked changes but stay pending. Nothing consumes them.
- `WidgetConfiguration` has no widget runtime.
- `ServiceVariant` and `ServiceSegment` have no salon page and are not read by storefront booking or checkout. Edit them in the generic list screens if needed.
- `IntakeForm`, `FormSubmission`, and `ProductSale` are legacy lists, replaced by consultation versions and `SalonTransaction`.
- Clients cannot buy gift cards, pay deposits, or pay for visits online. The storefront gift-card page says "In-salon purchase only".
- Membership billing is not automated. `nextBillingDate` is stored, but no job charges it.
- Tax is a single `TAX_RATE` (default `0.08`). There is no tax provider.
- Tests in `tests/` cover helpers and security rules without a database. They are not an end-to-end check.
