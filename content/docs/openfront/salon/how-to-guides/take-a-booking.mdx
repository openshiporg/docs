---
title: Take a booking
description: Book a client at the desk, record a deposit, and handle consultations.
icon: CalendarPlus
---

import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

<Steps>
<Step>
### Start a booking
Open `/dashboard/platform/appointments/new`. Pick the service, stylist, and location.
</Step>
<Step>
### Choose a time
Select a slot from the picker. If the day is full, add the client to the [waitlist](/docs/openfront/salon/waitlist).
</Step>
<Step>
### Choose or create the client
Search by name, email, or phone, or enter a new client.
</Step>
<Step>
### Confirm
Save. The appointment starts as `pending`. Open it and move it to `confirmed`.
</Step>
<Step>
### Collect the deposit
If the service needs one, record it on the appointment page with a payment method. It is credited at checkout.
</Step>
<Step>
### Consultation
For services needing a consultation, ask the client to complete the form at `/account`. Staff with `canManageCareRecords` review it at `/dashboard/platform/consultations` and set `accepted_for_service`.
</Step>
</Steps>

On the day: move the appointment to `in_progress` at arrival, mark service steps `started` and `completed`, then go to checkout. A client who does not arrive is marked `no_show` once the start time has passed.
