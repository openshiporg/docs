`/dashboard/platform/inventory` needs `canManageInventory`.

## What you see

Products with quantity, reorder point, and status. Items at or below `reorderPoint` are flagged for replenishment.

## Adjust stock

`adjustSalonInventory` takes `productId`, `locationId`, `delta`, a required `reason`, and an idempotency key. Each call writes an `InventoryMovement` with `balanceBefore` and `balanceAfter`.

Movement types: `receive`, `adjustment`, `sale`, `return`.

- Checkout writes `sale` movements.
- Reversing a retail line with disposition `return_to_stock` writes a `return` movement.
- Receiving stock and corrections use the adjustment form on the page.

Stock is checked against the movement ledger. A mismatch is refused so counts cannot drift. `salonLocationStock` returns stock for a location and supports search and paging.
