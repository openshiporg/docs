Reception and managers run the day from `/dashboard/platform/appointments`. It needs `canManageAppointments`.

## Screens

| Route | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/dashboard/platform/appointments` | Day board and booking list by stylist |
| `/dashboard/platform/appointments/new` | Book at the desk |
| `/dashboard/platform/appointments/[id]` | Status changes, reschedule, service steps, deposit, care notes |

## Book at the desk

<Steps>
<Step>
### Pick service, stylist, and location
The form lists active services and the stylists who offer them. With more than one active location, choose the location too.
</Step>
<Step>
### Choose a time
The slot picker calls `checkAvailability` for the stylist and date. Slots respect weekly hours, breaks, time off, existing bookings, the service buffer, and required resources.
</Step>
<Step>
### Choose the client
Search existing clients by name, email, or phone, or enter a new client with name, phone, and email.
</Step>
<Step>
### Book
`bookAppointment` stores a service snapshot, appointment segments, and a schedule revision. Operators skip the booking policy checkbox that online clients must accept. Add notes (up to 2000 characters) before saving.
</Step>
</Steps>

Services flagged `requiresConsultation` or `requiresPatchTest` cannot be booked online. Desk staff can book them.

## Statuses

`transitionAppointment` moves an appointment between `pending`, `confirmed`, `in_progress`, `completed`, `cancelled`, and `no_show`. Allowed moves from the code:

- `pending` to `confirmed`, `cancelled`, `no_show`
- `confirmed` to `in_progress`, `cancelled`, `no_show`
- A no-show is only accepted after the start time has passed.
- A checked-out appointment cannot be restarted.

Cancelling or marking a no-show records the reason you give. Completion happens through [checkout](/docs/openfront/salon/checkout).

## Reschedule

On the detail page, pick a new start time and give a reason. The page calls `scheduleAppointmentSegments`, which re-checks stylist and resource capacity for every segment and writes a new `AppointmentScheduleRevision`. Old revisions stay.

## Service steps

Each appointment has `AppointmentSegment` rows of type `service`, `processing`, `finishing`, or `cleanup`. Move a step with `transitionAppointmentSegment` to `planned`, `started`, `completed`, `skipped`, or `superseded`. The detail page shows steps once there is more than one, or once the appointment is in progress.

## Deposits

When a service has `depositRequired`, record the deposit from the detail page. `processAppointmentDeposit` takes the payment method (`cash`, `credit_card`, `debit_card`, `apple_pay`, `google_pay`), a provider code, and an idempotency key. The deposit is credited against the final checkout total.
