`/dashboard/platform/gift-cards` needs `canManagePayments`.

## Gift card fields

`code`, `initialValue`, `currentBalance`, `recipientName`, `recipientEmail`, `message`, `expiresAt`, `isActive`, `purchasedBy`, and `GiftCardTransaction` rows (`purchase`, `redemption`, `refund`).

## Sell a card

Fill in purchaser (an existing client account), amount, optional recipient name and email, and message. `purchaseGiftCard` creates the card with the full balance and a purchase transaction through the installed manual provider. Confirm with `confirmGiftCardPurchase`. A recipient email triggers the gift-card email when SMTP is set.

## Change a balance

Use the Action menu on a card. `applyGiftCardBalance` takes `giftCardId`, `action` (`redemption` or `refund`), `amount`, optional `appointmentId`, reason, and idempotency key.

- Redemption fails when the balance is too low or the card is expired.
- A refund cannot push the balance above `initialValue`.

## Client lookup

Clients and guests check a balance at `/gift-cards` with `giftCardBalance(code)`.
