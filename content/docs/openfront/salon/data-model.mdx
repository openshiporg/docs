The schema has 58 lists in `features/keystone/models`. The source repo keeps a fuller map in `docs/schema-map.md`.

## Rules

- Money is integer cents.
- Times are instants. Local time comes from `Location.timezone`.
- Most business lists block generated create, update, and delete. They change through custom mutations that check the role, run in a serializable transaction, and take an idempotency key.
- Revisions, transitions, reviews, ledger entries, reversals, and adjustments are append-only.

## Lists by area

- **Identity**: `User`, `Role`
- **Catalog**: `ServiceCategory`, `Service`, `ServiceVariant`, `ServiceSegment`, `SalonImage`, `Product`, `InventoryMovement`
- **People and capacity**: `Location`, `StoreSettings`, `Stylist`, `StylistSchedule`, `SalonResource`
- **Clients and care**: `Client`, `ClientNote`, `ColorFormula`, `FormTemplate`, `ConsultationTemplateVersion`, `ConsultationSubmission`, `ConsultationReview`, `Review`
- **Booking**: `Appointment`, `AppointmentLineItem`, `AppointmentScheduleRevision`, `AppointmentSegment`, `SegmentTransition`, `WaitlistEntry`
- **Money**: `SalonTransaction`, `SalonTransactionLineItem`, `Payment`, `PaymentProvider`, `PaymentSession`, `PaymentWebhookEvent`, `TransactionReversal`, `TransactionReversalLine`, `Commission`, `CommissionAdjustment`, `GratuityLedgerEntry`, `DepositAdjustment`
- **Benefits and loyalty**: `Membership`, `ClientMembership`, `ServicePackage`, `ClientPackage`, `ClientPackageUsage`, `ClientPackageAdjustment`, `GiftCard`, `GiftCardTransaction`, `LoyaltyProgram`, `ClientPoints`, `Referral`
- **Messages and system**: `MessageTemplate`, `MessageLog`, `DomainEvent`, `WidgetConfiguration`
