## Staff schedules

`/dashboard/platform/schedules` needs `canManageSchedules` (all stylists) or `canManageOwnSchedule` (own only).

A `StylistSchedule` has a `type`: `recurring` (weekly, with `dayOfWeek`), `one_time`, or `time_off` (both with `localDate`). It has `startTime`, `endTime`, optional `breakStart` and `breakEnd`, `isAvailable`, `reason`, and a `revision`.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Set weekly hours
Use the weekly editor. `upsertStylistSchedule` with `type: "recurring"`, a day, start, end, and an optional break. The break must fall inside working hours.
</Step>
<Step>
### Add time off
Enter a date, times, and reason. It saves as `type: "time_off"` with `isAvailable: false`. The "Block day" menu is a shortcut.
</Step>
<Step>
### Withdraw
`setStylistScheduleAvailability` turns a row on or off. It needs the `expectedRevision`; a stale one fails with "Schedule was changed by another operator".
</Step>
</Steps>

## Resources

`/dashboard/platform/resources` needs `canManageResources` or `canManageAppointments`.

A `SalonResource` has `name`, `type` (`station`, `room`, `shampoo_bowl`, `equipment`, `color_bar`, `other`), `capacity`, `location`, `notes`, and `isActive`. Services list the resources they need, and availability counts each booking against capacity.

Create and edit with `saveSalonRecord` (`kind: "resource"`). Turn one off with `setSalonResourceAvailability`; it fails while the resource has active appointments or segment assignments. Capacity and location cannot change while it has active reservations.
