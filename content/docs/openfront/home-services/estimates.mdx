An estimate offers good, better and best options priced from the published price book. It is presented and sold as a version, and an expired estimate is requoted. Change orders add work to a sold job.

At every sale and approved change order, the bound home-solicitation policy is evaluated and stored. When a cancellation right arises, a notice is issued with the statutory statement and cancellation forms. While the notice is open, the covered work cannot start unless a second person approves an override with a reason. A buyer's cancellation received before the deadline cancels the covered lines and opens a refund. If there is no policy, the calendar is unknown or there is no verified notice text in the sale's language, the sale stops.
