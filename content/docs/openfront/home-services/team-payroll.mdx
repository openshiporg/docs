**Technicians** lists credentials, skills, shifts and on-call. A timesheet is proposed from the visit record, and the technician adds what is missing and sends it in. Pay plans hold hourly, overtime, commission and spiff terms as versions. A pay period is calculated, reviewed, approved and exported. **My pay** shows each person their hours, overtime, commission and anything taken back.

## Pages

| Page | Route | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| My timesheet | `/dashboard/platform/my-timesheet` | Your day from the visit record: add what is missing, send it in. |
| My pay | `/dashboard/platform/my-pay` | Your pay by period: hours, overtime, commission and anything taken back. |
| Timesheets | `/dashboard/platform/timesheets` | Approve submitted days; add time that was missed. |
| Pay plans | `/dashboard/platform/pay-plans` | Hourly, overtime, commission and spiff terms, versions and who is on which. |
| Pay periods | `/dashboard/platform/pay-periods` | Calculate, review, approve and export a pay period. |
| Technicians | `/dashboard/platform/team` | Technicians, credentials, skills, shifts and on-call. |

## Commands

Named mutations for this area. Each checks permissions on the server, takes an idempotency key and writes an audit event.

`verifyCredential`, `revokeCredential`, `proposeTimesheet`, `addTimeSegment`, `removeTimeSegment`, `submitTimesheet`, `approveTimesheet`, `rejectTimesheet`, `adjustTimesheet`, `definePayPlan`, `savePayPlanDraft`, `requestPayPlanPublish`, `publishPayPlanVersion`, `assignPayPlan`, `setAttribution`, `openPayPeriod`, `calculatePayPeriod`, `addPayAdjustment`, `requestPayPeriodApproval`, `approvePayPeriod`, `reopenPayPeriod`, `exportPayPeriod`.
