Permits and inspections gate job completion. The refrigerant log records cylinders, charges and recoveries for EPA 608, and leak rates are evaluated against the threshold. **Liens** lists Texas lien dates by month for completed jobs. **Policies** shows each jurisdiction policy pack with its versions and sources.

## Pages

| Page | Route | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Permits | `/dashboard/platform/permits` | Permits and inspections that gate job completion. |
| Refrigerant log | `/dashboard/platform/refrigerant` | EPA 608 log, cylinders and leak rates. |
| Liens | `/dashboard/platform/liens` | Texas lien dates by month for completed jobs. |
| Policies | `/dashboard/platform/policies` | Jurisdiction policy packs, versions and sources. |

## Commands

Named mutations for this area. Each checks permissions on the server, takes an idempotency key and writes an audit event.

`openPermit`, `advancePermit`, `requestInspection`, `recordInspectionResult`, `reinspect`, `saveRefrigerantCylinder`, `logRefrigerant`, `resolveLeakRate`, `checkRefrigerantInstall`, `trackLien`, `advanceLien`.
