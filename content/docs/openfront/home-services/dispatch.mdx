---

The dispatch board shows arrival windows by technician. `recommendDispatch` ranks eligible technicians by licence, skill, shift and drive time. Assignment is a compare-and-set on the visit plus a lock on each technician's day, so two dispatchers, or a dispatcher and an agent, cannot both win.

**Jobs** shows jobs, visits, the timeline and work lines. The field view at `/dashboard/field/today` is a technician's day on a phone: visits, status, the checklist and photos, with each visit at `/dashboard/field/visits/[id]`. Completing a job checks open permits and cancellation notices first.

## Pages

| Page | Route | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dispatch board | `/dashboard/platform/dispatch` | Arrival windows by technician; assign, reassign, recommendations. |
| Jobs | `/dashboard/platform/jobs` | Jobs, visits, timeline and work lines. |
| Field app | `/dashboard/field/today` | A technician's day on a phone: visits, status, checklist, photos. |

## Lifecycles

| List | Statuses |
| --- | --- |
| `Job` | `draft`, `booked`, `scheduled`, `in_progress`, `on_hold`, `work_complete`, `awaiting_inspection`, `completed`, `closed`, `cancelled` |
| `Visit` | `unassigned`, `scheduled`, `dispatched`, `en_route`, `on_site`, `working`, `paused`, `done`, `cancelled`, `no_access` |
| `ScheduledJob` | `pending`, `running`, `done`, `failed`, `cancelled` |
| `WorkLine` | `planned`, `completed`, `removed`, `cancelled_by_buyer` |

## Commands

Named mutations for this area. Each checks permissions on the server, takes an idempotency key and writes an audit event.

`recommendDispatch`, `assignVisit`, `unassignVisit`, `submitChecklist`, `setVisitStatus`, `holdJob`, `releaseJobHold`, `cancelJob`, `completeJob`.
