A customer has contacts with consent by channel and one or more service locations. Gate codes and lockbox codes are sealed with AES-256-GCM, and revealing one is a logged command.

## Pages

| Page | Route | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Customers | `/dashboard/platform/customers` | Customers, contacts, service locations and consent. |

| List | Statuses |
| --- | --- |

## Commands

Named mutations for this area. Each checks permissions on the server, takes an idempotency key and writes an audit event.

`addCustomer`, `addServiceLocation`, `addContactPoint`, `storeAccessSecret`, `revealAccessSecret`.
