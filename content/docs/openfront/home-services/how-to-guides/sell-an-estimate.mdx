<Steps>
<Step>

### Draft

Open and draft the estimate from the price book (`openEstimate`, `draftEstimate`, `saveEstimateOption`).

</Step>
<Step>

### Present

Present the options to the customer (`presentEstimate`).

</Step>
<Step>

### Sell

Sell the chosen option (`sellEstimate`). The home-solicitation policy is evaluated.

</Step>
<Step>

### Deliver the notice

If a cancellation right arose, deliver the notice (`deliverCancellationNotice`). Covered work waits until it closes.

</Step>
</Steps>
