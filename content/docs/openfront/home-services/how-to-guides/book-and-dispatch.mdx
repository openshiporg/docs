import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

<Steps>
<Step>

### Book

On **Book a job**, pick the customer and location, the job type and a window (`holdWindow`, `bookJob`).

</Step>
<Step>

### Recommend

On the **Dispatch board**, ask for a recommendation (`recommendDispatch`).

</Step>
<Step>

### Assign

Assign the visit to a technician (`assignVisit`).

</Step>
<Step>

### Work the visit

The technician sets the status and submits the checklist from the field view (`setVisitStatus`, `submitChecklist`).

</Step>
</Steps>
