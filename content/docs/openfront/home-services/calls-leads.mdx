**Today** shows booked hours against capacity, calls, unassigned visits, technicians, revenue and open approvals. **Leads** is the inbox for demand from every channel, with response timers, source and disposition. **Calls** is the call log with recordings and transcripts, from the AI receptionist and from people.

**Book a job** picks the customer and location, the job type and an arrival window. A hold reserves hours in the window, and a full window is refused.

The AI receptionist answers recorded calls through MCP tools: customer and location lookup, job-type match, open windows, hold, release and confirm, consent, hand-off to a person, and paging on-call. Each write needs a grant for that exact tool. Access codes, merges and cancellations are not available to it.

## Pages

| Page | Route | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Today | `/dashboard/platform/today` | Booked vs capacity, calls, unassigned visits, techs, revenue and open approvals. |
| Leads | `/dashboard/platform/leads` | Inbox with response timers, source and disposition. |
| Calls | `/dashboard/platform/calls` | Call log with recordings and transcripts, AI and human. |
| Book a job | `/dashboard/platform/book` | Customer and location, job type, arrival window, confirm. |

## Commands

Named mutations for this area. Each checks permissions on the server, takes an idempotency key and writes an audit event.

`materializeWindows`, `holdWindow`, `releaseHold`, `bookJob`, `ingestLead`, `markLeadContacted`, `qualifyLead`, `loseLead`, `markLeadSpam`, `mergeLead`, `recordConsent`, `handoffToHuman`, `pageOnCall`.
