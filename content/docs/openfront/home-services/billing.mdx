An invoice is drafted from the job, posted, and paid by card, cash or check. A void, credit memo, write-off or refund over policy needs a second person. Payments group into daily batches by tender, and unknown card results are listed until they are resolved. Third-party payers such as home-warranty companies and property managers have payer profiles and authorizations.

**Accounting** closes receivables by period and stages the accounting export.
