The price book holds categories and items, labour rates, after-hours multipliers and tiered markup tables. It is edited as a draft version, submitted for second-person approval, and published on its effective date. Supplier costs can be imported into a draft.

## Pages

| Page | Route | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price book | `/dashboard/platform/pricebook` | Categories, items, markup tables and publishing. |

## Lifecycles

| List | Statuses |
| --- | --- |
| `PriceBookVersion` | `draft`, `pending_approval`, `published`, `active`, `retired`, `discarded` |

## Commands

Named mutations for this area. Each checks permissions on the server, takes an idempotency key and writes an audit event.

`openPriceBook`, `startPriceBookDraft`, `saveDraftItems`, `removeDraftItems`, `saveDraftSettings`, `submitPriceBookVersion`, `reopenPriceBookDraft`, `discardPriceBookVersion`, `importSupplierCosts`.
