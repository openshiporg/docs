---
title: Inventory and purchasing
description: Warehouse and truck stock, replenishment, purchase orders, receiving and cycle counts.
icon: Warehouse
---

Stock is held in the warehouse and on each truck. Min/max rules propose replenishment, which is decided and filled. Purchase orders are approved, sent and received, and vendor bills are three-way matched, with variances accepted by a second person. Cycle counts post the difference after approval. Serialized units are tracked to the installed equipment.

## Pages

| Page | Route | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inventory | `/dashboard/platform/inventory` | Warehouse and truck stock. |
| Replenishment | `/dashboard/platform/replenishment` | Min/max proposals for trucks and the warehouse. |
| Purchase orders | `/dashboard/platform/purchase-orders` | Orders to suppliers, approval and three-way match. |
| Receiving | `/dashboard/platform/receiving` | Orders on their way, received into stock. |
| Cycle counts | `/dashboard/platform/counts` | Count a location and post the difference. |

## Commands

Named mutations for this area. Each checks permissions on the server, takes an idempotency key and writes an audit event.

`saveStockItem`, `saveStockLocation`, `consumeStock`, `installSerializedUnit`, `transferStock`, `adjustStock`, `saveMinMaxRule`, `proposeReplenishment`, `decideReplenishment`, `fillReplenishment`, `startCycleCount`, `submitCycleCount`, `requestCycleCountApproval`, `postCycleCount`, `openPurchaseOrder`, `submitPurchaseOrder`, `approvePurchaseOrder`, `markPurchaseOrderSent`, `cancelPurchaseOrder`, `receivePurchaseOrder`, `requestOverReceipt`, `recordVendorBill`, `requestBillVariance`, `acceptBillVariance`.
