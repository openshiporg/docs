Membership plans are drafted and published as versions, with a price and what each includes. Selling a membership keeps a card on file through Stripe Checkout. **Due for service** is the call list of visits members and agreements are owed. **Renewals** lists memberships ending soon, with the message to send and a CSV.

**Billing runs** charges the month's membership payments when staff press the button. There is no scheduler. Failed charges are retried from the run. **Service agreements** cover planned maintenance for commercial customers.

## Pages

| Page | Route | What it shows |

| Renewals | `/dashboard/platform/renewals` | Memberships ending soon, with the message to send and a CSV. |

## Commands

Named mutations for this area. Each checks permissions on the server, takes an idempotency key and writes an audit event.
