Openfront Analytics records pageviews, custom events, revenue, and Core Web Vitals from a small script, and turns them into traffic, source, location, device, goal, funnel, realtime, and performance reports.

## Privacy model

- No cookies and no browser storage for tracking.
- A visitor is `sha256(daily salt, site, IP, User-Agent)`. The salt comes from `SESSION_SECRET` and the UTC date. The IP is never stored.
- Global Privacy Control is always honored. Bots are ignored by User-Agent.
- Hits are accepted only from the site's domain and its subdomains.
- A visit closes after 30 minutes of inactivity.

## Who uses it

- **Site owners and teams** read reports and manage sites, goals, funnels, shared links, and keys.
- **Public viewers** open a public dashboard or a shared link and see aggregate reports only.
- **Developers** send server-side events, import historical visits, call GraphQL, or build their own dashboard.

## Surfaces

| Surface | Location |
|---|---|
| Tracking script | `/assets/script.js` |
| Collector | `POST /api/track` |
| Operator dashboard | `/dashboard/platform/*` |
| Public site | `/`, `/sites/<slug>`, `/share/<slug>` |
| GraphQL | `/api/graphql` |
| MCP and AI chat | `/api/mcp-transport/[transport]`, `/api/completion` |
