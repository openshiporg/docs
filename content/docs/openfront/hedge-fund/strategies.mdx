A strategy version is drafted, sealed and backtested on a recorded dataset, with statistics such as the deflated Sharpe ratio. It then trades a sleeve on the firm's broker paper trading account before any promotion.

**Promotion and capital** moves a version toward real money: requested, evidence checked, approved by a second person and effective. A capital allocation sets what a live sleeve may trade with. It is versioned, and a new amount is a new row that supersedes the effective one.

## Pages

| Page | Route | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Strategy studio | `/dashboard/platform/strategies` | Strategies, versions, backtests and promotion evidence. |
| Promotion and capital | `/dashboard/platform/capital` | Paper to live promotions and capital allocations. |

## Lifecycles

| List | Statuses |
| --- | --- |
| `Strategy` | `active`, `archived` |
| `StrategyVersion.stage` | `draft`, `sealed`, `backtested`, `paper_validated`, `live_approved`, `retired`, `rejected` |
| `Backtest` | `queued`, `running`, `completed`, `failed`, `cancelled` |
| `Promotion` | `requested`, `evidence_checked`, `approved`, `rejected`, `effective`, `withdrawn` |
| `CapitalAllocation` | `proposed`, `approved`, `effective`, `superseded`, `rejected` |

## Commands

Named mutations for this area. Each checks permissions on the server, takes an idempotency key and writes an audit event.

`addStrategy`, `draftStrategyVersion`, `sealStrategyVersion`, `runBacktest`, `requestPaperValidation`, `openStrategySleeve`, `requestPromotion`, `withdrawPromotion`, `proposeAllocation`.
