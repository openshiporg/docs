A NAV run goes from locked inputs through pricing, accruals, fees, allocation and review. Management fees accrue daily and performance fees are recomputed against the class high-water mark and hurdle, each figure rounded once, half-even.

In the month-end close, the administrator's NAV file is imported and the run is tied to it within a tolerance, finalised and published as investor statements. Performance fees crystallise, series roll up into the lead series, and a finalised run can be restated with its impact on investors. **Reconciliation** matches prime-broker and administrator statements and works breaks. It also records the trade blotter and capital files sent to the administrator.
