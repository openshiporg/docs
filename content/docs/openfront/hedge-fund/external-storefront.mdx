To build a custom fund home page, copy the skill and give it to your LLM. The skill explains that `features/storefront` holds the built-in page, and that public text comes only from published marketing pieces.

Skill file: [`/skills/openfront-hedge-fund-custom-storefront/SKILL.md`](/skills/openfront-hedge-fund-custom-storefront/SKILL.md)

## What a separate site must keep

- Signed-out visitors see only a published `fund_page` piece for a `public_506c` vehicle.
- Fund figures are shown only to signed-in users through the same access rules.
- Statements are served by the statement route, which checks delegation and the figure hash.
