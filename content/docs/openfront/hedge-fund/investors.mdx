The investor register holds investors, delegates, eligibility, sanctions screening, holdings, liquidity terms and statements. Tax ids and bank details are sealed, and revealing one is an audited command.

A subscription moves through documents, eligibility, screening, funds received and acceptance before units are issued. A redemption is validated against the notice period and lock-up, queued for dealing, run through the gate and priced. The gate is deterministic: an investor-level cap first, then fund capacity shared by priority and pro rata. Payments are approved by a second person, sent and confirmed.
