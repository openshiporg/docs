Openfront Hedge Fund is the operating system for a hedge fund. Research strategies, test them, trade them on a broker paper trading account, promote them to live trading under risk limits, and keep the fund's books, investors and compliance on one system. Strategies are versioned and sealed before they are backtested. Orders come from people and agents, pass an ordered pre-trade pipeline and maker-checker approval, and route to a connected broker. The fund side covers vehicles and share classes, the general ledger, shadow and official NAV, the month-end close with the administrator, investors, subscriptions and redemptions, and the compliance program.

The live site is [openfront-hedge-fund-production.up.railway.app](https://openfront-hedge-fund-production.up.railway.app). It runs the sample firm, Halvorsen Ridge Capital. The staff dashboard is at `/dashboard`.

To install it, see [Getting started](/docs/openfront/hedge-fund/getting-started).

## Concepts

| Concept | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Vehicle and share class | A fund vehicle (master, feeder or paper) with share classes and their fee terms. |
| Strategy and version | A strategy definition. A version is sealed, backtested, validated on paper and approved for live trading. |
| Sleeve | A slice of a vehicle that a strategy trades, bound to one broker account. |
| Order and broker order | An order is the firm's intent. A broker order is what is sent to the venue, with its own status. |
| Risk policy | A published version of the limits every order is checked against. |
| Control state | A halt or arm switch per firm, vehicle, sleeve, connection or agent. Arming is time-boxed. |
| NAV run | A shadow or official valuation of a vehicle, from locked inputs to fees, allocation, review, administrator tie and publication. |
| Approval request | A maker-checker request bound to the exact payload. The person who asked, and any agent, can never approve it. |

## Stack

Next.js 16 (App Router) with React 19, Keystone 6 with a GraphQL API at `/api/graphql`, Prisma with PostgreSQL, Tailwind CSS 4 and shadcn/ui. Sign-in uses Keystone password auth with stateless session cookies. Node.js 20 or later.

## Tenancy and ownership

One deployment serves one firm and its vehicles. Staff act through roles and permissions. Tax ids, bank details and broker API keys are sealed with AES-256-GCM, and reading one is an audited command. Investor users see only the investors they are live delegates for. Paper books never post to the fund's general ledger or NAV.

## Rules the server enforces

- Money posted to a ledger is integer minor units with a currency code. Prices and quantities are exact decimals. Float arithmetic on money is blocked by lint.
- Every state change is a custom mutation that checks the expected version, writes a hash-chained audit event in the same transaction and takes an idempotency key.
- Approvals are maker-checker: the person who asked can never approve, and an agent can never be the approver. An approval is bound to the payload hash and used once.
- Agents have no generic create, update or delete tools. Every change is a named domain command, and compliance decisions are made only by people.
- With no broker bound, order entry stops at "a broker must be connected". Nothing fills without a broker.
- A halt is never blocked. Arming a live scope needs a second person.
- No figure is edited in place. A correction, restatement or new allocation is a new row.
- Venue I/O never runs inside a database transaction.
