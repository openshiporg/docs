The **Compliance calendar** lists filings, attestations and personal reports due, and the firm's policies. Every order and personal trade is checked against the restricted and watch lists. Personal trading covers pre-clearance, brokerage accounts, holdings and transaction reports, gifts and political contributions.

**Filings** plans each filing from the firm and its books, with the ownership monitor and the Form 13F information table, exported as EDGAR XML or CSV. **Marketing review** publishes nothing until compliance approves the exact text. **Incidents** move from open to contained, assessed, notified and closed, with the Reg S-P notice clock. Compliance decisions are made only by people.
