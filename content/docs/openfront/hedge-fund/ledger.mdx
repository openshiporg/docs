The general ledger holds journals posted from trading, accruals and dealing. A posted journal is reversed, never edited. A manual journal needs approval, and an accounting period is soft-closed and then locked.

**Fund structure** holds vehicles, share classes, fee terms and unit dealing. Corporate actions are announced, verified and applied.

## Pages

| Page | Route | What it shows |

| Ledger | `/dashboard/platform/ledger` | Trial balance, journals and accounting periods. |

| `Vehicle` | `forming`, `active`, `winding_down`, `liquidated` |
| `ShareClass` | `open`, `closed` |
| `CorporateAction` | `announced`, `verified`, `applied` |

## Commands

Named mutations for this area. Each checks permissions on the server, takes an idempotency key and writes an audit event.

`lockAccountingPeriod`, `openShareClass`, `addFeeTerms`, `dealUnits`, `recordCorporateAction`, `verifyCorporateAction`, `applyCorporateAction`, `requestManualJournal`, `reverseJournal`.
