People and agents propose orders. The pre-trade pipeline runs in order: structure, account binding and the pooled-vehicle guard, controls, quote sanity, compliance (restricted and watch lists, Reg SHO, locates), risk on the projected portfolio, and capacity and duplicates. Every check is recorded. A fail rejects the proposal, and a warning sends it to a person.

An approved order routes to the sleeve's bound broker account. Fills are booked as executions, deduplicated by the venue fill id, into tax lots and positions. **Brokers** connects Alpaca and binds discovered accounts to vehicles. **Market data** lists quote sources and recorded research history.
