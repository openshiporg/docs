A risk policy is drafted and published as a version, and every order is checked against the governing version: order size, ADV, position, sector, gross, net, daily loss, drawdown, VaR and buying power. Breaches are acknowledged, cured or waived, and daily risk is snapshotted.

Controls are halt and arm switches per firm, vehicle, sleeve, broker connection and agent. Arming is time-boxed, needs every parent scope armed and the connection active, and a live scope trades only under a published risk policy. Paper scopes arm directly. Live scopes need a second person. A halt is never blocked.

## Commands

Named mutations for this area. Each checks permissions on the server, takes an idempotency key and writes an audit event.

`armControl`, `haltControl`, `draftRiskPolicy`, `withdrawRiskPolicyDraft`, `releaseSoftHold`.
