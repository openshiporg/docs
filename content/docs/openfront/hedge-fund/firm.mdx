**Approvals** lists requests waiting for a second person with the required permission. Firm controls such as promotion thresholds, the arm window, AI live autonomy and agent-owner separation change only this way. **Audit log** recomputes the hash chain on every view and shows where it breaks. **Operations** lists background jobs and their last runs. **Firm settings** holds the firm profile, calendars and approval policies.

## Pages

| Page | Route | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |

| Audit log | `/dashboard/platform/audit` | Hash-chained audit events and chain verification. |
