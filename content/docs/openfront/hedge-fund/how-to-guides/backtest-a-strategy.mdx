---
title: Backtest and promote a strategy
description: Seal a strategy version, backtest it, validate it on paper and promote it.
icon: TrendingUp
---

import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

<Steps>
<Step>

### Draft

In **Strategy studio**, add the strategy and draft a version (`addStrategy`, `draftStrategyVersion`).

</Step>
<Step>

### Seal and backtest

Seal the version and run a backtest (`sealStrategyVersion`, `runBacktest`).

</Step>
<Step>

### Paper

Open a sleeve on the paper account and request paper validation (`openStrategySleeve`, `requestPaperValidation`).

</Step>
<Step>

### Promote

Request promotion (`requestPromotion`). A second person approves it under **Approvals**.

</Step>
</Steps>
