## Prices

`setRetailPrice` publishes a `PriceBookEntry` for a price book and variant: `amountCents`, `minQuantity`, `startsAt`, `endsAt`. The store's default price book wins; the variant default price is the fallback.

## Tax

`configureStoreTaxRate` sets a store tax rate in basis points per `TaxCategory` (onboarding creates Standard and Exempt).

## Promotions

`configurePromotion` creates a `Promotion` with rules. `/dashboard/platform/promotions` lists them by status, window, and use. Percentage promotions need basis points: "Percentage promotions require basis points". Cashiers apply them with `applyPromotionToCart`; each use writes a `PromotionRedemption`.
