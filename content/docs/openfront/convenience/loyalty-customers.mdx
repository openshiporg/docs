import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

Pages: `/dashboard/platform/loyalty`, `/customers`, `/customers/[id]`.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Configure the program
`configureLoyaltyProgram`. Onboarding creates "Store Rewards": 1 point per dollar, 100 points worth one dollar.
</Step>
<Step>
### Enroll
`enrollLoyaltyAccount` for a customer.
</Step>
<Step>
### Adjust or expire
`adjustLoyaltyPoints` with a reason; `expireLoyaltyPoints` expires due points. Each change writes a `LoyaltyLedgerEntry`.
</Step>
<Step>
### Redeem at the register
`applyLoyaltyRedemptionToCart`.
</Step>
</Steps>

Customers see their balance and last 10 entries on the storefront (`storefrontRewards`).
