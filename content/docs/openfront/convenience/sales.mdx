`/dashboard/platform/sales` lists receipts by status. `/sales/[id]` shows lines, tenders, refunds, returns, ID checks, and promotions.

Sale statuses: open, completed, partially_refunded, refunded, voided. Tender statuses: pending, authorized, captured, partially_refunded, refunded, failed, cancelled.

## Refund

<Steps>
<Step>
### Pick the sale and lines
From the sale page or the refund picker on POS.
</Step>
<Step>
### Choose reasons
`refundPosSale` needs `reason` (customer_return, price_correction, duplicate_charge, service_recovery, fraud, other) and, per line, a return reason (unwanted, defective, expired, wrong_item, recall, other). "Open register session not found for refund" means the drawer is closed.
</Step>
<Step>
### Allocate to tenders
Refunds go back across the original tenders (`tenderAllocations`). "At least one original tender allocation is required".
</Step>
<Step>
### Stock disposition
Each line restocks to the original lot, goes to quarantine, or is destroyed. Non-returnable items need `overrideNonReturnable` with an `overrideReason`, which can need a `managerPin`.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Void

`voidCompletedPosSale` voids a cash sale and reverses its stock and drawer effects. It is a separate command from refunds and may need a manager PIN.
