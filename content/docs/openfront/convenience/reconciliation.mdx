`/dashboard/platform/reconciliation`.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Generate the day
`generateStoreDayReconciliation` builds a `StoreDay` with `StoreDayLine` rows.
</Step>
<Step>
### Review and approve
`approveStoreDayReconciliation`. Statuses: draft, in_review, reconciled, locked, reopened.
</Step>
<Step>
### Prepare the deposit
`prepareBankDeposit`. "Deposit amount exceeds unreserved counted cash".
</Step>
<Step>
### Track it
`transitionBankDeposit`: prepared, in_transit, deposited, verified (needs "Bank reference is required to verify a deposit"), voided.
</Step>
</Steps>
