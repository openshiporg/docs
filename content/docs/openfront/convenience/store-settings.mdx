title: Store settings

icon: Settings
---

`/dashboard/platform/store-settings`.

- `configureBusinessBrand`: name, brand mark (1 to 4 uppercase letters or digits), logo, colors, address, hours, and draft or published storefront.
- `configureRegister`: add or retire registers.
- `configureInventoryLocation`: add or deactivate stock locations. "Move or adjust the stock in this location before deactivating it".

Publishing sets the storefront store used by [the public site](/docs/openfront/convenience/external-storefront). Only one business may be published.
