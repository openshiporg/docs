`/dashboard/platform/tills` lists sessions by status; `/tills/[id]` shows one.

## Statuses

`RegisterSession`: open, closing, suspended, closed.

## Cash movements

`recordCashMovement` with `type` and a required reason: opening_float, paid_in, paid_out, safe_drop, correction, plus system-written sale_payment, refund, lottery_sale, lottery_payout, money_service_in, money_service_out, closing_count. Direction is in or out.

## Close a till

<Steps>
<Step>
### Count the drawer
Enter `countedCashCents`.
</Step>
<Step>
### Close
`closeRegisterSession`. Expected cash is computed from the float, cash sales and refunds, movements, lottery, and money services; the difference is over/short.
</Step>
<Step>
### Close the shift
`closeEmployeeShift`. "Close all linked registers before completing this shift".
</Step>
</Steps>

Registers themselves are added in [Store settings](/docs/openfront/convenience/store-settings) with `configureRegister`.
