## Stock

`/dashboard/platform/stock` shows stock by location with low stock, negative stock, and expiring lots. `StockLevel` rows hold quantity; `InventoryLot` tracks lot, expiry, and status (active, quarantined, consumed, expired, recalled). Every movement writes an `InventoryLedgerEntry`.

## Counts

<Steps>
<Step>
### Start
`startInventoryCount` with a location and type full, cycle, or spot.
</Step>
<Step>
### Count lines
`recordInventoryCountLine` with `actualQuantity`. A count increase needs a unit cost: "Count increase is missing its accepted unit cost".
</Step>
<Step>
### Submit and approve
`transitionInventoryCount`: draft, counting, submitted, approved, cancelled. `approveInventoryCount` posts differences.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Adjustments

`postInventoryAdjustment` for corrections, spoilage, waste, and theft. A zero adjustment or one that makes stock negative is refused.

## Transfers

`draftInventoryTransfer`, then `transitionInventoryTransfer`: draft, approved, shipped, received, cancelled. Lot allocations are recorded per line.
