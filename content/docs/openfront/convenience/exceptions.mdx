title: Exceptions
description: Operational alerts and heartbeats at /dashboard/platform/exceptions.
icon: TriangleAlert
---

`evaluateOperationalAlerts` checks cash, fuel, stock, expiry, and settlement variances and creates `OperationalAlert` rows. Staff work them with `transitionOperationalAlert`: open, acknowledged, resolved, dismissed. `recordOperationalHeartbeat` records a component's health.

Alerts are evaluated when staff press **Evaluate now** on the Exceptions page. There is no scheduled alert worker.
