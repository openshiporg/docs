`/dashboard/platform/payments`. `configurePaymentProvider` sets an adapter on a `PaymentProvider`.

| Adapter | Behavior |
|---|---|
| `manual_external` | Records a card or wallet payment an outside terminal already took. No webhooks |
| `hmac_json_v1` | Also accepts signed webhooks at `/api/webhooks/payment/[providerId]` |

Both require a short-lived signed authority proof for each non-cash tender and refund. The signing key is an `env://NAME` credential reference whose value is 32+ characters.

## Webhooks

Headers: `x-openfront-timestamp`, `x-openfront-signature: v1=<hex>` (HMAC-SHA256 of `timestamp.body`, within five minutes). Events: `payment.captured`, `payment.failed`, `refund.completed`, `refund.failed`. Duplicate event ids are replayed, not re-applied (`WebhookEvent`).
