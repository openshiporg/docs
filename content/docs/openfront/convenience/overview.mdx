`/dashboard/platform` loads `conveniencePlatformProjection` and shows:

- today's net sales, transaction count, and refunds
- open drawers (`RegisterSession` in status open)
- people on shift (`Shift`)
- a "needs you" list: open operational alerts, open compliance incidents, pending approvals

The sidebar is built from `conveniencePlatformNavigation`, so it shows only the areas your role can read. See [Roles and permissions](/docs/openfront/convenience/roles-permissions).

Everything is scoped to the business and the stores you are assigned to as an `Employee`.
