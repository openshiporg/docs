## Reports

`/dashboard/platform/reports` shows `convenienceReportingSummary` for a period, by store.

## Exports

`/dashboard/platform/exports`.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Add a destination
`configureExportDestination` for the stores to include. "At least one export store is required".
</Step>
<Step>
### Generate
`generateDailyAccountingExport` produces a CSV artifact up to 5 MiB with a checksum.
</Step>
<Step>
### Download
`downloadExportArtifact`.
</Step>
<Step>
### Mark delivery
`transitionExportBatch`: ready, delivering, delivered, failed, cancelled. "Failed export requires a failure message".
</Step>
</Steps>
