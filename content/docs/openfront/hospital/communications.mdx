## Sending

`sendSecurePatientMessage` takes `facilityId`, `patientId`, optional appointment, template, and installation, plus `channel` (`sms`, `email`, `phone`), `purpose`, `subject`, and `body`.

| Channel | Result |
| --- | --- |
| `phone` | `manual_required` task. Staff call, then complete it with `transitionPatientMessage` |
| `email`, `sms` | `queued` for an active `communications` integration, then `sent` and `delivered` from webhooks |

The app does not send email or SMS by itself. Without an active communications installation, use phone tasks. Message bodies are protected by a specific permission as well as facility scope.

## Templates and preferences

On the communications tab of `/dashboard/platform/operational-fabric`:

- `registerCommunicationTemplate` and `reviseCommunicationTemplate` keep versioned templates (`templateKey`, `channel`, `subjectTemplate`).
- `setCommunicationPreference` stores per-patient `allowed` or `opted_out` by channel and purpose.
