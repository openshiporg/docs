## Bed lifecycle

`available` → `reserved` → `occupied` → `cleaning` → `available`. `out_of_service` removes a bed from use. Bed status is changed only by workflows.

## Actions

| Action | Mutation |
| --- | --- |
| Hold a bed (expiry within 24 hours) | `reserveBed` |
| Release a hold | `releaseBedReservation` |
| Admit | `admitPatient` |
| Transfer or discharge | `transitionAdmission(..., bedId, dischargeDisposition)` |
| Finish cleaning or mark out of service | `transitionBedReadiness` |

An admission needs an open or in-progress encounter. A transfer needs a different destination bed. Reservations move `active` → `consumed`, `released`, or `expired`. `BedOccupancyEvent` records every assignment, transfer, and release.

Facility setup (wards, rooms, beds) is on `/dashboard/platform/facilities`. Room types are `private`, `semi_private`, `icu`, and `procedure`.
