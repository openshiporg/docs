---
title: Confirm a request and check the patient in
description: From a public request to an open encounter.
icon: UserCheck
---

import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

<Steps>
<Step>
### Confirm
On `/dashboard/platform/appointments` confirm the `requested` appointment. If the provider is blocked, the confirm fails; change the time or the schedule.
</Step>
<Step>
### Review intake
At `/dashboard/platform/intake` mark the submitted form reviewed.
</Step>
<Step>
### Check in
On the day, check the appointment in. A care queue ticket appears.
</Step>
<Step>
### Call and open
At `/dashboard/platform/care-queue` call the patient, then open an encounter from the ticket.
</Step>
</Steps>
