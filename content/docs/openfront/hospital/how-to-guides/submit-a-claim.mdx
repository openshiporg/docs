---
title: Prepare and track a claim
description: From coverage to payer acknowledgement.
icon: Receipt
---

import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

<Steps>
<Step>
### Record coverage
On the billing tab, add the coverage and mark it `active`.
</Step>
<Step>
### Prepare
At `/dashboard/platform/claims` choose the encounter and coverage and enter the billed amount.
</Step>
<Step>
### Submit
Move the claim to `submitted` once sent to the payer by your own process.
</Step>
<Step>
### Record the result
Mark it acknowledged or rejected, with a denial reason. Correct and resubmit if needed.
</Step>
</Steps>
