## Staff

`/dashboard/platform/staff`. Create users, assign a `Role` and facilities, and edit roles. Needs `canManagePeople` and `canManageRoles`.

## Audit trail

`/dashboard/platform/audit` shows `ClinicalAuditLog` (event type, actor type, time, metadata) and `WorkflowTransition` (from and to status and version, result). `PhiReadAudit` is written by the GraphQL endpoint whenever PHI fields are read, and is visible on `/dashboard/phi-read-audits`.

## Reports

`/dashboard/platform/reports` needs `canViewReports` plus the scope of each underlying record. It summarizes appointments, intake, messages, and inventory, and provider completion.

## Practice identity

`/dashboard/platform/store-settings` edits `HospitalProfile`: name, short name, logo, color, theme, address, support phone and email.
