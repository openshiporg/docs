## Exports

Needs `canManageDataPortability`. `requestExportJob` queues an encrypted export of patients, appointments, audit, or inventory in CSV or JSON, and `processExportJob` builds it. Download from `/api/exports/{jobId}` with the one-time token. Jobs go `queued` → `processing` → `ready` → `expired`, or `failed`.

## Retention

Needs `canManageRetention`.

| Record | Mutation |
| --- | --- |
| `RetentionPolicy` per record type | `establishRetentionPolicy`, `transitionRetentionPolicy` |
| `RecordHold` (legal hold) | `placeRecordHold`, `releaseRecordHold` (reason required) |
| `RetentionDisposition` | `recordRetentionDisposition` |

A record cannot have two active holds, and a disposition is checked against the active policy and holds. Dispositions are append-only.

## External identity links

`linkExternalIdentity` records the ID a patient or record has in another system (`system`, `localType`).
