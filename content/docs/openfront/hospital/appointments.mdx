## Lifecycle

| Status | Meaning | Next |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `requested` | Submitted from the public site or created by staff | `confirmed`, `cancelled` |
| `confirmed` | Slot accepted | `checked_in`, `cancelled`, `no_show` |
| `checked_in` | Patient arrived; a care queue ticket exists | `in_visit` |
| `in_visit` | Visit under way | `completed` |
| `completed`, `cancelled`, `no_show` | Closed | none |

`source` is one of `patient_portal`, `front_desk`, `phone`, or `referral`.

## Actions

- **Confirm, start, complete, cancel, no-show** call `transitionAppointment(operationKey, appointmentId, toStatus, expectedVersion, reason, operationalNotes)`. A reason is recorded for cancellations.
- **Check in** is the atomic `arriveAppointment` workflow, which creates the care queue ticket. `transitionAppointment` cannot check in.
- Confirming runs a provider schedule check (`appointmentScheduleConflicts`) and fails if the provider is blocked or double-booked.

The page shows status counts and the queue. Each row links the patient, provider, visit type, and facility.

See [Care queue and encounters](/docs/openfront/hospital/care-queue-encounters) for what happens after check in.
