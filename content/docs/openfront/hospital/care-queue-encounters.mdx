## Care queue

`/dashboard/platform/care-queue`. A `CareQueueTicket` is created by `arriveAppointment` with a `ticketNumber`, `queuePosition`, and `priority` (`routine` or `time_sensitive`).

`waiting` → `called` → `in_care` → `completed`, or `cancelled`. Move a ticket with `transitionCareQueue`. Opening an encounter from a ticket links the two.

## Encounters

`/dashboard/platform/encounters`. `openEncounter` creates an `Encounter` with a chief complaint. An appointment can have only one encounter.

`open` → `in_progress` → `closed`, or `cancelled`. Add a `diagnosisSummary` when closing.

## Episode closure policy

Each facility has a revisioned `EpisodeClosurePolicy` (`reviewEpisodePolicy`). `transitionEncounter` to `closed` checks the policy against the encounter's open children, such as orders, results, and documents. `episodeClosureWorkspace` and `episodeChildWorkspace` show what blocks closure. If a closure was interrupted, `recoverEpisodeClosure(encounterId, operationKey)` finishes it.
