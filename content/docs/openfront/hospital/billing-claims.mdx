Needs `canManageBilling`.

## Coverage

`recordPatientCoverage` stores payer, member ID, group, and effective date. `unverified` → `active`, `inactive`, or `rejected`.

## Claims

`/dashboard/platform/claims`. `prepareInsuranceClaim` needs active coverage matching the patient and facility, and an encounter. Billed amounts are non-negative integer cents. Every change adds an immutable `ClaimVersion`.

`draft` → `prepared` → `submitted` → `external_acknowledged` or `external_rejected` → `corrected`; `cancelled`. Recording `external_*` results can also arrive from the payer through the inbound webhook (`claim.acknowledged`, `claim.rejected`).

## Payments and refunds

These are on the billing tab of `/dashboard/platform/operational-fabric`.

| Record | Methods and states |
| --- | --- |
| `PatientPayment` | Methods `cash`, `check`, `card_external`, `bank_external`. `pending` → `succeeded`, `failed`, `cancelled` |
| `PatientRefund` | `pending` → `succeeded`, `failed`, `cancelled`. Tracks `refundedAmountCents` on the payment |

Payments are recorded against money received outside the app. Add an `externalReference` when you mark one succeeded.
