To create a custom Construction storefront, copy the skill and give it to your LLM. The skill will explain how `features/storefront` implements the public contractor website and customer account, and how `features/portal` implements the client and trade partner project portal.

After that source orientation, it will ask what you dislike, what should stay, what workflows and users matter, and what the replacement should look and feel like. It then traces the exact routes, feature slices, GraphQL calls, schema, access rules, sessions, and provider boundaries in the supplied Construction codebase before proposing work.

Skill file: [`/skills/openfront-construction-custom-storefront/SKILL.md`](/skills/openfront-construction-custom-storefront/SKILL.md)

## Storefront routes

Routes live directly under `app/` (there is no route group). Screens live in `features/storefront/screens`, `features/storefront/account`, and `features/portal`. The website shows the `PublicSiteProfile` selected by `PUBLIC_SITE_KEY`, and only when that profile is published and its company is active.

**Public website**

| Route | Customer journey |
|---|---|
| `/` | Home: brand, services, past projects, start-a-project call to action |
| `/services` | Published services |
| `/services/[slug]` | Service detail with a link to request a quote |
| `/projects` | Published portfolio projects, filterable by market |
| `/projects/[slug]` | Project case study |
| `/start-a-project`, `/client-intake` | Project inquiry form (`project_inquiry`) |
| `/estimate` | Estimate request form (`estimate_request`) |
| `/rfp` | RFP invitation form (`rfp_invitation`) |
| `/trade-partners`, `/bid` | Trade partner interest form (`trade_partner_interest`) |
| `/contact` | General contact form (`general_contact`) |
| `/requests/[publicReference]/confirmation` | Inquiry receipt (`publicIntakeReceipt`) |
| `/privacy` | Privacy notice from the site profile |

All inquiry forms call `submitPublicIntake`. It creates an `IntakeSubmission` and links a lead and opportunity for staff.

**Customer account**

| Route | Customer journey |
|---|---|
| `/account` | Sign in, sign up (`registerConstructionCustomer`, needs a published site), profile, quote request form, request history |
| `/account/requests/[id]` | One quote request: status, quote version, amount, terms, expiry; accept or cancel |
| `/account/requests/[id]/payment` | Pay the deposit on an accepted quote through Stripe Checkout (card or US bank account). Returns with `?session_id=` and `reconcileCustomerPayment` settles it; `?cancelled=1` shows that no payment was taken |
| `/account/reset` | Password reset (needs SMTP) |

Quote requests use `commandConstructionRequest` with `request`, `accept`, or `cancel`. Staff use `quote` and `cancel`. States are requested, quoted, accepted (stored as `payment_pending`), and cancelled.

**Project portal**

| Route | Participant journey |
|---|---|
| `/accept-invitation?token=…` | Accept a one-time project invitation (`acceptProjectInvitation`) |
| `/portal` | Projects the participant belongs to |
| `/portal/projects/[projectId]` | Project overview and inbox (`constructionPortalInbox`) |
| `/portal/projects/[projectId]/[tool]` | Tools: `preconstruction` (Bids), `execution` (RFIs & submittals), `documents`, `billing` (Billing & waivers), `schedule`, `field` (Field updates), `quality-safety` |

Owners also see **Pay approved claims** in the portal project page (`createClaimPayment`, `checkPaymentStatus`). Portal actions call `submitPortalBid`, `respondPortalRfi`, `reviewPortalSubmittal`, `submitPortalProgressClaim`, `submitPortalLienWaiver`, `decideConstructionApproval` (owner approvals, after `constructionPortalApprovalSnapshot`), and `acknowledgeDocumentDistribution`. Unknown tool slugs fall back to the overview.

## Journey rules

- Website inquiries are anonymous. They are not linked to a customer and do not appear in `/account`.
- An accepted quote is stored as `payment_pending`. The customer pays the deposit with `createCustomerPayment` when the company has Stripe set up. Otherwise the payment page shows that online payment is not set up for the contractor.
- Portal users see only items for their own project membership and portal organization. Financials, contracts, procurement, reports, team, and audit are not available in the portal.
- Invitation links are shown once to the operator, who shares them with the invitee.
