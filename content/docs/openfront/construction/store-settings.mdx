---
title: Store settings
description: Brand, public site profile, services, and portfolio projects.
icon: Settings
---

`/dashboard/platform/store-settings` (`constructionStoreSettings`, `updateConstructionStoreSettings`) edits the company brand and logo, the `PublicSiteProfile` (contact email, service area, capability summary, privacy notice), `PublicService` entries, and `PublicProject` portfolio entries.

Publishing the first portfolio project needs a summary of at least 20 characters. The website shows the profile whose key equals `PUBLIC_SITE_KEY` (default `default`).
