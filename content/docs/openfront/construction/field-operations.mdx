## Daily logs

<Steps>
<Step>
### Start
`startDailyLog`.
</Step>
<Step>
### Add entries and photos
`addDailyLogEntry`, `registerFieldPhoto`. Submitting needs at least one entry: "A daily log requires at least one field entry before submission".
</Step>
<Step>
### Submit and approve
draft, submitted, approved (`transitionDailyLogStatus`). A submitted log can return to draft.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Labor and resources

`recordLaborTime` records labor entries. `recordResourceActivity` logs equipment or material use. The resource log register supports recovery of interrupted records with `reconcileResourceActivityIntent` and `closeResourceActivityIntent`.
