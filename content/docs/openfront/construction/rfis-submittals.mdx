## RFIs

<Steps>
<Step>
### Raise
`raiseRfi`: draft, open, answered, closed (or voided).
</Step>
<Step>
### Route
`routeRfi` to a reviewer.
</Step>
<Step>
### Respond
`respondToRfi`, or an external member uses `respondPortalRfi`.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Submittals

<Steps>
<Step>
### Register
`registerSubmittal`. It needs a review route: "A submittal requires a persisted review route for its current revision".
</Step>
<Step>
### Route and review
`routeSubmittalReview`, then `recordSubmittalReview` (approved, revise_resubmit, rejected) or `reviewPortalSubmittal`.
</Step>
</Steps>

Statuses: draft, submitted, under_review, revise_resubmit, approved, rejected, closed, voided. Specification sections are registered with `registerSpecificationSection`. Registers: `constructionRfiRegister`, `constructionSubmittalRegister`.
