## Portfolio

`/dashboard/platform/portfolio` (`constructionPortfolio`) shows budget, cost, and open-item metrics for each project in the companies you can see.

## Create a project

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open Projects
`/dashboard/platform/projects`, then the create form (`createConstructionProject`).
</Step>
<Step>
### What it creates
The project, your membership, a "GEN" cost code and budget code, the budget and its first line, and the approval policies.
</Step>
<Step>
### Add structure and people
Add budget codes and locations in [Cost structure](/docs/openfront/construction/financials), then invite the team on [Team](/docs/openfront/construction/team).
</Step>
</Steps>

## Project status

`transitionProjectStatus`: planning to active or archived; active to on_hold or completed; on_hold to active or completed; completed to archived. Every transition needs a reason of 3 to 500 characters.

## Project tools

Open a project to reach its tools at `/dashboard/platform/projects/[projectId]/<tool>`. Financials, contracts, procurement, billing, reports, team, and audit are internal-only.
