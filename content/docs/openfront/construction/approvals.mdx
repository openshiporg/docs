---
title: Approvals
description: Approval policies and the approval panel.
icon: CheckCircle
---

Budget changes, prime contracts, change orders, progress claims, and retainage releases can require an `ApprovalRequest` under the project's `ApprovalPolicy`. Onboarding creates five policies with the administrator role as approver.

- `configureConstructionApprovalPolicy` edits a policy.
- `requestConstructionApproval` creates a request; the requester assigns another eligible person.
- `decideConstructionApproval` approves or rejects. Owners decide from the portal after `constructionPortalApprovalSnapshot`.
- `constructionApprovalSnapshot` feeds the approval panel on financials, contracts, and billing.

Self-approval is rejected: "A different authorized person must decide this approval request". Governance lists: ApprovalPolicy, ApprovalRequest, ApprovalDecision.
