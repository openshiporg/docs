Route: `/projects/[projectId]/team`.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Add a member
`addProjectMember` for existing users. Access level: viewer, contributor, manager. Portal type: internal, owner/client, designer, subcontractor, vendor.
</Step>
<Step>
### Invite
`inviteProjectMember` returns a one-time `/accept-invitation?token=...` link. Share it. Invitees need a password of at least 10 characters.
</Step>
<Step>
### Manage
`revokeProjectInvitation`, `reissueProjectInvitation`.
</Step>
</Steps>

External members use [/portal](/docs/openfront/construction/project-portal).
