## Trade partners

`/dashboard/platform/trade-partners` and `/[id]`. The directory holds subcontractors, vendors, owners, designers, consultants, and authorities. Create with `registerTradePartner`, edit, and set status: prospective, approved, inactive, blocked. A trade partner can be linked to a project member as their portal organization.

## Cost codes

`/dashboard/platform/cost-codes`: the company library. Create, edit name and division, and activate or deactivate. Project budget codes are created from these in the project's Cost structure tool.

## Resources

`/dashboard/platform/resources`: the equipment fleet and material catalog. Create with `createResourceCatalogItem`, edit, and change status. Field staff log equipment and material use against these on [Field operations](/docs/openfront/construction/field-operations).
