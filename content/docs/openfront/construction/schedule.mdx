Route: `/projects/[projectId]/schedule`.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Register a schedule
`registerSchedule`: draft, published, archived. Publishing needs a task: "A schedule requires at least one planned task before publication".
</Step>
<Step>
### Add tasks and dependencies
`registerScheduleTask`, `linkScheduleDependency`. "A schedule task cannot depend on itself".
</Step>
<Step>
### Update progress
`updateScheduleTaskProgress`. Statuses: not_started, in_progress, delayed, on_hold, completed. Completion needs 100 percent progress.
</Step>
</Steps>
