## Observations

`recordSafetyObservation`: open, assigned, resolved, closed.

## Incidents

`recordSafetyIncident` then `recordSafetyIncidentPrivateDetails` for restricted detail, read back with `safetyIncidentPrivateDetail`. Statuses: reported, investigating, corrective_action, closed. Closing needs verified actions: "All incident corrective actions must be verified before closure".

## Inspections

<Steps>
<Step>
### Template
`registerInspectionTemplate`.
</Step>
<Step>
### Inspect
`createInspectionWithItem`, `recordInspectionItemResult`. scheduled, in_progress, passed or failed, closed.
</Step>
<Step>
### Follow up
A failed inspection needs a failed item. Create corrective actions or punch items.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Corrective actions and punch

`registerCorrectiveAction`, `recordCorrectiveActionEvidence`: open, in_progress, ready_for_verification, verified, closed. `recordPunchItem`: open, in_progress, ready_for_review, closed, voided.
