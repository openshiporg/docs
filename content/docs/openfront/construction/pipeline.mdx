## Routes

- `/dashboard/platform/pipeline`: leads, opportunities, and website inquiries
- `/dashboard/platform/pipeline/create`: new opportunity
- `/dashboard/platform/pipeline/[id]`: opportunity detail
- `/dashboard/platform/pipeline/requests/[id]`: one `IntakeSubmission`

## How inquiries arrive

Forms on the public site call `submitPublicIntake`. Each creates an `IntakeSubmission` of type `project_inquiry`, `estimate_request`, `rfp_invitation`, `trade_partner_interest`, or `general_contact`, plus a lead and an opportunity. The visitor gets a receipt at `/requests/[publicReference]/confirmation`.

## Work an opportunity

<Steps>
<Step>
### Review the inquiry
Open it from the Website inquiries list.
</Step>
<Step>
### Qualify
Opportunities move from qualifying through go/no-go, estimating, submitted, and negotiating to won, lost, or declined.
</Step>
<Step>
### Convert
When won, create the project on [Portfolio and projects](/docs/openfront/construction/portfolio-projects).
</Step>
</Steps>

Lead statuses: new, qualified, nurturing, converted, disqualified.
