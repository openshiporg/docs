import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

Route: `/projects/[projectId]/billing`. Internal only.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Prepare a claim
`prepareProgressClaim` snapshots the schedule of values. Owner claims draw from the prime contract; subcontractor claims from a commitment (also `submitPortalProgressClaim`).
</Step>
<Step>
### Review
draft, submitted, under_review, approved, partially_paid, paid, closed (rejected returns to draft).
</Step>
<Step>
### Record and allocate payment
`recordProgressPayment`, then `allocateSettledPayment`. Statuses: pending, authorized, settled, reversed, failed, voided. "Allocation exceeds remaining claim payment due after effective settlements".
</Step>
<Step>
### Retainage and waivers
`requestRetainageRelease`: requested, approved, released. `recordLienWaiverEvidence`: requested, received, accepted.
</Step>
</Steps>

`reverseSettledPayment` reverses a settled payment. A claim closes only when allocations cover the payment due.

Owners can pay approved claims online; staff can send a link. See [Payment providers](/docs/openfront/construction/payment-providers).
