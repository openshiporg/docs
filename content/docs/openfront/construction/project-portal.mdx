External members accept an invitation, then work in `/portal`.

| Tool | Actions |
|---|---|
| Bids (`preconstruction`) | `submitPortalBid` |
| RFIs and submittals (`execution`) | `respondPortalRfi`, `reviewPortalSubmittal` |
| Billing and waivers (`billing`) | `submitPortalProgressClaim`, `submitPortalLienWaiver`, owner approvals, pay approved claims |
| Documents | acknowledge distributions |
| Schedule, Field updates, Quality and safety | read project items |

`constructionPortalInbox` is the overview inbox. Members see only items tied to their membership and organization.
