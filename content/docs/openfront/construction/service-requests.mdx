Signed-in website customers request a quote for a published service. Staff price it on `/dashboard/platform/service-requests` (`constructionOperatorRequests`).

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open the request
Status `requested`.
</Step>
<Step>
### Quote
`commandConstructionRequest` with `quote`: amount, terms, and expiry. Each quote is a version. "Accepted or cancelled requests cannot be repriced".
</Step>
<Step>
### Customer decides
The customer accepts (status becomes `payment_pending`) or cancels in `/account/requests/[id]`.
</Step>
<Step>
### Collect the deposit
If Stripe is set up, the customer pays on `/account/requests/[id]/payment`. See [Payment providers](/docs/openfront/construction/payment-providers).
</Step>
</Steps>

Staff can also cancel with `cancel`. Status values: requested, quoted, payment_pending, cancelled. Events are stored as `ServiceRequestEvent`.
