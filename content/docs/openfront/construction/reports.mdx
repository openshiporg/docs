Route: `/projects/[projectId]/reports` (internal only). Reports cover job cost and forecast, commitment and change exposure, and aging for RFIs, submittals, claims, lien waivers, safety, and corrective actions. The export button (`features/platform/reports/csv.ts`) downloads CSV.
