## Time and expenses

`/dashboard/platform/time-expenses`: a timer and manual time, expenses, review, and pricing. Move entries with `transitionTimeEntry` and `transitionExpense`. Billing on a matter needs `canBill` on its team.

## Rate cards

`RateCard` and `RateRule` price time by role, matter, or other rules. Manage them on `/dashboard/platform/billing`.

## Invoices

<Steps>
<Step>

### Assemble
`assembleDraftInvoice` selects approved time and expenses into `InvoiceLine` rows. Money is minor units.

</Step>
<Step>

### Issue
`transitionInvoice`: `draft` → `approved` → `issued` → `partially_paid` → `paid`. Also `overdue` and `void`.

</Step>
<Step>

### Pay
`recordManualPayment` against an open issued invoice, then `allocatePayment`.

</Step>
<Step>

### Reverse
`reversePaymentAllocation` undoes an allocation. An invoice with allocations must be reversed or refunded before voiding.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Refunds

`requestRefund` → `reviewRefund` → processing → `succeeded` or `failed`. A refund of a trust deposit needs its exact trust disbursement posted.

## Retainers

`/dashboard/platform/retainers`: `prepareRetainerAgreement` and `transitionRetainerAgreement`. An active retainer with a trust ledger and deposit amount can fund trust deposits.
