## Retention and holds

`/dashboard/platform/governance` needs `canManageRetention`.

| Action | Mutation |
| --- | --- |
| Activate or retire a policy | `transitionRetentionPolicy` |
| Assign a policy to a document | `assignDocumentRetentionPolicy` |
| Place a hold on a matter, document, or contact | `placeRetentionHold` |
| Release a hold | `releaseRetentionHold` |
| Dispose of a document | `disposeRetainedDocument` |

A document retention policy is frozen after archival, and a hold cannot be placed after disposal. Disposal writes `RetentionAction` evidence.

## Reports

`/dashboard/platform/reports` shows deadline and audit event reports. Needs `canViewAudit`. Audit events are append-only.

## Import and export

`/dashboard/platform/data`. Import contacts from CSV with `previewContactImport`, then `applyContactImport`. `preparePracticeExport` exports contacts, matters, invoices, time entries, or audit events, up to 500 rows.
