## Firm and offices

`/dashboard/platform/firm` edits time zone, currency, and offices (`name`, `code`, timezone, contact, status `active`, `inactive`, `closed`).

## Team

`/dashboard/platform/team` needs `canManageUsers` or equivalent.

- `inviteFirmMember` sends an invitation with role and office scope.
- `changeFirmMemberAccess` changes role, primary office, and allowed offices.
- `updateFirmRoleAuthority` edits a role's permission toggles.

Membership states: `invited` → `active` → `suspended` → `ended`. Onboarding seeds Firm administrator, Attorney, Paralegal, Billing, and Intake coordinator roles.

See [Roles and permissions](/docs/openfront/law-firm/roles-permissions).
