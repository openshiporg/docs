Route: `/dashboard/platform/intake`. Needs `canManageIntake`; conflict work needs `canRunConflictChecks` and `canResolveConflicts`.

## Intake

Create with `startClientIntake`. Add parties with `addIntakeParticipant` and `changeIntakeParticipant`. Move it with `transitionIntake`.

`new` → `screening` → `conflict_review` → `consultation` → `decision` → `converted`, `declined`, or `withdrawn`.

## Consultations

`scheduleConsultation` sets host and time; `transitionConsultation` moves `requested` → `scheduled` → `completed`, `cancelled`, or `no_show`. Consultations also appear on `/dashboard/platform/work`.

## Conflict check

<Steps>
<Step>

### Request
`requestConflictCheck` for exactly one intake or matter. An initial search term (500 characters or fewer) is required.

</Step>
<Step>

### Search
`addConflictSearchTerm` for each name. `addConflictMatch` records a hit with a match type and score, linked to exactly one verified contact, matter, or matter party.

</Step>
<Step>

### Dispose
`recordConflictDisposition` records each reviewer's outcome and rationale.

</Step>
<Step>

### Finalize
`finalizeConflictCheck` with `cleared` or `conflict_found`. Clearance needs non-empty search terms, completed-search evidence, and no unresolved disposition.

</Step>
<Step>

### Convert
`convertIntakeToMatter` creates the matter, parties, and team.

</Step>
</Steps>

Check status: `requested` → `running` → `review_required` → `cleared` or `conflict_found`.
