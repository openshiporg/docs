`/dashboard/platform/my-work` is the signed-in person's own desk. Every section runs as that person, so the role and matter-access rules decide what appears. A section the role cannot read says "Your role does not include this." instead of failing the page.

| Section | What it lists | Link to all |
|---|---|---|
| Consultations I host | Open consultations hosted by you, soonest first | Calendar & tasks |
| Conflict checks | Open checks you requested or review, or on your intakes | Conflict review on [Intake](/docs/openfront/law-firm/intake-conflicts) |
| My matters | Open matters where you are the responsible attorney or an active team member | [Matters](/docs/openfront/law-firm/matters) |
| Tasks | Open legal tasks assigned to you, by due date | |
| Deadlines | Pending deadlines assigned to you, by due date | |
| Recent time | Your last 10 time entries | [Time & expenses](/docs/openfront/law-firm/time-billing) |
| Documents on my matters | Documents on matters you are on | [Documents](/docs/openfront/law-firm/documents-communications) |

Each section shows up to 25 rows. The page only reads; act on a row from its own screen.
