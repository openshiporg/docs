## Public site

`/dashboard/platform/public-site`.

- A `PublicSitePolicy` holds the privacy notice, first-contact notice, about text, and switches for accounts and booking. Accounts open when enabled with a privacy notice. Booking opens when enabled with a first-contact notice.
- Mark a `PracticeArea` public with `publicSlug`, `consultationFeeMinor`, and `consultationMinutes`.
- Publish `AttorneyProfile` records.

Exactly one active firm must exist for the public site to serve.

## Store settings

`/dashboard/platform/branding`: display name, logo, accent color, tagline, and support email. These feed the public site and email.

## Booking slots

Consultation slots run 9:00 to 17:00, Monday to Friday. `consultationAvailability(serviceId)` returns open times.
