Openfront Law Firm runs the operations of a practice: intake, conflicts, matters, calendar, documents, messages, time, billing, trust accounting, and a client portal.

| Surface | Who | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public site | Prospective clients | `/`, `/practice-areas`, `/attorneys`, `/consultation`, `/account` |
| Client portal | Clients | `/portal` |
| Staff dashboard | Firm staff | `/dashboard/platform` |
| GraphQL API | All of the above, API keys, OAuth clients, agents | `/api/graphql` |

## Concepts

| Concept | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Firm | The tenant. Every firm-owned record carries a `firm` and cannot link to another firm's records |
| Membership and role | Staff access comes from an active `FirmMembership` and its `Role` (39 `can*` flags) |
| Matter team | `MatterTeamMember` rows with `canEdit`, `canBill`, `canAccessRestricted` |
| Ethical wall | Blocks named users from a matter, even administrators |
| Controlled record | Status, money, access, and evidence fields change only through named mutations with a serializable transaction and an `AuditEvent` |
| Minor units | Money is integer minor units plus a currency code |
| Trust ledger | Client or matter ledger inside a trust account, posted with balanced entries |

## The path of a client

1. A visitor registers (`registerConsultationAccount`) and books a consultation (`bookClientConsultation`).
2. Booking creates a contact, an intake, a `requested` consultation, and a conflict check when an opposing party is named.
3. Staff clear the conflict check and convert the intake to a matter (`convertIntakeToMatter`).
4. The team records time, deadlines, documents, and messages.
5. Invoices are assembled, issued, and paid. Retainer money sits in trust.
6. The firm shares documents and invoices with the client through the portal.

See [Intake and conflicts](/docs/openfront/law-firm/intake-conflicts), [Matters](/docs/openfront/law-firm/matters), and [Roles and permissions](/docs/openfront/law-firm/roles-permissions).
