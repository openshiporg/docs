## Calendar and tasks

`/dashboard/platform/work` lists deadlines, tasks, and consultations. Drill into `/[kind]/[recordId]`. Use `transitionLegalTask` for tasks and `transitionDeadline` for deadlines. `reviseDraftDeadline` edits a deadline before it is confirmed.

## Courts and dockets

`/dashboard/platform/dockets` and `/[docketId]` hold `Court`, `Docket`, and `DocketEntry` records.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Record
`recordDocketEntry` adds an entry to a docket.

</Step>
<Step>

### Revise
`reviseDocketEntry` corrects it.

</Step>
<Step>

### Verify
`verifyDocketEntry` marks that a person checked it against the court record.

</Step>
<Step>

### Link deadlines
Deadlines linked to an entry show on the docket detail.

</Step>
</Steps>

Deadlines are entered and verified by staff.
