## Structure

| Record | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `TrustAccount` | The bank account |
| `TrustLedgerAccount` | Client or matter ledger inside it |
| `TrustTransaction` and `TrustLedgerEntry` | Balanced postings |
| `TrustBalanceSnapshot` | Balances at a date |
| `TrustReconciliation` | Three-way reconciliation |

Setup: `setTrustAccountBankIdentity` (only while the account is pending and has no postings), `transitionTrustAccount`, `transitionTrustLedgerAccount`. Close every client ledger, each at zero balance, before closing the account.

## Posting

`postTrustTransaction` needs at least two entries that balance, an effective date, an active trust account, and an idempotency key of 12 to 160 characters. A client-liability entry needs a ledger account. A source payment or refund must be a same-account trust deposit in the same currency, and a transaction cannot reference both.

Postings are never edited. Correct with a reversing transaction.
