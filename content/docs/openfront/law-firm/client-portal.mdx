Route: `/dashboard/platform/portal`. Needs `canManagePortal`. Portal clients are `PortalAccount` records with their own session cookie, separate from storefront users.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Set the policy
`recordPortalDiscoveryPolicy` sets how clients are matched to matters (`portalPolicyWorkspace`).

</Step>
<Step>

### Invite
`invitePortalAccount` emails an invitation for a valid address. The client opens `/portal/accept`.

</Step>
<Step>

### Grant
`grantPortalMatterAccess` or `executePortalGrant` gives access to a matter from the versioned directory.

</Step>
<Step>

### Share
`sharePortalResource` shares a document, invoice, or thread. Clients see only shared items under an active grant. Drafts and internal notes are not shown.

</Step>
<Step>

### Revoke
`revokePortalGrant` removes access.

</Step>
</Steps>

Clients sign in at `/portal/signin` with an emailed link. Needs SMTP.
