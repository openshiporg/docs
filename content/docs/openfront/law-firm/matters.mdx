## Contacts

`/dashboard/platform/contacts` and `/[contactId]`: search, create, and view addresses, contact methods, matters, intakes, and portal access. `Contact.contactType` is a person or organization, with a status from `prospective_client` to `client`.

## Matters

`/dashboard/platform/matters` and `/[matterId]`. The workspace shows parties, team, walls, confidentiality, status history, and registers for tasks, deadlines, documents, messages, and invoices.

| Action | Mutation |
| --- | --- |
| Change status | `transitionMatter` |
| Change confidentiality | `changeMatterConfidentiality` |
| Set team access | `setMatterTeamAccess` |
| Add or change a party | `addMatterParty`, `changeMatterParty` |

Status: `proposed` → `pending_conflict` → `open` ⇄ `on_hold` → `closing` → `closed`; or `declined`. Each change writes a `MatterStatusEvent`.

## Access

A user sees a matter when the firm role allows it, they are on the team, and no wall blocks them. `authorizedUsers` and `blockedUsers` are maintained by the team and wall mutations and cannot be written directly.

## Ethical walls

`transitionEthicalWall` moves a wall `draft` → `active` → `lifted`. `setEthicalWallMember` sets each user `allowed` or `blocked`. A firm administrator does not pass a wall.
