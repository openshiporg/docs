---
title: Take a consultation to a matter
description: From booking through conflict clearance to an open matter.
icon: Briefcase
---

import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

<Steps>
<Step>

### Review the request
Find the `requested` consultation on `/dashboard/platform/work` and schedule it.

</Step>
<Step>

### Run the conflict check
On `/dashboard/platform/intake` add search terms and matches, record dispositions, and finalize as `cleared`.

</Step>
<Step>

### Convert
Convert the intake to a matter. The parties and team come across.

</Step>
<Step>

### Open
Move the matter from `pending_conflict` to `open`.

</Step>
</Steps>
