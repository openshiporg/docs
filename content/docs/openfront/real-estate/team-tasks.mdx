## Agents and teams

`/dashboard/platform/people` (`platformPeople`). Needs `canManagePeople`. Owners and admins invite (`inviteBrokerageMemberCommand`) and suspend (`suspendBrokerageMemberCommand`) members. Only an owner can add another admin. Membership status is `invited`, `active`, or `suspended`.

Without `canManagePeople`, agents work only on records assigned to them.

## Tasks

`/dashboard/platform/tasks`. Create, edit, and move tasks with `transitionAgentTaskCommand`: `open` → `in_progress` → `done`, or `blocked`.

## Reports

`/dashboard/platform/reports` (`platformReports`). Needs `canViewReports`. Shows the funnel, lead sources, and per-agent numbers.
