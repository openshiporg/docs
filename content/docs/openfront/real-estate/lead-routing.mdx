---
title: Lead routing
description: Assign new leads to agents or teams by rule.
icon: Route
---

Route: `/dashboard/platform/routing`.

A `LeadRoutingRule` has `name`, `status` (`active` or `paused`), `priority`, and filters on `intent`, `source`, `city`, `state`, and `postalCode`. It assigns an `assignedAgent` or `assignedTeam`. One rule can be marked `isFallback`.

## Order

1. The first matching active rule by priority.
2. The listing agent.
3. The fallback rule.

The agent must still hold an active membership; otherwise the lead stays unassigned. Create, edit, pause, activate, and delete from the routing page.
