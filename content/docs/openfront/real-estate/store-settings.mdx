---
title: Store settings
description: Public identity, contact details, and showing length.
icon: Settings
---

Route: `/dashboard/platform/store-settings`. Saved with `updatePlatformStorefrontSettings` into `BrokerageSettings`.

| Field | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `businessName`, `marketLabel`, `city`, `state` | Identity and market |
| `tagline`, `description` | Home page text |
| `publicPhone`, `publicEmail` | Contact |
| `equalHousingNotice` | Footer notice |
| `showingDurationMinutes` | Default tour length, 15 to 240 |
| `brandHue`, `logoIcon`, `logoColor` | Branding, shown at `/brand-mark.svg` |

The storefront reads these through `publicBrokerageIdentity`.
