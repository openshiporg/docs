Routes: `/dashboard/platform/leads`, `/leads/[id]`, `/showings`, `/showings/[id]`. Need `canManageLeads` and `canManageShowings`.

## Leads

Sources are `website`, `zillow`, `referral`, `open_house`, `phone`, and `import`. Intent is `buy`, `rent`, `sell`, `invest`, or `unknown`. A lead needs a valid email or phone.

Status: `new` → `attempted_contact` → `qualified` → `tour_scheduled` → `offer` → `closed` or `lost` (`transitionLeadStatus`).

- **New lead** drawer creates a lead by hand.
- `addLeadActivityCommand` logs a call, note, or message to the timeline.
- Duplicate groups appear on the leads page; `resolveDuplicateLeadCommand` merges them.

## Showings

`requestPropertyShowing` from the site creates a `requested` showing. Staff can also use `scheduleShowingForLeadCommand`.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Confirm
`reviewShowingCommand` confirms. It fails if the property or agent already has a confirmed showing at that time.

</Step>
<Step>

### Complete
`completeShowingWithFeedback` completes it and stores `ShowingFeedback`.

</Step>
<Step>

### Or close
`transitionShowingStatus` marks `no_show` or `cancelled`.

</Step>
</Steps>

Showing types are `in_person`, `virtual`, and `open_house`. Length comes from `showingDurationMinutes` in store settings (15 to 240 minutes). Requests must be in the future, within one year.
