---
title: Refund a client
description: Refund part of a card payment, with an approval above the threshold.
icon: Undo2
---

import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

You need `canIssueRefunds`. Only card payments are refunded in the app.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open the payment
Open the trip, then the **Payments** tab. A card payment with money still refundable shows a **Refund** button.
</Step>
<Step>
### Enter the amount and reason
The amount cannot exceed what was paid less what is already refunded. A reason is required.
</Step>
<Step>
### Under the threshold: confirm
At or below `refundApprovalThresholdCents` (default $500.00), click **Confirm**. The refund goes to the original card, the payment shows its refunded amount, and a `refund_to_client` entry appears in client funds.
</Step>
<Step>
### Over the threshold: request approval
The button reads **Request approval**. Another person with `canManageSettings` opens **Approvals** and approves it.
</Step>
<Step>
### Finish an approved refund
Call `refundClientPayment` with the same `paymentId`, `amountCents`, and `reason`, plus the `approvalId`. The approval is used once.
</Step>
<Step>
### If the status is unknown
Check the payment in Stripe before trying again. Do not use a new key for the same refund.
</Step>
</Steps>
