---
title: Record a commission
description: Record a supplier payment, handle a short pay or over-payment, and write off what will not arrive.
icon: HandCoins
---

import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

You need `canManageCommissions`.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Check what is expected
Open `/dashboard/platform/commissions`. The **expected** and **invoiced** tabs show what suppliers still owe.
</Step>
<Step>
### Record the receipt
Call `recordCommissionReceipt` with the supplier, the amount, a reference, and allocations that add up to the amount. A component that reaches its expected amount becomes `received`. One that falls short becomes `short_paid` and opens a follow-up task.
</Step>
<Step>
### Over-payment
If an allocation goes above the expected amount, first request a `commission_overpayment` approval for the excess, have another person approve it, then repeat the receipt with the `approvalId` and a reason on that allocation.
</Step>
<Step>
### Write off a shortfall
On a `short_paid` or `expected` row, click **Write off**. Another person must approve the request. After that, **Write off** confirms it and the component becomes `written_off`.
</Step>
<Step>
### Read the payouts
The **Advisor payouts** list totals each advisor's ledger after the host-agency split.
</Step>
</Steps>
