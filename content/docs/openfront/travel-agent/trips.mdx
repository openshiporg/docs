## Trip list

`/dashboard/platform/trips` has a tab for each status plus `all`. Each row shows the title, status label, trip number, client, advisor, dates, total sell, and the balance due.

Money shown on a trip:

- **Total sell**: the planning fee plus every component that is not cancelled.
- **Collected**: what the client has paid across invoices.
- **Balance due**: only what the agency collects, meaning the planning fee plus components set to `agency_collects`, less what has been collected. Components the client pays to the supplier directly are not counted.
- **Commission expected**: the sum over components that are not cancelled.

## Trip workspace

`/dashboard/platform/trips/[id]` has ten tabs in the URL (`?tab=`):

| Tab | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| Overview | Money cards, advisor, destinations, planning fee, client, next payments |
| Travelers | Names, nationality, passport on file or missing |
| Proposals | Every version with its options, items, and totals |
| Components | Supplier items with booking status, payment model, confirmation number, and a Cancel action |
| Itinerary | Days and their items, with **Share itinerary** |
| Payments | Invoices with their card payments and a Refund action, **Generate schedule**, and the schedule rows |
| Client funds | The balance held and every ledger entry |
| Commissions | Expected and received commission per component |
| Documents | Titles and kinds |
| Tasks | Reminders and follow-ups |

A header button moves the trip to its next state: `planning` to `proposal_sent`, `accepted` to `booked`, `booked` to `final_payment_due`, `final_payment_due` to `paid_in_full`, through `transitionTrip`. `proposal_sent` to `accepted` normally happens when the client accepts a proposal.

The trip's completed view shows commission received against expected, and the short amount.

## Rules

- Allowed moves are fixed: each state has one next state, and `cancelled` is allowed from every state except `completed` and `cancelled`.
- The header button offers the next state up to `paid_in_full`. `traveling` and `completed` are reached by `sweepTripDates`, and cancelling is a `transitionTrip` call with `status: cancelled`.
- Moving to `paid_in_full`, `traveling`, or `completed` fails with "Client balance must be zero before this status" while the client owes money the agency collects.
- `sweepTripDates` moves `paid_in_full` trips to `traveling` once the start date passes and `traveling` trips to `completed` once the end date has passed. It is a mutation you run; no scheduler is built in.
- Every transition is compare-and-set and writes an `AuditEvent`.

## What the dashboard cannot set

The planning fee, travelers, and component due dates are set by the seed. No screen or mutation edits them, and raw updates are closed on trips, components, and travelers. A new trip from an inquiry starts with a zero planning fee and no travelers.
