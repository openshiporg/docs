`/dashboard/platform/suppliers` lists suppliers in name order. A row shows the name, kind, notes, contact emails, default commission rate, payment terms, and the number of components booked.

Supplier kinds: `hotel`, `cruise_line`, `tour_operator`, `dmc`, `airline`, `consolidator`, `insurer`, `transfer`.

## Fields

- `defaultCommissionBps`: the usual commission rate in basis points.
- `commissionPaymentTermsDays`: how long the supplier usually takes to pay.
- `hostAgencyRelation`, `bookingPortalUrl`, and `iataCliaNotes`: free text.
- Contacts (`SupplierContact`): name, email, phone, and title.

Writing suppliers needs `canManageSuppliers`. The list page is read-only; create and edit suppliers from the generic list screen at `/dashboard/suppliers`. Deleting is closed.

Recording a payment to a supplier needs `canManagePayments`, and recording a commission receipt needs `canManageCommissions`.
