`/dashboard/platform/tasks` lists open tasks ordered by due date. A row shows the title, status, type, trip, assignee, and due date, and links to the trip's Tasks tab. The page is read-only.

Task types: `deposit_due`, `final_due`, `passport_expiring`, `commission_overdue`, `follow_up`. Status: `open`, `completed`, `cancelled`.

## Where tasks come from

- `generatePaymentSchedule` creates a deposit or final reminder seven days before each due date. Each has a key, so running it twice does not repeat it.
- `recordCommissionReceipt` opens a `commission_overdue` task when a component is short-paid.
- The seed adds reminders for its schedule rows. Reminders for dates already past are marked `completed`.

Nothing in the app creates `passport_expiring` tasks. The type exists on the list.

Writing tasks needs `canManageTasks`.
