---
title: Book and announce a show
description: Hold a date, make an offer, price the event and put it on sale.
icon: CalendarPlus
---

import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

<Steps>
<Step>

### Hold the date

In **Calendar**, place a hold on the venue date. Your hold takes the next rank.

</Step>
<Step>

### Make the offer

In **Deals**, draft an offer with the deal type, guarantee, percentage and expense cap. Check the calculator, then send it.

</Step>
<Step>

### Accept and confirm

Record the artist's acceptance; the terms are frozen. Confirm the date hold.

</Step>
<Step>

### Price the event

On the event page, add price levels per section and any price phases. Set production holds for seats you keep back.

</Step>
<Step>

### Announce and go on sale

Publish the event. It appears on the storefront and sells from the first active price phase.

</Step>
</Steps>
