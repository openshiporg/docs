---
title: Settle a show
description: Record merchandise, compute the settlement, sign it, and record the artist payment.
icon: Calculator
---

import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

Needs `canManageSettlements`. Commands go to `/api/graphql` unless a button is named.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Check the inputs
The event must be `completed` or `settling` and have an accepted offer. Expenses are on the event's **Expenses** tab. Enter actual amounts at `/dashboard/event-expenses`.
</Step>
<Step>
### Record merchandise
`recordMerchSales` with `eventId`, `softGrossCents`, and `hardGrossCents`. The venue share comes from the deal's frozen merch split.
</Step>
<Step>
### Record deposits
`recordArtistPayment` with `kind` `deposit`, an amount, and a reference, for each deposit paid before the show. Deposits count when computing the balance.
</Step>
<Step>
### Compute
Press **Recompute** on the settlement page, or send `computeSettlement` with `eventId`. A draft settlement appears and the event moves to `settling`.
</Step>
<Step>
### Review and sign
On `/dashboard/platform/settlements/[id]` press the button for the next step: **Mark reviewed**, **Sign as promoter**, **Sign for artist**, **Finalize**. The artist signature must be a different user from the promoter signature.
</Step>
<Step>
### Pay the balance
`recordArtistPayment` with `kind` `balance` and the amount due, once the settlement is `final`. The payment is recorded only. Pay the artist outside the app. When the balance reaches zero the event and its offers become `settled`.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Corrections

A signed settlement is frozen. To correct it, send `adjustSettlement` with `settlementId`, `amountCents`, `reason`, and `approvedById`. The approver must be a different user with `canApproveAdjustments`. It creates the next version as a draft with an adjustment line.
