---
title: Settle a show
description: Record merch and bar, compute the worksheet, sign it and pay the artist.
icon: Calculator
---

import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

<Steps>
<Step>

### Record merch and bar

After the show, record merch and bar sales for the event.

</Step>
<Step>

### Compute

In **Settlements**, compute the worksheet. It uses ticket sales, expenses, merch, bar and the frozen offer terms.

</Step>
<Step>

### Adjust if needed

A user with `canApproveAdjustments` adjusts a line. Each adjustment creates a new version.

</Step>
<Step>

### Sign

Sign for the promoter, then record the artist's signature. The settlement becomes final.

</Step>
<Step>

### Pay

Record the artist's balance payment against the deposit already paid.

</Step>
</Steps>
