## Calendar and holds

Route: `/dashboard/platform/calendar`. Needs `canManageDeals`.

The calendar shows each venue's dates with ranked holds. `placeDateHold` adds a hold at the next rank. A lower-ranked hold can `challengeHold`; the first hold then confirms (`confirmHold`) or releases (`releaseDateHold`). Holds move between `held`, `challenged`, `confirmed` and `released`.

## Deals

Route: `/dashboard/platform/deals`. Needs `canManageDeals`.

An offer is one of four deal types:

| Deal | Artist is paid |
|---|---|
| `flat_guarantee` | A fixed amount |
| `versus` | The guarantee or a percentage of net, whichever is higher |
| `plus` | The guarantee plus a percentage above a break-even |
| `door_deal` | A percentage of the door |

The calculator previews the payout from expected sales before anything is sent. Offers move from `draft` to `sent`, then `accepted` or `declined`, then `contracted` and `settled`; a revision supersedes the earlier version. When an offer is accepted its terms are frozen by hash, and the settlement uses those frozen terms.

| Action | Mutation |
|---|---|
| Draft and revise | `draftOffer`, `reviseOffer` |
| Send, accept, decline, contract | `sendOffer`, `acceptOffer`, `declineOffer`, `contractOffer` |
| Record a deposit | `recordArtistDeposit` |
