## Venues and seating charts

Route: `/dashboard/platform/venues`. Needs `canManageVenues`.

A venue has a city, timezone, address and capacity, and one or more seating charts. A chart has sections that are general admission (with a capacity) or reserved (with seats). `publishSeatingChart` publishes a draft. A published chart is immutable; to change it, create a new draft and publish it. Charts move between `draft`, `published` and `retired`.

## Artists and tours

Route: `/dashboard/platform/artists`. Needs `canManageArtists`. Artists belong to an agency and can be on a tour.

## Promo codes

Route: `/dashboard/platform/promo-codes`. Promo and presale codes. A code is reserved with the hold and committed only when payment succeeds.

## Settings and store

`/dashboard/platform/settings` holds the promoter name, currency and `refundApprovalThresholdCents`. `/dashboard/platform/store` sets the logo and branding shown on the storefront.
