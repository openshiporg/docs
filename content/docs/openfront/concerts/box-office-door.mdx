## Box office

Route: `/dashboard/platform/box-office`. Needs `canManageOrders`.

Staff find an order by code or email, then refund, transfer or comp. At the window, `previewDoorSale` prices a sale and `issueDoorSale` completes it with a cash or card tender. Each shift opens and closes a cash drawer (`openCashDrawer`, `closeCashDrawer`). Door cash sales and cash refunds need your open drawer. **Count and close** records the counted cash and a variance reason.

`/dashboard/platform/box-office/drawers` is the cash drawer report for a date range in the store's clock: totals, then every shift with expected cash, counted cash, and variance (up to 200 most recent). It needs `canManageOrders` or `canReadPayments`. A user with `canApproveAdjustments` can close someone else's open drawer with a reason (`forceCloseCashDrawer`).

`refundTicketOrder` refunds an order through the provider that took the payment. It needs `canIssueRefunds`, a reason and an idempotency key. A refund above `refundApprovalThresholdCents` is sent for approval (`requestRefundApproval`), and a different user with `canApproveAdjustments` decides it (`decideRefundApproval`).

`transferTicket` voids the old ticket token and issues a new one to the new holder in one transaction.

## Door scan

Route: `/dashboard/platform/door`. Needs `canScanTickets`.

`scanTicket` admits a valid ticket once and records a `ScanEvent`. A second scan returns duplicate. Void, refunded and transferred tickets are refused. The page shows a live admitted count.

## Orders and customers

`/dashboard/platform/orders` lists every order with its source: `online`, `box_office`, `comp` or `presale`. `/dashboard/platform/customers` lists customers, staff and roles.
