Route: `/dashboard/platform/settlements` and `/dashboard/platform/settlements/[id]`.

A `Settlement` is the money document for one event. The list has tabs: Open (everything but `void`), draft, reviewed, promoter signed, artist signed, final.

## The worksheet

The detail page shows each figure and then the lines behind it:

| Figure | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Gross tickets | Paid order totals less refunds |
| Pass-through fees | Service and facility fees. They are never part of the artist split |
| Tax | Tax on paid orders |
| Net box office | Gross less tax and pass-through fees |
| Counted expenses | Capped expenses up to the cap, plus uncapped expenses |
| Breakeven | Counted expenses, guarantee, and promoter profit allowance |
| Split base | The amount the percentage applies to |
| Artist guarantee, overage, total | What the artist earns |
| Venue merch share | The venue's cut of soft and hard merchandise |
| Deposits paid | Deposits recorded as paid |
| Balance due to artist | Artist total less deposits and payments |
| Promoter net | What is left for the promoter |

Integer cents only. Each percentage rounds down. The rounding remainder is saved on a `rounding` line.

## Computing

`computeSettlement` needs the event in `completed` or `settling`, and at least one accepted artist deal. It reads the paid orders, the expenses, the merch record, and the paid artist payments. It saves a `computedSnapshot` with a `hash` and moves a `completed` event to `settling`. Recomputing a `draft` or `reviewed` settlement voids it and creates the next version. The command checks that the artist lines add up to the balance due.

## Sign-off

`signSettlement` moves a settlement one step: `draft`, `reviewed`, `signed_promoter`, `signed_artist`, `final`. The button label names the next step. The artist signature must come from a different user than the promoter signature. Before each step it checks that the saved snapshot still matches its hash.

A signed settlement (`signed_promoter` or later) is frozen. `computeSettlement` and `recordMerchSales` refuse to change it.

## Corrections and payments

These are GraphQL commands. The page has no form for them.

- `adjustSettlement` creates a new draft version with an adjustment line. It needs a reason and a different user with `canApproveAdjustments` as approver.
- `recordMerchSales` stores soft and hard merchandise gross and computes the venue share from the frozen deal terms.
- `recordArtistPayment` records a deposit or a balance. A paid balance needs a `final` settlement and cannot exceed the balance due. Paying the balance in full moves the event and its offers to `settled`. Money is recorded, not moved. No payout runs.

The page lists existing artist payments with kind, reference, status, and amount.
