---
title: Settlements
description: The post-show worksheet, adjustments, sign-off and artist payments.
icon: FileSpreadsheet
---

Route: `/dashboard/platform/settlements` and `/dashboard/platform/settlements/[id]`. Needs `canManageSettlements`.

After the show, `computeSettlement` builds the worksheet from ticket sales, fees, expenses, merch and bar sales, and the offer's frozen terms. The math is in `features/keystone/lib/settlement.ts` and has unit tests. The computed snapshot is hashed, so later edits create a new version instead of changing the old one.

| Step | Mutation |
|---|---|
| Record merch and bar | `recordMerchSales` |
| Compute the worksheet | `computeSettlement` |
| Adjust a line | `adjustSettlement` (needs `canApproveAdjustments`) |
| Sign for promoter and artist | `signSettlement` |
| Pay the artist | `recordArtistPayment` |

Settlement statuses are `draft`, `reviewed`, `signed_promoter`, `signed_artist`, `final` and `void`. Artist payments are a deposit or a balance, and are `scheduled`, `paid` or `void`.
