---

Route: `/dashboard/platform/customers`.

The page lists the first 200 users with name, email, and role, and then the roles with the number of users assigned. Users with no role show as Customer. A role row links to its edit page at `/dashboard/roles/[id]`.

Customers who buy tickets do not need a user. A guest checkout stores the name and email on the order. A signed-in user's order links to their `User` record.

Create users and assign roles at `/dashboard/users` and `/dashboard/roles`. Those need `canManagePeople` and `canManageRoles`. See [Roles and permissions](/docs/openfront/concerts/roles-permissions).
