Route: `/dashboard/platform/calendar`.

The page groups `DateHold` records by venue and date. Each row shows the rank (1st, 2nd, and so on), the artist, the status, and either the linked event or the expiry date.

## Hold rules

- A hold has a `rank`. The pair of venue, date, and rank is unique, so there is one 1st hold, one 2nd hold, and so on for each venue and date.
- Statuses: `held`, `challenged`, `confirmed`, `released`.
- `challengeHold` moves a `held` hold to `challenged`. It fails once the hold has expired.
- `confirmHold` needs `held` or `challenged` status, an unexpired hold, and no earlier-ranked live hold on that venue and date ("Earlier ranked hold must be released"). It also needs a **published** seating chart that belongs to the same venue. It creates an event in `hold`, makes the hold's artist the headliner, and moves the hold to `confirmed`.

## What the page does

Only **Challenge** has a button, and only on a `held` row. Placing a hold (`placeDateHold`) and confirming one (`confirmHold`) are GraphQL commands with no button today. See [GraphQL API](/docs/openfront/concerts/api-reference).

No command releases a hold.
