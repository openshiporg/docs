Route: `/dashboard/platform/events` and `/dashboard/platform/events/[id]`. Needs `canManageEvents`.

The list has tabs by status, with tickets sold, open capacity and gross for each event. Statuses are `hold`, `announced`, `presale`, `on_sale`, `sold_out`, `doors`, `in_progress`, `completed`, `settling`, `settled`, `cancelled` and `postponed`.

The event page has overview, billing (headliner and support), pricing, seating map, orders, expenses and settlement.

## Pricing

`savePriceLevel` and `setPriceLevelStatus` manage a price level per section. `savePricePhase` adds a phase, for example an early price that ends on a date. Fees and tax are copied onto each order line at purchase.

## Seating and production holds

The seating map shows each seat as open, held, sold or killed. `setProductionHold` keeps seats off sale for production, artist or house use; `releaseProductionHold` puts them back.

## Expenses

`saveExpense` records an event expense. Expenses feed the settlement and can be capped by the offer.

## Publishing and changes

| Action | Mutation |
|---|---|
| Announce or put on sale | `publishEvent`, `advanceEvent` |
| Postpone to a new date | `postponeEvent` |
| Cancel and refund | `cancelEvent` |
