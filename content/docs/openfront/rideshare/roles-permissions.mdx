`Fleet` is the tenant boundary. A user's access to a fleet's records comes from an active `FleetMembership` and its role.

| Capability | Roles | Covers |
|---|---|---|
| operations | `owner`, `dispatcher` | Dispatch, trips, cancellations, services view |
| drivers | `owner`, `dispatcher` | Drivers, vehicles, documents, availability |
| finance | `owner`, `finance` | Payments, refunds, ledger |
| safety | `owner`, `support` | Safety incidents, support cases, review moderation |
| fleetAdmin | `owner` | Services and fares setup, team, settings |

A `viewer` has none. A global role with `canManageRoles` sees every fleet. Membership status must be `active`.

## Probes

The dashboard calls `rideshareCapabilities`, `dispatchQueueScope`, `safetyQueueScope`, and `financeQueueScope` to decide which pages to load.

## Locked fields

Tenant relationships (`fleet`, `driver`, `vehicle`, `trip`) are immutable after creation. Dispatch-gating statuses (driver status, dispatch status, vehicle status) change only through review and dispatch mutations. Plate, VIN, and file references are not readable through the API.

## Riders and drivers

Riders are `User` records, optionally with a `RiderProfile`. Guests prove ownership with a request proof or trip token. Drivers reach their offers and trips through `myDriverWorkspace`.
