---
title: Collect payment and refund
description: Collect a fare by card or manually, and refund it.
icon: HandCoins
---

import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

## Card

<Steps>
<Step>
### Rider pays
A signed-in rider presses Pay on the ride page after a driver is assigned.
</Step>
<Step>
### Check the payment
On the trip page the payment shows `captured` once Checkout completes.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Manual

<Steps>
<Step>
### Record the charge
On the trip press Record payment.
</Step>
<Step>
### Confirm
After you collect the fare, press Confirm payment. It becomes `captured`.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Refund

<Steps>
<Step>
### Request
On a captured payment press Refund, enter the amount (up to what remains) and a reason.
</Step>
<Step>
### Confirm
For manual payments press Confirm refund after returning the money. The refund becomes `succeeded`.
</Step>
</Steps>
