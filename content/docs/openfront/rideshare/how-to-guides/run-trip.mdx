---
title: Run a trip
description: Move a trip from assignment to completion.
icon: Route
---

import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

<Steps>
<Step>
### Start heading to pickup
The driver presses On my way (`start_en_route`). Scheduled trips open two hours before pickup.
</Step>
<Step>
### Arrive
Press Arrived (`arrive`). The rider sees the status on their ride page.
</Step>
<Step>
### Start the trip
Press Start trip (`start_trip`) when the rider is in the vehicle.
</Step>
<Step>
### Complete
Press Complete (`complete`). Payment can now be recorded or paid by card.
</Step>
</Steps>

If the rider does not show, press No-show after arriving (`no_show`). The trip's timeline lists every transition.
