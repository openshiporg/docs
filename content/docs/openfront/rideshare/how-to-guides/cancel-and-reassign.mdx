---
title: Cancel and reassign a trip
description: Cancel a trip with the right fee, or send the request back to dispatch.
icon: RotateCcw
---

import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open the trip
Go to the trip page. Cancel is available while the trip is `driver_assigned`, `driver_en_route`, or `driver_arrived`.
</Step>
<Step>
### Review the fee
Press Cancel trip. The dialog shows the cancellation fee from the fare rule.
</Step>
<Step>
### Choose a reason
Rider requested, Driver unavailable, Vehicle unavailable, Operations change, or Other.
</Step>
<Step>
### Decide on reassignment
Choose Cancel and reassign to send the request back to Dispatch instead of cancelling the rider's request.
</Step>
<Step>
### Confirm
If the screen says "Cancellation unconfirmed", press Recover cancellation to replay the same command.
</Step>
</Steps>

Riders cancel an unassigned request from their ride page.
