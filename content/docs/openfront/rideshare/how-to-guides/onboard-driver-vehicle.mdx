---
title: Onboard a driver and vehicle
description: Add a driver and vehicle, accept documents, approve eligibility, and set hours.
icon: UserCheck
---

import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

<Steps>
<Step>
### Add the driver
On Drivers press Add driver and enter the public name and email. The profile starts `pending_review` and offline.
</Step>
<Step>
### Add the vehicle
On Vehicles add label, make, model, year, color, masked plate, capacity, and wheelchair access. Assign the driver.
</Step>
<Step>
### Record documents
Add the driver's license, insurance, and background check, and the vehicle's registration, insurance, and inspection, with issuer, masked reference, and expiry. Accept each one after you check it.
</Step>
<Step>
### Review eligibility
Press approve on the driver and the vehicle. Each records a policy evaluation and sets the status to `active`. Use `ineligible`, `paused`, or `offboarded` for drivers, and `maintenance`, `suspended`, or `retired` for vehicles.
</Step>
<Step>
### Set weekly hours
On the driver page set the time zone and the days and hours they work. Add time off for planned absences.
</Step>
<Step>
### Go online
The driver opens My driving and turns on Online.
</Step>
</Steps>
