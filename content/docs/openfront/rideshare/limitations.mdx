## Dispatch and routing

- Dispatch is manual. Fleet operations mode is `off` or `manual`; there is no automatic matching, batching, or surge pricing.
- Route distance and duration are estimates from labels. There is no geocoding, map display, live ETA, or turn-by-turn navigation.
- Location tracking is limited to coarse, expiring `LocationEvent` rows submitted through `recordTripLocation`.

## Payments and settlement

- Only the manual payment adapter is installed. It records obligations and does not charge cards, capture funds, or issue refunds.
- `Payout` exists in the schema, but no mutation or page creates payouts. The code states that no payout or revenue journal is posted until a reviewed allocation policy and settlement workflow exist.
- There are no payment webhooks.

## Riders

- Guest riders can book, track, and cancel with their request proof, but cannot rate a trip, open a support case, report a safety issue, or message the driver. Those mutations require a signed-in participant.
- Rider signup is one global switch, not per fleet. It opens when any active fleet sets rider accounts to `open`, or when `PUBLIC_SIGNUPS_ALLOWED` is `true`. A new rider account is a plain `User`; no `RiderProfile` is created at signup.
- The storefront has no saved places, payment methods, or in-app payment step. Payment is recorded by operators on the trip.

## Drivers and compliance

- Compliance documents store kind, issuer, masked reference, and dates. There is no file upload for documents and no external verification.
- Identity and background-check provider connections have no working adapters. Eligibility decisions are operator reviews recorded as `PolicyEvaluation` rows.
- The software does not license drivers or vehicles, verify insurance, or establish that a fleet may operate a ride-for-hire service.

## Notifications

- The only notification adapter writes to the console and is disabled in production. Riders and drivers do not receive SMS, push, or email trip updates.

## No certification

The repository does not provide PCI, privacy, accessibility, or transportation-regulator certification. Tests, type checks, and builds show only the behavior they exercise.
