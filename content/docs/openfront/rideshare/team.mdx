`/dashboard/platform/team` manages `FleetMembership` rows. Add members with `addFleetMember(fleetId, email, role)`; the person must already have an account. It needs the fleet admin capability (`owner`).

| Role | Capabilities |
|---|---|
| `owner` | operations, drivers, finance, safety, fleet admin |
| `dispatcher` | operations, drivers |
| `finance` | finance |
| `support` | safety |
| `viewer` | none |

Membership status: `invited` → `active` ⇄ `suspended`. Change a role or suspend and reactivate from the row actions; they call `updateFleetMember(membershipId, role, status)`. You cannot remove your own owner access, and a fleet always keeps at least one active owner. See [Roles and permissions](/docs/openfront/rideshare/roles-permissions).
