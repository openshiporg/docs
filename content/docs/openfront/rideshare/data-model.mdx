38 lists in `features/keystone/models`. Create and delete are closed on tenant lists; records are created through named mutations. Money is integer minor units.

| Domain | Lists |
|---|---|
| Identity | `User`, `Role`, `Fleet`, `FleetMembership`, `DriverProfile`, `RiderProfile`, `FleetBrandingChange` |
| Operations | `Vehicle`, `ComplianceDocument`, `Policy`, `PolicyEvaluation`, `ProviderConnection` |
| Service | `ServiceArea`, `RideService`, `AvailabilityRule`, `AvailabilityException`, `FareRule`, `RideQuote` |
| Ride | `RideRequest`, `RequestCancellationCommand`, `MatchOffer`, `Trip`, `TripEvent`, `LocationEvent`, `Cancellation` |
| Safety | `SafetyIncident`, `SupportCase`, `Conversation`, `Message`, `Review` |
| Finance | `PaymentProvider`, `Payment`, `Refund`, `PaymentObservation`, `LedgerEntry`, `Payout` |
| Integration | `Notification`, `MarketplaceRegistration` |

## Relationships

- `Fleet` has memberships, drivers, vehicles, service areas, services, fare rules, rides, trips, policies, provider connections, and a marketplace registration.
- `RideService` links to many `ServiceArea` rows; a `FareRule` prices one service in one area.
- `RideQuote` snapshots the fare rule and links to one `RideRequest`.
- `RideRequest` has many `MatchOffer` rows and at most one `Trip`.
- An accepted `MatchOffer` creates the `Trip`, which links rider, driver, vehicle, `TripEvent`, `LocationEvent`, `Cancellation`, `Payment`, `LedgerEntry`, `SafetyIncident`, `SupportCase`, `Conversation`, and `Review`.
- `Payment` has many `Refund`, `LedgerEntry`, and `PaymentObservation` rows.
- `DriverProfile` and `Vehicle` have `ComplianceDocument` and `PolicyEvaluation` rows; vehicles link to many drivers.
