`/dashboard/platform/trips` lists scheduled, active, and completed trips. `/dashboard/platform/trips/[id]` is the trip detail. Operators need the operations capability to act on trips.

## Lifecycle

| Action | From | To |
|---|---|---|
| `start_en_route` | `driver_assigned` | `driver_en_route` |
| `arrive` | `driver_en_route` | `driver_arrived` |
| `start_trip` | `driver_arrived` | `in_progress` |
| `complete` | `in_progress` | `completed` |
| `no_show` | `driver_arrived` | `no_show` |

`transitionTrip(tripId, action, reason, idempotencyKey, expectedActorId, expectedAcceptedOfferId)` takes the expected actor and accepted offer, so a stale screen cannot move the wrong trip. Each transition writes a `TripEvent`. Trip statuses: `driver_assigned`, `driver_en_route`, `driver_arrived`, `in_progress`, `completed`, `cancelled`, `no_show`.

## Cancel a trip

Press Cancel trip or Cancel and reassign. The dialog loads `tripCancellationPreview` to show the fee, then you choose a reason: Rider requested, Driver unavailable, Vehicle unavailable, Operations change, or Other. `cancelRide` records a `Cancellation` with the actor (`rider`, `driver`, `operator`, `system`), fee, and fee outcome (`no`, `partial`, `full`, `manual_review`). Cancellation is allowed from `driver_assigned`, `driver_en_route`, and `driver_arrived`. With reassign on, the request goes back to dispatch. See [Cancel and reassign](/docs/openfront/rideshare/how-to-guides/cancel-and-reassign).

## Detail sections

- **Stage rail and route:** status and pickup to drop-off.
- **Driver, vehicle, rider, passengers.**
- **Payments:** record a charge, confirm a manual payment, request a refund. See [Payments](/docs/openfront/rideshare/finance).
- **Timeline:** `TripEvent` rows and any cancellation.
- **Messages:** the trip `Conversation`. Operators send with `sendTripMessage`.
- **Safety and support:** incidents and cases linked to the trip.
- **Reviews:** ratings left by the rider and driver.

The page reads `operatorTripHandoff(tripId, cursor)` for the timeline.

## Recovery

If a command response is lost, the page shows "Cancellation unconfirmed" or a recovery button. Retrying replays the same idempotency key, so the command is not applied twice.
