`/dashboard/platform/dispatch` is the dispatcher's board. It needs the operations capability (`owner` or `dispatcher` membership) and the fleet's operations mode set to `manual`. With operations `off`, the page shows a notice and actions are disabled.

## What you can do

- review open `RideRequest` rows (`requested`, `matching`)
- search eligible driver and vehicle candidates with `dispatchCandidatePage(requestId, cursor, query)`
- send an offer with `offerRideToDriver`
- see cancelled and expired history

## Offer a ride

<Steps>
<Step>
### Pick a request
Select a request to see pickup, drop-off, passengers, accessibility needs, notes, and the scheduled time.
</Step>
<Step>
### Choose a driver and vehicle
Candidates are drivers whose latest `PolicyEvaluation` is `eligible`, with a vehicle that is also eligible and fits the passengers and accessibility needs. Immediate rides need the driver `available`. Scheduled rides can go to active drivers who are not currently available, as long as availability rules and time off allow the slot.
</Step>
<Step>
### Send the offer
`offerRideToDriver(requestId, driverId, vehicleId, expiresInSeconds, idempotencyKey, expectedActorId)` creates a `MatchOffer` (`offered`), reserves the driver and vehicle for the slot, and moves the driver to `offered`.
</Step>
<Step>
### The driver responds
In My driving the driver accepts or declines (`respondToRideOffer`). Accepting creates the `Trip` with status `driver_assigned`. Declining or expiry releases the reservation.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Offer statuses

`offered`, `accepted`, `declined`, `expired`, `withdrawn`. `expireRideOffers(fleetId)` and `expireRequestFleetOffers(requestId)` release stale offers.

## Guards

- A driver or vehicle with an overlapping active reservation is refused.
- If eligibility changes after the offer, acceptance fails with "Driver or vehicle eligibility changed after the offer was made."
- Every dispatch command takes an idempotency key (12 to 200 characters). A key reused for another command is refused.

See [Dispatch a ride](/docs/openfront/rideshare/how-to-guides/dispatch-ride).
