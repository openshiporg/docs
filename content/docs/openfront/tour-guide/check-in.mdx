`/dashboard/platform/check-in` lists today's departures. Open one with `?departure=<id>` to see its manifest. It needs `canCheckIn`. The layout works at 402 pixels wide.

## The manifest

Each booking shows the lead booker, booking code, status, pickup location, and special requests. Under it, each participant shows:

- Name and a check-in state.
- Dietary notes.
- A medical flag. The note text shows only to a role with `canReadMedicalNotes`. Other staff see "Medical note".
- "Waiver missing" when the participant has not signed.

The manifest lists bookings in `confirmed`, `checked_in`, `completed`, `no_show`, and `partially_refunded`.

## Actions

- **Check in** (`checkInParticipant`) is available when the booking is `confirmed` or `checked_in`. A missing waiver warns but does not block. When every participant is checked in, the booking moves to `checked_in`.
- **No show** (`markNoShow`) applies to a `confirmed` booking. It also marks its pending participants as no-show.

## Audit

When a manifest includes medical notes and the viewer can read them, the read is written to the audit log as `medical_notes.read`.
