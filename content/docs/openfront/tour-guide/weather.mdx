---
title: Weather cancellations
description: Cancel a departure for weather, then rebook or refund each guest.
icon: CloudLightning
---

**Weather cancel** on [Today](/docs/openfront/tour-guide/today) cancels a departure with a required reason (`weatherCancelDeparture`, needs `canManageDepartures`). No guest is notified by the app.

`/dashboard/platform/weather` then lists every weather-cancelled departure and waits for a choice on each booking:

- **Rebook** onto another departure of the same tour (`rebookWeatherBooking`, needs `canManageBookings`). What the guest paid is the credit. The new booking is priced from the same tickets and add-ons and must cost exactly that.
- **Full refund**, through the normal refund. A refund above the approval threshold still needs a second manager.

A reseller booking says "Reseller booking: refund, or re-plan with the reseller." When every guest is handled, the departure shows "Every guest on this departure has been rebooked or refunded."
