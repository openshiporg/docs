`/dashboard/platform/settings` edits the one `StoreSettings` row (`updateStoreSettings`, needs `canManageSettings`). The form has **Operator name**, **Timezone**, **Address**, **Tax rate (%)**, the refund amount that needs a second manager, and **Waitlist offer window (hours)**. A role without access sees "No operator settings are available to your role." Logo icon, color and SVG are on `/dashboard/platform/store`.

## Fields

- `name`: the operator name. It is the dashboard brand and the storefront header and page title. The fallback is "Openfront Tour Guide".
- `timezone`, `address`, `currencyCode` (default `USD`).
- `taxBps`: tax in basis points, applied to the amount after discount.
- `refundApprovalThresholdCents`: a refund above this amount needs a manager approval. Default 10000.
- `waitlistOfferHours`: how long a waitlist offer holds seats. Default 12, from 1 to 168.

There is one settings row. Onboarding creates it.

Staff screens show times and days in this time zone. A tour's own `timezone` decides its start times.
