`/dashboard/platform/gift-cards` lists gift cards, newest first, with their ledger and the outstanding balance (`giftCardBoard`). It needs `canManagePayments`. **Find a gift card** searches by the last four characters, recipient, or full code.

## Issue a gift card

**Issue a gift card** (`issueGiftCard`) takes an amount from 1.00 to 2,000.00, an expiry, recipient name and email, and a note. **Funded by** is **Sold for cash** or **Complimentary voucher**. The page shows the code once: "Give the guest this code. They enter it at checkout". **Show code** (`revealGiftCardCode`) shows it again for a guest who lost it, and the reveal is audited.

The database keeps only a hash of the code. Every balance change is a `GiftCardEntry`.

## Redeem

At storefront checkout the guest opens **Have a gift card?** and enters the code. `giftCardBalance` shows what it is worth, and gives the same answer for a wrong code and a void one. The card pays first and the bank card pays the rest. A refund of the gift card part goes back on the card, never out as cash.

## Void

`voidGiftCard` voids a lost card or one sold in error. The remaining balance is written off in the ledger.
