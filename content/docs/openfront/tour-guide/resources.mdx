`/dashboard/platform/resources` is a generic Keystone list screen for `Resource`. It needs `canManageResources`.

## Fields

- `kind`: `van`, `kayak`, `bike`, or `boat`.
- `seats`: seats per unit.
- `quantity`: how many units the pool holds, such as 16 kayaks.
- `status`: `active` or `maintenance`. Only an `active` resource can be assigned. Set it at creation. Update cannot change it, so a resource cannot be moved into or out of maintenance from the dashboard.

## Assigning to a departure

`assignResource` (needs `canManageResources`) takes a departure, a resource, and a quantity. It checks that:

- the resource kind matches the tour's `requiresResourceKind`,
- the quantity fits the pool,
- the pool is not already used by an overlapping departure (half-open time intervals, summed by quantity),
- the new seat limit still covers booked and held seats.

A departure holds one resource assignment. A new one replaces the old. The departure's capacity becomes the smaller of the tour maximum and `seats × quantity`.

`resizeDeparture` can lower capacity but not below booked and held seats. It cannot exceed the tour maximum or the assigned resource seats.
