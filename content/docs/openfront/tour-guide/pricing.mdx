`/dashboard/platform/pricing` edits pricing rules (`savePricingRule`, `removePricingRule`, needs `canManageTours`). Without rules, guests pay the listed ticket prices.

## A rule

**Add a rule** with:

- **Rule name** and **Applies to**, a tour and either **Every ticket type** or one ticket type.
- **First day** and a last day.
- **Weekdays (none picked means every day)**.
- **Change type**, either **Percent up or down** (`adjustBps`) or **Fixed price per ticket** (`overridePriceCents`).

Rules are applied at quote time, on the storefront and for staff bookings. Every booking keeps its own price snapshot, so saving or removing a rule never changes a booking that already exists. Each change is audited with before and after.
