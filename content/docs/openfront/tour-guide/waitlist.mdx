`/dashboard/platform/waitlist` lists guests waiting for full departures and the offers held for them (`waitlistBoard`, needs `canManageBookings`).

## How a guest joins

On the storefront tour page, a party that does not fit a departure can **Join the waitlist** (`joinWaitlist`). The guest gets a private link to `/waitlist`. It shows their place and lets them book when seats are held. The proof travels in the URL fragment.

Staff add a phone caller with **Add a phone caller** (`addToWaitlist`): guest name, email, phone, party size, and participants (one per line). Give the guest the private link it returns.

## Offers

When seats free up (a cancel, a move away, a resize, or an expired hold), offers go to the oldest entry first. Each offer is a real seat hold for the party until the offer window ends, so a new arrival cannot take the seats. The window is **Waitlist offer window (hours)** on [Settings](/docs/openfront/tour-guide/settings), 12 hours by default. After that the seats go to the next guest.

- The guest books the held seats through the normal checkout (`claimWaitlistOffer`).
- Staff take cash for an open offer with **Book for cash** (`bookWaitlistOffer`).
- The guest can leave from their link (`leaveWaitlist`). Staff remove an entry with `withdrawWaitlistEntry`.

**Run daily check** on [Today](/docs/openfront/tour-guide/today) also makes due waitlist offers.
