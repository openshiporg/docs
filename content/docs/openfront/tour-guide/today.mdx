`/dashboard/platform/today` lists each departure that starts today in the operator's time zone (America/New_York). It needs `canCheckIn` because it reads the departure list that guides use.

## What a card shows

- Tour name, start and end time, and the departure status.
- **Seats**: booked seats over capacity. Held seats are shown separately and do not count as booked.
- **Guides**: accepted guides over the guides needed.
- **Held**: seats in unexpired holds.

Guides needed is the booked seats divided by the tour's guests-per-guide ratio, rounded up. The ratio is shown, not enforced. A departure that has bookings but too few accepted guides shows "Not staffed". A departure with unsigned waivers shows how many are missing. A departure in `min_not_met` shows "Minimum not met".

## Actions

- **Manifest** opens the [check-in](/docs/openfront/tour-guide/check-in) page for that departure.
- **Cancel departure** opens a popover with two choices: **Weather cancel** and **Operator cancel**. Both call `cancelDeparture`, which needs `canManageDepartures`. See [Cancel a departure](/docs/openfront/tour-guide/how-to-guides/cancel-a-departure).

## Notes

The page reads `departuresInRange`. That query allows at most 31 days. It does not offer the "offer credit" choice that `cancelDeparture` supports. Use GraphQL for that.
