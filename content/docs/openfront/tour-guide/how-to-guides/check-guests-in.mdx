---
title: Check guests in
description: Use the manifest on a phone to check guests in and mark no-shows.
icon: ListChecks
---

You need `canCheckIn`. A guide's role from setup has it.

1. Open `/dashboard/platform/check-in`. It lists today's departures.
2. Open the departure. Each booking lists its guests.
3. Read the flags: dietary notes, a medical flag, and "Waiver missing".
4. Select **Check in** for each guest who arrives. A missing waiver warns but does not stop check-in.
5. When every guest in a booking is checked in, the booking becomes `checked_in`.
6. For a booking that did not show up, select **No show**.

Medical notes show only to a role with `canReadMedicalNotes`. Viewing a manifest that includes them is written to the audit log.

After the departure ends, a manager calls `closeDeparture`. See [Guides](/docs/openfront/tour-guide/guides) for what it does to payouts.
