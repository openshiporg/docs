---
title: Customers
description: Guests from recent bookings. Guests book without an account.
icon: UserRound
---

`/dashboard/platform/customers` lists guests from the latest 500 bookings, newest first. It is read-only; open Bookings to act on one.

Guests do not need an account. A booking stores the lead booker's name, email, and phone. A signed-in user who books has the booking linked to them through `customer`. The storefront has no sign-in or sign-up page.

The page groups bookings by email, so it needs `canManageBookings`. Signed-in users are in the generic `User` list. Who can see them depends on `canSeeOtherPeople` or `canManageCustomers`; editing needs `canEditOtherPeople`.

Bookings, participants, and waivers contain personal data. Participant medical notes are readable only with `canReadMedicalNotes`.
