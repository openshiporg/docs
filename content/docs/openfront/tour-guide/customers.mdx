`/dashboard/platform/customers` is a generic Keystone list screen for `User`. It is the same list as the dashboard's Users model.

Guests do not need an account. A booking stores the lead booker's name, email, and phone. A signed-in user who books has the booking linked to them through `customer`. The storefront has no sign-in or sign-up page.

Who can see users depends on the `Role` flags `canSeeOtherPeople` and `canEditOtherPeople`. `canManageCustomers` exists as a flag but no screen or operation reads it.

Bookings, participants, and waivers contain personal data. Participant medical notes are readable only with `canReadMedicalNotes`.
