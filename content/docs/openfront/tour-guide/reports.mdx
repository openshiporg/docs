---
title: Sales report
description: Sales by tour, channel and source, occupancy, a weekly trend, and a CSV export.
icon: ChartColumn
---

`/dashboard/platform/reports` builds a sales report for a date range (`salesReport`). It needs `canReadPayments`. The range is 400 days or less.

- Choose **Date booked** or **Departure date**, then **Run report**.
- **Totals** come grouped by tour, channel, and source.
- **Occupancy** is seats sold against capacity for running departures that start in this range.
- **Weekly trend** shows the same totals by week.

The CSV export (`exportSalesCsv`) is built on the server. Each export is audited with who ran it, the range, the row count, and the totals.
