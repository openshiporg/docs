Bags are created at check-in with `acceptCheckedBag`. After that, `/dashboard/platform/baggage` is where scans are recorded.

## Find a bag

Search by bag tag number or booking record locator. `/dashboard/platform/baggage/[id]` shows the bag, its flight, the passenger, and a timeline of `BagEvent` records.

## Record a scan

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open the bag
Only the actions allowed from the bag's current status are shown.
</Step>
<Step>
### Enter station and note
Station is an optional three-letter airport code. The note is up to 300 characters.
</Step>
<Step>
### Choose the action
`recordBagEvent` with `bagId`, `action`, `airportCode`, `note`. Mishandled and Close ask for confirmation.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Status transitions

| From | Actions available |
|---|---|
| accepted | load, transfer, delay, mishandle |
| loaded | transfer, arrive, delay, mishandle |
| in_transit | load, arrive, delay, mishandle |
| arrived | deliver, delay, mishandle |
| delayed | transfer, arrive, deliver, mishandle, close |

Statuses: accepted, loaded, in_transit, arrived, delivered, delayed, mishandled, closed. Bag types: checked, gate_checked, special_item, mobility_aid.

Delayed and mishandled bags appear in the "Mishandled bags" queue on [Carrier operations](/docs/openfront/airline/operations).
