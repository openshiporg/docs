The Fares desk has four tabs. Create, edit, and change status from each tab. Mutations: `CreateCabin`, `CreateFareBrand`, `CreateFareRule`, `CreateAncillaryProduct`, and their update and status counterparts.

## Cabins

A `Cabin` has a `code`, `name`, `cabinClass` (economy, premium_economy, business, first), and `displayOrder`. Cabin codes are unique per carrier: "A cabin with this code already exists."

## Fare brands

A `FareBrand` belongs to a cabin and is what a traveler sees as a fare choice.

- `code`, `name`, `description`, `displayOrder`, `status`
- `includedBaggage` (for example one checked bag), `benefits`
- `refundabilitySummary`, `changeabilitySummary`, `seatSelectionSummary`

Dated service requires an active fare brand. Brand codes are unique per carrier.

## Fare rules

A `FareRule` attaches conditions to a fare brand, optionally for one route.

- `fareBasisPattern` using letters, digits, `*` and `?`
- `effectiveFrom`/`effectiveTo`, `travelFrom`/`travelTo`
- `salesChannels`, `passengerTypes`
- `minimumStayDays`, `maximumStayDays` (maximum must be at least minimum), `minimumAdvancePurchaseHours`
- `isRefundable`, `isChangeable`, `changeFeeAmount`, `cancellationFeeAmount`, `noShowFeeAmount`, `currencyCode`
- `status`: draft, active, inactive, archived

## Ancillary products

An `AncillaryProduct` has a `type` (seat, checked_bag, cabin_bag, priority, lounge, meal, wifi, assistance, other), a `fulfillmentType` (associated or standalone), `defaultPriceAmount`, `taxAmount`, `currencyCode`, and `requiresInventory`. Status is draft, active, inactive, or archived.

All amounts are integer minor units, for example cents.
