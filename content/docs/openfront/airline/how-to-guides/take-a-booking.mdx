---
title: Take a booking
description: Hold seats and accept an offer for a caller or counter customer.
icon: BookOpenCheck
---

import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

<Steps>
<Step>
### Search
On `/dashboard/platform/offers` pick the passenger party and search flights.
</Step>
<Step>
### Hold
Create the offer for the chosen flight and bucket. It holds the seats for 15 minutes.
</Step>
<Step>
### Accept
On `/dashboard/platform/bookings`, find the offer under **Held offers** and accept it with the passenger list. The booking gets a record locator.
</Step>
<Step>
### Give the traveler their access
Use **New access code** to issue an access code. Give the traveler the locator and code for Manage booking.
</Step>
<Step>
### Take payment
Continue with [Settle and ticket](/docs/openfront/airline/how-to-guides/settle-and-ticket).
</Step>
</Steps>

Unpaid storefront bookings expire after 24 hours when the worker is running. Held offers expire after 15 minutes.
