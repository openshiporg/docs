---
title: Handle a disruption
description: Publish a delay or cancellation and move affected bookings.
icon: RefreshCw
---

import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

<Steps>
<Step>
### Publish
On `/dashboard/platform/disruptions` choose the flight, the type, and a public summary of up to 500 characters. It appears on `/status`.
</Step>
<Step>
### Prepare a replacement
Provision or open a flight on the same origin and destination and confirm it has authorized inventory.
</Step>
<Step>
### Reaccommodate
For each affected booking choose the replacement flight and bucket and confirm.
</Step>
<Step>
### Refund where needed
If the traveler declines, cancel through [Payments and refunds](/docs/openfront/airline/payments-refunds) using the reason `disruption`.
</Step>
</Steps>

Boarded and flown passengers are not eligible for recovery.
