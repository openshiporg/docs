---
title: Launch a carrier
description: From the first sign-in to a storefront that sells seats.
icon: Rocket
---

import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

<Steps>
<Step>
### Create the first user
Open `/dashboard/init` on an empty database. Enter a name, email, and a password of at least 10 characters. This creates the carrier and a full-permission role.
</Step>
<Step>
### Run onboarding
Choose **Full network** or **Custom launch** in the onboarding dialog (also `/dashboard/onboarding`). Custom launch asks for carrier name and code, currency, timezone, origin, destination, flight number, date, times, capacity, and fare. See [Getting started](/docs/openfront/airline/getting-started#onboarding).
</Step>
<Step>
### Add a second payment operator
Launch review needs at least two users with `canManagePayments`. Create a role and user on `/dashboard/users`. See [Roles and permissions](/docs/openfront/airline/roles-permissions).
</Step>
<Step>
### Issue the settlement credential
Issue a machine credential with audience `settlement-import` and scope `settlements:write` from the onboarding form.
</Step>
<Step>
### Set the storefront copy
On [Storefront settings](/docs/openfront/airline/store-settings) enter the homepage title, description, brand colour, and customer service details.
</Step>
<Step>
### Activate a payment provider
On [Payment providers](/docs/openfront/airline/payment-providers) activate the manual provider.
</Step>
<Step>
### Check the storefront
Open `/`. Search should show your flight. If it says booking is unavailable, check direct sales on `/dashboard/platform/settings`.
</Step>
</Steps>
