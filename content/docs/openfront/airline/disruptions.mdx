`/dashboard/platform/disruptions` is the recovery desk. `/dashboard/platform/disruptions/[id]` shows one disruption and its affected bookings.

## Publish a disruption

`publishFlightDisruption` takes `flightInstanceId`, `type`, and `publicSummary` (up to 500 characters). Types: delay, cancellation, schedule_change, misconnection, diversion, equipment_change, other.

- A cancellation sets severity high and the flight to `cancelled`.
- A delay sets the flight to `delayed`.
- The public summary shows on the storefront `/status` page.

Disruption status: detected, published, recovering, resolved, cancelled.

## Reaccommodate a booking

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open the disruption
Affected bookings list with their segments.
</Step>
<Step>
### Pick the replacement
`reaccommodateBooking` takes `disruptionId`, `bookingId`, `replacementFlightInstanceId`, and `replacementInventoryBucketId`. The replacement must be a different future flight of the same carrier with the same origin and destination and enough inventory.
</Step>
<Step>
### Confirm
The command moves ticketed passengers' coupons and records a `Reaccommodation`. Boarded or flown passengers are not eligible.
</Step>
</Steps>

Recovery is unavailable after the original service has departed or arrived.
