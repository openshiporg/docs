`/dashboard/platform/store-settings` edits the public face of the carrier with `updateCarrierStorefrontSettings`. The page has a live preview.

## Fields

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| Carrier name, legal name | `name`, `legalName` |
| Brand colour | slate, blue, indigo, violet, rose, emerald, or amber. Anything else: "Choose one of the approved storefront colors." |
| Homepage title and description | `storefrontHomepageTitle`, `storefrontHomepageDescription` |
| Customer service | `storefrontContactEmail`, `storefrontContactPhone`, `storefrontServiceHours` |

Email and phone are validated ("Enter a valid contact email.", "Enter a valid contact phone.").

## What it controls

- The `/` homepage title and description, and the search section.
- `/contact`, which shows the email, phone, and hours.
- Whether online booking is available. Search and booking show only when the carrier is active, has direct sales on, an approved colour, a title, and a description.

## Readiness

`/dashboard/platform/settings` shows direct-sales state and provider connections with last success and last attempt, read only. Use it as the readiness check after setup.
