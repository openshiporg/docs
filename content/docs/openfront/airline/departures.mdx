`/dashboard/platform/departures` shows the departure manifest (`departureManifest`) by date and station. `/dashboard/platform/departures/[id]` opens one flight.

Station actions require an active [station assignment](/docs/openfront/airline/stations) for the flight's origin airport with the matching flag: `canCheckIn`, `canAcceptBags`, or `canBoard`.

## Work a passenger

<Steps>
<Step>
### Register the travel document
`registerPassengerTravelDocument` takes `documentType`, `documentNumber`, `issuingCountryCode`, optional `nationalityCode` and `issuedAt`, `expiresAt`, `providerReference`, `evidenceVersion`, and `verificationExpiresAt`. The number is tokenized before storage.
</Step>
<Step>
### Verify it
`verifyPassengerTravelDocument` records the verification for the segment. Check-in requires "A current authoritative travel-document verification."
</Step>
<Step>
### Check in
`checkInBookingPassenger` needs a paid, ticketed booking and a published seat map. It creates a `CheckIn`, a `SeatAssignment`, and a `BoardingPass`. An optional `seatId` picks the seat.
</Step>
<Step>
### Accept bags
`acceptCheckedBag` takes `bookingPassengerId`, `flightInstanceId`, and `weightGrams` (0 to 100000). The bag needs a checked-in passenger and a matching unused bag entitlement. A `Bag` with a tag number is created.
</Step>
<Step>
### Board or offload
`boardBookingPassenger` needs a checked-in coupon and a confirmed seat. `offloadBookingPassenger` needs a reason.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Statuses

Check-in: not_started, eligible, checked_in, boarded, blocked, offloaded, cancelled. Boarding pass: issued, reissued, boarded, revoked.

Seat rule: a paid seat needs an authorized, audited entitlement before check-in. A traveler cannot hold more than one active seat on a flight.
