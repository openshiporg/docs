Dated flights are what travelers buy. Use `/dashboard/platform/schedules` to create them from published schedules and `/dashboard/platform/inventory` to manage seats for sale.

## Provision a dated flight

<Steps>
<Step>
### Choose the schedule
Only published schedules appear. Open the schedule at `/dashboard/platform/schedules/[id]`.
</Step>
<Step>
### Enter the service
Pick the cabin and fare brand, service date, scheduled departure and arrival, capacity (1 to 300), base amount, tax amount, currency, and optional terminal and gate.
</Step>
<Step>
### Submit
`provisionDatedFlight` creates the `FlightInstance`, one `InventoryBucket`, and a generated `SeatMap` with seats. Scheduled departure must be in the future and on the service date in the carrier timezone, and arrival must be after departure within 24 hours.
</Step>
</Steps>

The currency must match the carrier default. Carrier code, currency, and timezone cannot change once dated flights, offers, or bookings exist.

## Dated flight status

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `scheduled` | Created, not yet for sale |
| `open_for_sale` | Visible to offers and the storefront |
| `closed_for_sale` | Held back from sale |
| `delayed`, `departed`, `arrived` | Set from [Flight operations](/docs/openfront/airline/flight-ops) |
| `cancelled` | Set when a cancellation is published |

`setDatedFlightSalesStatus` accepts only `open_for_sale` or `closed_for_sale`.

## Inventory buckets

`/dashboard/platform/inventory` lists buckets per flight with `capacity`, `authorized`, `sold`, `held`, and `blocked`. Change the number of seats offered for sale with `setInventoryAuthorization`. Sold, held, and blocked quantities cannot exceed what you authorize: "Sold, held, and blocked quantities exceed authorized inventory."

Each bucket carries a `bookingClassCode`, `baseAmount`, `taxAmount`, `currencyCode`, and `status`.
