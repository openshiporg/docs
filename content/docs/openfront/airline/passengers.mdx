`/dashboard/platform/passengers` lists `Passenger` records for the carrier. `/dashboard/platform/passengers/[id]` shows one profile.

## What you see

- Name, contact, and date of birth
- Counts of bookings, service requests, check-ins, and bags
- Travel documents and their verification state

Passenger fields: `title`, `givenName`, `middleNames`, `familyName`, `suffix`, `dateOfBirth`, `genderMarker`, `nationalityCode`, `email`, `phone`, `preferredLanguage`, `status`, `loyaltyPrograms`, `preferences`.

## Travel documents

Document numbers are not stored in clear text. `TravelDocument` keeps `documentNumberToken`, `documentNumberHash` (SHA-256), `encryptedPayload`, `issuingCountryCode`, `nationalityCode`, `expiresAt`, and `status`. Tokens use `TRAVEL_DOCUMENT_TOKENIZATION_KEY`. A rejected, expired, or revoked document cannot be reactivated. Registering and verifying documents happens at the departure station: see [Departures](/docs/openfront/airline/departures).

Editing `Passenger` records beyond the list needs `canManagePassengers`. Reading secure passenger data needs `canManageSecurePassengerData`.
