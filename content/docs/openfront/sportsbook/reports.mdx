description: Compliance reports, data exports, and retention.
icon: FileChartColumn
---

Route: `/dashboard/platform/reports`.

- `generateComplianceReport` creates a `ComplianceReport` with `ReportArtifact` references.
- `requestDataExport` then `applyApprovedDataExport` produce a `DataExport` under dual control.
- `runRetentionDryRun` previews; `requestRetentionExecution` then `applyRetentionExecution` act under dual control (`RetentionExecution`, `RetentionPolicyVersion`).

Artifacts store a reference string, not an uploaded file.
