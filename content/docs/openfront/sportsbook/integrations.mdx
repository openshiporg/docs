## Provider connections

`/dashboard/platform/providers` shows health, certification runs, trading replays, and operational state (`runAdapterCertification`, `runTradingReplay`, `setProviderOperationalState`, `mapProviderEntity`). `/dashboard/platform/integrations` (with `/[connectionId]`, `/inbox`, `/webhooks`) lists connections, inbound messages, and webhook endpoints with their recorded deliveries.

`ProviderConnection.certificationStatus`: `INTERNAL`, `PENDING`, `CERTIFIED`, `SUSPENDED`. Kinds: `sports`, `results`, `kyc`, `aml`, `geo`, `payments`, `messaging`, `analytics`, `tax`, `reporting`, `integrity`, `affiliates`, `pam`. Onboarding creates a `house-<kind>` connection per kind, with `INTERNAL` certification and config `house:<kind>`.

## Adapter contract

`features/sportsbook/adapters/contracts.ts` defines `ProviderAdapter`: `manifest`, `capabilities`, `validateConfig`, `execute` (with idempotency key), `query`, `verifyWebhook`, `normalize`, `health`, `reconcile`. The bundled `HouseAdapter` (`features/sportsbook/adapters/internal.ts`) implements it for every kind. The operator's own traders, results staff, and compliance staff are the source; it never claims third-party certification. A contracted provider replaces it by installing that provider's adapter. Payment adapters live in `features/integrations/payment` (`stripe`, `authorizenet`, `manual`).

## Inbound webhooks

`/api/webhooks/providers/[kind]` accepts the thirteen kinds, caps the body, and requires `x-openfront-timestamp` within five minutes, `x-openfront-tenant-id`, `x-openfront-connection-id`, and an `x-openfront-signature` HMAC under `PROVIDER_WEBHOOK_SECRET` (32+ characters; otherwise derived from `SESSION_SECRET`). The connection must be the tenant's active `house-<kind>` installation. It rate-limits, records replay protection in `WebhookReplay`, stores the message in `RawEnvelope` and `InboxMessage`, and normalizes it in the same transaction.

## Outbound webhooks

`requestWebhookEndpoint` and `applyWebhookEndpoint` (dual control) create `WebhookEndpoint` rows. Destinations must be HTTPS on port 443, public, and listed in `WEBHOOK_EGRESS_HOSTS`. Signed deliveries use HMAC-SHA256 with `OUTBOUND_WEBHOOK_SECRET` and the headers `openfront-delivery-id`, `openfront-event-type`, `openfront-event-version`, `openfront-timestamp`, `openfront-key-id`, and `openfront-signature`. `/dashboard/platform/integrations/webhooks` shows each `WebhookDelivery` with its `IntegrationDeliveryAttempt` history.

## Card processors

Customer card deposits go to the first `ACTIVE` `stripe` or `authorizenet` connection (by failover priority) whose secret variable is set. The wallet card form tokenizes for that processor: Stripe Elements with `NEXT_PUBLIC_STRIPE_KEY` (or `STRIPE_PUBLISHABLE_KEY`), or Authorize.net Accept.js. The server confirms a Stripe PaymentIntent with `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY` under the attempt's idempotency key, so a retry never charges twice.

An `ACTIVE` `manual` connection turns on cage cash deposits and manual bank payouts.

## No background work

There is no worker, outbox, or scheduled job. Work that used to wait in a queue now runs on a request:

- Inbound events are normalized on receipt.
- Approving a result grades and settles straight wagers.
- Payments the provider has not answered stay `UNKNOWN` with funds held until staff press **Check status** on the Payments page.
- Audit anchoring advances when `/api/ready` is read.

## Email

Nodemailer with `SMTP_HOST`, `SMTP_PORT` (default 587), `SMTP_USER`, `SMTP_PASSWORD`, `SMTP_FROM`, `SMTP_STORE_LINK` sends operator password reset and customer recovery emails.

## MCP and AI chat

`/api/mcp-transport/[transport]` forwards the caller's cookie to GraphQL; tools are `listModels`, `queryData`, `searchModels`, `getFieldsForType`, `lookupInputType`, `createData`, `updateData`, `deleteData`, `modelSpecificSearch`. Most sportsbook lists deny raw writes, so write tools are refused for them. `/api/completion` is the dashboard assistant via OpenRouter (`OPENROUTER_API_KEY`, `OPENROUTER_MODEL`, `OPENROUTER_MAX_TOKENS`).
