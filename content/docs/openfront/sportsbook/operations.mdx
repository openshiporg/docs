---
title: Recovery and incidents
description: Incident handling and recovering stuck payments.
icon: Siren
---

- **Unknown payments** (`/dashboard/platform/payments`): press **Check status** to ask the pinned provider again. It never re-sends the payment.
- **Settlement after approval**: if settling fails when a result is approved, settle from `/dashboard/platform/settlements`.
- **Incidents** (`/dashboard/platform/incidents`): `openOperationalIncident` and `setIncidentStatus` (open, mitigate, resolve). `Incident` rows feed `/api/metrics`.
