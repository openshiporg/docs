title: Audit

---

Needs `canReadAudit`. Routes: `/dashboard/platform/audit`, `/audit/[auditId]`.

Every custom mutation appends an `AuditEvent` linked by hash. The page shows events, runs a chain check, and lists `AuditAnchor` rows. The worker anchors the chain head to signed files in `AUDIT_ANCHOR_DIR` using `AUDIT_ANCHOR_SECRET`; without both, anchoring is skipped and `/api/ready` reports it. `npm run verify:audit` checks the chain from the command line.
