---
title: Responsible gaming
description: Player limits, cool-offs, and self-exclusion.
icon: HeartPulse
---

Route: `/dashboard/platform/responsible-gaming`. Needs `canManageCompliance`.

- `setResponsibleGamingLimit` sets a `PlayerLimit` (deposit, stake, loss, session).
- `selfExclude` records a `SelfExclusion`.
- Cool-offs and exclusions block wager acceptance.

Customers set their own limits and take breaks at `/account/limits` and `/account/responsible-play`.
