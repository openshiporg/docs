---
title: Settlement
description: Settle wagers, resettle, and correct cash-outs.
icon: WalletCards
---

Routes: `/dashboard/platform/settlements`, `/settlements/[wagerId]`.

- `settleWager` settles simple straight wagers. `settleCombinationWager` settles wagers with parts and routes full straight wagers. `fullStraightSettlementPlan` previews a plan.
- Settlements are versioned. A `Settlement` has `SettlementLine` rows for stake return, profit, tax, and rounding. Each posts a balanced `JournalTransaction`.
- **Resettle:** `requestResettlementApproval` then `resettleWager`, or `requestCombinationResettlement` then `resettleCombinationWager`.
- **Cash-out corrections:** `requestCashOutCorrection` then `correctCashOut`.

Settlement runs are `SettlementRun` rows, started by `settlement.requested` events that the worker drains.
