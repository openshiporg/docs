Operator pages are `app/dashboard/(admin)/platform/<route>/page.tsx`, backed by `features/platform/<slice>`. Most actions need operator two-factor assurance (valid 12 hours after a TOTP check) and the right role flag. Sensitive changes are requested by one operator and decided by another.

The Control room shows today's queues, exceptions, exposure, latest wagers, and shortcuts. Lists without a dedicated page appear on the generic `/dashboard/<list-key>` pages, mostly read-only.

## Dual control

A request writes an `ApprovalRequest` with the reviewed payload and a payload hash; it expires after 24 hours. A different operator with `canApproveSensitiveActions` decides it in `/dashboard/platform/approvals` (`decideSensitiveApproval`). The requester cannot approve their own request (`DUAL_CONTROL_REQUIRED`). The apply mutation checks the approval, hash, and expiry before changing anything.

Pairs: settings, roles, grants, revocations, operator admission, legal entities, jurisdiction activation, brands, currencies, locales, house rules, feature flags, promotions, webhook endpoints, trader overrides, correlation rules, tax rules, ledger adjustments, resettlements, cash-out corrections, dispute resolutions, retention executions, data exports.
