title: Risk and limits
description: Exposure, limit profiles and rules, and correlation rules.
icon: ShieldCheck
---

Needs `canManageRisk`. Route: `/dashboard/platform/risk`.

- **Exposure:** `ExposurePosition` and `WagerExposureAllocation` by market and selection.
- **Limits:** `LimitProfile` and `LimitRule`. `createRiskLimit` adds stake, payout, and liability limits.
- **Correlation:** `CorrelationGroup` and `CorrelationRule`, requested with `requestCorrelationRule` and applied with `applyCorrelationRule` under dual control.

Liability is capped by `SANDBOX_MAX_LIABILITY_MINOR` and the configured maximum stake and payout.
