Needs `canManageCompliance`. Routes: `/dashboard/platform/customers`, `/customers/[playerId]`.

The player page shows identity verification (`VerificationCase`, `VerificationCheck`), AML cases, `AccountRestriction` rows, `Consent`, `PlayerStatement`, sessions and devices (`CustomerSession`, `CustomerDevice`), closures, and notifications. `PlayerIdentity` payloads are encrypted with `PII_ENCRYPTION_KEY`. Customer credential and session lists are not exposed through GraphQL.

Customer sessions use the `openfront_customer` cookie; its digest is stored in `CustomerSession`.

Account closures (`/dashboard/platform/closures`) list requests and their blockers, and notifications (`/dashboard/platform/notifications`) list messages sent to customers and staff.
