Needs `canTrade`. Routes: `/dashboard/platform/catalog` (and `/events`), `/catalog/[eventId]`, `/catalog/reference`.

## Reference data

`/catalog/reference` creates and activates or deactivates sports, competitions, seasons, stages, venues, and competitors (`createCatalogSport`, `createCatalogCompetition`, `createCompetitionSeason`, `createCatalogStage`, `createCatalogVenue`, `createCatalogCompetitor`, `setCatalogReferenceStatus`).

## Events

`createCatalogEvent` creates an event with its first market. `Event.status`: `SCHEDULED`, `LIVE`, `ENDED`, `CANCELLED`, `SUSPENDED`. The event page shows participants (`attachEventParticipant`), schedule history (`rescheduleEvent` writes `EventScheduleChange`), venue, season, stage, and markets (`createEventMarket`).

Market definitions from onboarding: match result, moneyline, spread, total. A new market starts suspended until every selection has a price. Only published sports and events appear on the storefront.
