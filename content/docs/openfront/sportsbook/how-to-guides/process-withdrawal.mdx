---
title: Process a withdrawal
description: Pay or reject a customer withdrawal.
icon: Banknote
---

import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open the queue
At `/dashboard/platform/payments` see Withdrawal queue.
</Step>
<Step>
### Send the funds
Pay the customer outside the app.
</Step>
<Step>
### Mark paid
Enter the bank reference and press Mark paid. To refuse, press Reject; the hold is released.
</Step></Steps>
