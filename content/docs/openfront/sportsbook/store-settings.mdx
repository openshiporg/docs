---
title: Store settings
description: Storefront branding and typed policy settings.
icon: Settings2
---

Route: `/dashboard/platform/settings`. Edit the store name, logo icon, and color, and request typed setting changes with `requestSettingChange` and `applyApprovedSetting` (dual control). The storefront resolves one brand per host: the tenant named by `STOREFRONT_TENANT_SLUG`, the brand on the `PUBLIC_APP_URL` host, a brand whose domain matches the host, or the only operator-owned brand.
