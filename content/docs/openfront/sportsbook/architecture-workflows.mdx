## Schema by domain

Money is stored as integer minor units in text fields (for example `stakeMinor`, `amountMinor`) with a currency code. Every money movement posts a balanced `JournalTransaction`.

## Dual control

Many changes are split into a request and an apply step. The request writes an `ApprovalRequest` with the reviewed payload and a payload hash; it expires after 24 hours. A different operator with `canApproveSensitiveActions` decides it in the Approval inbox with `decideSensitiveApproval`. The requester cannot approve their own request (`DUAL_CONTROL_REQUIRED`). The apply mutation then checks the approval, the hash, and expiry before it changes anything. Request and apply pairs include settings, roles, role grants and revocations, operator admission, legal entities, jurisdiction activation, brands, currencies, brand locales, house rules, feature flags, promotions, webhook endpoints, trader overrides, correlation rules, tax rules, ledger adjustments, resettlements, cash-out corrections, dispute resolutions, retention executions, and data exports.

## Workflow: set up the operator

1. Onboarding creates the tenant, brand, locale, legal entity, jurisdiction, channel, currency, config, providers, and a demo catalog (see [Getting started](/docs/openfront/sportsbook/getting-started)).
2. Configuration and jurisdictions: request and apply legal entities, jurisdiction policy drafts and activation, brands, currencies, locales, house rules, and feature flags.
3. Settings: request and apply typed setting changes (`requestSettingChange`, `applyApprovedSetting`) and edit store branding.
4. Roles: create the independent approver, request role definitions, grants, revocations, and new operator admissions.

## Workflow: catalog and trading

1. Catalog reference: create sports, competitions, seasons, stages, venues, and competitors, and activate or deactivate them (`createCatalogSport`, `createCatalogCompetition`, `createCompetitionSeason`, `createCatalogStage`, `createCatalogVenue`, `createCatalogCompetitor`, `setCatalogReferenceStatus`).
2. Create an event with its first market (`createCatalogEvent`), add participants (`attachEventParticipant`), reschedule (`rescheduleEvent`, which writes `EventScheduleChange`), and add markets (`createEventMarket`).
3. New markets start suspended until every selection is priced. Set prices with `setMarketPrices` (or `ingestSandboxPrice` from the sandbox feed), then open with `setMarketStatus`, which checks the reviewed market version.
4. Move events through live, ended, and other states with `setEventTradingState`. Suspend markets with `suspendMarket`. Each change writes `TradingStatusChange`.
5. Request and apply trader overrides and correlation rules; create stake, payout, and liability limits with `createRiskLimit`.

## Workflow: customer wager (sandbox)

1. The customer registers, signs in, and completes verification. Verification calls the sandbox KYC and geo adapters and writes `PlayerIdentity`, `VerificationCase`, `VerificationCheck`, `GeoSession`, and `GeoCheck`.
2. The customer funds the wallet through the sandbox payments adapter (`PaymentIntent`, `PaymentAttempt`, `Hold`, journal postings).
3. The bet slip requests a quote (`/quote` or `/quote-combination`). The quote and its immutable terms are stored with an expiry.
4. Placement (`/wager` or `/wager-combination`) runs the acceptance gates: tenant and player active, KYC approved, location allowed, not self-excluded or restricted, within player limits, market open, price not stale (`MAX_PRICE_AGE_MS`), funds available, liability within the cap. The result is stored as an `AcceptanceDecision` with a rejection code such as `KYC_REQUIRED`, `GEO_REQUIRED`, `STALE_PRICE`, or `LIMIT_EXCEEDED`.
5. Accepted wagers reserve funds, post journals, and update exposure. Customers can request and accept cash-out offers from `/account/bets`.

## Workflow: result, grade, and settle

1. Enter a result for an event that has started (`ingestSandboxResult`) and send it for approval (`requestResultApproval`). A pending version can be withdrawn (`withdrawResultVersion`).
2. The independent approver decides the request. `approveResultVersion` (needs `canManageRisk`) then marks the version approved, supersedes the previous approved version, and emits `settlement.requested`.
3. Grade selections from the approved result under the current house rules (`gradeMarketSelection`). `gradeMarket` always refuses with `SELECTION_RESULT_REQUIRED`.
4. Settle: `settleWager` for simple straight wagers, `settleCombinationWager` for wagers with parts (it also routes full straight wagers). `fullStraightSettlementPlan` previews a plan. Settlements are versioned with lines for stake return, profit, tax, and rounding.
5. Correct with `requestResettlementApproval` and `resettleWager`, or `requestCombinationResettlement` and `resettleCombinationWager`. Cash-out corrections use `requestCashOutCorrection` and `correctCashOut`.

## Workflow: finance and reconciliation

- `sandboxPayment` posts an operator deposit or withdrawal for a player through the sandbox payments adapter. `openChargeback` opens a chargeback.
- `requestLedgerAdjustment` and `applyLedgerAdjustment` post manual adjustments under dual control.
- `runReconciliation` and `runSafeguardingReconciliation` compare the ledger with provider and safeguarded-funds figures.
- `requestTaxRule`, `applyTaxRule`, and `assessSettlementTax` manage tax rules and assess tax on settled wagers.

## Workflow: customers, compliance, and safer play

- Verification: `openVerificationCase`, `decideVerificationCase`. AML: `reviewAMLCase`, `recordComplianceScreening`. Privacy: `transitionPrivacyRequest`.
- Safer play: `setResponsibleGamingLimit` and `selfExclude` from the operator side; customers set limits and take breaks from their account.
- Support: `setSupportCaseStatus`, and dispute resolution with `requestDisputeResolution`, `withdrawDisputeProposal`, and `applyDisputeResolution`.
- Wagers: `openWagerInvestigation` opens an integrity case for a wager.

## Workflow: operations and governance

- Promotions: `createPromotionCampaign`, then `requestPromotionActivation` and `applyPromotionActivation`.
- Providers: `runAdapterCertification`, `runTradingReplay`, `setProviderOperationalState`, `mapProviderEntity`.
- Webhooks: `requestWebhookEndpoint`, `applyWebhookEndpoint`, `testWebhookEndpoint`.
- Recovery: `enqueueRecovery`, `replayQueueItem`, `scheduleOperationalJob`. Incidents: `openOperationalIncident`, `setIncidentStatus`.
- Reports: `generateComplianceReport`, `requestDataExport`, `applyApprovedDataExport`, `runRetentionDryRun`, `requestRetentionExecution`, `applyRetentionExecution`.
- Audit: every custom mutation appends to the hash-chained `AuditEvent` log. The worker anchors the chain head when anchoring is configured.

## Access control

<Callout>
Dashboard visibility is not the control. Every list and custom operation checks the operator's role, tenant, and two-factor assurance on the server.
</Callout>

- Most Sportsbook lists allow only `query` through GraphQL, gated by one role flag (for example `canTrade` for catalog and trading lists, `canManageCompliance` for players and limits) and filtered to the operator's tenant. Create, update, and delete are denied; changes go through custom mutations.
- Customer identity lists (`CustomerSession`, `CustomerCredential`, and similar) are omitted from GraphQL entirely.
- `Role` is readable by signed-in users in their tenant and cannot be changed through GraphQL CRUD; roles change through the request and apply mutations.
- Custom mutations call `requireTenant` with a capability, require MFA assurance, and re-read the acting user inside the transaction (`assertCurrentActor`), so a revoked role or removed factor takes effect immediately.
- In production, GraphQL error messages are masked except for upper-case refusal codes such as `DUAL_CONTROL_REQUIRED`.
