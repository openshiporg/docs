title: Support and disputes
description: Support cases and dispute resolution.
icon: LifeBuoy
---

Routes: `/dashboard/platform/support`, `/support/[caseId]`.

- Change a case status with `setSupportCaseStatus`. Events are in `SupportCaseEvent`.
- Disputes (`Dispute`, `DisputeEvent`): propose a resolution with `requestDisputeResolution`, withdraw it with `withdrawDisputeProposal`, apply it after approval with `applyDisputeResolution`.

Customers open cases and disputes at `/account/disputes`.
