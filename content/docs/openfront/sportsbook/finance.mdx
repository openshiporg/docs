title: Finance
description: Ledger journals, payments, adjustments, reconciliation, safeguarding, and tax.

Needs `canManageFinance`. Routes: `/dashboard/platform/finance`, `/finance/reconciliation`, `/finance/tax`.

Money is integer minor units stored as text (`stakeMinor`, `amountMinor`) with a currency code. Every movement posts a balanced `JournalTransaction` of `JournalEntry` rows against `LedgerAccount` rows; `BalanceProjection` tracks balances and `Hold` reserves funds.

- **Payments:** `PaymentIntent` and `PaymentAttempt`. `sandboxPayment` posts an operator deposit or withdrawal; `openChargeback` opens a chargeback.
- **Adjustments:** `requestLedgerAdjustment` and `applyLedgerAdjustment` under dual control.
- **Reconciliation:** `runReconciliation` and `runSafeguardingReconciliation` create `ReconciliationRun` and `ReconciliationItem` rows comparing ledger, provider, and safeguarded funds (`SafeguardingAccount`).
- **Tax:** `requestTaxRule`, `applyTaxRule`, and `assessSettlementTax` produce `TaxAssessment` rows on settled wagers.

Withdrawals needing operator action are on [Licences and payments](/docs/openfront/sportsbook/payment-providers).
