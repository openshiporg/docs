## Refills

`/dashboard/platform/refills` lists `RefillRequest` rows across prescriptions. Patients create them from their storefront account with `requestPrescriptionRefill`.

Statuses: `requested`, `under_review`, `prescriber_contacted`, `approved`, `fulfilled`, `rejected`, `cancelled`.

A refill is offered to the patient only when the prescription has refills left and the current authorized fill is completely dispensed. An open request for the same entitlement blocks a second one.

The patient's request reserves a refill and creates a requested `PrescriptionFill`. Staff with `canReviewPrescriptions` resolve it with `resolvePrescriptionRefill(refillRequestId, decision, reason, attested, expectedFillVersion)`. The decision is `approved` or `rejected`. Rejecting releases the reserved refill. Approving makes the fill ready, and the pharmacist dispenses it from the prescription page, which marks the request `fulfilled`. An expired reservation cannot be approved; release it instead. See [Handle a refill request](/docs/openfront/pharmacy/how-to-guides/handle-refill-request).

## Online requests

`/dashboard/platform/requests` lists `PrescriptionRequest` rows sent from `/prescriptions` on the storefront with `submitPharmacyPrescriptionRequest`.

| Kind | What the patient sends |
|---|---|
| `registration` | Legal name, date of birth, phone, email, allergies, insurance |
| `transfer` | Pharmacy to transfer from, its phone, and medications |
| `new_prescription` | Prescriber name and phone, and medications |

Statuses: `requested`, `in_review`, `completed`, `declined`. Staff choose one with `resolvePharmacyPrescriptionRequest` and may add a resolution note. A registration request can be turned into a patient through the Create patient flow, which carries over the reported details.

## Permissions

Both queues need `canManagePrescriptions`. Resolving a refill also needs the pharmacist attestation.
