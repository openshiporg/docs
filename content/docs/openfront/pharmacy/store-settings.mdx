`/dashboard/platform/store-settings` edits the `Organization` branding that the storefront shows.

| Setting | Where it appears |
|---|---|
| Store name (`operatorDisplayName`) | Header, labels, and receipts |
| Tagline (`operatorTagline`) | Storefront home |
| Logo icon and color | Pick an icon (or paste SVG code) and a color. A live preview shows the storefront header |
| Support email and phone | Storefront footer and order pages |
| Privacy notice | `/privacy` and the checkout consent |
| Location hours | Store and pharmacy-counter hours per location, shown on the storefront home and `/prescriptions` |

Save with the Save button; the page reports success or the validation error.

Cash tax rate and fill policy are not here. They are on [Payments](/docs/openfront/pharmacy/payment-providers) and [Credential review](/docs/openfront/pharmacy/staff-credentials) because they need an attested reason.
