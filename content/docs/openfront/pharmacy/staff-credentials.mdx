## Staff

`/dashboard/platform/staff` lists `OrganizationMembership` rows. Add a member by choosing a user, a role, and either the whole organization or one location.

Membership role: `owner`, `administrator`, `pharmacist`, `technician`, `cashier`, `delivery`, `read_only`. Status: `invited`, `active`, `suspended`, `revoked`. Only owners can add or change owners. Needs `canManageUsers`.

Dashboard permissions come from the user's `Role`, edited at `/dashboard/roles`. See [Roles and permissions](/docs/openfront/pharmacy/roles-permissions).

## Credential review

`/dashboard/platform/credentials` shows the dispensing setup checklist and the review forms:

| Review | Command |
|---|---|
| Staff pharmacist credential | `recordPharmacyStaffCredentialReview` |
| Prescriber credential | `recordPharmacyPrescriberCredentialReview` |
| Drug reference | `recordPharmacyDrugReferenceReview` |
| Cash and tax policy | `recordPharmacyCashPolicy` |
| Fill policy | `recordPharmacyFillPolicy` |

Staff and prescriber reviews take the credential type, jurisdiction, identifier, expiry (in the future), and a verification reference. The app stores the evidence you enter. Intake requires a current review on the pharmacist's membership.

## Locations

`/dashboard/platform/locations` and `/locations/[id]`. Fields: `name`, `code`, `status` (`active`, `temporarily_closed`, `archived`), address, `timezone`, `phone`, and the switches `onlineOrderingEnabled`, `pickupEnabled`, `deliveryEnabled`. Creating a location needs an organization-wide membership with `canManageLocations`.

Products, lots, prescriptions, orders, and recalls belong to a location. Onboarding turns delivery off.
