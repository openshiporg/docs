---
title: Handle a refill request
description: Link a patient account, review a refill request, and dispense it.
icon: RefreshCw
---

import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

<Steps>
<Step>
### Link the patient to their account
A patient can only request refills once their profile is linked to their storefront user. On the patient page, use the account link (`linkPharmacyPatientAccount`).
</Step>
<Step>
### The patient requests
From `/account` the patient chooses a dispensed prescription with refills left and adds a note.
</Step>
<Step>
### Review the queue
Open `/dashboard/platform/refills`. The request is `requested`.
</Step>
<Step>
### Decide
Open the prescription, run `resolvePrescriptionRefill` with `approved` or `rejected`, a reason, and attestation. Rejecting releases the reserved refill.
</Step>
<Step>
### Dispense
Approved refills are dispensed through the prescription's dispense form. The request becomes `fulfilled` and the refills remaining count drops.
</Step>
</Steps>

If the reservation window passes, release it and ask the patient to request again.
