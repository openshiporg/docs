import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

Dispensing is blocked until six records exist. The checklist is on `/dashboard/platform/credentials`.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Review a pharmacist credential
Open Staff (`/dashboard/platform/staff`), pick the pharmacist's membership, and record the credential type, jurisdiction, identifier, an expiry in the future, and your verification reference. Command: `recordPharmacyStaffCredentialReview`.
</Step>
<Step>
### Add and review a prescriber
Create the prescriber with an NPI at `/dashboard/platform/prescribers`, then record the credential review (`recordPharmacyPrescriberCredentialReview`).
</Step>
<Step>
### Review the drug reference
Open the drug at `/dashboard/platform/drug-references`, enter the source name, version, reference, marketing status `active`, and schedule `none`. Command: `recordPharmacyDrugReferenceReview`.
</Step>
<Step>
### Link a product to the drug
Create the product with `prescriptionRequirement` set to `prescription_required` and the drug reference selected, then publish it.
</Step>
<Step>
### Record the cash and tax policy
On `/dashboard/platform/payments`, enter the sales-tax rate and a policy reference. Command: `recordPharmacyCashPolicy`.
</Step>
<Step>
### Record the fill policy
On the credentials page, set version, reference, partial fills allowed, reservation minutes, and release reasons. Command: `recordPharmacyFillPolicy`.
</Step>
</Steps>

Then receive stock ([Receive an inventory lot](/docs/openfront/pharmacy/how-to-guides/receive-inventory-lot)) and [fill a prescription](/docs/openfront/pharmacy/how-to-guides/fill-prescription).
