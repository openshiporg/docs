---
title: Run a recall
description: Quarantine recalled lots, contact exposed patients, dispose of stock, and close the recall.
icon: TriangleAlert
---

import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open the recall
`/dashboard/platform/recalls/open`. Enter the recall number, classification, reason, source, and source reference. Select every affected lot.
</Step>
<Step>
### Quarantine
On the recall page press Quarantine. Available stock moves to quarantined.
</Step>
<Step>
### Work the exposure list
Each patient who received the lot is listed. Call or message them, then record contact method, outcome, and status (`contacted`, `returned`, `unable_to_reach`, `resolved`).
</Step>
<Step>
### Dispose of stock
For each lot, record the full quarantined quantity with a disposition reference.
</Step>
<Step>
### Close
Close the recall. If the page shows "Exposure reconciliation incomplete", finish the open exposures or lot dispositions first.
</Step>
</Steps>

Class I recalls are counted separately on the [Overview](/docs/openfront/pharmacy/overview).
