## Transfer in

<Steps>
<Step>
### Receive the request
A patient sends a `transfer` request from `/prescriptions` on the storefront with the other pharmacy and medications. It appears at `/dashboard/platform/requests`.
</Step>
<Step>
### Register the patient
If they are new, create the patient from the request.
</Step>
<Step>
### Take in the prescription
Enter it at `/dashboard/platform/prescriptions/intake` with source `transfer`.
</Step>
<Step>
### Approve the transfer
On the prescription page, review the inbound transfer and press Approve (`approvePrescriptionTransfer`). It must be `consent_recorded` with accepted consent.
</Step>
<Step>
### Resolve the request
Mark the online request `completed`.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Transfer out

On the prescription page open the transfer-out form. Enter the receiving pharmacy name and phone and record the patient's consent with the policy version. `requestPrescriptionTransfer` creates an `outbound` transfer. Statuses: `requested`, `consent_recorded`, `under_review`, `accepted`, `completed`, `rejected`, `cancelled`.
