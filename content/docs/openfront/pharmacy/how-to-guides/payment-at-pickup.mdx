---
title: Take payment at pickup
description: Prepare a pay-at-pickup order, hand it off, and refund if needed.
icon: HandCoins
---

import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

<Steps>
<Step>
### Install pay at pickup
On `/dashboard/platform/payments` confirm the "Pay at pickup" provider is installed.
</Step>
<Step>
### The customer orders
At checkout the customer chooses pickup and Pay at pickup. The order commits with a pending payment.
</Step>
<Step>
### Prepare
Open `/dashboard/platform/orders/[id]` and move the fulfillment to `preparing`, then `ready`.
</Step>
<Step>
### Hand off
Collect payment at the counter. The assigned staff member records `handed_off`. The order becomes `completed`.
</Step>
<Step>
### Refund
For a returned order, run the cash refund with the cash returned amount, a reason, and attestation. It must equal the captured amount.
</Step>
</Steps>

For walk-in OTC sales use [Point of sale](/docs/openfront/pharmacy/pos).
