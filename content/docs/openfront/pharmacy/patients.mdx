Pages: `/dashboard/platform/patients`, `/dashboard/platform/patients/create`, and `/dashboard/platform/patients/[id]`. Data comes from `pharmacyPatientDirectory` and `pharmacyPatientSafeView`.

## Create a patient

Open Create patient and enter the patient number, first and last name, date of birth, email, phone, preferred contact method (`phone`, `sms`, `email`, `portal`, or `none`), and address. Record the identity evidence: the method and a reference. Add a reason and attest. The command is `createPharmacyPatient`. A new patient gets identity status `confirmed` when you attest the match; the other states are `unverified` and `rejected`.

## Patient detail

- **Contact:** `updatePharmacyPatientContact` edits email, phone, address, and preferred contact method.
- **Allergies:** `recordPharmacyPatientAllergy` takes the allergen, reaction, and severity.
- **Medications:** `recordPharmacyPatientMedication` takes the name, directions, and status.
- **None known:** `recordPharmacyPatientNoneKnown` records an explicit "no known allergies" or "no current medications" entry. Clinical review needs either entries or this record.
- **Consents:** privacy, communications, transfer, and delivery consents collected at checkout and transfer.
- **Account link:** `linkPharmacyPatientAccount` connects the patient to a storefront `User` so they can request refills. `pharmacyPatientAccountCandidates` lists candidates.

Statuses: `active`, `inactive`, `archived`.

## Private data

Identity and clinical details need `canViewPatientPrivateData`. Without it the page shows the safe projection only.
