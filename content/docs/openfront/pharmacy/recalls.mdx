Pages: `/dashboard/platform/recalls` (queue from `pharmacyRecallQueue`), `/recalls/open`, and `/recalls/[id]`.

## Recall fields

`recallNumber`, `classification` (`class_i`, `class_ii`, `class_iii`, `market_withdrawal`, `unverified`), `status` (`draft`, `active`, `quarantined`, `resolved`, `closed`, `cancelled`), `reason`, `source`, `sourceValidationReference`, `initiatedAt`, `closedAt`, and the affected `lots`.

## Steps

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open the recall
Choose the location, recall number, classification, reason, source, and source reference, then select the affected lots. `openPharmacyRecall` requires every lot to belong to the pharmacy. Exposure rows are built from `dispense` and `sale` movements on those lots.
</Step>
<Step>
### Quarantine
`quarantineRecallLots` moves the remaining available stock to `quantityQuarantined`, writes `quarantine` movements, and sets the recall to `quarantined`.
</Step>
<Step>
### Reach exposed patients
Each `RecallExposure` starts as `outreach_required`. Record the contact method, outcome, and notes with `recordRecallExposureAction`, and set the status to `contacted`, `returned`, `unable_to_reach`, or `resolved`.
</Step>
<Step>
### Dispose of quarantined stock
`disposeRecallLot` takes the lot, the full quarantined quantity, and a disposition reference.
</Step>
<Step>
### Close
`closePharmacyRecall` succeeds once every lot's quarantined quantity has a disposition, every sale or dispense movement has an exposure, and every exposure is `returned`, `resolved`, or `unable_to_reach`.
</Step>
</Steps>

Needs `canManageRecalls`. See [Run a recall](/docs/openfront/pharmacy/how-to-guides/run-recall).
