The built-in storefront lives under `app/(storefront)/(main)/` and is served at `/`. It resolves the pharmacy from the request host (`storefrontKey`, `storefrontHost`, or `slug`). Its route table is on [External storefronts](/docs/openfront/pharmacy/external-storefront).

## Customer journeys

### Shop OTC products
1. Pick a location on `/`. The page shows its hours and counter services.
2. Browse `/products` with the `?category=` track and search. `/products/[handle]` shows related products from the same category.
3. Add to cart. The location locks once the cart has items.

### Check out
`/checkout` collects contact details, pickup or delivery (with an address), payment choice, and privacy consent. Pay at pickup is hidden for delivery. The card step appears when Stripe is installed and `NEXT_PUBLIC_STRIPE_KEY` is set. A successful guest order returns a `guestOrderToken`.

### Track an order
`/order-status` looks up by order number and email, or opens with `orderId` and the guest `token`. Signed-in customers see orders at `/account` and `/account/orders/details/[id]`.

### Refill, transfer, or register
`/prescriptions` explains the options. Refills are requested from `/account` for linked patients. Transfer and new-prescription requests need sign-in. Registration requests collect name, date of birth, phone, email, allergies, and insurance. History is on the account page.

### Account
`/account` has sign in, register (`registerPharmacyCustomer`), and reset at `/account/reset`. `/account/profile` edits name, email, and password through `updateActiveUser`, which checks the current password.

## What the catalog shows

Only published products with `prescriptionRequirement` `none` and `onlineAvailable` on. If a location has online ordering off, the storefront shows a call-to-order notice.

## Staff view

Staff process what customers send in the [Orders](/docs/openfront/pharmacy/orders), [Refills and requests](/docs/openfront/pharmacy/refills-requests), and [Patients](/docs/openfront/pharmacy/patients) pages. Set branding in [Store settings](/docs/openfront/pharmacy/store-settings).
