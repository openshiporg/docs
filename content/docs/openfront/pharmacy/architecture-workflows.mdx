## Models

Most clinical, inventory, and money lists set `create`, `update`, and `delete` to deny in the generic API. Their records change only through the named commands below. The generic create and edit drawers are used only for Product, DrugReference, Supplier, Prescriber, PharmacyLocation, PaymentProvider, OrganizationMembership, and Organization.

| Area | Lists |
|---|---|
| Tenant and access | `Organization`, `PharmacyLocation`, `OrganizationMembership`, `User`, `Role`, `MachineCredential` |
| Catalog and inventory | `DrugReference`, `DrugReferenceReview`, `Product`, `Supplier`, `InventoryLot`, `InventoryMovement`, `TemperatureExcursion` |
| Patients | `PatientProfile`, `PatientAllergy`, `PatientMedication`, `ConsentRecord`, `Prescriber` |
| Prescriptions | `Prescription`, `PrescriptionItem`, `PrescriptionReview`, `ClinicalSafetyReview`, `PrescriptionFill`, `PrescriptionFillEvent`, `DispensingLabel`, `RefillRequest`, `PrescriptionTransfer`, `PrescriptionRequest` |
| Orders and payments | `Cart`, `CartItem`, `Order`, `OrderLineItem`, `Fulfillment`, `PaymentProvider`, `PaymentSession`, `Payment`, `PaymentWebhookEvent` |
| Recalls, integrations, evidence | `Recall`, `RecallExposure`, `IntegrationProvider`, `ExternalIntegrationWorkflow`, `IntegrationWebhookEvent`, `HumanApproval`, `AuditEvent`, `OutboxEvent` |

`Product.prescriptionRequirement` is one of `none`, `prescription_required`, or `pharmacist_review_required`. Only `none` products can enter a public cart or appear in the public catalog.

## Prescription lifecycle

Statuses: `draft` → `pending_review` → `needs_information` or `approved` → `ready_to_dispense` → `partially_dispensed` → `dispensed` → `fill_closed`. A prescription can also end as `rejected` or `cancelled`.

1. **Intake.** `intakePharmacyPrescription` records the patient, prescriber, location, source, dates, and items. Every product must have an active drug reference review that marks it active and non-controlled.
2. **Clinical safety review.** `recordPrescriptionClinicalSafetyReview` records seven checks: allergies, medication history, interactions, duplicate therapy, contraindications, dose, and counseling plan. It also records an outcome. `holdPrescriptionForClinicalReReview` sends a stale review back for another look.
3. **Pharmacist review.** `recordPrescriptionReview` records the decision.
4. **Authorize.** `authorizePrescriptionDispensing` moves an approved prescription to `ready_to_dispense`.
5. **Fill.** `reservePharmacyPrescriptionFill` and `releasePharmacyPrescriptionFill` manage per-fill entitlement under the organization's fill policy (`recordPharmacyFillPolicy`).
6. **Dispense.** `recordPrescriptionDispensing` checks the lot (location, product, expiry, quantity, quarantine, recall, and cold-chain state). It then writes the movement, a `DispensingLabel`, an order, and a cash payment. Dispensing needs a recorded cash and tax policy. Cash is the only tender on this path.

Refills: a patient calls `requestPrescriptionRefill` from the account page, and staff call `resolvePrescriptionRefill`. Transfers: `requestPrescriptionTransfer` records the request and consent, and `approvePrescriptionTransfer` approves it. Online requests (`registration`, `transfer`, `new_prescription`) come in through `submitPharmacyPrescriptionRequest`. Staff resolve them with `resolvePharmacyPrescriptionRequest`.

<Callout type="warn">
These checks do not make a prescription valid or dispensing lawful. The pharmacist is still responsible for clinical and legal review, identity, prescriber authority, substitution, counseling, and the physical act of dispensing.
</Callout>

## Inventory, recall, and cold chain

- `receivePharmacyInventoryLot` creates a lot with a `receive` movement. It rejects cold-chain lots ("Cold-chain receiving is not supported yet").
- `adjustPharmacyInventoryLot` sets a counted quantity and writes an `adjust` movement.
- A recall goes through `openPharmacyRecall` (selected lots), `quarantineRecallLots`, `recordRecallExposureAction` (patient outreach), `disposeRecallLot`, and `closePharmacyRecall`. Exposure rows are built from `dispense` and `sale` movements on the recalled lots.
- `recordTemperatureExcursion` exists in `coldChainWorkflows.ts`, but it is not registered in the GraphQL schema. No page records or resolves excursions.

## Commerce and payments

- Carts: `createPharmacyCart`, `addPharmacyCartItem`, `updatePharmacyCartItem`, `removePharmacyCartItem`. A guest cart has an opaque token, and only its hash is stored. Cart tax is always 0 in the current cart hook.
- Checkout: `initiatePharmacyPaymentSession`, then `submitPharmacyOrder`. Card payments must be `captured` or `succeeded` with the provider before the order commits. Pay-at-pickup orders commit with a pending payment that is collected at handoff. A successful guest order returns a `guestOrderToken`.
- Fulfillment: `transitionOrderFulfillment` moves through `preparing`, `ready`, `out_for_delivery` (delivery only), and `handed_off`. Only the assigned staff member can record the handoff. Cancelling goes through a refund, not a transition.
- Counter sales: `submitPharmacyPosCashOrder` records an OTC cash sale using the organization's cash tax rate. `pharmacyPosCashReceipt` reads the receipt back.
- Money: `refundPharmacyPayment`, `refundPharmacyCashPayment`, `reconcilePharmacyPaymentSession` (for sessions left `settled_unordered` or `compensation_pending`), and `handlePaymentProviderWebhook`.
