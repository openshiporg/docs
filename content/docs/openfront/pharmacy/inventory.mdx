Stock is tracked by `InventoryLot`. Lots change only through named commands, and every change writes an `InventoryMovement`.

## Pages

- `/dashboard/platform/inventory`: lot queue (`pharmacyInventoryQueue`) with status tabs
- `/dashboard/platform/inventory/receive`: receive a lot
- `/dashboard/platform/inventory/[id]`: lot task page with movements, recalls, and the adjustment form

## Lot fields

`lotNumber`, `serialNumber`, `expirationDate`, `receivedAt`, `quantityReceived`, `quantityAvailable`, `quantityQuarantined`, `storageBin`, `status`, plus `product`, `location`, and `supplier`.

Status: `available`, `quarantined`, `expired`, `recalled`, `depleted`.

## Receive a lot

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open Receive
Choose the product (`pharmacyInventoryReceiptProductWorklist`), location, and optional supplier.
</Step>
<Step>
### Enter the lot
Lot number, optional serial number, a future expiration date, and quantity.
</Step>
<Step>
### Confirm
Add a reason and attest. `receivePharmacyInventoryLot` creates the lot and a `receive` movement. The form is for room-temperature lots, so cold-chain products are refused.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Adjust a count

On the lot page enter the counted quantity, a reason, and attest. `adjustPharmacyInventoryLot` sets the quantity and writes an `adjust` movement. The new available quantity cannot be lower than the quarantined quantity.

## Movement types

`receive`, `reserve`, `dispense`, `sale`, `return`, `quarantine`, `release`, `recall`, `expire`, `destroy`, `adjust`. Each row records the quantity change, reason, actor, and the patient, prescription, or order it relates to.

## Rules

- A lot with stock cannot be deleted.
- Dispensing and POS sales only draw from `available`, unexpired lots at the same location and product.
- Expired or expiring within 60 days, quarantined, and recalled counts appear on the [Overview](/docs/openfront/pharmacy/overview).

Needs `canManageInventory`. Related: [Receive an inventory lot](/docs/openfront/pharmacy/how-to-guides/receive-inventory-lot) and [Recalls](/docs/openfront/pharmacy/recalls).
