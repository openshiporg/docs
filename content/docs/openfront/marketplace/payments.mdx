---
title: Payments
description: Stripe and PayPal through the store's own providers.
icon: CreditCard
---

Stores provide payment. Marketplace:

1. Reads the store's payment providers and publishable keys (`GetStore`, `ListPaymentProviders`).
2. Starts a payment session on the store (`initiatePaymentSession`).
3. Renders Stripe card elements or PayPal buttons in the browser with the store's publishable key.
4. Calls `completeCart` so the store creates the order.

Card data goes from the browser to Stripe or PayPal. Marketplace takes no fee.

Restaurant stores use `MarketplaceRestaurantPaymentMethods`, `MarketplaceInitiateRestaurantPayment` and `MarketplaceCompleteRestaurantCart`.

Alternatively `getCheckoutLink` sends the buyer to the store's own checkout.
