| Route | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `/` | Chat screen with thread tabs, store editor, cart dropdown, AI settings and MCP UI cards |
| `/ethos` | Static page about the open marketplace idea |

There are no product, cart, checkout or account pages. All shopping happens in cards.

## First visit

The setup dialog asks for:

1. Marketplace name (default "Common Goods") and a theme: `clay`, `forest` or `midnight`.
2. AI mode: Global or Local.
3. A risk confirmation before chat is enabled.

## Buying

1. Ask for a product or a meal.
2. Open a discovery card, pick a variant, add to cart.
3. Review the cart card, change quantities, enter an address and shipping.
4. Pay in the chat, or follow the store's checkout link.

See [Cart and checkout](/docs/openfront/marketplace/cart-checkout) and [Restaurant ordering](/docs/openfront/marketplace/restaurant-ordering).

## Card URIs

`ui://marketplace/discover-products`, `ui://marketplace/product/...`, `ui://marketplace/cart/...`, `ui://marketplace/login`, `ui://marketplace/email-conflict`.

## Button safety

The browser checks every card action against the card it came from (`lib/mcp-action-policy.ts`). A cart card for one store and cart can only call cart tools for that store and cart.
