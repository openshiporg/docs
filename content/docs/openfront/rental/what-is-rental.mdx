Openfront Rental is for people who manage short-term accommodation.

| Surface | Who | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Guest storefront | Guests | `/`, `/search`, `/stays/[slug]`, `/checkout/[quoteToken]`, `/trips/[token]` |
| Host dashboard | Owners, admins, managers, operations, finance | `/dashboard/platform` |
| GraphQL API | Both | `/api/graphql` |

## Concepts

| Concept | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Host organization | The tenant. Host-scoped records are visible through an active `HostMembership` |
| Property and unit | The building and the bookable `AccommodationUnit` inside it |
| Listing | The public page for a unit, with `bookingMode` `instant` or `request` |
| Rate plan | Nightly rate, stay limits, notice, window, and a cancellation policy |
| Quote | A `StayQuote` snapshot of dates, rates, fees, taxes, deposit, and guests |
| Reservation | A stay created from a quote |
| Trip link | A 48-character private token for the guest's trip, agreement, and messages |

## Life of a stay

1. The guest searches and asks for a quote (`quoteStay`).
2. They reserve (`createStayReservation`) and sign the agreement (`signStayAgreement`).
3. Payment is taken by card on the trip page or recorded by the host.
4. The host confirms, checks in, and completes the stay (`transitionStayReservation`).
5. Tasks are generated for turnover, deposits are released, owner statements are produced.
6. The guest leaves a review (`submitStayReview`).

See [Reservations](/docs/openfront/rental/reservations) and [Roles and permissions](/docs/openfront/rental/roles-permissions).
