## Memberships

`HostMembership` links a user to a host with a role (`owner`, `admin`, `manager`, `operations`, `finance`) and a status (`invited`, `active`, `suspended`). Users whose role has `canManagePeople` or `canManageRoles` act as platform administrators. Administrators cannot grant the owner role or a higher role, and cannot modify an owner membership.

## Flags

Read and manage pairs exist for properties, listings, calendar, pricing, reservations, guests, messages, payments, payouts, reports, channels, and reviews (for example `canReadReservations`, `canManageReservations`).

Other flags: `canAccessDashboard`, `canManageSettings`, `canManageOnboarding`, `canManagePeople`, `canManageRoles`, `canSeeOtherPeople`, `canEditOtherPeople`.

| Job | Suggested flags |
| --- | --- |
| Operations | Properties, calendar, reservations, tasks |
| Finance | Payments, payouts, reports |
| Manager | Everything except people and roles |

The sidebar lists every entry; each page and command checks the user's own permissions and membership.
