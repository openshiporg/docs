## Reports

`/dashboard/platform/reports` needs `canReadReports`. `hostOperationsReport` shows occupancy, revenue, owner net, task health, and channel evidence. `exportHostArchive` exports the host archive.

## Store settings

`/dashboard/platform/store-settings` needs `canManageSettings`. It edits `MarketplaceSettings`: name, tagline, contact details, policy links, currency, locale, timezone, logo, accent color, and whether test bookings are allowed.
