---
title: Administration
description: Practice hub, needs-attention list and onboarding.
icon: Settings
---

`/platform/administration` shows what needs attention across the practice. The first operator comes from `/dashboard/init`. Onboarding runs once per database and seeds the practice, default roles, a manual cash provider (`pp_manual_cash`) and a Stripe provider (`pp_stripe`).

Other staff join by invitation at `/join`. Requires `canManageOnboarding` or `canManagePractice`.
