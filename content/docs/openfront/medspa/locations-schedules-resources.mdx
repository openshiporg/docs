title: Locations, schedules and resources
description: Addresses, hours, provider schedules, rooms and devices.
icon: MapPin
---

- `/platform/locations`: `upsertLocation` with address, timezone and opening hours.
- `/platform/schedules`: `upsertProviderSchedule` for weekly hours, one-off hours and time off; `setProviderScheduleAvailability`.
- `/platform/resources`: `upsertResource`, `setResourceAvailability`, `transitionDeviceReadiness`.

Availability uses the practice timezone. `ResourceScheduleBlock` blocks are read by readiness checks.
