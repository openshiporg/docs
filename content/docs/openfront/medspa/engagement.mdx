`/platform/engagement` (also `/waitlist`).

- **Waitlist** (`medspaWaitlistBoard`): add with `joinWaitlist`, then `transitionWaitlistEntry` to `notified`, `converted` or `removed`.
- **Templates:** `MessageTemplate` has direct CRUD for `canManageCommunications`.
- **Log:** `MessageLog` (`medspaMessageLog`) records messages.

Email is sent for password reset, appointment request receipts and gift cards when SMTP is set.
