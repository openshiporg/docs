`/platform/payments` lists settled `Payment` records; `/platform/payments/[id]` shows one with its refunds and tips.

## Actions

- **Refund a manual deposit session:** `refundPayment`.
- **Distribute a tip:** `distributeTip` to providers.

Amounts are integer cents. Actions take an idempotency key.

Requires `canManageFinance`.
