---
title: Inventory
description: Products, vendors, purchase orders, receiving, lots and movements.
icon: Boxes
---

`/platform/inventory` and `/platform/inventory/purchase-orders/[id]`. Requires `canManageInventory`.

- **Products:** `upsertProduct`.
- **Vendors:** direct CRUD.
- **Purchase orders:** `draftPurchaseOrder`, `transitionPurchaseOrder` (`draft`, `submitted`, `partially_received`, `received`, `cancelled`), `receivePurchaseOrder` creates `GoodsReceipt` and `InventoryLot` records.
- **Lots:** `dispositionInventoryLot`, `returnRetailLot`.
- **Adjustments:** `adjustMedSpaInventory`.

`StockMovement` is lot-level and `InventoryMovement` is product-level.
