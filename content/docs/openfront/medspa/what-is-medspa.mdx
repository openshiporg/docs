Openfront Medspa runs a medical-aesthetics practice. Clients book on the storefront. Staff confirm, review intake, document consultation, consent and treatment, check clients out, and track stock, memberships and follow-up.

## Who uses it

| Role | Surface | Work |
|---|---|---|
| Client | Storefront | Browse treatments and providers, book, complete intake, reply to aftercare, review |
| Front desk | Dashboard | Appointments, arrivals, clients, waitlist, checkout |
| Provider | Dashboard | Consultation, plan, consent, treatment, notes |
| Practice owner | Dashboard | Staff, roles, catalog, locations, reports, integrations, privacy |

## Core ideas

- **Practice tenancy.** 98 lists, each scoped to a `Practice`.
- **Named workflows.** Nearly every list is written only by a named mutation, so records carry idempotency keys and revisions.
- **Gated treatment.** Clinical services need consultation, plan, order, consent and reviewed intake before a session can run.
- **Cash checkout.** Staff record the payment in the checkout workspace.
