`/platform/catalog` and `/platform/catalog/[id]`. `Service`, `ServiceCategory`, `ServiceVariant` and `MedSpaImage` allow direct CRUD with `canManagePractice`.

## Service fields

Duration, price, deposit, cleanup buffer, online booking flag, required resource, and gates: `requiresConsultation`, `requiresIntakeReview`, `staffAuthorizationRequired`, `requiresLotTraceability`.

Checkout and clinical checks use an immutable snapshot of these gates taken at booking.
