Routes live in `app/(storefront)/(main)/**`.

| Route | Use |
|---|---|
| `/` | Home with featured services and providers |
| `/services`, `/services/[id]` | Treatments |
| `/providers`, `/team`, `/team/[id]` | Providers |
| `/book` | Treatment, provider and slot, then `bookAppointment` |
| `/booking/[id]` | Confirmation; guests view or cancel with a link token |
| `/account/sign-in`, `/account/sign-up` | `registerCustomer` |
| `/account` | Visits, aftercare, reviews (`clientPortal`) |
| `/account/intake/[appointmentId]` | Intake forms |
| `/reviews`, `/gallery`, `/contact`, `/gift-cards` | Reviews, gallery, hours, gift cards by contact |

The storefront serves the practice pinned by `MEDSPA_PRACTICE_SLUG`, or the single published practice. Gift cards are issued by staff.

When no slot fits, `requestWaitlistSpot`. Cancel with `cancelGuestAppointment` (guests) or from the account.
