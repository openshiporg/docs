---
title: Follow-ups
description: Aftercare tasks and client responses.
icon: PhoneCall
---

`/platform/follow-ups` lists `FollowUpTask` records.

1. `scheduleFollowUp` after a visit.
2. Staff append actions.
3. The client replies from their account with `submitFollowUpResponse`.

Tasks change only when an operator acts on them; there is no scheduler.
