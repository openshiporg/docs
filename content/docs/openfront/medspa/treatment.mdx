Open `/platform/appointments/[id]/treatment`. Requires `canManageClinical`.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Consultation
`recordConsultation`: candidacy `candidate`, `deferred`, `declined` or `referred`.
</Step>
<Step>
### Contraindications
`recordContraindicationFinding`, then `decideContraindication` for the clinician's disposition.
</Step>
<Step>
### Plan
`approveTreatmentPlan` creates the `TreatmentPlan` and items. States: `draft`, `active`, `paused`, `completed`, `cancelled`.
</Step>
<Step>
### Order
`issueClinicalOrder` authorizes a performer. `transitionClinicalOrder` moves it through `active`, `partially_fulfilled`, `fulfilled`, `held`, `revoked`, `expired` or `entered_in_error`.
</Step>
<Step>
### Consent
`signTreatmentConsent` against a published template; `withdrawTreatmentConsent` withdraws.
</Step>
<Step>
### Session
`prepareTreatmentSession` then `startTreatmentSession`: `planned`, `ready`, `in_progress`.
</Step>
<Step>
### Administration
`recordTreatmentAdministration` records what was given and, for lot-traced products, the `AdministrationLot` and `StockMovement`.
</Step>
<Step>
### Note
`appendSignedClinicalNote` adds an append-only, supersedable `ClientNote`.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Readiness gate

Before a session runs, `clinicalExecutionReadiness` checks that: the exact approved plan, item and consultation still authorize the encounter; a current order and matching consent exist; current reviewed intake covers every required template version; no contraindication review blocks; the session is inside the appointment window; and no required resource has a maintenance or unavailable block. The check confirms records exist and match. Clinical appropriateness remains the clinician's judgment.

Intake evidence must be newer than `MEDSPA_INTAKE_MAX_AGE_DAYS`. If a template changes after an order, treatment is refused with `Required intake template changed after the clinical order was issued`.
