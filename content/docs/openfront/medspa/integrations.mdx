`/platform/integrations` shows payment methods, email status, event log and webhooks. `PaymentProvider` has direct CRUD for `canManageIntegrations`.

## Payment adapters

`features/integrations/payment/index.ts` loads a code-owned adapter by the provider's `adapter` field.

| Adapter | Config | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `manual` | none | Operator-recorded cash at checkout |
| `stripe` | `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY`, `STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET` | Payment intents, refunds and signed webhooks |

## Webhooks

`POST /api/payments/webhooks/[provider]` takes `pp_...` codes, rejects bodies over 1 MB, verifies `stripe-signature` and records each event in `PaymentWebhookEvent` under a replay key.

## Email, storage, AI

- SMTP sends reset, receipts and gift card emails. Configured when `SMTP_HOST` and `SMTP_FROM` are set.
- `MedSpaImage` uses S3-compatible storage with 5-minute signed URLs.
- The AI sidebar uses `OPENROUTER_API_KEY`, `OPENROUTER_MODEL`, optional `OPENROUTER_MAX_TOKENS`, or a key the user supplies.
- `/api/mcp-transport/[transport]` returns HTTP 503 until an owner-approved data and tool policy exists.

## Events

`DomainEvent` is written by appointment, payment and gift card workflows with a replay policy. `OutboxEvent` states: `pending`, `processing`, `delivered`, `dead_letter`.
