---
title: Staff
description: Providers, invitations, credentials and treatment authority.
icon: UserCog
---

`/platform/staff` and `/platform/staff/[id]`.

- `inviteStaff`, then the invitee uses `acceptStaffInvitation` at `/join`.
- `createProviderProfile`, `updateProviderProfile`.
- Credentials and authorization (`ProviderCredential`, `ProviderAuthorization`) decide which treatments a provider may perform.

Requires `canManagePeople`.
