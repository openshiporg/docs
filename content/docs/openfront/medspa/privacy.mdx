`/platform/privacy` and `/privacy/requests/[id]`.

`requestPatientRights`, `appendPatientRightsAction`, `placeLegalHold`, `releaseLegalHold`, `publishRetentionPolicy`, `openBreakGlass`. Clinical photos use `grantPhotoConsent`, `withdrawPhotoConsent`, `createClinicalAssetIntent`, `markClinicalAssetReady`, `authorizeClinicalAssetAccess`, `releaseClinicalAsset`, `tombstoneClinicalAsset`. Requires `canManageSensitiveRecords`.
