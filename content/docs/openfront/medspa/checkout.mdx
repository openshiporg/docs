import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

Routes: `/platform/checkout` and `/platform/checkout/[id]`.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open the ticket
Pick a `confirmed` or `in_progress` appointment.
</Step>
<Step>
### Apply benefits
Membership discounts, package credits and manual discounts. Discretionary discounts need practice-manager authority and a reason.
</Step>
<Step>
### Add retail and tip
Retail lines write `InventoryMovement` records. Tip is split later with `distributeTip`.
</Step>
<Step>
### Settle in cash
`completeAppointmentCheckout` records the `MedSpaTransaction`, `Payment` and `PaymentSession`, writes `Commission` records, and sets the appointment to `completed`.
</Step>
</Steps>

Checkout supports `paymentMethod: "cash"` recorded by the operator. Tax is a single rate from `Practice.taxRate` or `TAX_RATE`.

## Errors

`Appointment is not eligible for checkout`, `Appointment is already checked out`, `Clinical services require a completed authorized consultation before checkout`, `Each restricted service requires matching completed treatment evidence before checkout`, `Discounts and benefits cannot exceed the service subtotal`, `Controlled checkout currently supports operator-recorded cash only`, `Idempotency key was already used for another checkout request`.
