## Architecture

| Layer | Location | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Keystone schema | `features/keystone/models/**` | 98 lists, all scoped to a `Practice` |
| Workflow mutations and projections | `features/keystone/mutations/**`, `features/keystone/extendGraphqlSchema/**` | Named GraphQL operations registered in `features/keystone/mutations/index.ts` |
| Access | `features/keystone/access.ts`, `features/keystone/models/tenantAccess.ts` | Capability checks and practice tenancy |
| Operator dashboard | `features/dashboard` (shell), `features/platform` (Medspa workspaces), `app/dashboard/**` (routes) | Staff UI |
| Storefront | `features/storefront`, `app/(storefront)/(main)/**` | Client UI |
| Payments | `features/integrations/payment` | Manual and Stripe adapters |

Almost every list is write-locked. The lists with direct GraphQL CRUD are `Service`, `ServiceCategory`, `ServiceVariant`, `MedSpaImage`, `WidgetConfiguration` (`canManagePractice`), `Vendor` (`canManageInventory`), `MessageTemplate` (`canManageCommunications`), and `PaymentProvider` (`canManageIntegrations`). `Practice` and `User` allow updates only. Everything else is written by a named mutation or as a side effect of one, and a dashboard page for those lists must call the mutation rather than `create<List>`.

## Booking to checkout workflow

1. **Book.** A client books on the storefront, or staff book from `/dashboard/platform/appointments/new`. `bookAppointment` checks availability, creates the `Client` and `User` if needed, the `Appointment` in `pending`, `AppointmentLineItem` snapshots, and room, device, and supervisor reservations. A guest booking returns a link token that lets the guest view and cancel that request (`storefrontAppointment`, `cancelGuestAppointment`). No payment is taken at booking.
2. **Waitlist.** Demand without a slot goes to `WaitlistEntry` through `joinWaitlist` (staff) or `requestWaitlistSpot` (storefront). Entries move `active` to `notified` to `converted` or `removed` through `transitionWaitlistEntry`.
3. **Confirm and arrive.** `transitionAppointment` allows `pending` to `confirmed`, `cancelled`, or `no_show`; `confirmed` to `in_progress`, `cancelled`, or `no_show`; and `in_progress` to `cancelled`. Changes are revision-checked.
4. **Intake.** The client submits intake from `/account/intake/[appointmentId]` or staff record it (`submitIntake`). Clinical staff review it with `reviewIntake` (`submitted`, `in_review`, `reviewed`, `superseded`).
5. **Check out.** `completeAppointmentCheckout` accepts a `confirmed` or `in_progress` appointment, applies membership, package, and manual discounts, retail lines, tip, and tax, records the `MedSpaTransaction`, `Payment`, and `PaymentSession`, writes retail `InventoryMovement` and `Commission` records, and sets the appointment to `completed`. Current checkout supports operator-recorded cash only.
6. **Aftercare.** `scheduleFollowUp` creates a `FollowUpTask`; staff append actions and clients reply with `submitFollowUpResponse`. Clients can review a completed visit with `submitAppointmentReview`, and staff approve or hide reviews with `moderateReview`.

## Clinical treatment workflow

Treatments flagged as clinical pass through recorded gates before execution. The treatment workspace at `/dashboard/platform/appointments/[id]/treatment` drives these mutations:

1. `recordConsultation` records the assessment and candidacy (`candidate`, `deferred`, `declined`, `referred`).
2. `recordContraindicationFinding` and `decideContraindication` record rule hits and the clinician's disposition.
3. `approveTreatmentPlan` creates the `TreatmentPlan` and items (`draft`, `active`, `paused`, `completed`, `cancelled`).
4. `issueClinicalOrder` authorizes a performer; `transitionClinicalOrder` moves it through `active`, `partially_fulfilled`, `fulfilled`, `held`, `revoked`, `expired`, or `entered_in_error`.
5. `signTreatmentConsent` records consent against a published `ConsentTemplateVersion` (`publishConsentTemplate`); `withdrawTreatmentConsent` withdraws it.
6. `prepareTreatmentSession` and `startTreatmentSession` move the `TreatmentSession` from `planned` to `ready` to `in_progress`.
7. `recordTreatmentAdministration` records what was administered and, for lot-traced products, the `AdministrationLot` and `StockMovement`.
8. `appendSignedClinicalNote` adds an append-only, supersedable `ClientNote`.

Before a session can run, `features/keystone/mutations/clinicalExecutionReadiness.ts` checks that the exact approved plan, item, and consultation still authorize the booked encounter; that a current clinical order and matching consent exist; that current reviewed intake covers every required template version; that no contraindication review blocks treatment; that the session falls inside the appointment window; and that no required resource has an overlapping maintenance or unavailable block. Provider credentials (`registerProviderCredential`) and per-treatment, per-location authority (`configureProviderAuthorization`, `transitionProviderAuthority`) are checked separately.

<Callout type="warn">
These checks confirm that records exist and match. They do not verify a license with an issuing board, judge clinical appropriateness, or replace the supervising clinician.
</Callout>

## Custom GraphQL queries

- **Storefront:** `publicStorefrontSettings`, `storefrontServices`, `storefrontProviders`, `storefrontProvider`, `checkAvailability`, `storefrontReviews`, `storefrontAppointment`, `clientPortal`, `clientIntakeForms`.
- **Operator projections:** `medspaOperationsWorkspace`, `medspaAppointmentSchedule`, `medspaAppointmentClinicalHeader`, `medspaPlanJourney`, `medspaConsultationQueue`, `medspaClientDirectory`, `medspaClientRecord`, `medspaWaitlistBoard`, `medspaMessageLog`, `medspaReviewBoard`, `calculateCommission`.
- **Auth:** `registerCustomer` plus Keystone's password authentication and reset mutations.

Mutations that change money or clinical state take an idempotency key and, where relevant, a record revision, and many run in serializable transactions (`serializableWorkflow.ts`).

## Roles and access control

Each `Role` carries these capabilities: `canManagePeople`, `canManageRoles`, `canAccessDashboard`, `canManageOnboarding`, `canManagePractice`, `canSchedule`, `canReadClients`, `canManageClinical`, `canManageSensitiveRecords`, `canManageInventory`, `canManageFinance`, `canManageCommunications`, `canViewReports`, and `canManageIntegrations`.

Access checks re-read the user and role on each request and deny access when the user is disabled, has moved practice, or the role no longer grants the flag carried in the session. Clinical and governance lists (`ClientNote`, consent, incidents, patient rights, legal holds) require `canManageClinical` or `canManageSensitiveRecords` rather than general dashboard access.
