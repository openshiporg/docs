---
title: Clients
description: Client directory and record.
icon: Users
---

`/platform/clients` is the directory (`medspaClientDirectory`). `/platform/clients/[id]` is the record (`medspaClientRecord`) with appointments, intake, notes, memberships, packages and messages.

Edit contact and profile data with `updateClientProfile`. Needs `canReadClients`; clinical notes need `canManageClinical` or `canManageSensitiveRecords`.
