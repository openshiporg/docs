`/dashboard/platform/customers` (`platformCustomers`) is the directory. Add one at `/customers/new`.

## Profile

- Visit history and receipts
- Notes: `addCustomerNote` with `body`, `noteType`, `visibility` (staff or owner only), optional `appointmentId`. Only an owner can add owner-only notes.
- Consent: `setCustomerConsent` sets `acceptsMarketing` and `preferredContactMethod`.
- Status: `setCustomerStatus`.
- Waivers: `issueCustomerWaiver` creates a waiver record (title, type such as Minor consent or Photo release, delivery status). Customers sign through `signPublicWaiver`.
- Memberships and packages: see below.

Customer fields: `firstName`, `lastName`, `phone`, `email`, `status`, `preferredContactMethod`, `acceptsMarketing`, `birthday`, `preferredBarber`, `lastVisitAt`.

## Sell and manage a membership or package

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open the customer
Choose **Membership** or **Package** on the profile.
</Step>
<Step>
### Run the action
`manageMembership` with `action`: `purchase` (plan), `purchase_package` (package), `renew`, `pause`, `resume`, or `cancel`. Payment method is cash. The sale writes a `CheckoutTransaction` and a `MembershipEvent`.
</Step>
</Steps>

Rules: a cancelled membership cannot be renewed; only active memberships can be paused; only paused ones resumed; only active or paused ones cancelled. Balances are `CustomerPackage` rows with `remainingVisits`, `status` (active, paused, used_up, expired, cancelled), `startsAt`, `expiresAt`.
