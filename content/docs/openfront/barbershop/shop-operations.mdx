`/dashboard/platform/barbershop` is the operations page (`platformOperations`).

## Needs attention

The list flags:

- low or expiring stock
- unpaid or unapproved commissions
- open card payments
- a missing no-show policy
- chairs in maintenance or unassigned
- unsigned waivers

Each item links to the page that resolves it.

## Waitlist

The waitlist is for customers who want a slot that is not available. `manageWaitlist` takes an `action`:

- `create`: `customerName`, `phone`, optional `customerId`, `requestedServiceId`, `requestedBarberId`, `preferredAt`, `notes`. Duplicates (same phone, same service, still waiting or notified) are refused.
- `notify`: waiting entries move to `notified`.
- `expire`: waiting or notified entries move to `expired`.

Book an entry from the booking board by passing its id to `createStaffAppointment`; the entry becomes `booked`. The full list opens from the "All requests" link.
