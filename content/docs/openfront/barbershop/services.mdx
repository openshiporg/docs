`/dashboard/platform/services` (`platformServices`) lists the menu. Create and edit pages live under it.

## Categories

`/dashboard/platform/services/categories`. Create, edit, order, and show or hide categories. A service appears on the storefront only if its category is active.

## Services

`/services/new` and `/services/[id]`.

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| `name`, `description`, `imagePath` | Image uploads use S3 |
| `price` | Entered in dollars, stored in cents |
| `durationMinutes`, `bookingBufferMinutes` | Buffer is cleanup after the visit |
| `depositRequired`, `depositAmount` | Deposit paid online with Stripe |
| `credentialRequired` | Needs a barber with an effective practitioner credential |
| `waiverRequired`, `waiverTemplate*` | Customer must sign before service |
| `specialists` | Barbers who offer the service |
| `status` | active or not |

## Packages

`/services/packages/new`. A `ServicePackage` has a price and `ServicePackageItem` rows for included services. Sold at the counter with `manageMembership` (`purchase_package`). Only active packages can be sold.

## Membership plans

`/services/plans/new`. A `MembershipPlan` has `price`, `billingInterval`, `includedVisits`, `discountPercent`, and `status`. Only active plans can be purchased. A customer cannot hold two active or paused balances for the same plan.

## Retail products

`/services/new-product` and `/services/products/[id]`: `name`, `sku`, `price`, `cost`, `productType`, `inventoryTracked`, `status`. Stock is managed on [Inventory](/docs/openfront/barbershop/inventory).
