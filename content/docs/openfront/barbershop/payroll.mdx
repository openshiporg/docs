Each completed checkout creates a `Commission` for the barber, calculated from `serviceCommissionRateBps`. The Payroll page shows commissions by status and barber, amounts owed per barber, payouts, and tips by barber.

## Commission states

`projected` to `approved` to `paid`, or `voided`.

## Pay barbers

<Steps>
<Step>
### Approve
Select commissions and approve or void them with `transitionCommission` (`commissionId`, `toStatus`, `reason`). Bulk selection is supported.
</Step>
<Step>
### Pay
Choose **Pay** for a barber. `payApprovedCommissions` takes `commissionIds` and `notes` and creates a `CommissionPayout`. A payout contains one barber's commissions; only approved commissions can be paid.
</Step>
</Steps>

Approving and voiding happen with `transitionCommission`. Recording payment happens only through the payout: "Commissions can be approved or voided here. Record payment with Create payout."
