import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open the ticket
`/dashboard/platform/pos`, find the in-chair visit.
</Step>
<Step>
### Choose the tender
Cash, card (recorded after the terminal), or a package visit.
</Step>
<Step>
### Enter tip, discount, and tax
In dollars. Package visits take no tip or tax in the same ticket.
</Step>
<Step>
### Complete
The appointment is completed, the receipt is written, and a commission is projected.
</Step>
</Steps>

To refund a cash receipt, the owner records it from the receipt. See [POS checkout](/docs/openfront/barbershop/pos).
