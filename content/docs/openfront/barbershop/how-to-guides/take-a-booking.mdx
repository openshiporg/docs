import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open the board
`/dashboard/platform/booking`, choose the shop day.
</Step>
<Step>
### New appointment
Pick or create the customer, then the service, barber, and time.
</Step>
<Step>
### Collect a deposit if needed
For deposit services the customer pays by Stripe on the appointment page, or you take it at the counter.
</Step>
<Step>
### Confirm
Move the appointment from requested to confirmed.
</Step>
</Steps>

On the day: check in, then seat. See [Booking board](/docs/openfront/barbershop/booking).
