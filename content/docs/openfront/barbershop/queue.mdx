The walk-in line holds guests without a booking. Customers can join from `/queue` on the storefront or staff add them here (`platformQueue`).

## Add a walk-in

`createStaffQueueEntry` takes `customerName`, optional `phone`, `partySize` (1 to 8), `requestedServiceId`, `requestedBarberId`, `notes`, and `quotedWaitMinutes` (0 to 480).

## Work the line

| Action | Command | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Assign service and barber | `assignWalkInQueueEntry` | Only waiting guests can be reassigned |
| Mark texted | `transitionQueueEntry` to `texted` | A status marker set by staff |
| Seat | `seatWalkInQueueEntry` | Creates an appointment and puts the guest in the chair |
| Served or left | `transitionQueueEntry` | `served`, `left` |

States: `waiting`, `texted`, `in_chair`, `served`, `left`. Waiting entries from earlier days close as `left`.

Seating requires a service ("Service needed to seat") and a free barber who provides it. If nobody is working: "No active barber is available for this walk-in."

## Public view

`getPublicQueue` feeds the now-serving board. A guest who joins gets a ticket at `/queue` (`getPublicQueueEntry`) and the access token is kept in the `sf_queue_<id>` cookie for 2 days.
