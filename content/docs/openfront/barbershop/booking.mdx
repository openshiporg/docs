The booking board shows one shop day: appointments by time, the schedule by barber, chairs, and a status filter. It reads the `platformBooking` projection.

## What you can do

- Create an appointment for a new or existing customer.
- Confirm, check in, seat, cancel, reschedule, and mark no-show.
- Book a waitlist entry into a slot.
- Open the checkout for a seated visit (see [POS checkout](/docs/openfront/barbershop/pos)).

## Create an appointment

<Steps>
<Step>
### Choose the customer
Pick an existing customer or enter first name, last name, phone, and email.
</Step>
<Step>
### Choose service, barber, and time
`createStaffAppointment` takes `serviceId`, `barberId`, `startsAt`, optional `notes`, and `source` (phone, staff, or walk_in). The barber must offer the service, work that day (weekly hours, shifts, no time-off), and be free. Pass `waitlistEntryId` to convert a waitlist entry.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Status flow

`requested` to `confirmed` to `checked_in` to `in_chair` to `completed`. Use `transitionAppointment` (`appointmentId`, `toStatus`, `idempotencyKey`). `completed` is reached only through a paid checkout. `cancelled` and `no_show` are terminal.

- Check-in and seating happen only on the appointment's day: "Check-in and seating happen on the appointment's day."
- A service with a required waiver cannot start until the customer signs it.
- A licensed service needs a barber with an effective practitioner credential.

## Cancel and reschedule

`manageStaffAppointment` with `action` cancel or reschedule, a `reason`, and for reschedule a new `startsAt` or `barberId`. Only requested or confirmed appointments can be changed this way. Guests already in the shop use the board status actions.

## No-show

`markAppointmentNoShow` records the no-show against the active no-show policy. It is allowed after the start time plus the policy's `graceMinutes`: "No-show can be recorded after the N-minute grace period." An active no-show policy is required.

## Key fields

`Appointment`: `startsAt`, `endsAt`, `durationMinutes`, `status`, `source`, `paymentStatus`, `price`, `depositAmount`, `cancellationReason`, snapshots of the service title, barber, chair, booking policy, and waiver requirement, and `customerRescheduleCount`. Every change writes an `AppointmentChangeEvent`.
