`/dashboard/platform/pos` (`platformPos`) shows open tickets, the counter, retail, recent receipts, and cash refunds.

## Check out a visit

<Steps>
<Step>
### Find the ticket
Open tickets are visits that are in the chair. Search by name or receipt number.
</Step>
<Step>
### Choose the tender
`checkoutAppointment` takes `paymentMethod`: `cash`, `card`, or `package`. Card is a payment taken on a terminal and recorded by staff. A package tender spends a visit from the customer's balance (`packageBalanceId`) and covers the visit only.
</Step>
<Step>
### Add tip, discount, tax
`tipAmount`, `discountAmount`, `taxAmount` are whole cents entered by staff. Total is price plus tip plus tax minus discount.
</Step>
<Step>
### Complete
The appointment moves to `completed`, a `CheckoutTransaction` is written, `Tip` and projected `Commission` rows are created, and any deposit paid online is applied.
</Step>
</Steps>

Rules: only an in-chair appointment can be completed; cancelled or no-show appointments cannot be checked out; "Package tender covers a visit only. Collect tips/tax separately; mixed tender is not supported."; "Package has no visits remaining."

A deposit paid by Stripe is passed in as `paymentSessionId`, and the payment session must be captured for the exact total.

Transaction states: `open`, `paid`, `partially_refunded`, `refunded`, `voided`.

## Retail sales and returns

`recordProductSale` (`productId`, `quantity` 1 to 100, `paymentMethod`, `taxRateBps`) decrements stock from lots. `returnProductSaleLine` (`saleLineId`, `quantity`, `reason`, `cashReturned`, `restock`) records a return.

## Cash refunds

`refundCashCheckout` (`transactionId`, `reason`, `cashReturned`) refunds a cash-only appointment receipt that has not been refunded. Only the shop owner can record it. If a commission was already paid, owner reconciliation is required first.
