Pick a start and end date and choose **Show range**. `platformReports` returns figures in the shop timezone and currency.

## Figures

- **Appointments:** total, completed, cancelled, no-show
- **Revenue:** paid total, tips, transaction count, product sales and count
- **Queue:** waiting, served, left
- **Payouts:** total and count
- **Rates:** completion, queue conversion, utilization
- **Liabilities:** memberships and active balances
- **Service performance** and **Barber performance:** revenue and appointments per row. Duplicate names are flagged.

Barber and service rows count settled service lines only: "No settled service lines in this range." Viewing needs `canViewReports`, held by owner, manager, and viewer roles. Invalid ranges and a missing shop timezone return a message on the page.
