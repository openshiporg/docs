`/dashboard/platform/team` (`platformTeam`) lists the shop's `ShopMembership` rows.

## Add someone

1. The person creates an account (sign-up, or an owner adds them under `/dashboard/users`).
2. An owner or manager enters their email and a role. `addShopMemberByEmail` takes `email` and `membershipRole`.
3. Managers can grant `front_desk`, `barber`, or `viewer`. Only owners grant `owner` or `manager`.

Roles: `owner`, `manager`, `front_desk`, `barber`, `viewer`. Status: `invited`, `active`, `suspended`, `revoked`. Suspend, restore, or remove access from the row. Memberships are not deleted, and each shop keeps at least one active owner.

See [Roles and permissions](/docs/openfront/barbershop/roles-permissions) for what each role can do.
