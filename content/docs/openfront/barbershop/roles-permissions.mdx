`Shop` is the tenant. A user's global `Role` flags apply only inside shops where their `ShopMembership` allows them.

| Membership role | Dashboard permissions inside the shop |
|---|---|
| `owner` | canAccessDashboard, canManageOnboarding, canManageServices, canManageStaff, canManageSchedules, canManageAppointments, canManageQueue, canManageCustomers, canManageCustomerNotes, canManageMemberships, canManagePayments, canViewReports |
| `manager` | Same as owner |
| `front_desk` | canAccessDashboard, canManageAppointments, canManageQueue, canManageCustomers, canManageCustomerNotes, canManageMemberships, canManagePayments |
| `barber` | canAccessDashboard, canManageAppointments, canManageQueue, canManageCustomers, canManageCustomerNotes |
| `viewer` | canAccessDashboard, canViewReports |

People and role administration (`canManagePeople`, `canManageRoles`) are global, not shop scoped.

## Owner-only actions

- Cash refunds (`refundCashCheckout`)
- Owner-only customer notes
- Granting the `owner` or `manager` membership role
- Linking staff accounts is for owners and managers

## First user

The first user gets an `Admin` role with every flag from `/dashboard/init`. Customers who sign up on the storefront have no role and no shop access.
