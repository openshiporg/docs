`/dashboard/platform/inventory` lists retail stock on hand with a needs-reordering tab, search, and margin. `/inventory/[id]` is one product. `/inventory/suppliers` manages suppliers.

## Receive a delivery

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open the product
`/dashboard/platform/inventory/[id]`.
</Step>
<Step>
### Enter the lot
`receiveInventoryLot`: `lotNumber`, `quantity`, optional `supplierId`, `unitCost`, `receivedAt`, `expiresAt`. The expiry must be after the received date.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Waste and corrections

`recordInventoryMovement` on a lot: `movementType` (receive, sale, return, waste, adjustment), `quantityDelta`, `reason`, `referenceKey`. Receive and return must be positive; sale and waste negative; movements cannot be zero. "Insufficient inventory for this movement." blocks going below zero.

The stock ledger on the product lists every `InventoryMovement`.

## Suppliers

`Supplier`: `name`, `contactName`, `email`, `phone`, `website`, `notes`, `status` (use, pause, archive).

Low and expiring stock shows in [Shop operations](/docs/openfront/barbershop/shop-operations).
