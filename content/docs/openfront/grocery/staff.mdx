`/dashboard/platform/staff` needs `canManageUsers`. Data comes from `groceryStaffDirectory`.

## Invite staff

<Steps>
<Step>
### Invite
`inviteGroceryStaff(name, email, capabilities, delivery)`. The capability map must list the flags explicitly, and you can only grant capabilities you hold yourself. Any capability needs `canAccessDashboard`.
</Step>
<Step>
### Share the link
The invitation lasts 48 hours. With `delivery: "manual"` you hand over the link yourself. Only one pending invitation may exist per email; revoke it with `revokeGroceryStaffInvitation` before replacing it.
</Step>
<Step>
### Accept
The invited person opens `/staff/accept`, which calls `acceptGroceryStaffInvitation`.
</Step>
</Steps>

An email that already belongs to an account cannot be invited. Use a separate staff identity.

## Change access

`updateGroceryStaffAccess(userId, capabilities, reason)` replaces a person's flags. Each change writes a `StaffAccessEvent`.

## Capabilities

`canAccessDashboard`, `canManageProducts`, `canManageOrders`, `canManageInventory`, `canManageSuppliers`, `canManageDelivery`, `canManageUsers`, `canManagePayments`, `canManageOnboarding`. See [Roles and permissions](/docs/openfront/grocery/roles-permissions).

## Customer erasure

`reviewGroceryCustomerErasure` takes `requestId`, `approve` (true or false), and a `reason`. A declined request becomes `rejected`. An approved one erases the customer's data and suppresses their queued messages.
