Openfront Grocery runs one grocery store. Shoppers use the storefront. Staff use the dashboard at `/dashboard/platform/*`.

## Who uses what

| User | Surface | Jobs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shopper | Storefront | Browse, build a basket, choose pickup or delivery, pay, answer substitution requests, check in at the curb |
| Picker | Fulfillment | Pick items, record weights, propose substitutions |
| Order manager | Orders, pickup, subscriptions, messages | Advance orders, cancel, run recurring baskets, answer messages |
| Delivery coordinator | Delivery | Slots, routes, stop outcomes |
| Inventory and buying | Inventory, suppliers, purchasing | Lots, counts, recalls, purchase orders, receiving |
| Finance | Payments, reports | Refunds, voids, close, statement import |
| Admin | Settings, staff, communications | Store profile, delivery policy, invitations |

## An order

1. The shopper fills a basket and picks a delivery or pickup time. `initiatePaymentSession` places an authorization hold with manual capture.
2. `submitGroceryOrder` re-reads prices, stock lots, the slot, the coupon, and the delivery policy, then allocates lots in expiry order and writes the order.
3. Staff move the order to `picking`, record what was actually picked (weight or quantity per line), and propose or record substitutions.
4. The shopper accepts or declines substitutions on the order page.
5. `finalizeGroceryOrderActuals` freezes the picked goods and captures payment. A higher final total needs the shopper to authorize the difference.
6. Pickup orders are handed off at the curb. Delivery orders go on a route and each stop is resolved.
7. Refunds, cancellations, and returns are recorded against the order and its lots.
