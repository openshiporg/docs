`/dashboard/platform/orders` needs `canManageOrders`. Data comes from `groceryPlatformOrders`.

## Statuses

`pending`, `picking`, `packed`, `out_for_delivery`, `delivered`, `delivery_failed`, `delivery_refused`, `returned`, `cancelled`. Pickup readiness is stored in order metadata, not as a status.

## Advance an order

`advanceOrderFulfillment(orderId, target)` accepts `picking`, `packed`, or `ready_for_pickup`.

- Moving to `picking` needs a payment that is `authorized`, `succeeded`, or `partially_refunded`.
- Moving to `packed` needs a captured payment (`succeeded` or `partially_refunded`), which comes from [finalizing the sale](/docs/openfront/grocery/fulfillment).
- Delivery orders reach `out_for_delivery` through [routes](/docs/openfront/grocery/delivery).

## Cancel

`cancelGroceryOrder(orderId, reason, idempotencyKey, disposition, conditionNote)`.

- The reason must be 3 to 500 characters.
- A packed or returned order needs a `disposition` (`return_to_stock`, `waste`, or `quarantine`) and a condition note.
- Cancellation releases lot allocations and refunds or voids the payment. It refuses to continue when refund evidence is incomplete.
- Active or paid pickup orders cannot release their reserved slot by hand.

## Recover interrupted checkouts

A `CheckoutAttempt` is `pending`, `settled_pending_finalize`, `finalizing`, `finalized`, `compensation_required`, `compensation_processing`, `compensated`, or `failed`. Use the recover action on the Orders page (`reconcileCheckoutAttempts`) to finish attempts where payment succeeded but the order was not written.

Shoppers can also resume or restart from the checkout page with `resumeGroceryCheckout` and `restartGroceryCheckout`. A fresh checkout is only available once payment compensation is confirmed.

## Refund

Refunds are started from here or [Payments](/docs/openfront/grocery/payments) with `refundPayment` or `refundGroceryOrderLines`.
