`/dashboard/platform/fulfillment` needs `canManageOrders`. Data comes from `groceryPlatformFulfillment`.

## Record what was picked

For each line, `resolveOrderLineFulfillment` stores an `OrderLineFulfillment` with a `resolution`:

- `picked`: the product was found. Weighed products (`pricingMethod` `weight` or `volume`) take `quantity`, optional `tare`, and `unitOfMeasure`. A unit product picked at a smaller count must use `short` instead.
- `short`: fewer or none picked. A reason is required.
- `substituted`: a different product replaces it. A reason is required, plus consent fields (`consentChannel`, `consentCustomer`, `consentEvidence`) when staff record the customer's approval.

Quantities are stored in thousandths (`quantityMilli`). The price is recomputed from the product price and the picked amount.

## Substitutions

- **Customer pre-set**: each basket item has a substitution preference (`call_me`, `best_match`, `refund`).
- **Propose**: `proposeOrderLineSubstitution` sends the shopper a proposed product, quantity, and reason. They answer on `/order/[id]` with `respondToOrderLineSubstitution`.
- **Record**: `recordOrderItemSubstitution` logs one already agreed, which creates an `OrderItemSubstitution` with `customerApproved` and `approvedAt`.

## Finalize the sale

<Steps>
<Step>
### Resolve every line
Each line needs a recorded actual and no unanswered proposal.
</Step>
<Step>
### Finalize
`finalizeGroceryOrderActuals(orderId)` totals the picked goods, tax, and fees, and captures payment.
</Step>
<Step>
### If the total went up
The capture needs a new authorization from the shopper (`authorizeOrderAdjustment`). Staff recover a stuck one with `recoverRevisedAuthorization`.
</Step>
<Step>
### Mark packed
Move the order to `packed` or `ready_for_pickup` from the order queue.
</Step>
</Steps>
