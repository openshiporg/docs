`/dashboard/platform/settings` needs `canManageOnboarding`. Data comes from `groceryPlatformSettings`.

## Profile

`updateGroceryStoreProfile` saves `name`, `tagline`, `homepageTitle`, `homepageDescription`, `contactEmail`, `contactPhone`, `address`, `countryCode`, `locale`, `timezone`, `taxRateBps`, and `policyNotes`. Tax is a store-level rate in basis points (800 is 8%).

Settings left at the onboarding placeholder text are not shown to shoppers, and the store counts as ready only after it has a real name.

## Delivery policy

`updateGroceryDeliveryPolicy(policy)`:

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `pickupMinimumCents` | Minimum basket for pickup |
| `deliveryMinimumCents` | Minimum basket for delivery |
| `postalPrefixes` | Service area. Each prefix is 3 to 12 letters, digits, or hyphens, up to 200 |
| `deliveryFeeCents` | Flat fee |
| `feeTaxRateBasisPoints` | Tax on the fee |

Checkout evaluates the policy against the basket after discounts. Delivery to a postal code outside the prefixes is refused and the shopper is told to pick pickup.

## Logo and brand

`updateGroceryStoreLogo` sets `logoIcon` and `logoColor`. `updateGroceryStorefrontBrandHue` sets `brandHue`. `/api/store-icon` serves the logo as an icon.
