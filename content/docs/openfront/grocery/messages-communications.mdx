## Customer inbox

`/dashboard/platform/messages` needs `canManageOrders`. Messages come from the Help page at `/support` (`submitGroceryContactMessage`). A `ContactMessage` has `reference`, `subject`, `topic`, `orderNumber`, `message`, `status` (`new`, `in_progress`, `replied`, `closed`), and `staffNotes`. Update status and notes with the generated `updateContactMessage`.

## Communications

`/dashboard/platform/communications` needs `canManageOnboarding`. Data comes from `groceryOutboxStatus`.

Customer emails and outbound events are queued as `GroceryOutboxEvent` rows. On this page:

- `deliverGroceryOutboxNow` sends a queued message now
- `replayGroceryOutboxEvent` retries a failed event
- `reconcileGroceryCustomerDelivery` resolves a delivery whose result is unknown

The `worker:events` process claims events (`claimGroceryOutboxEvents`), sends emails and alerts, and posts events to `GROCERY_EVENT_WEBHOOK_URL` signed with HMAC-SHA256. Without SMTP, queued messages stay pending.
