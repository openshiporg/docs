`/dashboard/platform/customers` needs `canManageUsers`. `groceryPlatformCustomers` lists customers and `groceryPlatformCustomer(id)` returns one with recent orders.

## Provision an account

`provisionGroceryCustomer` creates a customer account for a shopper who has not registered. It needs a valid name and email.

## Shopper-owned data

Shoppers manage their own addresses (`Address`), shopping lists, favorites, notification preferences, and recurring baskets from `/account` and related routes. Privacy requests (`CustomerPrivacyRequest`) go to staff; erasure requests are reviewed on the [Staff](/docs/openfront/grocery/staff) page with `reviewGroceryCustomerErasure`.
