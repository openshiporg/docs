---
title: Pick, pack, and finalize an order
description: Take an order from authorized to packed with actual weights.
icon: PackageCheck
---

import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

<Steps>
<Step>
### Start picking
In `/dashboard/platform/orders` open the order and move it to `picking`. The payment must be authorized.
</Step>
<Step>
### Record each line
On `/dashboard/platform/fulfillment` mark each line `picked`, `short`, or `substituted`. For weighed items enter the weight and tare.
</Step>
<Step>
### Wait for substitution answers
Lines with an open proposal block finalization until the shopper responds.
</Step>
<Step>
### Finalize
Press finalize. Payment is captured on the picked total. If the total is higher than the hold, the shopper is asked to authorize the difference on `/order/[id]`.
</Step>
<Step>
### Pack
Move the order to `packed` for delivery, or `ready_for_pickup` for curbside.
</Step>
</Steps>
