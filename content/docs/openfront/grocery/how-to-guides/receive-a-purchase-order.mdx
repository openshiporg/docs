---
title: Order and receive stock
description: Raise a purchase order, receive it into lots, and settle the supplier invoice.
icon: PackagePlus
---

import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

<Steps>
<Step>
### Draft
At `/dashboard/platform/purchasing` pick a supplier, add products with quantity and unit cost, and save the draft.
</Step>
<Step>
### Send and confirm
Mark it `sent` when you place it with the supplier, `confirmed` when they confirm.
</Step>
<Step>
### Receive
Enter, per item, the quantity received, supplier lot number, expiry date, location, and condition.
</Step>
<Step>
### Capture the invoice
Record the supplier invoice. A variance needs approval from a different operator.
</Step>
<Step>
### Claim shortages
Open a claim for short or damaged goods and resolve it as `credit` or `rejected`.
</Step>
<Step>
### Settle
Someone with `canManagePayments` settles the invoice.
</Step>
</Steps>
