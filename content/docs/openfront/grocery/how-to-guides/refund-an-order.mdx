---
title: Refund an order
description: Refund selected lines, void an authorization, or cancel an order.
icon: Undo2
---

import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

<Steps>
<Step>
### Choose the path
Before capture, void the authorization or cancel the order. After capture, refund lines.
</Step>
<Step>
### Refund lines
At `/dashboard/platform/payments` open the order, select the lines, give a reason (3 to 500 characters), and choose a disposition for each: `return_to_stock`, `waste`, or `quarantine`.
</Step>
<Step>
### Watch the status
The refund shows `processing` until the provider confirms. The refund worker updates it. A stuck refund appears in the recovery list.
</Step>
</Steps>
