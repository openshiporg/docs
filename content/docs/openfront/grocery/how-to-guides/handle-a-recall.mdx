---
title: Handle a recall
description: Quarantine lots, notify affected customers, and close the recall.
icon: ShieldAlert
---

import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open the recall
At `/dashboard/platform/inventory` choose the product or lots and run the recall action with a reason. Lots become `recalled` and unsellable. Affected orders are listed.
</Step>
<Step>
### Notify customers
Record a notice per affected order: channel, outcome, and reference.
</Step>
<Step>
### Record dispositions
For recalled stock record `released`, `recovered`, `disposed`, or `unaccounted` with quantity and custody.
</Step>
<Step>
### Close
Close the recall. It is refused until every affected order has a delivered notice.
</Step>
</Steps>

For a precaution without a recall use `hold` or `quarantine`, then `release`.
