An estimate is presented to the client, revised as versions and accepted, with overage approvals when the work goes past it. Visit charges are captured from the record and orders onto a draft invoice, which is posted, then paid by any provider. Deposits are applied to invoices. Refunds and write-offs over policy need a second person. A month's receivables close as an AR period.

Fee schedules are edited as a draft and published after a second person approves. Pet insurance is a claim packet the client sends to their insurer, with authorizations recorded when needed.
