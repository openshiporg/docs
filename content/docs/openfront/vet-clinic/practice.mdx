**Team & licences** lists staff, licences and DEA registrations with expiry dates. **Policy packs** shows the federal and state rules in force with sources. **Compliance** tracks clocks, tasks and incidents. **Read audit** shows who read which client or patient record. **Audit log** shows the hash-chained events and checks the chain.

**Production and pay** computes production and commission by rule version and runs pay periods to approval and export. **Import** brings records over from Avimark or ezyVet. **Practice settings** holds locations, calendars and practice controls, which change only through an approval request. **Approvals** lists requests waiting for a second person.

## Commands

Named mutations for this area. Each checks permissions on the server, takes an idempotency key and writes an audit event.

`draftRuleVersion`, `requestRulePublication`, `requestProductionAdjustment`, `openPayPeriod`, `calculatePayPeriod`, `reopenPayPeriod`, `requestPayPeriodApproval`, `exportPayPeriod`, `lockPayPeriod`, `previewImport`, `applyImport`, `exportData`, `planCampaign`, `cancelCampaign`, `approveCampaign`, `decideApprovalRequest`, `cancelApprovalRequest`, `requestPracticeControls`.
