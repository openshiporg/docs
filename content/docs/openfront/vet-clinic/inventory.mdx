Stock is held in lots at stock locations. Purchase orders are drafted, submitted and received, and reorder rules flag low stock. Counts reconcile what is on the shelf. A manufacturer recall finds exposed patients. A temperature excursion in a fridge is logged and resolved before the stock is used.

## Pages

| Page | Route | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inventory | `/dashboard/platform/inventory` | Items, lots, orders and counts. |

## Lifecycles

| List | Statuses |
| --- | --- |
| `InventoryLot` | `available`, `quarantined`, `disposed` |
| `PurchaseOrder` | `draft`, `submitted`, `partially_received`, `received`, `cancelled` |
| `StockCount` | `closed` |
| `RecallCase` | `open`, `closed` |
| `RecallExposure` | `to_contact`, `contacted`, `resolved` |
| `TemperatureExcursion` | `open`, `resolved` |

## Commands

Named mutations for this area. Each checks permissions on the server, takes an idempotency key and writes an audit event.

`addStockLocation`, `receiveStock`, `adjustStock`, `addSupplier`, `draftPurchaseOrder`, `submitPurchaseOrder`, `cancelPurchaseOrder`, `receivePurchaseOrder`, `setReorderRule`, `countStock`, `openRecall`, `markRecallExposure`, `closeRecall`, `logTemperature`, `resolveExcursion`.
