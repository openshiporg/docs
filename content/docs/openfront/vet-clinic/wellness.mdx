A wellness plan is a monthly bundle of services sold as versions. Enrolling a patient starts plan billing, which charges the saved card each period and retries failures. Entitlements are used at visits and returned if a charge is reversed.

## Pages

| Page | Route | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wellness plans | `/dashboard/platform/wellness` | Monthly plans, members and billing. |

## Lifecycles

| List | Statuses |
| --- | --- |
| `WellnessPlan` | `active`, `retired` |
| `WellnessPlanVersion` | `draft`, `published`, `retired` |
| `Enrollment` | `pending_payment`, `active`, `cancelling`, `cancelled`, `suspended`, `completed`, `ended_by_death` |
| `PlanBillingAttempt` | `processing`, `succeeded`, `failed`, `unknown` |

## Commands

Named mutations for this area. Each checks permissions on the server, takes an idempotency key and writes an audit event.

`savePlanVersion`, `enrolPatient`, `runPlanBilling`, `retryPlanCharge`, `checkPlanCharge`, `replacePlanCard`, `cancelEnrollment`, `useEntitlement`, `returnEntitlement`.
