import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the queue

In **Prescriptions**, open the refill queue. Requests arrive from the client site, the receptionist or the desk.

</Step>
<Step>

### Decide

A veterinarian approves or declines it (`decideRefill`).

</Step>
<Step>

### Dispense

Dispense the fill from a stock lot (`dispenseFill`) and print the label (`printDispenseLabel`).

</Step>
</Steps>
