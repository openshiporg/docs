title: Vaccines and reminders
description: Administered and outside vaccines, rabies certificates and reminders.
icon: Syringe
---

Vaccines are recorded as administered in the clinic or entered from outside records. Reminder protocols set when each patient is next due. Staff can suppress a reminder or add one by hand. Because the app sends no texts or emails, reminders are lists staff work from.

## Pages

| Page | Route | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vaccines | `/dashboard/platform/vaccines` | Vaccines, rabies certificates and reminders. |

## Lifecycles

| List | Statuses |
| --- | --- |
| `PatientReminder` | `scheduled`, `due`, `overdue`, `satisfied`, `suppressed`, `lapsed` |
| `ReminderProtocolVersion` | `draft`, `published`, `superseded` |

## Commands

Named mutations for this area. Each checks permissions on the server, takes an idempotency key and writes an audit event.

`administerVaccine`, `reverseVaccineAdministration`, `recordOutsideVaccine`, `suppressReminder`, `addManualReminder`.
