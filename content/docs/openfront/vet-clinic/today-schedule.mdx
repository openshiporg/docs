**Today** shows appointments by status, the whiteboard, calls and the due-and-overdue clocks. The schedule books one or more patients with a provider and room. Surgery and dental appointment types arrive as requests that staff accept (`acceptAppointmentRequest`). Check-in requires acknowledging the patient's alerts first. The whiteboard tracks patient flow through the visit, and the waitlist holds clients who want an earlier time.

**Refresh due and overdue** recomputes treatment tasks due or overdue, unsigned-record tasks and vaccine reminder status.

## Pages

| Page | Route | What it shows |

| Today | `/dashboard/platform/today` | Appointments by status, whiteboard, calls and clocks. |

| Whiteboard | `/dashboard/platform/whiteboard` | Live patient flow. |

## Lifecycles

| List | Statuses |
| --- | --- |
| `Appointment` | `requested`, `booked`, `confirmed`, `checked_in`, `in_progress`, `ready_for_checkout`, `completed`, `cancelled`, `no_show`, `declined` |
| `AppointmentPatient` | `scheduled`, `checked_in`, `in_progress`, `done`, `cancelled`, `no_show` |
| `WhiteboardEntry` | `waiting`, `in_exam`, `treatment`, `diagnostics`, `hospitalised`, `surgery`, `recovery`, `boarding`, `ready_for_discharge`, `discharged` |
| `WaitlistEntry` | `waiting`, `offered`, `accepted`, `expired`, `declined`, `cancelled` |

## Commands

Named mutations for this area. Each checks permissions on the server, takes an idempotency key and writes an audit event.

`bookAppointment`, `cancelAppointment`, `rescheduleAppointment`, `confirmAppointment`, `acceptAppointmentRequest`, `checkInAppointment`, `markNoShow`, `moveWhiteboardEntry`, `addToWaitlist`, `refreshClinicalClocks`.
