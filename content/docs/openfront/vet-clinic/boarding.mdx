Boarding reservations are confirmed, checked in to a run and checked out. Daily care is logged during the stay, and belongings are returned at checkout. Grooming bookings move through their steps with style notes kept for next time.

## Pages

| Page | Route | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Boarding | `/dashboard/platform/boarding` | Reservations, runs and daily care. |
| Grooming | `/dashboard/platform/grooming` | Grooming bookings and style notes. |

## Lifecycles

| List | Statuses |
| --- | --- |
| `BoardingReservation` | `requested`, `confirmed`, `checked_in`, `checked_out`, `cancelled`, `no_show` |
| `BoardingStay` | `in_house`, `checked_out` |
| `GroomAppointment` | `booked`, `checked_in`, `in_progress`, `ready`, `completed`, `cancelled`, `no_show` |

## Commands

Named mutations for this area. Each checks permissions on the server, takes an idempotency key and writes an audit event.

`saveRunType`, `setRunStatus`, `reserveBoarding`, `confirmBoarding`, `cancelBoarding`, `checkInBoarding`, `moveBoardingRun`, `logBoardingCare`, `returnBelonging`, `checkOutBoarding`, `bookGroom`, `advanceGroom`, `saveGroomNote`.
