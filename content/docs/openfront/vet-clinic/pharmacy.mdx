Prescriptions are written by a veterinarian, and refills arrive from the client site, the receptionist or the desk for a veterinarian to decide. Dispensing draws from stock lots. A prescription can also be sent to an outside pharmacy.

The controlled-substance register tracks each container from receipt to destruction: opening, administration, waste with a witness, counts, transfers between locations and DEA order forms. The database refuses a negative balance and a Schedule II refill. Discrepancies open a case, and losses are reported. PDMP files are generated for the state. None of these actions is reachable by an AI agent.
