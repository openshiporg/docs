A client is a household with one or more people and contact points. A patient has species, breed, weight history, alerts, microchips and its veterinarian-client-patient relationship (VCPR) status. A new caller or a client who registers online becomes a pending household with pending pets; staff confirm it, and duplicates are flagged. Recording a patient's death ends reminders and wellness billing.

## Pages

| Page | Route | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Clients | `/dashboard/platform/clients` | Households, people, consents, pets. |
| Patients | `/dashboard/platform/patients` | Patients with alerts, weights and VCPR. |

## Lifecycles

| List | Statuses |
| --- | --- |
| `Client` | `prospect`, `active`, `inactive`, `merged`, `closed` |
| `Patient` | `pending`, `active`, `inactive`, `deceased`, `merged` |
| `ContactPoint` | `active`, `invalid`, `ported` |
| `VcprRecord` | `active`, `expired`, `terminated` |

## Commands

Named mutations for this area. Each checks permissions on the server, takes an idempotency key and writes an audit event.

`registerClient`, `registerPendingClient`, `confirmRegistration`, `addPatient`, `recordWeight`, `addPatientAlert`, `recordConsent`, `recordPatientDeath`.
