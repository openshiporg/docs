`/dashboard/platform/accounting` and `/accounting/[id]` (`accountingWorkspace`).

<Steps>
<Step>
### Map accounts
`configureAccountingMapping`. Onboarding adds 11 mappings.
</Step>
<Step>
### Stage a batch
`stageAccountingBatch` builds a balanced batch from mappings. "Active accounting batch already exists for source".
</Step>
<Step>
### Post and reconcile
`manageAccountingBatch`: queue post, record posted or failed, reconcile, lock. Statuses: staged, posting, posted, failed, reconciled, locked.
</Step>
</Steps>

Only the `operator-recorded` provider is accepted. Accounting administration permission is required.
