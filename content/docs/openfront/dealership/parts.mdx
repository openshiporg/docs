Pages: `/dashboard/platform/parts`, `/parts/[id]`, `/parts/purchase-orders`, `/parts/purchase-orders/[id]`.

- `configurePart` creates or edits a part.
- `recordPartsMovement` writes a movement of any type to the ledger. "Part inventory changed concurrently" means reload.
- `managePartsPurchaseOrder` creates, edits, orders, receives, closes, or cancels. Statuses: draft, ordered, partially-received, received, cancelled. Receiving posts receipt movements. A PO can have at most 200 lines.
