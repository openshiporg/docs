`/dashboard/platform/test-drives`. Customers book from `/test-drive` or a vehicle page (`submitTestDrive`). A vehicle can hold only one open test drive in a 60-minute window.

Staff use `updateTestDriveWorkflow` to schedule, verify the driver's license, complete, or mark no-show. "A test drive can only be marked no-show after its scheduled time".
