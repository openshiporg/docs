---
title: Store settings
description: Dealership profile, hours, tax, doc fee, logo, and rooftop time zone.
icon: Settings
---

`/dashboard/platform/store-settings` edits `DealershipProfile` and `Rooftop` through generated updates: name, contact, address, hours, sales tax, doc fee, logo, and the rooftop time zone. New deals read tax and doc fee from the profile. The storefront serves the oldest active rooftop in an active dealer group.
