---
title: Roles and permissions
description: Role flags, memberships, and record scoping.
icon: Shield
---

Access comes from the user's `Role`. Operating permissions can also come from an active `DealerGroupMembership` or `RooftopMembership`, for that scope only.

| Area | Flags |
|---|---|
| Dashboard | `canAccessDashboard`, `canManageOnboarding`, `canManageSettings` |
| People | `canReadPeople`, `canManagePeople`, `canSeeOtherPeople`, `canEditOtherPeople`, `canManageUsers`, `canManageRoles` |
| Inventory | `canViewInventory`, `canReadVehicles`, `canManageInventory`, `canViewDealerCost` |
| Leads and CRM | `canAccessLeads`, `canReadLeads`, `canManageLeads`, `canViewAllLeads`, `canManageTasks` |
| Trade-ins | `canAccessTradeIns`, `canReadTradeIns`, `canManageTradeIns` |
| Deals | `canAccessDeals`, `canReadDeals`, `canManageDeals`, `canApproveDeals`, `canReadDealApprovals` |
| Finance, service, payments | `canManageFinancing`, `canManageService`, `canReadPayments`, `canManagePayments` |
| Keys | `canManageKeys` |

People, users, roles, settings, onboarding, API keys, and payments are role-only. Leads, deals, approvals, and trade-ins are limited to the assigned salesperson unless `canViewAllLeads` applies across all rooftops. Onboarding creates up to 8 staff roles.
