`/dashboard/platform/customers` and `/customers/[id]`.

- `updateCustomerContact`: edit contact details
- `updateCustomerCommunicationPreferences`: consent; each change writes a `CustomerContactPreferenceEvent`
- `customerDuplicateCandidates` then `mergeCustomers` (a `CustomerMergeEvent` is kept)
- `linkCustomerHousehold`, `unlinkCustomerHousehold`. "Customer is not in a household"
- `openLead` from the customer

A CRM `Customer` is separate from the login `User`. Public submissions find or create a Customer per rooftop by email or phone. A storefront account links to Customers after `confirmCustomerEmailVerification`.
