Adapters live in `features/integrations/payment`: `stripe` and `manual`. Records are `PaymentProvider` rows on the Payments page.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Set keys
`STRIPE_SECRET_KEY` and `STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET`.
</Step>
<Step>
### Add the webhook
`POST /api/payment-webhooks/{providerCode}`, for example `/api/payment-webhooks/pp_stripe_stripe`. `handlePaymentProviderWebhook` verifies the signature and records a `PaymentProviderEvent`.
</Step>
<Step>
### Check the provider
Onboarding seeds Stripe (installed) and Manual. Stripe fails until the keys are set.
</Step>
</Steps>

Stripe creates Checkout Sessions and refunds. The manual adapter records payments staff take.
