## Applications

Customers apply at `/financing` (`submitFinancing`) with consent and privacy acknowledgment. Statuses: submitted, under-review, approved, declined, expired, cancelled.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Queue
`queueFinancingApplication` with the `operator-recorded` adapter. "Only submitted applications can be queued".
</Step>
<Step>
### Record the submission and decision
`recordLenderSubmissionStatus`, `recordLenderDecision`. `openFinanceFollowUp` for stipulations.
</Step>
</Steps>

## F&I menu

<Steps>
<Step>
### Catalog
`configureFinanceProduct`.
</Step>
<Step>
### Quote and present
`recordFinanceProductQuote`, `presentFinanceMenu` (creates a `FinanceMenuVersion`). "Every menu product requires current operator/provider quote evidence".
</Step>
<Step>
### Customer choice
`decideFinanceMenu`.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Close out

- `recordFinanceComplianceCheck`: identity, OFAC, red-flag, privacy
- `manageFinanceContractPackage`
- `reviewFinancePrerequisites`
- `manageFinanceFundingCase`
- `recordCashDealDeposit` for cash deals
- `recordFinanceProductCancellation` on `/finance/cancellations` with a pro-rata refund calculator. "Cancellation must reference an accepted menu product"
