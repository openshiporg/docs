`/dashboard/platform/delivery` (`deliveryWorkspace`).

<Steps>
<Step>
### Schedule and arrive
`manageDeliveryCase`: scheduled, arrived, ready, delivered, cancelled.
</Step>
<Step>
### Checklist and waivers
Recorded through the same command.
</Step>
<Step>
### Acknowledge
The customer acknowledgment must match the current worksheet: "Delivery acknowledgment is unbound or stale; record it for the current worksheet".
</Step>
<Step>
### Complete
`completeDealDelivery` delivers the deal and marks the vehicle sold. It needs deal and financing permissions.
</Step>
</Steps>

`manageWeOweItem` tracks promises after the sale: open, fulfilled, waived.
