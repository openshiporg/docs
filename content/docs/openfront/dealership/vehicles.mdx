## Bring a unit in

<Steps>
<Step>
### One step
`intakeInventoryVehicle` creates the vehicle, stock, and listing in one command.
</Step>
<Step>
### Or through an acquisition
`startInventoryAcquisition`, then `transitionInventoryAcquisition` through appraised, approved, purchased, arrived, reconditioning, frontline (or cancelled), then `establishInventoryStock`.
</Step>
<Step>
### Trade-ins
A trade-in can be sent to inventory as an acquisition. See [Trade-ins](/docs/openfront/dealership/trade-ins).
</Step>
</Steps>

## Keep the unit current

- `moveInventoryStock`: location moves
- `recordInventoryCost`: acquisition and recon costs
- `openInventoryReconWorkItem`, `transitionInventoryReconWorkItem`: open, in-progress, completed, cancelled
- `updateInventoryVehicleDetails`: identity and spec edits
- `addInventoryVehiclePhoto`, `arrangeInventoryVehiclePhotos`, `removeInventoryVehiclePhoto`. Image URLs must start with https:// or /. "Photos changed; reload and try again"
- Condition report and vehicle history are edited on the vehicle page (`VehicleConditionReport`, `VehicleHistory`). Condition must be new, used, or certified.

## Merchandising

`updateInventoryMerchandisingDraft`, `submitInventoryMerchandising`, then `decideInventoryMerchandising` approves or rejects the version. A vehicle appears on the storefront only when its current revision is approved, recon is complete, it is frontline, and its status is `available`. Price changes append `PriceHistory`.

## Vehicle status

`available`, `pending` (reserved when a deal is submitted), `sold` (on delivery), `service`.

## Feed

`recordInventoryFeedRun` records a run; `/vehicles/feed` lists runs and errors. See [Integrations](/docs/openfront/dealership/integrations).
