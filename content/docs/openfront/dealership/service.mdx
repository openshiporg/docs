## Appointments

Customers book at `/service` (`submitServiceBooking`); staff can add one on the page. `transitionServiceAppointment`: scheduled, checked-in, in-progress, waiting-parts, completed, cancelled. Bookings allow 4 per rooftop per hour.

## Repair orders

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open
`openServiceOrder` from the appointment.
</Step>
<Step>
### Record the story
`recordServiceOrderEvidence`: concern, cause, correction, authorization.
</Step>
<Step>
### Add lines
`addServiceOrderLineItem`, `updateServiceOrderLineItem`, `removeServiceOrderLineItem` for labor, part, and sublet. `manageLaborOperation` runs labor operations. `setServiceOrderTax`.
</Step>
<Step>
### Move it through
`transitionServiceOrder`: estimate, approved, in-progress, completed, invoiced, paid. "Invoice number is required before invoicing".
</Step>
</Steps>
