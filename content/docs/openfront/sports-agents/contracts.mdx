Two screens: the list at `/dashboard/platform/contracts` and the record at `/dashboard/platform/contracts/[id]`.

## List

Filter by status: `negotiating`, `signed`, `active`, `completed`, `terminated`, or `released`. Each row shows athlete, team, seasons, type, and total value.

## Record

The record shows the terms (type, total value, guaranteed, signing bonus, commission rate, signed date), the contract years, and the incentives.

| Action | Shown when | Command |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Record signed | Status is `negotiating` | `recordContractSigned` |
| Generate payment schedule | Status is `signed` or `active` | `generatePaymentSchedule` |

## Signing rules

`recordContractSigned` needs a linked agreement that:

- belongs to the same athlete;
- has scope `playing` or `both`;
- is `signed`, `filed_with_union`, or `active`, and in its effective dates;
- has a playing rate at or below its cap.

On success it snapshots the agreement's playing rate into `PlayingContract.commissionBps` and sets `signedAt`. Later changes to the agreement do not change a signed contract.

## Contract years and incentives

A `ContractYear` has a season, base salary, guaranteed amount, roster and workout bonuses, `payPeriods`, and a `paySchedule` (`game_checks`, `semi_monthly`, or `lump_sum`). An `Incentive` has a description, amount, `likelyToBeEarned`, and a status (`pending`, `earned`, `not_earned`). There is no screen or command that earns an incentive, and incentives do not generate payments.

## Payment schedule

`generatePaymentSchedule` splits each year's base, roster, and workout amounts across `payPeriods`, spreading any remainder across the first checks. It writes one `ScheduledPayment` per period with a unique `sourceKey`, so running it twice never duplicates a period. Due dates start on the first of November of the season and step weekly. The schedule is a plan. It does not read the team's real pay dates.
