To create a custom Sports Agents storefront, copy the skill and give it to your LLM. The skill will explain that a storefront starts from the Keystone schema and access rules, with routes added under `app/` and screens under `features/storefront`.

After that source orientation, it will ask what you dislike, what should stay, what workflows and users matter, and what the replacement should look and feel like. It then traces the exact routes, GraphQL schema, access rules, sessions, and provider boundaries in the supplied Sports Agents codebase before proposing work.

Skill file: [`/skills/openfront-sports-agents-custom-storefront/SKILL.md`](/skills/openfront-sports-agents-custom-storefront/SKILL.md)

## Public routes

See [Public routes](/docs/openfront/sports-agents/storefront) for what answers without a session. A storefront needs public data (an access rule that exposes only what customers may see), customer identity (sign-up creates a `User` with no role), and routes and UI.
