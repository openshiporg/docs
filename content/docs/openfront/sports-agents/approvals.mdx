The page at `/dashboard/platform/approvals` lists approval requests. An approval is single use, tied to one action and one target, and must be decided by a different person than the requester.

## Actions

| Action type | Used for | Requester needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `exclusivity_override` | Activating a deal that clashes with another | `canManageEndorsements` |
| `commission_writeoff` | Writing off an invoice balance | `canManageCommissions` |
| `refund_payment` | Reserved. No command uses it | `canIssueRefunds` |

Deciding an approval needs `canApproveActions`.

## Flow

1. The requester files a request with a reason: **Request override** on a blocked deal, or **Write off** on an invoice. The request is stored with a fingerprint of the action, target, amount, and reason.
2. A different user with `canApproveActions` opens Approvals and chooses **Approve** or **Reject**. Requesters cannot decide their own request. Requests expire after one hour.
3. The requester returns to Approvals and uses the approved request: **Activate deal** or **Write off**.
4. The command consumes the approval inside its own transaction. The status becomes `consumed`.

## Statuses

`requested` (shown as pending), `approved`, `rejected`, and `consumed`. The screen groups them as Waiting, Approved not yet used, and Decided and used.

## What consumption checks

- The approval's action and target match the request.
- The fingerprint matches. Changing the reason or amount after approval is refused.
- The user running the command is the original requester.
- The approver is a different user.
- It is still `approved` and not expired.
- It has not been used. A second use is refused.

## Exclusivity override

`activateEndorsementDeal(dealId, approvalId)` finds the clashing deals under the athlete lock. With a valid approval it consumes it, writes a `deal.exclusivity_overridden` audit event that lists the conflicting deal ids, and activates the deal. Without one it keeps the block.

## Write-off

`writeOffCommissionInvoice(invoiceId, reason, approvalId)` always needs an approval. The invoice must be `sent` or `partially_paid` with a balance. It posts a `write_off` ledger entry for minus the unpaid balance, moves the invoice to `written_off`, and writes an audit event. The approval fingerprint includes the balance, so the amount cannot change after approval.

## Roles

The Owner and Accountant seed roles have `canApproveActions`. The setup seed does not create an approver separate from the requester, so create a second user before you try an approval.
