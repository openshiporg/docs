---
title: Override an exclusivity clash
description: Request an override, have a second person approve it, and activate the deal.
icon: Stamp
---

import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

You need two users. The requester needs `canManageEndorsements`. The approver needs `canApproveActions`.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Find the blocked deal
Open `/dashboard/platform/endorsements`. A deal that clashes shows **Request override** where **Activate** would be.
</Step>
<Step>
### Request the override
Choose **Request override**, give a reason, and submit. The request waits on `/dashboard/platform/approvals`.
</Step>
<Step>
### Approve as another user
A different user opens Approvals and chooses **Approve**. You cannot approve your own request.
</Step>
<Step>
### Activate the deal
The requester opens Approvals and chooses **Activate deal** on the approved request. The approval is consumed and the deal becomes `active`.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Check

The audit log has a `deal.exclusivity_overridden` event that lists the conflicting deals. The approval shows as consumed and cannot be used again. Requests expire after one hour.
