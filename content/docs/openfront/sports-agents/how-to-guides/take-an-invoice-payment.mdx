title: Take an invoice payment
description: An athlete pays a sent invoice by card through the public invoice page.
icon: CreditCard
---

import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

You need an installed provider with code `pp_stripe_stripe` and a `sent` invoice.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Set the keys
Put your Stripe publishable key in `NEXT_PUBLIC_STRIPE_KEY` and secret key in `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY` in `.env`, then restart.
</Step>
<Step>
### Find the invoice
Open `/invoice`. Enter the athlete's email and the invoice number.
</Step>
<Step>
### Pay
The invoice page shows the lines and balance with a Stripe card form. The athlete enters their card and pays the balance.
</Step>
<Step>
### Read the confirmation
The confirmation page shows the invoice number as the code. In the dashboard, the invoice is `paid` and the Ledger tab has a receipt and split entries.
</Step>
</Steps>

## If it fails

See the invoice payment rows in [Troubleshooting](/docs/openfront/sports-agents/troubleshooting).
