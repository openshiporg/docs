title: Invoice a commission
description: Mark a payment received, create the invoice, send it, and record the payment.

import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

You need `canManageCommissions`.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Mark the athlete's payment received
When the athlete is paid, open `/dashboard/platform/commissions` and choose **Mark received** on the payment. An accrual entry is posted for `floor(gross x rate / 10000)`.
</Step>
<Step>
### Create the invoice
A **Ready to invoice** block groups an athlete's received payments. Choose **Create invoice**. The invoice starts as a draft with one line per payment.
</Step>
<Step>
### Send it
Open the **Invoices** tab and choose **Send**. The invoice is now `sent` and can be looked up and paid on the public site.
</Step>
<Step>
### Record the payment
When money arrives, choose **Record payment**. A receipt and one split entry per agent are posted. The invoice becomes `paid`.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Fix a mistake

On the **Ledger** tab, **Reverse** a receipt or accrual. The reversal is exact and happens once. Reversing a receipt lowers the invoice's paid amount and reverses its agent splits.
