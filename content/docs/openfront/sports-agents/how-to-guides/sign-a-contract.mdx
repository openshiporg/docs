---
title: Sign a contract
description: Sign an agreement, record a contract as signed, and generate its payment schedule.
icon: FileCheck
---

import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

You need `canManageContracts` for the first two steps and `canManageCommissions` for the schedule.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Sign the agreement
Open the athlete at `/dashboard/platform/clients`, go to the **Agreements** tab, and choose **Record signed** on the agreement. The playing rate must be at or below the cap. Another signed or active agreement for the same scope blocks it.
</Step>
<Step>
### Link the agreement to the contract
Open the contract in the generic list at `/dashboard/playingContracts` and set `representationAgreement` to the signed agreement. The agreement scope must be `playing` or `both`.
</Step>
<Step>
### Record the contract as signed
Open the contract at `/dashboard/platform/contracts/[id]` and choose **Record signed**. The agreement's playing rate is copied onto the contract.
</Step>
<Step>
### Generate the payment schedule
On the same page choose **Generate payment schedule**. One scheduled payment is created per pay period. Running it again adds nothing.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Check

The athlete's **Payments** tab lists the scheduled payments. The money view shows them under expected commission.
