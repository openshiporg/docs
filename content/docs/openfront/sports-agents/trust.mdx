Use trust when the agency collects endorsement money for an athlete. The screen at `/dashboard/platform/trust` is read-only. It lists each `ClientTrustAccount` with its balance and the latest 50 entries. The athlete record's Trust tab shows the same entries.

## Entries

`ClientTrustEntry.entryType` is one of:

| Type | Sign | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `deposit` | plus | Money received for the athlete |
| `commission_transfer` | minus | Commission moved against a sent invoice |
| `disbursement_to_client` | minus | Money paid out to the athlete |
| `reversal` | opposite | Exact reversal of an earlier entry |

## Rules

`postTrustEntry` enforces:

- The balance never goes below zero.
- A `commission_transfer` needs a `sent` or `partially_paid` invoice for the same athlete and currency, and the total transferred cannot exceed the invoice total.
- A reversal must name an entry in the same account. An entry is reversed once, and a reversal cannot be reversed.
- Every entry needs a reference and a positive whole-cent amount, and takes an idempotency key.

The command needs `canManageTrust` and access to the athlete. The accountant role has `canManageTrust`. A `commission_transfer` does not itself post a receipt on the invoice. Record the receipt separately.

## No screen for posting

The dashboard has no form to post entries. Post them through the GraphQL command or the generic list screens. The seed creates one account with a deposit.
