---
title: Gift Cards
description: The gift card records in the current source and what checkout does with them.
---

Openfront has gift card records and an operator page, but the current checkout does not redeem them.

## Model

`GiftCard` has `code`, `value`, `balance` (integers in minor units), `isDisabled`, `endsAt`, `metadata`, and links to a region, an order, carts and `GiftCardTransaction` records. A transaction has `amount`, `isTaxable`, `taxRate`, and links to the card and an order. Creating gift cards requires `canManageGiftCards`. Products also have an `isGiftcard` flag.

## Operator page

`/dashboard/platform/gift-cards` lists cards with status tabs, shows code, value, balance, expiry and transactions, and can enable or disable a card. Create and edit cards through the list's create drawer or the generic `/dashboard/gift-cards` editor.

Gift card redemption is not supported in the current checkout. `updateActiveCart` rejects any change to `giftCards` with "Gift-card redemption is outside the bounded launch boundary". Nothing in the checkout writes a `GiftCardTransaction` or lowers a balance. Selling a product with `isGiftcard` set does not create a gift card.

The cart still has a computed `giftCardTotal` field and the storefront has gift-card display code. They show nothing because no cart can have gift cards attached through the storefront.
