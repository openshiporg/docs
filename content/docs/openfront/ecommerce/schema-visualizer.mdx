The visualizer is built from a copy of the schema kept in this docs site. The authoritative sources are `features/keystone/models` and the generated `schema.prisma` and `schema.graphql` in the [source repository](https://github.com/openshiporg/openfront). The current source defines 85 Keystone lists.

## Model groups

### Users and access
- **User**, **Role**, **Team**, **UserField** – accounts, role permissions and teams
- **ApiKey**, **OAuthApp**, **OAuthToken** – programmatic access
- **WebhookEndpoint**, **WebhookEvent** – outgoing webhooks
- **IdempotencyKey** – recorded progress for checkout, invoice payment, refunds and webhooks

### Store and markets
- **Store** – the single store's name, logo and homepage text
- **Region**, **Country**, **Currency**, **TaxRate**, **TaxProvider** – where and how you sell
- **SalesChannel** – sales channel records

### Catalog
- **Product**, **ProductVariant**, **ProductOption**, **ProductOptionValue**, **ProductImage**
- **ProductCategory**, **ProductCollection**, **ProductTag**, **ProductType**
- **MoneyAmount**, **PriceSet**, **PriceRule**, **PriceList**, **RuleType** – pricing
- **Location**, **StockMovement**, **Measurement** – stock and dimensions

### Carts and orders
- **Cart**, **LineItem**, **LineItemAdjustment**, **LineItemTaxLine**, **Address**
- **Order**, **OrderLineItem**, **OrderMoneyAmount**, **OrderEvent**, **Note**, **DraftOrder**
- **Discount**, **DiscountRule**, **DiscountCondition**, **CustomerGroup**
- **GiftCard**, **GiftCardTransaction**

### Payments
- **PaymentProvider**, **PaymentCollection**, **PaymentSession**, **Payment**, **Capture**, **Refund**

### Fulfillment and shipping
- **ShippingOption**, **ShippingOptionRequirement**, **ShippingProfile**, **ShippingMethod**, **ShippingMethodTaxLine**, **CustomShippingOption**
- **ShippingProvider**, **ShippingLabel**, **FulfillmentProvider**, **Fulfillment**, **FulfillmentItem**

### After the sale
- **Return**, **ReturnItem**, **ReturnReason**
- **ClaimOrder**, **ClaimItem**, **ClaimImage**, **ClaimTag**
- **Swap** (model only; no workflow uses it)

### Business accounts
- **BusinessAccountRequest**, **Account**, **AccountLineItem**, **Invoice**, **InvoiceLineItem**

### Other
- **Notification**, **NotificationProvider**, **Invite**, **BatchJob**

## Key relationships

1. Catalog: Product → ProductVariant → MoneyAmount, with StockMovement records for stock changes.
2. Checkout: Cart → PaymentCollection → PaymentSession → Order → Payment.
3. Fulfillment: Order → Fulfillment → FulfillmentItem and ShippingLabel.
4. After the sale: Order → Return → Refund, and Order → ClaimOrder.
5. Business accounts: BusinessAccountRequest → Account → AccountLineItem → Invoice.
