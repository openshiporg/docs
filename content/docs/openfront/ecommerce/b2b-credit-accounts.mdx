Openfront lets a signed-in customer apply for a business account. Once approved, the customer can check out on credit, group unpaid orders into invoices, pay them, and use a customer token to order through the API.

## Workflow

<Steps>
<Step>
### Request
On `/[countryCode]/account/invoicing`, a signed-in customer submits a `BusinessAccountRequest` with business name, type (`wholesale`, `distribution`, `reseller`, `b2b_platform`, `other`), monthly order volume, requested credit limit and a description.
</Step>
<Step>
### Review
Staff with `canManageOrders` review requests at `/dashboard/platform/business-account-requests`. Status is `pending`, `approved`, `not_approved` or `requires_info`. Staff can add review notes and an approved credit limit.
</Step>
<Step>
### Account creation
When a request is set to `approved`, a hook creates an active `Account` of type `business` for the user, in the store's default currency, with the approved credit limit (or 100000 minor units if none is given). The account is linked back to the request.
</Step>
<Step>
### Customer token
A customer with an active business account creates a token from the invoicing page with `regenerateCustomerToken`. It returns a `ctok_` token once and stores only a digest. The token works as a `Bearer` credential for that user. It expires after `CUSTOMER_TOKEN_MAX_AGE_DAYS` (default 90). It does not grant dashboard access.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Ordering on account

`completeActiveCart` called without a `paymentSessionId` treats the order as an account order. It needs a signed-in user with an active business account. The cart currency must match the account currency. The order total must fit within the available credit (`creditLimit` minus unpaid balance), checked under a row lock. The order is recorded as an `AccountLineItem` with an unpaid status.

## Invoices

- `getUnpaidLineItemsByRegion(accountId)` lists unpaid account lines grouped by region.
- `createInvoiceFromLineItems(accountId, regionId, lineItemIds, dueDate)` creates an `Invoice` in `sent` status from selected lines.
- `createInvoicePaymentSessions`, `initiateInvoicePaymentSession`, `setInvoicePaymentSession` and `completeInvoicePayment` pay an invoice through Stripe or PayPal. Only `sent` or `overdue` invoices can be paid. The provider amount must match the invoice total. Retries resume from the recorded step.
- `getCustomerPaidInvoices` lists paid invoices for `/account/invoices`.

Staff see business accounts and their invoices at `/dashboard/platform/invoices`.

<Callout type="warn">
- Accounts only support one currency. Cross-currency account orders and invoices are refused.
- Manual invoice tender is refused. No screen records an offline invoice payment.
- A customer token can register a webhook with `registerWebhookEndpoint` only for the `fulfillment.created` event. Other events are refused for customer tokens.
</Callout>
