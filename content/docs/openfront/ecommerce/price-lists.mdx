---
title: Price Lists
description: Alternate variant prices with a status and an optional date window.
---

A `PriceList` groups alternate prices. Operators manage them at `/dashboard/platform/price-lists`.

## Model

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `name`, `description` | Labels |
| `type` | `sale` or `override` |
| `status` | `active` or `draft` |
| `startsAt`, `endsAt` | Optional window |
| `moneyAmounts` | The prices in this list. Each `MoneyAmount` has an amount, currency, optional region, variant, and optional `minQuantity` and `maxQuantity`. |
| `customerGroups` | Customer groups linked to the list |

`PriceSet` and `PriceRule` add more pricing structure. A price rule has a `type` (`fixed` or `percentage`), a `value`, a `priority`, and `ruleAttribute` and `ruleValue` fields.

## How a price is calculated

`MoneyAmount.calculatedPrice` does the work:

1. If the money amount belongs to a price list that is `active` and inside its date window, that list is reported as applied.
2. If the money amount belongs to a price set, the lowest valid price in the same currency is used. Prices tied to an inactive or out-of-window list are skipped.
3. The set's price rules run from highest priority down. `fixed` caps the amount at the rule value; `percentage` takes that percentage off.

The result includes `calculatedAmount`, `originalAmount`, `priceListId` and `priceListType`. The storefront product queries read these fields.

- `calculatedPrice` does not check the shopper's customer group, so linking a list to customer groups does not limit who sees the price.
- `PriceRule.ruleAttribute` and `ruleValue` are stored but not evaluated.
- `minQuantity` and `maxQuantity` are read but not compared with the cart quantity.
- Cart lines pick the first money amount for the variant in the cart's region. Test which price a cart actually uses before relying on a list.

## When to use it

Use a price list for a scheduled sale or an alternate price. Use [discounts](/docs/openfront/ecommerce/dashboard) for codes and promotions. Do not rely on price lists for wholesale or member-only pricing until group eligibility is enforced.
