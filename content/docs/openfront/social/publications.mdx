---
title: Publications
description: Every published version and consent record.
icon: History
---

Routes: `/dashboard/platform/history`, `/history/[id]`.

A `Publication` is an immutable snapshot of an item (title, slug, summary, body, url, type) with `snapshotProvenance`, `destination` (`personal_site`, `x`, `instagram`, `threads`), `status` (`queued`, `published`, `not_configured`, `failed`, `cancelled`), and `revision`. The page has destination and status tabs. A detail view shows the snapshot, the consent, and `PublishJob` attempts (`status`, `attempts`, `maxAttempts`, `errorMessage`).

Cancel a request that was never dispatched with `cancelExternal`. Publications for the personal site are created by `publishItem`; one canonical publication exists per item at a time.
