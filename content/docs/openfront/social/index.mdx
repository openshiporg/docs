Openfront Social is a self-hosted personal publishing application. A creator signs in to a studio, sets up one personal site, organizes work into collections, drafts items, and publishes versions to a public site at `/<handle>`. Visitors read published profiles, collections, items, and an RSS feed. People managers add creators and review privacy requests.

External destinations (X, Instagram, Threads) are recorded as per-item consent. Visitors can support a creator with tips and memberships through Stripe Checkout.

## Who it is for

- **Creators** who want a site they control for notes, writing, runs, projects, albums, films, books, links, or custom work.
- **Operators** (people managers) who host the application for one or more creators, add creator accounts when public sign-up is closed, and decide privacy requests.
- **Developers** who want a small, owner-scoped Keystone and Next.js publishing codebase to extend.

## What the codebase contains

| Area | Location | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Schema and workflows | `features/keystone/models`, `features/keystone/mutations` | Fourteen Keystone lists and the GraphQL commands, public reads, and privacy queue |
| Operator and creator dashboard | `features/platform`, `app/dashboard/(admin)/platform/**` | Items, collections, site, publications, destinations, activity, your data, creators, privacy requests |
| Admin shell | `features/dashboard` | Authentication, first-user init, generic list and item screens |
| Public site | `features/personal-site`, `app/[handle]/**`, `app/feeds/[handle]` | Profile, collection, and item pages, RSS, sitemap, themes, and logos |
| Landing and account | `features/storefront`, `app/page.tsx`, `app/account/**` | Product landing page, creator sign-in, and sign-up when it is open |
| Onboarding | `features/platform/onboarding` | Theme choice and a starter site from `seed.json`, or a blank site |

The schema map in the source repository (`docs/schema-map.md`) lists every list, field, and workflow; [Data model](/docs/openfront/social/data-model) summarizes it.

## Ownership and tenancy

There is no organization or tenant model. The ownership boundary is the creator: each `User` owns at most one `PersonalSite`, and every collection, item, publication, publish job, connected account, and media asset is scoped through that site to its owner. Access rules in `features/keystone/access.ts` filter all of these lists to the signed-in owner, generic create, update, and delete are closed, and every write goes through the `studioCommand` mutation. Public pages use separate sudo queries that return only published, exposable content.
