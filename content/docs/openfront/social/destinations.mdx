Routes: `/dashboard/platform/destinations`, `/destinations/[id]`.

A destination is a `ConnectedAccount`: `provider` (`x`, `instagram`, `threads`), `accountLabel`, and `status` (`unconfigured`, `pending_connection`, `connected`, `revoked`, `error`).

<Steps>
<Step>
### Add a label
`addDestination` records the account label. Edit it with `updateDestinationLabel`.
</Step>
<Step>
### Record consent
From a published item, `requestExternal` records your consent for the live version. It needs an idempotency key and the reviewed site, collection, canonical publication, and account versions. It creates a `Publication` with `not_configured` status and a `PublishJob`.
</Step>
<Step>
### Cancel or revoke
`cancelExternal` cancels one request. `revokeDestination` blocks new requests and cancels undispatched ones.
</Step>
</Steps>

Consent records are bound to a specific revision, so later edits need new consent. See [Integrations](/docs/openfront/social/integrations).
