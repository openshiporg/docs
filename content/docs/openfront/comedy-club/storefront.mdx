The storefront is deliberately plain. It calls only the custom GraphQL operations; the raw lists are not public.

| Route | What guests do |
|---|---|
| `/` | Upcoming shows (`showsOnSale`) |
| `/shows/[id]` | Lineup, tier or table choice, quantity, mixed tiers for a group (`showAvailability`), then a hold |
| `/checkout?hold=` | Name, email and phone, promo code, age confirmation, then Stripe payment with a hold timer |
| `/orders/[orderNumber]` | Confirmation with ticket codes and QR codes, self-cancel inside the refund window, ticket transfer |
| `/tickets/[code]` | One ticket for its holder (`holderTicket`) |
| `/lookup` | Find an order by email and order number (`lookupTicketOrder`) |
| `/open-mic` | Sign up for an open mic night |
| `/waitlist/claim` | Claim a waitlist offer |
| `/lineup/respond` | Comedians accept or decline a lineup offer |

## Checkout

`startTicketCheckout` freezes the quote and creates a Stripe payment. The browser confirms the card. `completeTicketCheckout` turns the hold into tickets only after the provider confirms the payment. If the browser never comes back, the signed webhook and the hold sweeper finish the order.
