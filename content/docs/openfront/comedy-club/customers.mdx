---
title: Customers
description: Buyers grouped by email, from paid orders.
icon: Users
---

Route: `/dashboard/platform/customers`.

This is a read-only roll-up. It reads the newest 500 orders that are `paid`, `partially_refunded`, or `comp`, groups them by lower-cased email, and shows each buyer's name, email, total spent, order count, and guest count. Buyers are listed by total spent.

Guests buy without an account, so there is no customer list model. The guest's email and name live on each order. If a buyer was signed in when they checked out, the order is also linked to their `User`.

The `User` list holds dashboard users. Manage them at `/dashboard/users` with `canManagePeople`, and roles at `/dashboard/roles` with `canManageRoles`.

The `notificationPreference` checkbox on `User` is stored for later use. Nothing sends reminders yet.
