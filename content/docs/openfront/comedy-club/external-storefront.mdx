To create a custom Comedy Club storefront, copy the skill and give it to your LLM. The skill explains how `features/storefront` and the routes under `app/(storefront)` implement show discovery, holds, checkout, orders and tickets.

After that source orientation, it asks what you dislike, what should stay, which journeys matter, and what the replacement should look like. It then traces the exact routes, GraphQL operations, schema, access rules and payment handoff in your checkout before proposing work.

Skill file: [`/skills/openfront-comedy-club-custom-storefront/SKILL.md`](/skills/openfront-comedy-club-custom-storefront/SKILL.md)

A separate client must keep the server as the authority for price, capacity, holds and payment state. Use the named operations listed in the [API reference](/docs/openfront/comedy-club/api-reference); never write to the raw lists.
