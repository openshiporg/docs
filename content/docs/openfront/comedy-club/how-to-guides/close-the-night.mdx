Every check must be closed. Open ones block the close.
</Step>
<Step>
### Settle payments and refunds
No order may be `pending_payment`, no hold may be active, and no refund may be `processing` or `unknown`.
</Step>
<Step>
### Close the show
On the show page, press *Close night*. It needs `canManagePayouts`.
</Step>
</Steps>

## What happens

- Every confirmed slot becomes `performed`.
- One draft payout is created for each comedian who performed. The amount is the sum of their slot fees and bonuses.
- The settlement is written: tickets, fees, discounts, refunds, food and drink, minimum shortfall, tips, talent cost, and net. See [Payouts](/docs/openfront/comedy-club/payouts).
- The show becomes `settled`.
- If the show is in a headliner run, the run moves from `scheduled` to `in_progress`.

A show can only be closed once. A closed settlement is frozen.

For the headliner of a run, the show payout holds only bonuses. The run fee is paid when the run closes. See [Close a headliner run](/docs/openfront/comedy-club/how-to-guides/close-a-headliner-run).
