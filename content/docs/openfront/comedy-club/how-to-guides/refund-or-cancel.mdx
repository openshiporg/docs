## Refund an order

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open the order
Go to `/dashboard/platform/orders` and open the order.
</Step>
<Step>
### Pick tickets
Tap tickets to refund some. Choose none to refund every remaining valid ticket. Checked-in tickets cannot be refunded.
</Step>
<Step>
### Reason and approval
Enter a reason. If the amount is above the venue threshold (default $100.00), a second user must approve first. See [Roles and permissions](/docs/openfront/comedy-club/roles-permissions). Paste the approval ID.
</Step>
<Step>
### Refund
Press *Refund*. The refund goes to Stripe. The order, tickets, and capacity change only when Stripe confirms.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Guest cancel

A guest can cancel at `/lookup` with their email and order number until `refundWindowHours` (default 24) before the show. It refunds the whole order.

## Cancel a show

Press *Cancel show* on the show page. It needs `canManageShows` and `canIssueRefunds`. The show is cancelled, its slots are cancelled, holds with no order are released, and every paid order is refunded in full. The result counts refunds that worked and refunds that failed. Open a failed order and refund it again.

## If the answer is unclear

If Stripe's answer is unclear, the refund shows `unknown` and nothing else changes. Check Stripe first, then try again.
