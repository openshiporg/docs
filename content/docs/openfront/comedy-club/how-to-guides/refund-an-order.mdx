---
title: Refund an order
description: Refund a whole order or chosen tickets, with approval when needed.
icon: Undo2
---

import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

<Steps>
<Step>

### Find the order

Open **Orders** and open the order.

</Step>
<Step>

### Choose what to refund

Refund the whole order, an amount, or chosen tickets, and enter a reason.

</Step>
<Step>

### Get approval if asked

If the amount is above the approval threshold, the request goes to **Approvals**. Another user with `canIssueRefunds` approves it.

</Step>
<Step>

### Confirm

The refund goes back to the card through Stripe. The order shows `refunded` or `partially_refunded` and the refunded tickets no longer scan.

</Step>
</Steps>
