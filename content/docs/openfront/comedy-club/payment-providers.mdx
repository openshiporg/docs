Route: `/dashboard/platform/payment-providers`. Needs `canManagePayments`.

Setup creates two providers: **Stripe** (`pp_stripe`) for cards and **Manual** for cash or comps at the door. The adapters live in `features/integrations/payment`.

## Stripe

1. Set `NEXT_PUBLIC_STRIPE_KEY` and `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY`.
2. Create a webhook endpoint in Stripe at `https://your-host/api/integrations/payment/pp_stripe/webhook` with `payment_intent.succeeded`, `payment_intent.payment_failed`, `payment_intent.canceled`, `charge.refunded` and `refund.updated`.
3. Put the endpoint's signing secret in `STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET`.

The webhook verifies the signature, stores each event once in `PaymentWebhookEvent`, and completes or fails the matching checkout. Refunds go back through the provider that took the payment, with a stable idempotency key.

Use test keys and the card `4242 4242 4242 4242` until a test purchase and refund both work.
