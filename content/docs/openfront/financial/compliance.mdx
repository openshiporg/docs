`/dashboard/platform/compliance` shows KYC checks, risk profiles, cases, alerts and audit history.

## Transitions

`transitionComplianceRecord` moves a record with a reason and appends a `ComplianceReview`.

| Record | Transitions |
|---|---|
| `KycCheck` | `pending` → `manual_review`, `passed`, `failed`; `manual_review` → `passed`, `failed` |
| `RiskProfile` | `draft` → `submitted`; `submitted` → `approved` or `rejected`; `approved` or `rejected` → `superseded` |
| `ComplianceCase` | `open` → `investigating` (with assignee) → `closed` |

Approving a risk profile can set options and margin permissions.

## Effect on trading

A failed or expired KYC check, or a rejected or superseded risk profile, blocks new orders.

## Four eyes

The reviewer cannot be the user who requested the change.
