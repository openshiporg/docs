---
title: Advisory
description: Trade recommendations, advisor rules, public profile and followed traders.
icon: Lightbulb
---

`/dashboard/platform/advisory` has:

- **Recommendations:** `processTradeRecommendation` approves (which places an order) or rejects. `Recommendation needs a suggested quantity before execution.` and `Recommendation cannot be processed from <status> state.` apply.
- **Advisor rules:** edit `AIAdvisorConfig`, including the maximum position size that `placeFinancialOrder` enforces.
- **Public profile:** edit `PublicProfileSettings`. When enabled, `/api/public/trades?userId=...&since=...` returns filled paper orders after the profile's trade delay.
- **Followed traders:** pause, resume or remove `FollowedTrader` records.

Requires `Current human advisory-operation permission is required.`
