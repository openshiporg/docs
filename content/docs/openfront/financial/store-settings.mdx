`/dashboard/platform/store-settings` edits `PlatformSettings` through `upsertPlatformSettings`: firm name, tagline, logo, colour, currency and the paper-trading flag.

The portal reads these through `publicFinancialIdentity`. Until settings exist the portal shows an unconfigured screen. The paper-trading disclosure on the portal comes from this record.
