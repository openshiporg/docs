`/dashboard/platform/ledger` shows the chart of accounts with balances and journals by type and status. `/ledger/[id]` shows postings and linked reversals.

## Structure

- `LedgerAccount`: an account in the chart. Inactive or different-currency accounts are refused (`Ledger account is inactive or uses a different currency.`).
- `LedgerJournal`: a balanced double-entry record.
- `LedgerPosting`: one debit or credit line.
- `LedgerEntry`: the client-facing activity line shown in the portal.

## Rules

- 2 to 100 postings per journal.
- Debits equal credits; one currency (`Ledger journal postings must be balanced: total debits must equal total credits.`).
- Amounts are positive decimals with at most four fractional digits.
- Direction is `debit` or `credit`; currency is a three-letter ISO code.
- `Journal occurredAt must be a valid timestamp.`

Every lifecycle mutation posts through `ledgerLifecycle`. A reversal links to the original journal.
