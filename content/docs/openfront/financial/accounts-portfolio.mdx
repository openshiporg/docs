## Portfolio

`/dashboard/platform/portfolio` shows holdings and account balances with stale-account flags. Values are recorded snapshots with an as-of time and coverage state.

## Accounts

`/dashboard/platform/accounts` shows connections, brokerage balances, funding sources, broker adapters and market-data sync runs. `/accounts/[id]` shows balances, positions, orders, transfers, statements and ledger lines, plus provider observation review.

## Records

- `BrokerageAccount` and `Position` carry virtual live-value fields.
- `PortfolioSnapshot` stores history for charts.
- `BankAccount` holds funding sources.
- `BrokerageConnection` stores an opaque `credentialReference` that GraphQL never returns.

## Connect a provider

1. In Tasks, **Connections**, use `provisionInstitutionConnection` with scopes and a consent policy version.
2. Grant consent with `recordConnectionConsent`.
3. Store the paper credentials in the environment and set the reference to `env://ALPACA_PAPER`.
4. Run `syncInstitutionConnection` (needs `canManageIntegrations`).

The sync reads accounts, positions and 90 days of activity and calls `importProviderSnapshot`. See [Integrations](/docs/openfront/financial/integrations).
