`/dashboard/platform/orders` shows the order queue with status counts and recent fills and trades. `/orders/[id]` shows terms, fills, trades and ledger lines.

## Placing an order

`placeFinancialOrder` runs these checks:

- The caller owns the brokerage account (`Brokerage account not found.`).
- KYC and risk profile are in good standing; a failed or expired KYC check, or a rejected or superseded risk profile, blocks orders.
- Margin permission, excluded symbols and the advisor's maximum position size.
- Prices: market orders cannot have limit or stop prices (`Market orders cannot contain limit or stop prices.`); limit and stop-limit orders need a limit price (`Limit price is required for limit and stop-limit orders.`); limit orders cannot have a stop price. Side is buy or sell and quantity above zero.
- Buying power (`Not enough buying power for this order.`) and, for sells, the position (`Insufficient <symbol> position to sell <qty>.`).

The portal offers market and limit orders.

## Approval

If an active `ApprovalPolicy` for `order` covers the amount, the order is refused until `requestOrderApproval` creates an `ApprovalRequest` and a different person approves it. See [Approvals and tasks](/docs/openfront/financial/approvals-tasks).

## Fill

Market orders fill by local simulation. Open orders fill with **Simulate fill** (`simulateOrderFill`), which accepts a quantity and price.

- `Fill quantity must be greater than zero and no more than remaining quantity <n>.`
- `Fill price violates the accepted limit.`
- `Insufficient buying power for simulated fill amount <amount>.`
- `Order cannot be filled from <status> state.`

A fill writes `OrderFill` and `Trade`, updates `Position` and posts `LedgerEntry` rows with a balanced `LedgerJournal`.

## Cancel

`cancelFinancialOrder` cancels an open order. Otherwise: `Order cannot be cancelled from <status> state.`

Machine sessions cannot fill directly. Agents propose an `order.place` action instead.
