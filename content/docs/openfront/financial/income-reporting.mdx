## Income

`/dashboard/platform/income` shows dividends, open and closed tax lots, and account statements.

## Reporting

`/dashboard/platform/reporting` shows portfolio snapshots and ledger activity.

## Export a month

`GET /api/platform/statements?month=YYYY-MM` exports ledger entries and snapshots as CSV for one month, up to 1000 rows. The reporting page links to it.

## Investors

Investors read their statements, dividends and tax lots at `/statements`, and ledger entries at `/activity`.
