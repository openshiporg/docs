## Scope

There is no firm or tenant model. Records belong to a user, and pages read through the operator's session. An operator sees their own connections, accounts, orders and transfers. KYC checks, risk profiles and compliance cases are visible across users.

## Writes

Pages call named mutations; each refuses with a message you can show to the operator. Generic lists such as `User`, `Role`, `Watchlist` and `PriceHistory` open at `/dashboard/<list-key>`.

See [Roles and permissions](/docs/openfront/financial/roles-permissions).
