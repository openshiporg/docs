---
title: Place and fill an order
description: Place a limit order and fill it from the console.
---

import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

<Steps>
<Step>
### Place
At `/trade` choose the account, symbol, side, quantity and limit price.
</Step>
<Step>
### Approve
For orders over the policy threshold, request approval and have someone else approve.
</Step>
<Step>
### Fill
In Orders open the order and choose Simulate fill with quantity and price.
</Step>
<Step>
### Check the books
Open the order to see the fill, trade and ledger lines.
</Step>
</Steps>
