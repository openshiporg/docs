---
title: Fund an account
description: Verify a bank, request a deposit and settle it.
---

import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

<Steps>
<Step>
### Link the bank
The investor links a bank in `/account` and confirms the two micro-deposit amounts.
</Step>
<Step>
### Request
At `/funding` the investor requests a deposit.
</Step>
<Step>
### Approve if required
If a transfer policy covers the amount, request approval and have a third person approve it.
</Step>
<Step>
### Settle
In Transfers choose complete. The cash balance and ledger update.
</Step>
</Steps>
