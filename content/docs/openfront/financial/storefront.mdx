| Route | Journey |
|---|---|
| `/` | Market board, and for signed-in users recorded totals and recent activity |
| `/markets`, `/markets/[symbol]` | Quote list and asset detail with price chart |
| `/trade` | Place a market or limit order (`placeFinancialOrder`) |
| `/orders`, `/orders/[id]` | History and detail with fills, trades and cancel |
| `/portfolio` | Accounts, positions and snapshots (`ownedPortfolio`) |
| `/funding` | Request a deposit or withdrawal |
| `/watchlist` | Add or remove symbols |
| `/activity` | Ledger entries |
| `/statements` | Statements, dividends and tax lots |
| `/account` | Sign in, sign up, password reset; profile, password and bank accounts |

Sign-up works when `PUBLIC_SIGNUPS_ALLOWED=true`. The portal signs in with the `keystonejs-session` cookie.

## Bank accounts

Link a bank, verify two micro-deposit amounts, set a primary account or deactivate. The amounts are generated by the app and stored as a hash.

## Messages

Show the server's message when an order is refused by an approval policy, KYC, risk profile, margin, excluded symbols or maximum position size.
