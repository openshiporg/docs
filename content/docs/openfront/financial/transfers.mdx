`/dashboard/platform/transfers` is the transfer queue. `/transfers/[id]` shows the bank, brokerage account, timeline and ledger lines.

## Flow

1. The investor links a bank with `linkOwnedBankAccount` and verifies it with `verifyOwnedBankAccount`.
2. `requestFinancialTransfer` creates a pending deposit or withdrawal between an owned active bank and a brokerage account.
3. An operator with `canOperateFinancial` settles it with `processFinancialTransfer` as `complete`, `fail` or `reverse`.

Settlement posts ledger entries and journals and changes the recorded cash balance.

## Rules and messages

- `Only paper-bank transfer records can be processed.`
- `Not enough cash for this withdrawal.` and `Completing this withdrawal would create a negative cash balance.`
- `Only completed transfers can be reversed. Current state: <status>.`
- `An action-bound reversal approval is required. Completion approval cannot authorize reversal.`
- `Current human settlement authority is required.`

## Approvals

When an active `transfer` policy covers the amount, completion needs an approved, unexpired `ApprovalRequest` decided by someone other than the requester and the settling operator. Create it with `requestTransferApproval`.

## Idempotency

Mutations need a stable idempotency key of 120 characters or fewer.
