---

Route: `/dashboard/platform/property`. Needs `canManageOnboarding`. Saved with `updateHotelPropertySettings` into the `HotelSettings` singleton.

| Section | What it sets |
| --- | --- |
| Details and contact | Name, address, phone, email |
| Stay times | Check-in and check-out times |
| Tax and fees | Tax rate and fee rules used in quotes |
| Deposit | Deposit applied at booking |
| Loyalty | Earning and redemption rules |
| Groups | Defaults for blocks |
| Approvals | Thresholds that require a second user |
| Storefront | Logo and media shown on the guest site |

Public values are read by the storefront through `publicHotelSettings`.

## Other pages

- `/dashboard/platform/analytics`: occupancy, ADR, RevPAR, posted revenue, tax, payments, refunds, and folio exposure for a date range (`hotelAnalyticsOperations`). Needs `canManageBookings`.
- `/dashboard/platform/outbox`: guest message and refund delivery status, with replay (`replayHotelOutboxEvent`). Needs `canManageAudit`.
- `/dashboard/platform/security`: TOTP second factor and recovery codes for your own account.
