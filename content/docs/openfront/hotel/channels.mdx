Route: `/dashboard/platform/channels`. Needs `canManageBookings` and `canManageIntegrations` (`hotelChannelOperations`).

A `Channel` stores its mode in its credentials: `disabled` or `live`. Only `live` channels send requests.

## Setup

1. Save a draft with `saveHotelChannelDraft`: name, HTTPS endpoints, `allowedOrigins`, and the signing secret.
2. Switch the channel to `live`.
3. Push inventory with `pushInventoryToChannel`, or pull reservations with `pullReservationsFromChannel`.
4. Retry failures with `retryFailedChannelSyncs`.

Every attempt is a `ChannelSyncEvent`. Imported reservations are `ChannelReservation` rows linked to bookings.

## Inbound webhook

`POST /api/webhooks/channel/[channelId]` needs live mode, an HMAC signature header, and a signed event id used for replay protection.

## Automatic sync

Workers push inventory every 15 minutes and pull reservations every 5 for live channels, with retries every 2 minutes.
