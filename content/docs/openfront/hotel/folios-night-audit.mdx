## Folio entries

A `Folio` is `open`, `closed`, or `voided`. Each `FolioEntry` has `entryType` (`room_charge`, `tax`, `fee`, `addon`, `payment`, `refund`, `adjustment`, `transfer`, `reversal`), `direction` (`debit`, `credit`), `amountMinor`, and a `postingKey` that prevents double posting.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Post

`postFolioEntry` adds a charge or credit with a description and idempotency key.

</Step>
<Step>

### Correct

`reverseFolioEntry` posts an opposite entry linked to the original. Entries are never edited or deleted.

</Step>
<Step>

### Take payment

`recordBookingPayment` records a payment made at the desk.

</Step>
<Step>

### Close

`closeReconciledFolio` closes a folio only when the balance is reconciled.

</Step>
</Steps>

`hotelFolioReceipt` prints a receipt.

## Night audit

`hotelNightAuditOperations` shows the checks for the current business date. `runHotelNightAudit` posts room charges and tax, records `DailyMetrics`, writes a `NightAuditRun`, and advances `HotelBusinessDate`. Resolve overdue checked-in stays first. A closed business date cannot receive new stays.
