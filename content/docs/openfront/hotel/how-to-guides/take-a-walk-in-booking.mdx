description: Create a staff booking, assign a room, and check in.
icon: UserPlus
---

import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

<Steps>
<Step>

### Create
On `/dashboard/platform/reservations` choose dates, room type, and rate plan, then save the guest. The booking source is `walk_in`.

</Step>
<Step>

### Assign a room
Open the booking on the front desk and pick a `vacant` clean room.

</Step>
<Step>

### Collect payment
On `/dashboard/platform/folios` record the payment or a deposit.

</Step>
<Step>

### Check in
Move the booking to `checked_in`.

</Step>
</Steps>
