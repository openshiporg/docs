---
title: Cancel a booking and refund
description: Quote, request, and track a refund.
icon: Undo2
---

import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

<Steps>
<Step>

### Check the policy
Open the guest cancellation quote or the booking's rate plan `cancellationPolicy`.

</Step>
<Step>

### Cancel
Cancel from the front desk. The booking is `cancellation_pending` until the refund resolves.

</Step>
<Step>

### Request the refund
On `/dashboard/platform/payments` open the payment, check the quote, and request the refund with a reason.

</Step>
<Step>

### Approve if required
A different user with `canApproveHotelExceptions` approves at `/dashboard/platform/approvals`.

</Step>
<Step>

### Follow up
If the refund stalls, replay it from `/dashboard/platform/outbox`.

</Step>
</Steps>
