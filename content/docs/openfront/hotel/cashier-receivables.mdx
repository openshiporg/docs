Both pages need `canManagePayments`.

## Cashier

`/dashboard/platform/cashier` (`manageHotelCashier`).

- Open a drawer with an opening count.
- Record drops during the shift.
- Count and close. A counted variance must be reviewed by a different payment manager.
- Confirm cash refunds.
- Place and release deposit holds with `manageHotelSecurityAuthorization`.

## Receivables

`/dashboard/platform/receivables` (`manageHotelReceivable`).

- Create company accounts for direct billing. Direct billing needs an open USD folio.
- Route charges to a payer using payer windows (`hotelPayerWindows`). A group member window can route only that member's attributable posted charges, and a reservation with posted folio history cannot be rerouted to a master folio.
- Issue invoices, record collections, and write off balances. Write-offs need an independent approval.
