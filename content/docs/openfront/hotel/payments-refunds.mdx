All pages need `canManagePayments`.

## Payments

`/dashboard/platform/payments` (`hotelPaymentOperations`) lists `BookingPayment` records with provider, status, and amount. Guests pay through a `BookingPaymentSession` (`initiateBookingPaymentSession`, then `completeBookingPayment` on return). Provider events are stored as `PaymentEvent`.

## Refunds

<Steps>
<Step>

### Quote

`hotelRefundQuote(paymentId)` shows the refundable amount.

</Step>
<Step>

### Request

`requestBookingPaymentRefund` takes the payment, amount, a bounded reason, and an idempotency key. It creates a `RefundIntent`.

</Step>
<Step>

### Execute

The refund worker sends it to the provider. Status goes `pending` → `processing` → `succeeded`, or `failed` and `dead_letter`.

</Step>
<Step>

### Replay

Replay a stuck intent with `replayRefundIntent`.

</Step>
</Steps>

A refund requires an independent approval when it exceeds the thresholds in [Property settings](/docs/openfront/hotel/property-settings). A booking cannot have two cancellation refunds in progress.

## Payouts

`/dashboard/platform/payouts` (`hotelPayoutOperations`). Import a reviewed provider statement, match each payment, and approve the bank deposit with `manageHotelPayout`.

## Disputes

`/dashboard/platform/disputes` lists Stripe dispute notices. Add an evidence note with `annotateHotelDispute`. Submit evidence in Stripe.
