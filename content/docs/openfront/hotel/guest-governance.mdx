## Guest governance

`/dashboard/platform/guest-governance` needs `canManageGuestPrivacy` (`hotelGuestGovernance`, `updateHotelGuestGovernance`).

- Record consent and communication preferences.
- Place and release legal holds. A legal hold prevents anonymization.
- Apply retention and anonymization.
- Store and review identity documents (`GuestDocument`).
- Handle data requests.
- Merge duplicate guests and unmerge them.

Search guests with `hotelGovernanceGuestSearch`.

## Approvals

`/dashboard/platform/approvals` (`hotelApprovalWorkspace`).

<Steps>
<Step>

### Request

A user requests approval for an exact action, target, and amount.

</Step>
<Step>

### Decide

A different user with `canApproveHotelExceptions` approves or rejects (`updateHotelApproval`).

</Step>
<Step>

### Use

The approval can be used once, by the operation it matches.

</Step>
</Steps>

Refunds, write-offs, cash variances, and rate publication can need approval depending on thresholds in property settings.
