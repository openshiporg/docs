## Guests

`/dashboard/platform/guests` and `/guests/[id]` need `canManageGuests` (`hotelGuestOperations`). The list searches and filters by segment. A profile shows contact details, stay history, `totalStays`, `totalSpent`, and flags `isVip` and `isBlacklisted`.

## Loyalty

Tiers are `bronze`, `silver`, `gold`, `platinum`, `diamond`. Points are written as `LoyaltyTransaction` rows. Staff redeem points on the front desk with `redeemHotelLoyalty`. Earning rules are set in property settings.

## Messages

`/dashboard/platform/messages` lists messages sent from `/contact` (`hotelGuestMessages`). Reply from your own email client through the `mailto:` link.

Guest privacy tools (consent, retention, merge) are in [Guest governance](/docs/openfront/hotel/guest-governance).
