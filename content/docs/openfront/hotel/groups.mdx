import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

Route: `/dashboard/platform/groups`. Needs `canManageBookings` (`hotelGroupWorkspace`).

A `GroupBlock` has `blockCode`, `arrivalDate`, `departureDate`, `releaseDate`, contact, and `billingType` (`guest_pays`, `master_folio`, `split`). Status is `tentative`, `definite`, `released`, or `cancelled`. Allocations hold the rooms per room type and date.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Create

`createHotelGroupBlock` with dates, allocations, and billing type.

</Step>
<Step>

### Confirm

`updateHotelGroupBlockStatus` to `definite`.

</Step>
<Step>

### Rooming list

`createHotelGroupRoomingList` creates bookings against the allocation. It cannot create stays in a closed business date.

</Step>
<Step>

### Pickup

`pickupHotelGroupBlock` records rooms picked up. A worker releases unused rooms after `releaseDate`.

</Step>
<Step>

### Close

`closeHotelGroupMasterFolio` after every stay has departed or been cancelled.

</Step>
</Steps>

`detachHotelGroupBooking` removes a booking from the block.
