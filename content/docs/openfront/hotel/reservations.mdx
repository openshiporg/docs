## Front desk

`/dashboard/platform/front-desk` (`hotelFrontDesk`) lists arrivals, in-house guests, departures, and guest change requests for the business date.

| Action | Mutation |
| --- | --- |
| Assign or change a room | `assignRoomToBooking` |
| Check in, check out, no-show | `updateBookingStatus` |
| Cancel | `cancelBooking` |
| Approve or decline a change request | `resolveBookingModificationRequest` |
| Handle a guest still checked in past departure | `resolveOverdueCheckedInBooking` |
| Move a guest to another room | `updateHotelRelocation` |

## Reservation calendar

`/dashboard/platform/reservations` shows a room-by-room stay calendar (`hotelReservationCalendar`).

<Steps>
<Step>

### Create

Choose dates, room type, rate plan, and guest, then save. This calls `createStaffBooking`.

</Step>
<Step>

### Change dates

Drag the stay or use `updateBookingStayDates`. Availability and rates are checked again.

</Step>
<Step>

### Amend

`amendStaffBooking` changes guests, notes, and pricing with an idempotency key.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Booking statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `pending` | Hold awaiting payment, cancelled after 30 minutes |
| `confirmed` | Paid or accepted |
| `checked_in`, `checked_out` | Stay progress |
| `cancellation_pending` | Cancelled, refund unresolved |
| `cancelled`, `no_show` | Closed |

`paymentStatus` is `unpaid`, `partial`, `paid`, or `refunded`. Source is `direct`, `website`, `phone`, `walk_in`, `ota`, `corporate`, or `group`.

The stay register (`updateHotelStayRegister`) records guest details required at check in.
