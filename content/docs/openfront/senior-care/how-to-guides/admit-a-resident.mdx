import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

<Steps>
<Step>

### Record the inquiry

On **Pipeline**, record the inquiry and schedule a tour (`recordInquiry`, `scheduleTour`).

</Step>
<Step>

### Reserve a place

Reserve the bed or apartment (`reservePlace`).

</Step>
<Step>

### Admit

Admit the resident to the place (`admitResident`). The lead moves to `moved_in`.

</Step>
<Step>

### Record the agreement

Record the admission agreement and notices (`recordAdmissionAgreement`, `recordNotice`).

</Step>
</Steps>
