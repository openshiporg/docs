---
title: Close the billing month
description: Derive charges, close the month and charge stored cards.
icon: Calculator
---

import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

<Steps>
<Step>

### Sign off the census

On **Midnight census**, sign off the last day of the month (`signOffCensusDay`).

</Step>
<Step>

### Derive

On **Billing**, bring the month in line with the census (`billingCommand` op `billing.derive`).

</Step>
<Step>

### Close

Close the month to freeze charges and issue statements (`billing.close`).

</Step>
<Step>

### Charge stored cards

Press **Charge stored cards** for residents on autopay (`billing.autopay`).

</Step>
</Steps>
