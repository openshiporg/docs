A Part A claim is derived from the month's per diem and coinsurance charges, with one line per HIPPS run. It is validated, released and exported as a 5010 837I file. Marking it submitted is a person's statement that they uploaded the exact file they downloaded. Nothing becomes accepted or paid without a 277CA or an 835.

835 posting splits contractual, patient responsibility and other adjustments. A denied or zero-paid claim enters a work queue. Ancillary invoices are received, matched to the Part A claim when consolidated billing applies, approved by someone other than the receiver, and paid.

## Command operations

Most clinical and billing actions run through two dispatching mutations, `clinicalCommand(op, input, idempotencyKey)` and `billingCommand(op, input, idempotencyKey)`. Each operation checks its own permissions and community. These are the operations for this area.

| Mutation | Operations |
| --- | --- |
| `billingCommand` | `claim.billing_profile`, `claim.create_month`, `claim.set_clinical`, `claim.validate`, `claim.release`, `claim.export`, `claim.mark_submitted`, `claim.cancel`, `claim.upload_ack`, `claim.work_denial`, `remittance.import`, `remittance.post`, `ancillary.receive`, `ancillary.match`, `ancillary.approve`, `ancillary.pay` |
