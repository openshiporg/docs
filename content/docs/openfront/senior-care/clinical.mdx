The chart holds progress notes (signed, amended or entered in error), vitals, intake, ADLs, wounds, behaviour and physician visits. Assessments are templates with scorers, including Braden, Morse, BIMS, PHQ-9, PAINAD and elopement. A PHQ-9 item 9 answer opens a same-day task.

A verbal order needs read-back and a different person's countersignature. An allergy match needs a stated override. Signing a baseline care plan satisfies its 48-hour clock. **Tasks** is the staff inbox for escalations and follow-ups.

## Commands

Named mutations for this area. Each checks permissions on the server, takes an idempotency key and writes an audit event.

`clinicalCommand`.

## Command operations

Most clinical and billing actions run through two dispatching mutations, `clinicalCommand(op, input, idempotencyKey)` and `billingCommand(op, input, idempotencyKey)`. Each operation checks its own permissions and community. These are the operations for this area.

| Mutation | Operations |
| --- | --- |
| `clinicalCommand` | `note.create`, `note.sign`, `note.amend`, `note.error`, `vitals.record`, `intake.record`, `adl.record`, `wound.open`, `wound.assess`, `behaviour.record`, `visit.record`, `assessment.complete`, `order.enter`, `order.countersign`, `order.discontinue`, `task.complete`, `task.create` |
