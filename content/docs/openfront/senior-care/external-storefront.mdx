To build a custom Senior Care storefront, copy the skill and give it to your LLM. The skill explains that `features/storefront` holds the built-in site, the family portal and the server actions that call the public and portal operations.

Skill file: [`/skills/openfront-senior-care-custom-storefront/SKILL.md`](/skills/openfront-senior-care-custom-storefront/SKILL.md)

## What a separate site must keep

- Public calls keep the rate limit and honeypot field.
- Portal reads go through the `portal*` queries, which return only granted data.
- Financial and clinical views need two-step sign-in.
