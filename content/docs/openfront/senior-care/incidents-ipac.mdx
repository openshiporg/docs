An allegation opens the F609 2-hour, 24-hour and 5-day clocks. Incidents are investigated and reported, and submitting the report satisfies the clock. Changing a reportable incident to not reportable is a two-person approval.

**Infection control** holds cases with a cluster check, precautions, the 72-hour antibiotic time-out, outbreaks and immunisations. The **NHSN weekly report** builds the respiratory vaccination and case CSV from immunisations and infection cases, with the layout's rules checked before the file exists.

## Command operations

Most clinical and billing actions run through two dispatching mutations, `clinicalCommand(op, input, idempotencyKey)` and `billingCommand(op, input, idempotencyKey)`. Each operation checks its own permissions and community. These are the operations for this area.

| Mutation | Operations |
| --- | --- |
| `clinicalCommand` | `incident.report`, `incident.investigate`, `incident.close`, `ipac.immunisation` |
| `billingCommand` | `nhsn.generate`, `nhsn.record` |
