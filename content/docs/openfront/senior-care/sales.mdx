The pipeline runs from inquiry to lead, tour, waitlist, offer, reservation and admission, with response clocks. A lead moves to `moved_in`, a waitlist entry to `converted` and a referral to `admitted` only through admission. Hospital referrals go through clinical review and a payer check.

The **Waitlist** groups entries by apartment type and setting, and an offer holds a place until it expires. **Storefront** sets the public copy, starting prices, availability and tour times. Publishing the site, moving a price and making a waitlist offer are human-only.

## Commands

Named mutations for this area. Each checks permissions on the server, takes an idempotency key and writes an audit event.

`reservePlace`, `releaseReservation`, `recordReferral`, `openTourSlots`, `scheduleTour`, `setStorefrontPublished`, `movePublishedPrice`, `recordInquiry`, `moveLead`, `logSalesActivity`, `moveTour`, `moveReferral`, `addToWaitlist`, `offerWaitlistPlace`, `resolveWaitlistOffer`.
