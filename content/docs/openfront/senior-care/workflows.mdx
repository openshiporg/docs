Staff describe a job in plain words. The builder makes one model call through the gateway, and the result is a graph drawn only from the block catalogue, such as low fluid intake, weight loss, an overdue wound assessment or missed doses. It is validated, dry-run over 30 days of history, reviewed by the proposer, and approved by the director of nursing and then the administrator before it is active.

The engine runs only on request: when the findings page loads, with **Run now**, and after the commands that change its source data. A finding carries its evidence and can be dismissed or rechecked. An AI draft stays a proposal until a person accepts it.

## Commands

Named mutations for this area. Each checks permissions on the server, takes an idempotency key and writes an audit event.

`runWorkflowsOnLoad`.

## Command operations

Most clinical and billing actions run through two dispatching mutations, `clinicalCommand(op, input, idempotencyKey)` and `billingCommand(op, input, idempotencyKey)`. Each operation checks its own permissions and community. These are the operations for this area.

| Mutation | Operations |
| --- | --- |
| `clinicalCommand` | `workflow.pause`, `workflow.run`, `workflow.build`, `workflow.retire`, `finding.dismiss`, `finding.recheck`, `proposal.accept`, `proposal.reject` |
