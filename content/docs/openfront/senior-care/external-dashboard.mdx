To create a custom Senior Care dashboard, copy the skill and give it to your LLM. The skill explains how `features/dashboard` supplies the Keystone administration shell while `features/platform` contains the community screens.

After that source orientation, it asks what you dislike, what should stay, which workflows and users matter, and what the replacement should look like. It then traces the exact routes, GraphQL calls, schema, access rules and provider boundaries in your Senior Care checkout before proposing work.

Skill file: [`/skills/openfront-senior-care-custom-dashboard/SKILL.md`](/skills/openfront-senior-care-custom-dashboard/SKILL.md)

## Current platform pages
