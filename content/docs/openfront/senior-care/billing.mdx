Charges are derived from census days, coverage, care levels, published rates and Medicaid liabilities. The same input always gives the same lines. Month-end close freezes the charges and issues one statement per resident, and a later census correction posts the difference as an adjustment. Statement PDFs are made on request.

Publishing a rate schedule is a maker-checker approval, and **Rates and notices** lists the residents who need a rate-change notice. A care-level increase bills only after its notice period. Refunds, adjustments and write-offs over the threshold wait for a different person. **Medicaid liability** records monthly patient liability with reply and appeal deadlines.
