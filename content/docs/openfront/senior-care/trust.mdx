Resident trust money is kept in double-entry ledgers that are append-only. A withdrawal over the threshold waits for a second signer, never the preparer, and a reversal is a new transaction. Interest is allocated across ledgers, the account is reconciled and signed, and quarterly statements are issued. Authorisations, the surety bond and conveyance at discharge or death are recorded.

## Command operations

Most clinical and billing actions run through two dispatching mutations, `clinicalCommand(op, input, idempotencyKey)` and `billingCommand(op, input, idempotencyKey)`. Each operation checks its own permissions and community. These are the operations for this area.

| Mutation | Operations |
| --- | --- |
| `billingCommand` | `trust.authorisation_record`, `trust.authorisation_revoke`, `trust.allocate_interest`, `trust.quarterly_statements`, `trust.record_bond`, `trust.open_ledger`, `trust.deposit`, `trust.withdraw`, `trust.sign`, `trust.reject`, `trust.reverse`, `trust.close_ledger`, `trust.convey`, `trust.reconcile`, `trust.sign_reconciliation` |
