Shifts are assigned with a credential check, and open shifts are offered by a signed link the staff member answers at `/shift-offer/[token]`. Staffing days are measured in hours per resident day against the Ohio minimum and the operator targets.

**Credentials** holds licences, the OIG exclusion file, Nursys and background results and training hours. Punches are imported, corrections are approved by someone other than the requester, and weekly timecards split overnight shifts at local midnight. The PBJ quarter is validated and exported. Agency invoices are matched to the shifts worked, and the payroll export is locked once it is made.

## Command operations
