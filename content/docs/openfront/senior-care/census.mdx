The **Bed board** shows units, rooms, beds and apartments with live status. Every admission, room change, leave, return, discharge and death is a server command that records a hash-chained census event and updates the stay. A released bed goes to `cleaning` and an emptied apartment to `turnover` until a person marks it ready.

**Midnight census** counts a resident for a day if present at 23:59 local time. Signing off a day closes it and every earlier day, and only a person can do it. A back-dated event is marked late, and events more than five minutes in the future are refused. Bed-hold, transfer and discharge notices open compliance clocks from the state pack.

**Residents** lists current and former residents with representatives. Family portal access is invited here, and each scope beyond updates is proposed by one person and granted by a second.

## Pages

| Page | Route | What it shows |

| Bed board | `/dashboard/platform/census` | Units, rooms, beds and apartments with live status. |
| Residents | `/dashboard/platform/census/residents` | Current and former residents with place, presence and payer. |

| List | Statuses |
| --- | --- |

## Commands

Named mutations for this area. Each checks permissions on the server, takes an idempotency key and writes an audit event.

`admitResident`, `changeRoom`, `startLeave`, `releaseBedHold`, `endLeave`, `dischargeResident`, `recordDeath`, `recordNotice`, `recordAdmissionAgreement`, `correctCensus`, `snapshotCensusDay`, `signOffCensusDay`, `markPlaceReady`, `setPlaceService`, `recomputeAvailability`, `invitePortalRepresentative`, `proposePortalScope`, `grantPortalScope`, `revokePortalScope`, `setPortalAccountStatus`.
