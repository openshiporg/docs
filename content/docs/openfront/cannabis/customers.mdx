## Directory

`/dashboard/platform/customers` lists customers with status tabs and search. `/customers/[id]` shows eligibility evidence, carts and purchases.

Customer `capacity` is `adult_use`, `patient` or `caregiver`. Status moves from `pending_verification` to `eligible`, `ineligible`, and evidence can become `expired` or `revoked`.

## Check-in at the counter

`checkInCannabisCustomer` records an ID check and starts a sale. It is launched from POS as **Check in and start sale**. Fields:

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| Register | An open shift |
| Buyer | Adult-use, plus Patient and Caregiver when medical sales are enabled in the rules |
| ID type | Driver's license, State ID, Passport, Military ID, Tribal ID |
| ID number, date of birth, ID expires | Required |
| Registry, card number, card expires | For patient and caregiver |
| Patient's registry number | Caregiver only |

Confirmations are required: `Confirm you checked the ID in person`, `Confirm you checked the registry card`, and `Confirm the caregiver is authorised for this patient`.

ID and card numbers are stored only as one-way hashes. The buyer must meet the configured age requirement (`Buyer does not meet the configured age requirement`).

## Online intake

`submitCannabisCustomerIntake` and `recordEligibilityDecision` are registered but closed by runtime capability, so every customer today is checked in at the counter. See [Roles and permissions](/docs/openfront/cannabis/roles-permissions) and [Storefront](/docs/openfront/cannabis/storefront).
