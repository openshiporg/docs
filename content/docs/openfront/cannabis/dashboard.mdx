## Control room

The control room summarises today's stock, open incidents and authority status, so an owner can see whether a license or credential is close to expiry.

## Reads and writes

- Pages read through the `cannabisOperationsWorkspace(section, options)` query. The response is validated client-side by `features/platform/workspaces/lib/contract.ts`.
- Writes use named mutations. Destructive or approval-bound actions show a browser confirmation (for example `Release PKG for sale?`).
- Forms mark themselves dirty on change and show field errors returned by the command.
- Rule messages up to 300 characters are returned to the form as `BAD_USER_INPUT`. Anything else is masked as an unexpected error.

## What you see depends on your role

Navigation and actions follow the role flags in [Roles and permissions](/docs/openfront/cannabis/roles-permissions). A user without `canAccessDashboard` cannot enter the dashboard.

For custom clients see [External dashboards](/docs/openfront/cannabis/external-dashboard).
