The staff page lists each person with their role, permission chips, activity chips and credential status.

## Add a staff member

`provisionStaffAuthority` creates a `User`, a single-purpose `Role` and a `StaffCredential` together. You choose:

- Name, email and a temporary password.
- Role name and the permission flags the person needs.
- Credential type and reference. The reference must contain at least 16 characters (`Credential evidence reference must contain at least 16 characters`).
- Allowed activities, from `authority`, `eligibility`, `inventory`, `sale`, `cash`, `state_reporting`, `incident`.
- Approval limit in cents. It cannot exceed your own (`Approval limit cannot exceed your own`).
- Effective and expiry dates.

A premises assignment is required (`A premises assignment is required`).

## Temporary passwords

`setStaffTemporaryPassword` sets a new password for a person at your premises. Roles are immutable after creation, so to change permissions, provision a new staff member or credential.

## Suspend or terminate

`revokeStaffCredential` moves a credential from `active` to `suspended` or `terminated`. A credential in another state is refused with `A <status> credential cannot be <next>`. Without an active credential a person can still sign in but every controlled command fails with `Current staff credential does not authorize this activity`.

## Premises scope

People management is premises-scoped. You can only see and change people at your own premises.

See [Roles and permissions](/docs/openfront/cannabis/roles-permissions) for what each flag allows.
