| Route | Screen | Content |
|---|---|---|
| `/` | `MenuPage` | Age gate, then the menu with form filter, adult-use or medical filter, search and sort |
| `/menu/[id]` | `ProductPage` | A released, in-stock product |
| `/visit` | `VisitPage` | Address, directions, today's hours and open-now, phone, minimum ages, accepted payment types |

## Age gate

Minimum ages come from the active sales rules. Confirmation is stored in a cookie. It is a notice, and ID is checked at the counter.

## Data

Two public queries feed the pages: `publicCannabisMenu` and `publicCannabisStorefrontSettings`, loaded in `features/storefront/lib/data/menu.ts`.

## Scope

The menu serves one organization with one active premises. Otherwise it shows an unavailable state. Customers buy in store.

## Customise

Edit [Store settings](/docs/openfront/cannabis/store-settings) and [Menu and products](/docs/openfront/cannabis/menu-products), or build your own client: [External storefronts](/docs/openfront/cannabis/external-storefront).
