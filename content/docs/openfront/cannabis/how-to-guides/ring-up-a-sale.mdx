---
title: Ring up a sale
description: Check in a buyer, add products and take payment.
---

import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open a shift
Open a register shift in Cash with the counted float.
</Step>
<Step>
### Check in
In POS choose **Check in and start sale**, select the register and buyer, enter ID details and tick the in-person confirmation.
</Step>
<Step>
### Add products
Choose a product, enter a quantity, price it, review, and add.
</Step>
<Step>
### Take payment
Select the payment type. Enter cash received, or the terminal approval code. Confirm to complete.
</Step>
</Steps>
