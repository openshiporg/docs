---
title: Refund a sale
description: Refund against the original payment with a second approver.
---

import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open the receipt
Go to `/dashboard/platform/sales` and open the sale.
</Step>
<Step>
### Fill the refund
Choose **Refund**, pick the original payment, enter the amount, a reason code like `damaged_product`, and the returned product disposition.
</Step>
<Step>
### Approve
For card or bank payments enter the terminal refund reference. A finance colleague enters their email and password.
</Step>
</Steps>
