---
title: Close a shift
description: Blind count the drawer and resolve variances.
---

import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

<Steps>
<Step>
### Count
In Cash open the shift and choose **Count and close**. Enter the counted cash.
</Step>
<Step>
### Approve
A second finance person enters their credentials.
</Step>
<Step>
### Review variance
If the shift is in `variance_review`, record a variance reason code with `approveCashVariance`.
</Step>
</Steps>
