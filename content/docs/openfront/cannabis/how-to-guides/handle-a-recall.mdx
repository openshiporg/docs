---
title: Handle a recall
description: Hold affected packages and close the recall.
---

import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open the case
In Recalls choose **Open a recall**, enter references, a reason and package UIDs or batches.
</Step>
<Step>
### Advance stages
Use **Move to next stage** through scope_confirmed, active, monitoring and disposition.
</Step>
<Step>
### Close
Choose release or destroy, enter the regulator closure reference, and have a compliance colleague approve.
</Step>
</Steps>
