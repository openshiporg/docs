## Compliance queue

`/dashboard/platform/compliance` brings customer verification, active package holds and recalls into one queue so a compliance lead can clear them in order. Each row links to the customer, package or recall page.

## Incidents

`/dashboard/platform/incidents` is the incident register (`ComplianceIncident`).

| Field | Values |
|---|---|
| `type` | refusal_of_sale, identity, patient_registry_outage, traceability_outage, payment_ambiguity, inventory_discrepancy, theft, loss, diversion, recall, privacy, security, system_outage, offline_attempt |
| `severity` | low, medium, high, critical |
| `status` | open, contained, investigating, reported, resolved, closed |
| `outageMode` | none, fail_closed, approved_degraded |

Also recorded: occurred, detected, notification due and reported times, `affectedPackageUids`, `description`, an owner and the acting user.

### Record an incident

`recordComplianceIncident` needs `canManageCompliance` and the `incident` activity. Describe what happened, choose type and severity, and enter the notification deadline from your regulator's rules.

### Move it forward

`transitionComplianceIncident` advances status in order. An out-of-order move is refused with `An incident cannot move from <status> to <status>`.
