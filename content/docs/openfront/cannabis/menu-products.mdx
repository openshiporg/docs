`/dashboard/platform/menu` lists products and what is ready to sell. `/menu/[id]` shows facts, packages with readiness and recent sales.

## Product fields

`sku`, `name`, `brand`, `form`, `categoryCode`, `taxCategoryCode`, `unitOfMeasure`, increment, equivalency, `price`, `channels`, `status` (`draft`, `active`, `inactive`), `publicDescription` and `publicImageUrl`.

Categories come from the active rule pack, so tax and purchase limits can resolve for every product.

## Commands

- `configureCannabisProduct` creates a product. Duplicate SKUs are refused with a message naming the existing product.
- `amendCannabisProduct` edits a product.

Both need `canManageInventory`.

## What reaches the public menu

`publicCannabisMenu` lists only products with a released, lab-passed, unexpired, unheld package and free quantity, each re-checked by `assertPackageSaleable`. An inactive product or one without sellable stock does not appear.

See [Storefront](/docs/openfront/cannabis/storefront).
