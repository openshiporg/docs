Authority is the set of records that lets the software act for a licensed business. Every controlled command checks it before it writes.

## What the page shows

- **Licenses** (`CannabisLicense`): license number, type, regulator, `authorizedActivities`, effective and expiry dates, status.
- **Staff credentials** (`StaffCredential`): credential type and reference, `allowedActivities`, `approvalLimitCents`, effective and expiry dates, status.
- **Premises**: name, premises code, address and phone, timezone and currency.
- **Sales rule versions** (`JurisdictionRulePack`): the effective version and superseded history.

## Activities

A license and a credential both list activities. Onboarding requires all seven on both: `authority`, `eligibility`, `inventory`, `sale`, `cash`, `state_reporting` and `incident`. The error is `Authority pack must explicitly include <activity>` when one is missing.

## Commands

| Action | Mutation | Permission |
|---|---|---|
| Renew a license or credential | `renewCannabisAuthority` | `canManageAuthority` |
| Amend sales rules | `amendCannabisRulePack` | `canManageCompliance` |
| Edit address and phone | `amendCannabisPremisesDetails` | `canManageAuthority` |
| Suspend or terminate a credential | `revokeStaffCredential` | `canManagePeople` |

### Renew

Choose a license or credential (`Choose a license or credential to renew` appears if you do not) and enter a new period. The period must end in the future (`Enter a valid period that ends in the future`) and the effective date must precede expiry.

### Amend sales rules

An amendment creates a new `JurisdictionRulePack` version and marks the previous one `superseded`. The pack holds:

- Minimum ages.
- Operating hours.
- Purchase limits by capacity, category and window. At least one is required and duplicates are refused.
- Tax rules. At least one is required.
- Discount rules. An empty list prohibits discounts.
- Allowed tender types: `cash`, `debit`, `ach`, `wallet`, `other`.
- Policies for labels, returns, outages, privacy and retention.

`TenderConfiguration` records are kept in step with the allowed tender types, and every configuration must be allowed by the pack.
