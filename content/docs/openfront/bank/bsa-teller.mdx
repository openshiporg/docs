Each teller drawer and vault is its own ledger account that cannot go below zero, so the drawer's balance is the expected count at every moment. A drawer is opened with a count and closed after a supervisor's balancing. Vault and Federal Reserve currency movements need dual control.

Posting teller cash writes an immutable cash transaction and updates the party's daily total. The teller is warned before posting when the day's cash in or out would exceed $10,000, so identification is captured at the window. A CTR is then drafted, built as FinCEN XML and recorded when filed. Sanctions lists are loaded from the published files.
