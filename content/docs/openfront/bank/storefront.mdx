The website lives in `features/banking` and mounts at the site root.

| Route | Page |
|---|---|
| `/`, `/personal`, `/business` | Home, personal and business banking |
| `/rates` | Published deposit rates, from the current product versions |
| `/locations`, `/help` | Branches and help |
| `/open` | Open an account |
| `/open/[id]` | Review and submit an application |
| `/signin` | Online banking sign-in |
| `/banking` | Online banking: accounts, transfers and payees |
| `/accounts/[code]` | One account, with activity and statements |

## Opening an account

A first account is opened with a banker, who verifies the person's identity, and the page says so. A banker then enrols the customer for online banking (`enrolCustomerLogin`). The temporary password is shown once to the staff member who hands it over. A signed-in customer can apply for more accounts as themselves: `startApplication` records the application and presents each product's disclosures, and `submitApplication` takes the customer's e-sign consent and acknowledgement.

## Online banking

Every read goes through the customer's own session, so the list access rules return only their rows. Every action is the same command staff use (`submitTransfer`, `addPayee`, `statementDocument`), which takes the party from the session and applies the customer's limits, payee cooling-off and review.
