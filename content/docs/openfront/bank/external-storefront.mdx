To build a custom bank website, copy the skill and give it to your LLM. The skill explains that `features/banking` holds the built-in site and online banking, and that every customer action is a staff command run in the customer's session.

Skill file: [`/skills/openfront-bank-custom-storefront/SKILL.md`](/skills/openfront-bank-custom-storefront/SKILL.md)

## What a separate site must keep

- Customer reads go through the customer's own session.
- Transfers and payees go through `submitTransfer` and `addPayee`, so limits, cooling-off and review apply.
- Statements come from `statementDocument`, which checks the hash and logs the read.
