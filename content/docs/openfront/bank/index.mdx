Openfront Bank is an open-source core and digital banking platform for US community banks, credit unions and program (BaaS/FBO) banks. It runs the ledger of record, deposit accounts and products, customer onboarding and verification, transfers and ACH, linked external accounts, teller drawers and the vault, cash transaction reports and sanctions screening, the business day and end of day, statements and interest, and the public website with online banking. Every money movement is governed by maker-checker approvals, an append-only hash-chained audit trail and idempotent commands.

The live site is [openfront-bank-production.up.railway.app](https://openfront-bank-production.up.railway.app). It runs the sample institution, Harbor Pine Community Bank, N.A. The staff dashboard is at `/dashboard`.

To install it, see [Getting started](/docs/openfront/bank/getting-started).

## Concepts

| Concept | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Institution and program | The bank, with its routing numbers, branches and calendars. A program is a retail, business or sponsored fintech line under it. |
| Settlement mode | A, chartered core: the bank's own charter and RTN. B, program (FBO): a sub-ledger over a sponsor bank's FBO account, reconciled daily. |
| Party and application | A person or business, onboarded through an application with identity checks, screening and a risk rating. |
| Deposit account and product version | An account opened on a published product version, which is frozen. A rate or fee change is a new version. |
| Journal and posting | Every money movement is a balanced journal. Each posting extends its account's sequence and running balance. |
| Business day and end of day | At cutoff the open day closes and the next opens. End of day then runs its steps for the closed day. |
| Approval request | A maker-checker request bound to an action. The person who asked, and any agent, can never approve it. |

## Stack

Next.js 16 (App Router) with React 19, Keystone 6 with a GraphQL API at `/api/graphql`, Prisma with PostgreSQL, Tailwind CSS 4 and shadcn/ui. Sign-in uses Keystone password auth with stateless session cookies. Node.js 20 or later.

## Source layout

| Path | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| `app/` | Thin Next.js routes for the dashboard, the public website and online banking, partner webhooks, health and MCP. |
| `features/keystone/` | Lists, access rules, mutations, the ledger, payments, teller, end-of-day and onboarding services, and seeds. |
| `features/integrations/` | Port interfaces and a registry for ACH, identity, open banking, screening and BSA filing. |
| `features/platform/` | Staff screens mounted at `/dashboard/platform/*`. |
| `features/banking/` | The public website, account opening and online banking. |

## Tenancy and ownership

One deployment serves one institution, and can run several programs side by side. Staff act through roles and permissions, and program staff see only their own program. Customers sign in to online banking with a login tied to their party, and the list access rules return only their rows. SSNs and account numbers are encrypted with blind indexes, and decrypting one needs `canReadPII` and writes a reveal log row. Agents never see full SSNs or account numbers, and never see SAR material.

## Rules the server enforces

- Money is `bigint` minor units with a currency code, in fields ending `Minor`. Rates are basis points. ESLint rules block floats on money.
- Status fields are read-only in GraphQL and change only through commands that check the version, take an idempotency key and write an audit event.
- The web process connects as a login in the DML-only `bank_app` role. The database refuses a broken posting sequence, a balance that is off its newest posting, and an overdraft the journal type does not allow.
- A posted journal is never edited. A reversal mirrors it exactly.
- Approvals are action-bound maker-checker requests. An agent never approves.
- There is no scheduler, timer, cron, outbox or worker. Due jobs run under fenced leases on dashboard loads and from staff buttons, and every job is idempotent.
- Nothing is sent to FedACH or FinCEN by itself. A person sends the file and records the acknowledgement.
- Partner production hosts stay closed unless `BANK_ALLOW_LIVE_PARTNERS=true` and a second person approves go-live.
