### Cut off

On **Operations**, press **Cut off now** if the cutoff time has not passed (`cutoffBusinessDayNow`).

</Step>
<Step>

### Run end of day

Press **Run end of day** (`runEodNow`).

</Step>
<Step>

### Resolve a failure

If a step fails, fix the cause and resume (`resumeEod`), or request a waiver (`requestEodStepWaiver`).

</Step>
