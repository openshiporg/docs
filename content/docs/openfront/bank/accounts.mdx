An account is opened on a published product version, with a random account number with a check digit, never sequential. It starts in `pending_funding` and moves through active, dormant, escheat and closing. Accounts close only at zero.

**Products** publishes a new version of a deposit product (rates, minimum to open, monthly fee) through a maker-checker approval. Accounts already open keep their version, and the public rates and new applications use the new one. **Statements** are rendered at cycle close, and a statement's PDF is served after its hash is checked.

## Commands

Named mutations for this area. Each checks permissions on the server, takes an idempotency key and writes an audit event.

`publishProductVersion`, `openAccount`, `statementDocument`.
