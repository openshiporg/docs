Every money movement is a balanced journal. A posted journal is reversed, never edited. **Ledger** shows journals, the trial balance, reconciliation and GL exports.

Reconciliation checks that every cached balance equals the sum of its postings and holds (L3), and that every GL balance equals its mapped ledger accounts with the whole ledger netting to zero (L4). For program banks, the FBO three-way reconciliation pauses outflows on a break.
