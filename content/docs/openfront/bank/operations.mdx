At the institution's cutoff the open day moves to cutoff and the next business date opens in the same transaction, so posting never stops. End of day then processes the closed day in order:

Freeze the day, accrue deposit interest, post the accrual to the GL, pay interest, assess cycle fees, release and expire holds, ACH return deadlines, balances match postings (L3), GL matches sub-ledger (L4), daily balances, statements, the dormancy scan, the FBO three-way reconciliation, the GL export, and close the day.

A failed step stops the run until it is resumed. Waiving a step or forcing a cutoff needs approval. Running end of day twice posts nothing twice.

`cutoffBusinessDayNow`, `requestForceCutoff`, `runEodNow`, `resumeEod`, `requestEodStepWaiver`.
