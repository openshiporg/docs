To create a custom Bank dashboard, copy the skill and give it to your LLM. The skill explains how `features/dashboard` supplies the Keystone administration shell while `features/platform` contains the institution screens.

After that source orientation, it asks what you dislike, what should stay, which workflows and users matter, and what the replacement should look like. It then traces the exact routes, GraphQL calls, schema, access rules and provider boundaries in your Bank checkout before proposing work.

Skill file: [`/skills/openfront-bank-custom-dashboard/SKILL.md`](/skills/openfront-bank-custom-dashboard/SKILL.md)

## Current platform pages

Route files are `app/dashboard/(admin)/platform/<route>/page.tsx`. The sidebar comes from `features/platform/lib/navigation.ts`.

| Page | Group | What staff do there |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/dashboard/platform/operations` | Top level | Business day, end of day, exceptions and jobs. |
| `/dashboard/platform/approvals` | Top level | Maker-checker requests waiting for a second person. |
| `/dashboard/platform/customers` | Banking | Parties, applications and verification. |
| `/dashboard/platform/accounts` | Banking | Deposit accounts, balances, statements, interest and products. |
| `/dashboard/platform/payments` | Banking | Transfers, ACH entries and files, returns and external links. |
| `/dashboard/platform/ledger` | Banking | Journals, trial balance, reconciliation and GL exports. |
| `/dashboard/platform/bsa` | Banking | Cash transactions, CTRs and screening. |
| `/dashboard/platform/audit` | Institution | Hash-chained audit events and chain verification. |
| `/dashboard/platform/institution` | Institution | Institution profile, calendars, rail windows, regulatory parameters and controls. |
