Shared commerce lists come from Openfront E-commerce. The lists below are specific to Collectibles.

## Cards, sets and stock

| List | Role |
|---|---|
| `CollectibleSet` | A set; has `cards` |
| `CatalogItem` | A card printing or sealed product |
| `Product` / `ProductVariant` | Sellable stock with card fields |
| `MarketPrice` | Append-only readings per card and finish |
| `PricingRule` | Repricing rules |

## Serialized items

`Specimen`, `SpecimenIdentifier`, `InventoryUnit`, `InventoryLocation`, `InventoryMovement`, `Reservation`, `Listing`. Movements and reservations refuse generic create and update.

## Evidence

`ConditionObservation`, `GradeObservation`, `GradingCompany`, `ProvenanceEvent`, `CustodyEvent`, `ValuationObservation`, `CollectibleMedia`. Append-only.

## Buying and consignment

`Buylist`, `BuylistEntry`, `TradeInSubmission`, `TradeInItem`, `ConsignmentIntake`, `ConsignmentItem`, `ConsignmentAgreement`, `ConsignmentSettlement`, `SettlementLine`, `SellerProfile`.

## Operations and collectors

`FulfillmentRequirement`, `ShipmentEvidence`, `DisputeCase`, `EvidenceAttachment`, `CollectorProfile`, `Binder`, `BinderCard`, `WishlistItem`, `SavedSearch`.

## Amounts

Money is integer minor units. Percentages are basis points.
