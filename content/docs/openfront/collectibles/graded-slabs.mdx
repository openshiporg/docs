`/dashboard/platform/graded` lists serialized slabs with company, grade, label and certificate.

## How a slab is stored

A slab is a product with one `graded` variant tied to an `InventoryUnit`. The unit points to a `Specimen` holding a `GradeObservation` (company, grade, label, certificate, verification state) and a certificate `SpecimenIdentifier`.

## Rules checked on receipt

`receiveCollectibleItem` validates:

| Rule | Message |
|---|---|
| Company is PSA, BGS or CGC | `Grading company must be PSA, BGS or CGC` |
| Grade 1 to 10 in half steps | `Grade must be 1 to 10 in half steps` |
| PSA has no 9.5 | `PSA does not grade 9.5` |
| Certificate is letters, digits and dashes | `Certificate number is invalid` |

## Certificate lookup

`GradingCompany` stores a lookup URL template. PSA uses `https://www.psacard.com/cert/{cert}` and CGC uses `https://www.cgccards.com/certlookup/{cert}/`. BGS has none. The storefront slab view shows a "Verify with" link when a template exists and the certificate is not flagged as a sample (`grade.sample`).

## Selling

A listing publishes one unit. `manageCollectibleListing` moves it between draft, published and withdrawn. See [Items and receiving](/docs/openfront/collectibles/items-receiving).
