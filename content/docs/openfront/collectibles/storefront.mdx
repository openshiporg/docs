The storefront is country-scoped under `/[countryCode]`.

## Browse

- Home shows a set checklist with stock, market prices and add buttons.
- `/sets` and `/sets/[handle]` show the full checklist (`publicCardSets`, `publicSetChecklist`).
- `/cards/[id]` shows the market reading and history (`publicCardMarket`) and stocked conditions.
- `/products/[handle]` is the product page for a single, slab or sealed product.
- `/store`, `/categories/[...category]`, `/collections/[handle]` are shelves.
- `/search` supports saved searches for signed-in customers.

## Sell to us

`/trade-in` lists buylist offers per condition (`publicBuylist`). The customer builds a sell list, chooses cash or credit and submits.

## Cart and checkout

Cart and checkout enforce pre-order limits. Payment is Stripe, PayPal or bank transfer, depending on installed providers. Confirmation is at `/order/confirmed/[id]`.

## Account

Under `/account`: orders, binder (`activeBinder`, `addToBinder`, `removeFromBinder`), want list (`activeWantList`, `toggleWantList`), saved searches, trade-ins (`activeTradeIns`), profile, addresses, business-account invoices.

The shop states it is independent and not affiliated with Pokémon's owners.
