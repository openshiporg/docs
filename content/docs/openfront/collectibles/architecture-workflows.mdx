## Data model

Models live in `features/keystone/models/**`. Shared commerce lists come from Openfront E-commerce; collectibles lists are in the `Collectible*.ts` files. `docs/schema-map.md` in the source repository lists every list with its fields and states.

### Cards, sets, and stock

| List | Role | Key relationships |
|---|---|---|
| `CollectibleSet` | A Pokémon set: name, code, series, printed and total card counts, release date, `releaseStatus` (a set can be a pre-order set before its card list exists), `tcgplayerGroupId` | `franchise`, `category`, `cards` |
| `CatalogItem` | One card printing (number, rarity, supertype, energy types, HP, artist, image URLs, `tcgplayerProductId`) or one sealed product (`kind: sealed`, `sealedFormat`) | `collectibleSet`, `products`, `marketPrices`, `specimens` |
| `Product` / `ProductVariant` | Sellable stock. A product links to its catalog card; each variant carries `stockKind` (`raw`, `graded`, `sealed`), `cardCondition` (NM, LP, MP, HP, DMG), `cardFinish`, `cardLanguage`, `pricingMode`, `lastRepricedAt`, and `preorder`, `preorderReleaseDate`, `preorderLimitPerCustomer` | `catalogItem`, prices (`MoneyAmount`) |
| `MarketPrice` | Append-only reading per card and finish: low, mid, high, market (minor units), source, `observedAt` | `catalogItem` |
| `PricingRule` | Per stock kind and condition: basis, percentage, minimum, rounding, maximum change, priority | — |

Raw singles are a quantity on a variant, so checkout decrements them like any product. A graded slab is a product with one `graded` variant tied to a serialized `InventoryUnit`, which points to a `Specimen` with a `GradeObservation` (company, grade, label, certificate, verification state) and a certificate `SpecimenIdentifier`.

### Serialized items and evidence

- `Specimen` is the physical object. Its `lifecycleStatus` (intake, review, available, reserved, sold, returned, withdrawn) is write-locked and moves only through system commands.
- `InventoryUnit` holds one specimen at one `InventoryLocation`, with an ownership basis and a version fence. `InventoryMovement` is its ledger and `Reservation` holds it for a cart, offer, auction, operator, or return. All three refuse generic GraphQL create and update.
- `Listing` publishes one unit for sale. It is written only by `manageCollectibleListing` and `receiveCollectibleItem`.
- Evidence lists are append-only: `ConditionObservation`, `GradeObservation` (with `GradingCompany`), `ProvenanceEvent`, `CustodyEvent`, and `ValuationObservation`. Each has a `publiclyVisible` flag that controls what the storefront item record shows.

### Buying, consignment, and collectors

- `Buylist` holds the terms (cash and credit percentages, per-condition shares, minimum offer, public notes). `BuylistEntry` targets a card printing with a minimum condition, hotlist flag, stock cap, quantity bought, and NM cash and credit offers.
- `TradeInSubmission` and `TradeInItem` record what a customer offers to sell, the inspected condition, quoted amounts, and accepted amounts.
- `ConsignmentIntake`, `ConsignmentItem`, `ConsignmentAgreement` (commission in basis points, fixed fee, pricing authority, terms snapshot), and `ConsignmentSettlement` with `SettlementLine` record consigned stock. Consignors are `SellerProfile` records.
- `Binder` and `BinderCard` record cards a collector owns (finish, condition, language, grade, quantity, cost basis). `WishlistItem` is the want list (card, finish, worst acceptable condition, maximum price). `SavedSearch` and `CollectorProfile` complete the customer side.
- `FulfillmentRequirement` and `ShipmentEvidence` cover high-value shipping; `DisputeCase` and `EvidenceAttachment` cover disputes.

## Operator workflows

### Import the catalog and refresh prices

1. `importCardCatalog` imports one bundled checklist per call (`features/keystone/lib/pokemon/checklists`), creating the `CollectibleSet`, its `CatalogItem` cards, and initial readings. It can also create an upcoming set and its sealed products.
2. On **Market & Repricing**, staff run `refreshMarketPrices`. For every set with a `tcgplayerGroupId` it fetches the day's TCGplayer prices from TCGCSV, appends `MarketPrice` rows, and updates buylist entries whose `pricingMode` is `market`. Per-set failures are reported, not thrown.
3. Staff preview `repriceInventory` (`apply: false`), then apply it. Only variants with `pricingMode: market` on products linked to a catalog card are considered. Each variant gets a rule by stock kind and condition; results are `priced`, `review` (the move exceeds the rule's maximum change and is not applied), or `skipped`. Applying updates the base price, scales other currencies proportionally, and stamps `lastRepricedAt`.

### Receive stock

**Receive Items** (`/dashboard/platform/items/receive`) calls `receiveCollectibleItem` against an existing product variant. It creates the catalog entry, specimen, condition observation, inventory unit, and receipt movement, and can publish a listing in the same call. For slabs it records the grade and certificate and rejects PSA 9.5. It can take an item in on consignment with a consignor name and commission.

### Sell

Singles and sealed product go through the standard cart (`createActiveCart`, `updateActiveCart`, `updateActiveCartLineItem`, `addActiveCartShippingMethod`, `initiatePaymentSession`, `completeActiveCart`). Pre-order variants are capped at the cart: the per-customer limit counts this cart plus the customer's earlier uncancelled orders; guests are limited per cart. Serialized items sell through their `Listing`, whose lifecycle is draft, published, withdrawn (with scheduled, paused, sold, and ended states). Orders paid by the bank-transfer provider are created with a pending payment and confirmed with `confirmManualPayment`.

### Trade-ins

A signed-in customer submits a sell list with `submitTradeIn`. The dashboard trade-in desk moves submissions through:

`draft` → `submitted` → `received` → `inspecting` → `quoted` → `accepted` → `paid`, with `submitted` or `quoted` → `declined` → `returned`.

Quotes and inspections can change only while `received` or `inspecting`; quotes prefill from the buylist. **Mark paid** records the status only; it does not move money or issue store credit.

### Consignment

- Intake: `submitted` → `received` → `inspecting` → `offered` → `accepted` or `declined` → `returned`.
- Item: `pending` → `accepted` → `listed` → `sold`; any active item can go to `return_requested` → `returned`.
- Agreement: `draft` → `sent` → `accepted`, `expired`, or `terminated`.

Settlements are read-only on the consignment and seller pages; no code generates them.

### High-value fulfillment, disputes, and sellers

- **High-value fulfillment:** a requirements queue per paid order; staff set or replace a `FulfillmentRequirement` (declared value, signature, insurance, packaging) and record `ShipmentEvidence`.
- **Disputes:** `open` → `evidence_requested` → `review` → `resolved` → `closed`, with assignee, resolution, and evidence attachments.
- **Sellers:** `applicant` → `active` (approve) or `closed` (decline); `active` ⇄ `paused`; `suspended` → `active` (reinstate); any → `closed`. Identity evidence state is set separately.

<Callout type="info">
Trade-in, consignment, dispute, and seller transitions are checked in the dashboard server actions (`features/platform/*/lib`) and then written with the list's update mutation. Role permissions protect the list, but a direct GraphQL client holding the same permission is not held to the transition map.
</Callout>

## Custom GraphQL operations

Registered in `features/keystone/mutations/index.ts`:

- **Public queries:** `publicCardSets`, `publicSetChecklist`, `publicCardMarket`, `publicBuylist`, `publicCollectiblesDiscovery`, `marketplaceCollectiblesCapabilities`, `getProductsSortedByPrice`.
- **Customer (owner-scoped):** `activeBinder`, `addToBinder`, `removeFromBinder`, `activeWantList`, `toggleWantList`, `activeWishlist`, `toggleWishlistListing`, `activeTradeIns`, `submitTradeIn`, plus account, address, order, invoice, and privacy operations.
- **Staff collectibles commands:** `importCardCatalog` (`canManageCatalog`), `refreshMarketPrices` (`canManageCatalog`), `repriceInventory` (`canManageProducts`), `receiveCollectibleItem`, `manageCollectibleListing`.
- **Disabled:** `placeCollectibleBid` and `submitCollectibleOffer` throw while `AUCTION_LIFECYCLE_RELEASED` and `OFFER_LIFECYCLE_RELEASED` are `false`.
- **Shared commerce:** cart and checkout, `transitionOrderStatus`, `confirmManualPayment`, `createOrderFulfillment`, `cancelOrderFulfillment`, provider shipping labels and rates, `processReturnRefund`, invoices, `adjustInventory`, `importInventory`, `registerWebhookEndpoint`, and `retryWebhookDeliveries`.

## Access control

Access combines a session (cookie or `of_` API key bearer token) with role permissions from `features/keystone/models/fields.ts`. Collectibles-specific permissions include `canReadCollectibles`, `canManageCatalog`, `canManageListings`, `canManageAuctions`, `canManageConsignments`, `canManageEvidence`, `canManageCollectibleCustomers`, `canManageRisk`, and `canManageIntegrations`, alongside the shared product, inventory, order, payment, fulfillment, return, discount, gift card, user, role, and webhook permissions. API keys are limited to their scopes and to the owner role's permissions.

`StoreMembership` stores staff roles in the store, but current access checks use role permissions, not memberships. Customer-owned lists are filtered to the signed-in human user.
