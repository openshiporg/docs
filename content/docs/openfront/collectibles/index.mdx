Openfront Collectibles is a self-hosted shop for Pokémon trading cards. It sells singles stocked per card, printing, language, and condition; graded slabs as single serialized items; and sealed product, including pre-orders with an allocation and a per-customer limit. It also runs a buylist for buying cards from customers, takes items in on consignment, stores market price readings, and applies repricing rules. Collectors get a binder and a want list in their account.

<Callout type="info">
Card data and image URLs come from Pokémon TCG API data bundled in the repository. Market prices come from TCGplayer readings mirrored by TCGCSV. Neither is a live partnership or an official data feed, and slab grades are recorded observations, not a grading-company guarantee.
</Callout>

## What the codebase contains

The application uses Next.js 16 (App Router, React 19), Keystone 6 with a GraphQL API at `/api/graphql`, Prisma, and PostgreSQL. Source is laid out as:

| Directory | Contents |
|---|---|
| `features/keystone` | Keystone models, access rules, custom GraphQL queries and mutations, checkout, Pokémon catalog and pricing helpers |
| `features/platform` | Operator pages mounted under `/dashboard/platform/*` and the onboarding flow |
| `features/dashboard` | Shared Keystone admin shell, sign-in, generic list and item screens |
| `features/storefront` | Customer storefront screens, modules, and the shared card UI slice |
| `features/integrations` | Payment (Stripe, PayPal, manual) and shipping (Shippo, ShipEngine, manual) adapters |
| `app` | Thin route files for the storefront, dashboard, and HTTP endpoints |

### Schema overview

The schema extends the shared Openfront E-commerce commerce lists (products, variants, carts, orders, payments, fulfillment, returns, regions, discounts, gift cards, users, roles) with collectibles lists:

- **Catalog:** `CollectibleSet` (a Pokémon set with series, release date, release status, TCGplayer group id), `CatalogItem` (one card printing or one sealed product), `Specimen`, `SpecimenIdentifier`, `CollectibleMedia`, `MarketPrice`, and `PricingRule`.
- **Stock and selling:** card fields on `ProductVariant` (`stockKind`, `cardCondition`, `cardFinish`, `cardLanguage`, `pricingMode`, pre-order fields), plus `InventoryUnit`, `InventoryMovement`, `Reservation`, and `Listing` for serialized items such as slabs.
- **Buying and consignment:** `Buylist`, `BuylistEntry`, `TradeInSubmission`, `TradeInItem`, `ConsignmentIntake`, `ConsignmentItem`, `ConsignmentAgreement`, and `ConsignmentSettlement`.
- **Evidence and operations:** condition, grade, provenance, custody, and valuation observations; `FulfillmentRequirement`, `ShipmentEvidence`, `DisputeCase`, `SellerProfile`, and `CollectorProfile`.
- **Collector records:** `Binder`, `BinderCard`, `WishlistItem` (the want list), and `SavedSearch`.

See [Architecture and workflows](/docs/openfront/collectibles/architecture-workflows) for relationships and lifecycles.

## Operator surfaces

Operators work in `/dashboard`. The product-specific pages cover Cards & Sets, Graded Slabs, Sealed & Pre-orders, Market & Repricing, Buylist, Trade-ins, Consignments, Receive Items, condition and grading evidence, disputes, high-value fulfillment, sellers, and the inherited commerce pages (orders, products, regions, discounts, gift cards, payment and shipping providers). The full route list is on [External dashboards](/docs/openfront/collectibles/external-dashboard).

## Storefront

The customer storefront is country-scoped under `/[countryCode]`. It shows each set as its full checklist with stock and the latest market reading, card and product pages, search, a "Sell to us" buylist page at `/trade-in`, cart and checkout, and an account area with orders, binder, want list, saved searches, and trade-in history. See [External storefronts](/docs/openfront/collectibles/external-storefront).

## Onboarding

The dashboard onboarding dialog offers three templates: **Complete card shop** (19 sets imported as checklists with market readings, singles, graded slabs with labelled sample certificates, sealed product including pre-orders, pricing rules, and a buylist), **Starter shop** (two modern sets, a few singles and sealed boxes, one region, bank transfer), and **Custom setup** (the starter data as editable JSON per section). The seed shop, "Eighth Street Cards", is fictional.

## Ownership and tenancy

A deployment is one shop. Collectibles commands such as catalog import, repricing, and trade-in submission look up the single `Store` record and refuse to run until exactly one exists. There is no multi-store or multi-tenant separation inside one database.

Staff access is controlled by role permissions such as `canManageCatalog`, `canManageProducts`, `canManageInventory`, `canManageConsignments`, `canManageEvidence`, and `canReadCollectibles`. Customer records are owner-scoped: binders, binder cards, want-list items, and saved searches are created against the signed-in user and filtered to that user on read, update, and delete. Inventory units, movements, reservations, and listings cannot be created or edited through generic GraphQL CRUD; they change only through named commands.
