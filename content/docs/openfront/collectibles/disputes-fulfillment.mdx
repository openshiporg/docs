## Disputes

`/dashboard/platform/disputes` and `/disputes/[id]`. Statuses: `open` → `evidence_requested` → `review` → `resolved` → `closed`. Assign an owner, record the resolution and attach `EvidenceAttachment` files.

## High-value fulfillment

`/dashboard/platform/high-value-fulfillment` is a queue of paid orders. Open `/[orderId]` to:

1. Set or replace the `FulfillmentRequirement`: declared value, signature, insurance, packaging.
2. Pack and create the fulfillment from the order.
3. Record `ShipmentEvidence` for the parcel.

Rates, labels and tracking come from the shipping providers in [Integrations](/docs/openfront/collectibles/integrations).
