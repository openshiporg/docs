title: Store settings
description: Store identity, regions, currencies, users and system settings.
icon: Settings
---

`/dashboard/platform/store` edits the single `Store` record: name, default currency and shop details. The store name, logo and logo hue are the storefront brand.

Related pages:

- `/platform/regions`, `/countries`, `/currencies`: where you sell and in which currency.
- `/platform/users`: staff accounts and roles.
- `/platform/api-keys`: bearer keys prefixed `of_`.
- `/platform/apps`: OAuth apps.
- `/platform/system`: system settings.

The default storefront country comes from `NEXT_PUBLIC_DEFAULT_REGION`.
