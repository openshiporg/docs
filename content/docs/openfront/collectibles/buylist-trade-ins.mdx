## Buylist

`/dashboard/platform/buylist` shows the active `Buylist` and its entries. Terms: cash and credit percentages, per-condition shares, minimum offer and public notes. Each `BuylistEntry` targets a card printing with minimum condition, hotlist flag, stock cap, quantity bought, and NM cash and credit offers.

## Trade-ins

Customers submit a sell list at `/[countryCode]/trade-in` with `submitTradeIn`. Staff work it at `/dashboard/platform/trade-ins` and `/trade-ins/[id]`.

Statuses: `draft` → `submitted` → `received` → `inspecting` → `quoted` → `accepted` → `paid`. From `submitted` or `quoted` it can be `declined`, then `returned`.

### Process a submission

1. Mark it `received` when the cards arrive.
2. Move to `inspecting` and record the inspected condition on each `TradeInItem`. Quotes prefill from the buylist.
3. Move to `quoted`, then `accepted` when the customer agrees.
4. **Mark paid** when you have paid cash or issued credit. This records the status and the customer's choice as `disposition`.

Quotes and inspections change only while `received` or `inspecting`. Errors the customer can see include `A buylist item exceeds the quantity the shop is buying` and `A selected buylist item is no longer being bought`.
