## Product card fields

Variants carry `stockKind`, `cardCondition`, `cardFinish`, `cardLanguage`, `pricingMode`, `lastRepricedAt` and the three pre-order fields.

## Order operations

- `transitionOrderStatus(orderId, status, reason)`.
- `confirmManualPayment(orderId, reference)` for bank-transfer orders.
- `createOrderFulfillment` and `cancelOrderFulfillment`.
- `processReturnRefund(returnId, paymentId, idempotencyKey)`.
- `adjustInventory(variantId, delta, reason)` and `importInventory`.
