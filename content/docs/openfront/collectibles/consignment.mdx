## Pages

- `/dashboard/platform/consignments` and `/consignments/[id]`: intakes.
- `/dashboard/platform/consignment`: the Consignment Payouts desk with settlement queues.

## Lifecycles

| Record | Flow |
|---|---|
| Intake | `submitted` → `received` → `inspecting` → `offered` → `accepted` or `declined` → `returned` |
| Item | `pending` → `accepted` → `listed` → `sold`; any active item → `return_requested` → `returned` |
| Agreement | `draft` → `sent` → `accepted`, `expired` or `terminated` |

## Agreement terms

`ConsignmentAgreement` stores commission in basis points (0 to 10000, `Commission must be 0 to 10000 basis points`), a fixed fee, pricing authority and a terms snapshot. Consignors are `SellerProfile` records.

## Take a consignment

1. Create an intake for a consignor.
2. Add items with condition and asking price.
3. Create the agreement and send it.
4. Mark items accepted and list them. Items can also be taken in at receiving with a consignor name and commission.
5. Review `ConsignmentSettlement` and `SettlementLine` records on the consignment and seller pages.
