## Sellers

`/dashboard/platform/sellers` and `/sellers/[id]` manage `SellerProfile` records.

`applicant` → `active` (approve) or `closed` (decline); `active` ⇄ `paused`; `suspended` → `active` (reinstate); any → `closed`. Set identity evidence state separately on the seller.

## Collectors

`/dashboard/platform/collectors` lists `CollectorProfile` records. Customers own their binders, want lists and saved searches, which are filtered to the signed-in user.
