`/dashboard/platform/sealed` shows sealed stock, pre-order street dates, allocations and units sold.

## Formats

Booster pack, sleeved booster, booster bundle, booster box, Elite Trainer Box, premium collection, tin, blister, case, other.

## Pre-order fields

On the `ProductVariant`: `preorder`, `preorderReleaseDate` and `preorderLimitPerCustomer`. A set with `releaseStatus: preorder` can exist before its card list.

## Limits at the cart

The per-customer limit counts the current cart plus the customer's earlier uncancelled orders. Guests are limited per cart. Pre-orders are paid in full at checkout.

## Set up a pre-order

1. Import the upcoming set and its sealed products with `importCardCatalog`.
2. Open the product variant and enable pre-order, set the street date and per-customer limit.
3. Set the price and stock allocation with `adjustInventory`.
4. Check the Sealed page for allocation against units sold.
