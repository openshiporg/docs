One page for daily back-of-house work (`getCoffeeOperations`) with recent records for each.

## Order stock

`createCoffeePurchaseOrder`: supplier, inventory item, `orderedQuantity`, `unitCostCents`, `expectedAt` (valid calendar date), notes.

## Receive a delivery

`receiveCoffeePurchaseOrder` creates an active `InventoryLot` and a `received` stock movement. Lot expiry must be after receipt: "Lot expiry must be after the receipt time. Record expired goods as waste instead of receiving them as usable stock." A PO can be received once: "Cannot receive purchase order with status ...".

## Prep batches

`completeCoffeePrepBatch` from an active recipe: quantity made and expiry. It consumes the recipe's ingredients (`PrepBatchConsumption`). Later orders draw from the batch until it expires or runs out. Batch states: planned, in_progress, completed, discarded.

## Waste

`recordCoffeeWaste`: item or batch, quantity, and a reason of expired, damaged, overproduction, or quality. Waste cannot exceed stock.

## Stock counts

`recordCoffeeStockCount` sets counted quantity and records an adjustment movement.
