`/dashboard/platform/catering` lists requests. `/catering/[id]` is the detail.

## Intake

Customers submit `requestCafeCatering` from `/catering`. Staff create one with **New inquiry** (`PlatformCreateCatering`).

## Fields

`cateringNumber`, `customerName`, `customerEmail`, `eventAt`, `guestCount`, `deliveryAddress`, `subtotalCents` (the quote), `notes`.

## Status

`inquiry` to `quoted` to `confirmed` to `completed`, or `cancelled`. Edit event facts and the quote with `PlatformUpdateCatering`, then use the status buttons: send quote, confirm, complete, cancel (`updateCateringOrderStatus`).
