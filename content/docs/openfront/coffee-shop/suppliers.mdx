`/dashboard/platform/suppliers` has status tabs. `/suppliers/[id]` shows the supplier, its purchase orders, and lots.

## Fields

`name`, `status`, `contactName`, `email`, `phone`, `website`, `leadTimeDays`, `notes`.

## Actions

- Create (`CreateCoffeeSupplier`) and edit (`UpdateCoffeeSupplier`).
- Pause, activate, or archive.
- Delete (`DeleteCoffeeSupplier`) only when the supplier is unused.

Pick a supplier when you create a purchase order on [Coffee operations](/docs/openfront/coffee-shop/coffee-operations).
