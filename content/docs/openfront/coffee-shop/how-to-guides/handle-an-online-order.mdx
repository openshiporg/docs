<Steps>
<Step>
### Accept
Open [Orders](/docs/openfront/coffee-shop/orders) and accept the request.
</Step>
<Step>
### Prepare
It appears on the [KDS](/docs/openfront/coffee-shop/kds).
</Step>
<Step>
### Collect payment
Record cash or card with the drawer open, unless the guest paid online.
</Step>
<Step>
### Complete
Mark the order completed once every item is ready.
</Step>
</Steps>
