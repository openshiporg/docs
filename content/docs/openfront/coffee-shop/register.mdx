`/dashboard/platform/register` lists shifts. `/register?view=payments` lists payments. `/register/[id]` shows one shift. These pages are read only; shifts are opened and closed on the POS.

## Shift detail

A `RegisterShift` records:

- `openingCashCents`, `cashSalesCents`, `cashRefundsCents`, `cardSalesCents`, `cardRefundsCents`, `tipsCents`
- `payInsCents`, `payOutsCents`
- `expectedCashCents`, `countedCashCents`, `cashVarianceCents`
- `openedBy`, `closedBy`, `openedAt`, `closedAt`, `status`

The page also lists `CashMovement` rows (number, type, amount, reason, who recorded it), POS receipts, and payments on the shift.

## Payments

`Payment` rows have `method`, `status` (pending, authorized, captured, failed, refunded, cancelled), `amount`, `refundedAmount`, `refundReason`, `provider`, and `providerPaymentId`. Card payments taken at the counter show `external_terminal:<approval code>`.
