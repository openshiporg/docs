`/dashboard/platform/store-settings` edits the `StoreSettings` singleton.

## Fields

- Identity: `name`, `tagline`, `description`, `brandColor`, `logoIcon`, `logoColor`
- Contact: `address`, `phone`, `contactEmail`
- `timezone`, `locale`, `currencyCode` (USD)
- `weeklyHours`
- Pickup: `pickupLeadTimeMinutes`, `pickupWindowMinutes`, `pickupCapacityPerWindow` (default 20)
- `taxRateBasisPoints`

Save with `UpdateCoffeePlatformStoreSettings`. On a fresh install the first save uses `CreateCoffeePlatformStoreSettings`.

## Online card payment

The switch (`SetCoffeeOnlinePayment`) enables Stripe Checkout for online orders. It needs `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY` and `STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET` on the server. See [Payment providers](/docs/openfront/coffee-shop/payment-providers).

## Pickup rules

An order must be at least the lead time ahead, falls into a window, and each window takes up to the capacity. Over capacity: "The requested pickup window is at capacity".
