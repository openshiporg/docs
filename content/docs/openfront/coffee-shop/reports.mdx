`/dashboard/platform/reports` (`getCoffeeReports`) is the analytics page.

## Period

Today, 7, 30, or 90 days, or a custom range up to 366 days. The default is 30 days. Dates follow the store timezone. A bad range returns "Choose a valid start and end date".

## Sections

- **Sales ledger** and totals over the whole period (database aggregates)
- **Top items** (top 8)
- **Items by station**
- **Low stock** (10 per page)
- **Top rewards accounts**

Each section is only shown to roles that own those facts (orders, payments, inventory, loyalty).
