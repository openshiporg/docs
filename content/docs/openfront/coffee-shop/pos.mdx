`/dashboard/platform/pos` (`getCoffeePosRegister`) is the counter. The left side is the menu with search and category tabs. The right side is the ticket.

## Open the drawer

<Steps>
<Step>
### Start a shift
Enter a register name and opening cash. `openRegisterShift` creates a `RegisterShift` with a `shiftNumber`.
</Step>
<Step>
### Ring up items
Tap items, choose modifiers (sold-out items are marked), adjust quantity, and add a customer name, tip, and ticket notes. `quotePosOrder` prices the ticket.
</Step>
<Step>
### Take tender
Cash: enter cash received and the app computes change. Card: enter the terminal approval code and confirm the terminal approved the total.
</Step>
<Step>
### Complete
`createCafePickupOrder` with the register shift creates a paid `CafeOrder`, a `PosOrder` receipt, and a `Payment`, and consumes stock.
</Step>
</Steps>

"Immediate tender requires an open register shift". Immediate-tender methods are staff only.

## Cash movements

`recordRegisterCashMovement` with `type` pay-in, pay-out, or drop, an amount, and a required reason.

## Close the drawer

`closeRegisterShift` takes the counted cash. The shift stores expected cash and variance. Expected cash is opening cash plus cash sales, pay-ins, minus cash refunds, pay-outs, and drops. History lives on [Register](/docs/openfront/coffee-shop/register).
