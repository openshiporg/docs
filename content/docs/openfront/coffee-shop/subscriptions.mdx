`/dashboard/platform/subscriptions` and `/subscriptions/[id]`.

## Create

Choose a rewards account and set `planName`, `billingInterval`, `priceCents`, `startsAt`, and `renewsAt`. The subscription gets a `subscriptionNumber`.

## Status

`trialing`, `active`, `paused`, `cancelled`. Activate, pause, resume, or cancel with `updateSubscriptionStatus`. Cancelling sets `cancelledAt`.
