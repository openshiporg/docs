`/dashboard/platform/loyalty` lists accounts. `/loyalty/[id]` shows one with its full `LoyaltyEvent` ledger.

## How points are earned

A customer links an order by email (`applyCafeLoyalty`) on `/order-confirmed` or staff do it from the order queue. The account earns 1 point per dollar of subtotal. A refund reverses the points; if the history needs reconciliation, the refund stops: "Loyalty account requires reconciliation before refund".

## Account fields

`customerName`, `customerEmail`, `customerPhone`, `marketingOptIn`, `status` (active, paused, closed), `tier` (regular, neighborhood, house_account), `pointsBalance`, `lifetimePoints`, `drinkCredits`, `visits`, `firstVisitAt`, `lastVisitAt`.

## Adjust an account

`adjustCoffeeLoyaltyAccount`: change points, drink credits, tier, or status, with a note. Pause, reactivate, or close from the same form. Each change writes an event.

Customers see their balance on `/account` (`getCafeLoyaltyAccount`).
