Openfront Coffee Shop is a self-hosted app for one cafe. Customers browse the menu, order ahead for pickup, ask for catering, and keep a rewards balance. Staff take counter sales on a POS, run a bar display (KDS), manage the register drawer, buy and count stock, build recipes, make prep batches, schedule shifts, and manage rewards, catering, and subscriptions.

Customers pay at pickup or at the counter (cash, or a card terminal the cashier confirms). Online card payment through Stripe Checkout is off by default. It needs `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY` and `STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET` on the server and the switch turned on in Store Settings.

Start with [Getting started](/docs/openfront/coffee-shop/getting-started) and [Deployment](/docs/openfront/coffee-shop/deployment). Each dashboard area has its own page, beginning with [Orders](/docs/openfront/coffee-shop/orders). Task guides are under [How-to guides](/docs/openfront/coffee-shop/how-to-guides/set-up-the-menu). Operations are listed in the [API reference](/docs/openfront/coffee-shop/api-reference).

## Stack

- Next.js 16 App Router and React 19.
- Keystone 6 with a GraphQL API at `/api/graphql`.
- Prisma and PostgreSQL.
- Lists live in `features/keystone/models`. Custom queries and commands live in `features/keystone/mutations` and are wired in `mutations/index.ts`. Platform projection types are in `mutations/coffeeBoundarySchema.ts`.
- `docs/schema-map.md` in the repository maps every list, its states, and its write path.

## Tenancy

One store per deployment. `StoreSetting` is a singleton (`singletonKey = "primary"`), and every other list has a required `store` relationship to it. Currency is USD only (the `currencyCode` field rejects anything else). Money is stored in cents. Stock quantities are decimals in each inventory item's own unit.

## Models

| Area | Lists |
|---|---|
| Store | `StoreSetting` (name, address, hours, timezone, pickup lead time, window, and capacity, tax rate in basis points, logo) |
| Menu | `MenuCategory`, `MenuItem` (price, availability, bar station: espresso, cold, or brew bar), `MenuItemImage`, `MenuItemModifier` (group, min/max selections, price adjustment, in-stock flag, inventory mapping) |
| Orders | `CafeOrder` (online, POS, or phone; pickup, counter, or dine-in), `OrderItem` (price and modifier snapshot, bar status), `PosOrder` (register receipt) |
| Money | `Payment`, `PaymentEvent` (webhook replay record), `PaymentProvider` (enablement only, no secrets), `RegisterShift`, `CashMovement` |
| Stock | `InventoryItem`, `InventoryLot`, `StockMovement` (ledger), `Supplier`, `PurchaseOrder` (one item per PO), `WasteLog` |
| Production | `Recipe`, `Ingredient`, `PrepBatch`, `PrepBatchConsumption` |
| People | `User`, `Role`, `StaffShift` |
| Retention | `LoyaltyAccount`, `LoyaltyEvent`, `Subscription` (a plan record), `CateringOrder` |
| System | `CoffeeDomainEvent`, `CoffeeOutboxDelivery` (transactional outbox, hidden and system-only) |

`Role` has read and manage flags for orders, payments, products, inventory, and loyalty, plus `canAccessDashboard`, `canManagePeople`, `canManageRoles`, `canManageSettings`, `canManageOnboarding`, `canSeeOtherPeople`, and `canEditOtherPeople`.

## Main workflows

**Online pickup order.** The customer builds a cart (kept in the browser), gets a quote (`quoteCafeOrder`), and places the order (`createCafePickupOrder`). The order starts as `open`. It is a request: staff accept it (`acceptCafePickupOrder`), which consumes stock from lots and prep batches. KDS bumps move each item from `queued` to `preparing`, `ready`, and `handed_off`. Staff record payment at pickup (`recordCafePayment`) and complete the order. Online requests pause when too many are open at once.

**Counter sale.** Open a register shift, ring up items on the POS, and take cash or a card terminal approval. `createCafePickupOrder` with a register shift creates a paid `CafeOrder`, a `PosOrder`, and a `Payment`, and consumes stock.

**Order states.** `open` can only be cancelled or accepted. `accepted` goes to `in_bar_queue` or `cancelled`. `paid` goes to `in_bar_queue`. Then `preparing`, `ready`, `completed`. Refunds move a paid order to `refunded`.

**Refund.** `refundCafePayment` refunds a counter payment from the current drawer, or a Stripe payment back to the card. It updates the order, the POS receipt, register totals, and reverses rewards points.

**Drawer.** `openRegisterShift`, `recordRegisterCashMovement` (pay-in, pay-out, drop), and `closeRegisterShift` with the counted cash and variance.

**Stock.** Create a purchase order, receive it (creates an active lot and a `received` movement), or cancel it. Record waste and stock counts. Low stock means at or below the reorder point.

**Prep.** Complete a prep batch from an active recipe. It consumes the recipe's ingredients. Later orders draw from the batch.

**Staff shifts.** Schedule a shift, then clock in, clock out, or mark it missed.

**Rewards.** `applyCafeLoyalty` links an order to a loyalty account by email and earns 1 point per dollar of subtotal. Staff can adjust points, drink credits, tier, and status with `adjustCoffeeLoyaltyAccount`.

**Catering.** Customers send an inquiry from `/catering` (`requestCafeCatering`), or staff create one. Status goes `inquiry`, `quoted`, `confirmed`, `completed`, or `cancelled` (`updateCateringOrderStatus`).

**Subscriptions.** Staff create a subscription against a loyalty account and move it between `trialing`, `active`, `paused`, and `cancelled` (`updateSubscriptionStatus`).

## GraphQL boundary

Custom operations are the intended write path. Most commands take an `idempotencyKey`, and many take an `expectedStatus` or `expectedUpdatedAt` to reject stale writes.

**Customer and public queries:** `getCoffeeStore`, `getCoffeeMenu`, `quoteCafeOrder`, `getCafeOrder` (needs the order `secretKey` or the owning session), `getCafeLoyaltyAccount`, `getCoffeeCustomerOrders`, `getCoffeeCustomerCatering`.

**Customer commands:** `registerCoffeeCustomer`, `updateCoffeeCustomerProfile`, `createCafePickupOrder`, `applyCafeLoyalty`, `requestCafeCatering`, `initiateCafePaymentSession`.

**Staff projections:** `getCoffeeOrderQueue`, `getCoffeeKdsBoard`, `getCoffeeKdsRecoveryScope`, `getCoffeeMenuManager`, `getCoffeeMenuInventoryOptions`, `getCoffeePosRegister`, `getCoffeeReports`, `getCoffeeOperations`.

**Staff commands:**

- Orders: `acceptCafePickupOrder`, `updateCafeOrderStatus`, `updateCafeOrderItemStatus`, `recordCafePayment`, `refundCafePayment`, `updatePosOrderStatus`.
- Menu: `createCoffeeMenuCategory`, `createCoffeeMenuItem`, `updateCoffeeMenuItem`, `createCoffeeMenuModifier`, `updateCoffeeMenuModifier`, `updateCoffeeModifierStock`.
- Register: `openRegisterShift`, `recordRegisterCashMovement`, `closeRegisterShift`.
- Stock: `createCoffeePurchaseOrder`, `receiveCoffeePurchaseOrder`, `cancelCoffeePurchaseOrder`, `completeCoffeePrepBatch`, `recordCoffeeWaste`, `recordCoffeeStockCount`.
- People and retention: `scheduleCoffeeStaffShift`, `updateCoffeeStaffShiftStatus`, `adjustCoffeeLoyaltyAccount`, `updateCateringOrderStatus`, `updateSubscriptionStatus`.
- Setup and system: `runCoffeeOnboarding`, `handleCafePaymentWebhook`, `redirectToInit`.

Some records still use generated Keystone CRUD from the platform pages: suppliers, inventory item metadata, recipes and ingredients, catering facts, subscription creation, store settings, and the payment provider switch. Menu item price and availability, modifier stock, stock levels, order state, and payments are command-only.

## Onboarding

The dashboard sidebar shows an onboarding card until the operator completes or dismisses it. The card opens a dialog that runs `runCoffeeOnboarding` with one of three templates:

- **Starter cafe** (`minimal`): 2 categories, 3 menu items, 4 modifiers, 5 inventory items, 2 recipes.
- **Full neighborhood cafe** (`full`): 4 categories, 5 menu items, 10 modifiers, 8 inventory items, 1 supplier, 3 recipes.
- **Custom setup**: paste your own JSON, review it, then confirm.

Both templates create the store "Neighborhood Roast" and three payment providers: Manual / Cash (enabled), Stripe (disabled), and PayPal (disabled). Onboarding only creates missing records. It never overwrites existing ones, so running it twice is safe. Template stores get an empty contact email until you set one.

## Integrations

- **Payments.** `features/integrations/payment` has Stripe, PayPal, and manual adapters. Webhooks arrive at `/api/payment-providers/[providerCode]/webhook` and only accept `pp_stripe_stripe` or `pp_paypal_paypal`. Stripe Checkout powers online payment and online refunds.
- **Email.** SMTP sends password reset emails.
- **Storage.** Menu images use S3-compatible storage.
- **Dashboard AI chat.** `/api/completion` uses OpenRouter (`OPENROUTER_API_KEY`, `OPENROUTER_MODEL`, `OPENROUTER_MAX_TOKENS`) and the MCP transport at `/api/mcp-transport/[transport]`.
- **Health.** `/api/health` checks the database and returns 200 or 503.
