## Schema by domain

**Catalog.** `Product` (status `draft` / `proposed` / `published` / `rejected`, `regulatedCommerceRequired` defaulting to true, exemption fields), `ProductVariant` (SKU, barcodes, inventory, one-to-one `regulatedClassification`), `ProductImage`, `ProductOption`, `ProductOptionValue`, `ProductCategory`, `ProductCollection`, `ProductTag`, `ProductType`, `Measurement`, `StockMovement`, `Location`.

**Pricing and markets.** `MoneyAmount`, `PriceList`, `PriceSet`, `PriceRule`, `RuleType`, `CustomerGroup`, `Region`, `Country`, `Currency`, `TaxRate`, `TaxProvider`, `Discount`, `DiscountRule`, `DiscountCondition`, `GiftCard`, `GiftCardTransaction`.

**Carts, orders, and fulfillment.** `Cart`, `LineItem`, `LineItemAdjustment`, `ShippingMethod`, `Order` (GraphQL create/update/delete disabled; changed only through custom mutations), `OrderLineItem`, `OrderEvent`, `DraftOrder`, `Fulfillment`, `FulfillmentItem`, `ShippingLabel`, `Return`, `ReturnItem`, `Refund`, `ClaimOrder`, `ClaimItem`, `Swap`, `IdempotencyKey`.

**Payments and shipping.** `PaymentProvider`, `PaymentCollection`, `PaymentSession`, `Payment`, `Capture`, `ShippingProvider`, `ShippingOption`, `ShippingProfile`, `FulfillmentProvider`.

**Customers and accounts.** `User`, `Address`, `Role`, `Team`, `Invite`, `BusinessAccountRequest`, `Account`, `AccountLineItem`, `Invoice`, `InvoiceLineItem`, `UserField`, `Notification`.

**Regulated authority.** `Jurisdiction` (`draft` → `active` → `retired`), `RegulatedRuleSet` (`draft` → `approved` → `active` → `retired` / `emergency_disabled`; signed policy hash, citations, validated rules JSON), `RegulatedMerchantProfile` (`draft` → `review` → `active` → `suspended` / `revoked`; PACT registration fields, launch jurisdictions, `killSwitch` defaulting to true), `MerchantLicense`, `ProviderApproval` (purpose identity, payment, tax, registry, or carrier; payment and carrier approvals bind to a `PaymentProvider` or `ShippingProvider`).

**Product evidence.** `RegulatedProductClassification` (one per variant: category, nicotine source and concentration, flavor, volume, marketing review) and `ProductAuthorization` (`federal_marketing_order`, `preexisting_status`, `state_directory`, `local_directory`).

**Transaction evidence and ledger.** `AgeVerificationAttempt`, `RegulatedTaxQuote`, `CarrierEligibilityDecision`, `ComplianceDecision` (immutable per-cart verdict with snapshot, expiring within five minutes), `ComplianceEvent` (append-only), `ComplianceReportRun`.

**Platform.** `ApiKey`, `OAuthApp`, `OAuthToken`, `WebhookEndpoint`, `WebhookEvent`, `BatchJob`, `Note`.

Evidence counts as current only between `effectiveAt` and `expiresAt`. Nothing expires records on a schedule; lapsed evidence stops counting when it is read. Once a regulated record reaches a usable state, its evidence fields are immutable (`validateAuthorityRevision`); it can only move to a terminal or suspended state, and a change needs a new revision.

## Workflow: go live

`regulatedCommerceReadiness` reports blockers; `releaseBlockers` in `features/regulated-commerce/core/release-gate.ts` is the hard gate on every cart, payment, and checkout step.

1. Store business details and all five policies saved in store settings, or the gate returns `STORE_POLICIES_REQUIRED`.
2. One active `RegulatedMerchantProfile` with PACT registration reference and SHA-256 digest, origin jurisdiction, and bounded dates.
3. The profile's kill switch turned off, or the gate returns `KILL_SWITCH_ENABLED`.
4. At least one active launch `Jurisdiction` with authority sources.
5. Verified `MerchantLicense` records where a rule set has a `license_required` rule.
6. Exactly one active, approved, signed `RegulatedRuleSet` per destination.
7. Active `ProviderApproval` records for identity, payment, tax, carrier, and registry.
8. At least one approved classification with a current federal authorization.

Only when the gate returns no blockers is `transactionMode` `enabled`; otherwise checkout throws "Checkout isn't available for this store yet."

## Workflow: product to publish

1. Create the product as a draft (a regulated product cannot be created as published).
2. Add options, variants, prices, images, and measurements.
3. Classify each variant and approve the classification with a reviewer, digest, dates, and approved marketing review.
4. Add an authorized federal `ProductAuthorization` (`authorized` needs `lastVerifiedAt` and a digest).
5. Publish. `assertProductPublicationEvidence` requires evidence for every variant, or a current reviewed exemption.

After publishing, customer-facing copy and variant identity edits are refused. Swapping an image, or moving a classification or authorization out of a usable state, moves the product back to `draft`.

## Workflow: checkout

1. `createActiveCart`, then `updateActiveCartLineItem` / `updateActiveCart`; each variant must pass publication evidence, and every cart change revokes prior evidence and open decisions.
2. Address resolves a destination jurisdiction such as `US-CA`.
3. `addActiveCartShippingMethod` evaluates the cart and requires an allowed `CarrierEligibilityDecision` for that option.
4. Age verification, committed tax quote, and carrier eligibility must be recorded for the same cart fingerprint (internal functions only; see limitations).
5. Payment sessions (`createActiveCartPaymentSessions`, `setActiveCartPaymentSession`, `initiatePaymentSession`) need an active payment approval covering the destination and categories.
6. `evaluateActiveCartCompliance` previews the decision; the storefront compliance panel shows readable reasons and keeps Place order disabled unless allowed.
7. `completeActiveCart` persists a `checkout` `ComplianceDecision`, uses an `IdempotencyKey`, creates the `Order`, line items, and payment, and binds the evidence to the order.

## Workflow: fulfillment, returns, and orders

- `getRatesForOrder` returns only the exact approved carrier rate for regulated orders; `createProviderShippingLabel` needs an allowing fulfillment decision and order-bound adult-signature carrier evidence. Manual `createOrderFulfillment` is refused for regulated orders.
- `cancelShippingLabel` must precede `cancelOrderFulfillment`; `trackShipment` reads label status.
- Orders move from `pending` to `completed` when every line is fulfilled. `transitionOrderStatus` cancels only fully refunded, unfulfilled orders.
- `processReturnRefund` records the refund and a `regulated.return_refunded` event with reason `TAX_AND_REPORTING_REVERSAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED`, shown on the order page.

## Workflow: safety stop and reporting

- `stopRegulatedCommerce(profileId, reason)` (needs `canOperateComplianceKillSwitch`, reason 10 to 500 characters) turns the kill switch on, suspends the active profile, revokes open decisions and unbound carrier allows, and writes `merchant.emergency_stop`, in one transaction. Resuming is done on the merchant profile page.
- `exportComplianceInventory` and `generateComplianceReport` (only `evidence_inventory`, period of 93 days at most) create `ComplianceReportRun` records; `recordComplianceReportSubmission` records a filing receipt as `submitted`, `accepted`, or `rejected`. A generated report is not a jurisdiction filing.

## Access control

<Callout>
Dashboard visibility is not the control. Every list and custom operation checks role permissions on the server.
</Callout>

- Role checkbox permissions (`features/keystone/models/fields.ts`, `features/keystone/access.ts`) decide human sessions. API keys and OAuth tokens need both the credential scope and the owner's role permission.
- `managedComplianceAccess` (authority and evidence lists): read with read or manage compliance, create and update with `canManageCompliance`, delete denied.
- `immutableComplianceAccess` (ledger lists): read with compliance read; create, update, and delete denied and omitted from GraphQL. Only system code writes them.
- Evidence digests are never readable; identity fields on `AgeVerificationAttempt` need `canAccessIdentityEvidence`. Updating `killSwitch` needs `canOperateComplianceKillSwitch`.
