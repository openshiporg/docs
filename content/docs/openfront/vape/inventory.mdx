Route: `/dashboard/platform/inventory`. Needs `canManageProducts`.

The list shows variant stock with links to the product. Adjust stock opens a sheet with a delta or a set count; the bulk paste option applies many changes. Both call `adjustInventory` and write `StockMovement` rows (`RECEIVE` or `REMOVE`, quantity, reason, note), an append-only trail.
