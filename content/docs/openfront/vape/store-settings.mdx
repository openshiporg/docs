Route: `/dashboard/platform/store`. Needs `canManageStore`-level access.

`Store.metadata.business`: legal name, email, phone, address, and licence lines. `Store.metadata.policies`: shipping, returns, age verification, privacy, and terms. The storefront serves the five policies at `/policies/[slug]`. The release gate blocks sales until the legal name, one licence line, and all five policies exist (`STORE_POLICIES_REQUIRED`). Required regulatory warning copy is yours to supply.

Also here: store name, homepage title and description, logo icon and color, and default currency.

## Regions

`/dashboard/platform/regions` (and `/countries`, `/currencies`, `/markets`). Onboarding creates region `us` with tax rate 0, which regulated carts require. Tax for regulated carts comes from the tax quote, not the generic rate.
