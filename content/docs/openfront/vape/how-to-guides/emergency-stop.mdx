---
title: Stop sales
description: Halt regulated checkout immediately.
icon: OctagonX
---

import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open Compliance Center
Needs `canOperateComplianceKillSwitch`.
</Step>
<Step>
### Stop
Press Emergency stop and enter a reason of 10 to 500 characters.
</Step>
<Step>
### Review in-flight orders
The event `merchant.emergency_stop` records that review is needed; check recent orders in the Compliance Ledger.
</Step>
<Step>
### Resume
After review, Resume sales on the merchant profile.
</Step></Steps>
