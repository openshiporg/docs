`/dashboard/platform/quality` summarises release quality from `uikitOperations(limit: 50)`.

## Summary cards

- **Approved reviews**: `ReviewWorkflow` with status `approved`, out of all reviews
- **Passing audits**: `AccessibilityAudit` with status `passed`, out of all audits
- **Supported targets**: `CompatibilityTarget` with `supportStatus` `supported`, out of all targets

Below the cards are three searchable tables: Reviews (release, reviewer, decision), Accessibility audits, and Compatibility targets.

## Record fields

- **AccessibilityAudit**: title, release, status (`queued`, `passed`, `failed`, `waived`), WCAG level (`a`, `aa`, `aaa`, default `aa`), violations count, summary, audited at.
- **CompatibilityTarget**: name, type (`browser`, `framework`, `runtime`, `package_manager`), minimum version, support status (`supported`, `experimental`, `deprecated`), release, notes.

Compatibility targets and changelog entries appear on the customer's component detail page. Reading needs `canReadProducts`.
