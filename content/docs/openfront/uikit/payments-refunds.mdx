## Payments

`/dashboard/platform/payments` lists `Payment` records with status tabs: `pending`, `authorized`, `captured`, `failed`, `canceled`, `refunded`. Each shows amount, `amountRefunded`, provider, order, and `capturedAt`. Provider events are stored as `PaymentEvent` and a replay key keeps the same event from being applied twice.

## Refunds

`/dashboard/platform/refunds` lists `Refund` records with tabs `pending`, `succeeded`, `failed`.

### Issue a refund

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open the order
From `/dashboard/platform/orders/[id]`, find the captured payment.
</Step>
<Step>
### Enter amount and reason
Amount is in the smallest currency unit. Reason is 3 to 500 characters. For Manual payments, also enter the **Offline reference**.
</Step>
<Step>
### Submit
`refundKitPayment(paymentId, amount, reason, idempotencyKey, providerReference)` reserves a refund intent, calls the provider, and records the result. A succeeded refund returns `Refund completed and local entitlement state reconciled.` A pending one returns `Refund request is pending provider completion.`
</Step>
<Step>
### Reconcile if pending
Open Refunds and run `reconcileKitRefund(refundId)` to ask the provider for the final status.
</Step>
</Steps>

Rules enforced by the code:

- Only captured payments can be refunded.
- Amount must be a positive integer within the remaining captured amount: `Refund amount exceeds the remaining captured amount.`
- The idempotency key is 8 to 128 characters. Repeating a key returns the original result with `replayed: true`.
- A full refund (refunded total equals captured amount) revokes the related entitlement. Partial refunds leave access in place.
- Needs `canManagePayments` and `canManageOrders`: `Refunds require payment and order management permission.`

## Offline settlement

Manual payment sessions stay pending until an operator records settlement with `settleKitOrderOffline(paymentSessionId, reference, idempotencyKey)`. The reference is 3 to 255 characters and the key is 8 to 128 characters. Only Manual sessions are accepted: `Only Manual/offline payment sessions can use operator settlement.` Amount and currency must match the payment snapshot. The mutation is available in GraphQL; the platform screens do not call it.
