---
title: Refund and revoke access
description: Refund a captured payment and withdraw the customer's entitlement.
---

import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

<Steps>
<Step>
### Find the order
Search at `/dashboard/platform/orders` and open it.
</Step>
<Step>
### Refund
Enter the amount in minor units and a reason, then submit. For a full refund the entitlement is revoked automatically. For a partial refund it stays active.
</Step>
<Step>
### Reconcile
If the refund shows `pending`, open `/dashboard/platform/refunds` and reconcile it once the provider finishes.
</Step>
<Step>
### Revoke manually (optional)
At `/dashboard/platform/access`, open the entitlement and revoke with a reason of at least 3 characters. This applies to refunds you handled outside UIKit, or to abuse.
</Step>
<Step>
### Check the audit trail
`/dashboard/platform/audit` lists the refund and revocation events.
</Step>
</Steps>
