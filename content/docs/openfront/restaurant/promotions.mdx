Openfront Restaurant has discount and gift-card models with admin screens. Gift cards work end to end in the POS. Discounts do not.

## Gift cards

Screen: `/dashboard/platform/gift-cards`.

- `GiftCard` holds the code, original value, balance, disabled flag, expiry date, and metadata.
- `GiftCardTransaction` is the ledger: `issue`, `redeem`, `refund`, `adjustment`, each with the balance after.
- Gift cards cannot be created or edited through generic CRUD. They change only through custom mutations.
- `issueGiftCard` issues a card with a code (8 to 64 letters, digits, or hyphens) and an amount. It needs both gift-card and payment management permission.
- `setGiftCardStatus` disables or re-enables a card with a reason.
- On the POS payment screen, `lookupGiftCard` checks the balance and `redeemGiftCard` charges up to the remaining balance on the check. Disabled and expired cards are refused.
- Refunding a gift-card payment returns value to the card.

Guests cannot buy or check gift cards in the storefront.

## Discounts

Screen: `/dashboard/platform/discounts`.

- `Discount` holds a code, dynamic flag, disabled flag, stackable flag, start and end dates, usage limit and count, and a valid duration.
- `DiscountRule` holds the type (`fixed`, `percentage`, `free_item`), value, and allocation (`total` or `item`).
- The screen lists discounts with status tabs and a create drawer.

No order path applies a discount code. The storefront has no code field, and the POS has none either. The `discount` amount on orders today comes only from the pickup discount in store settings. If you need coupon codes, the apply logic still has to be built.

## Customer email campaigns

The Customer Privacy page (`/dashboard/platform/privacy`) includes a manual campaign tool:

- `previewRestaurantCampaign` lists customers who opted in to marketing, checking at most 500 accounts.
- `createRestaurantCampaign` queues a subject and text for the selected customers. Consent is checked again.
- `dispatchRestaurantCampaign` sends the queued emails over SMTP.

Campaigns need both user and order management permission. Customers opt in or out from the privacy panel on their account pages.

## Summary

- **Gift cards**: usable in the POS.
- **Discounts**: records and admin screens only.
- **Campaigns**: manual email to opted-in customers, sent by an operator.

SMTP acceptance is recorded, but it does not prove the email reached the inbox.
