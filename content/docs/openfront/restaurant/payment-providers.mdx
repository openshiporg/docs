Openfront Restaurant uses adapter-based payment providers. Each `PaymentProvider` record names the adapter for create, capture, refund, status, payment link, and webhook work. The adapters live in:

- `features/integrations/payment/stripe.ts`
- `features/integrations/payment/paypal.ts`
- `features/integrations/payment/manual.ts`

The record's function fields must be `stripe`, `paypal`, or `manual`. Manage records at `/dashboard/platform/payment-providers`.

## Providers

### Stripe (`pp_stripe_stripe`)

Needs `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY`, `NEXT_PUBLIC_STRIPE_KEY`, and `STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET`. Used for:

- storefront card checkout with payment intents
- POS card payments (`credit_card`, `debit_card`, `apple_pay`, and `google_pay` tenders all route to Stripe)
- capture, status checks, and refunds
- signed webhooks

### PayPal (`pp_paypal_paypal`)

Needs `NEXT_PUBLIC_PAYPAL_CLIENT_ID`, `PAYPAL_CLIENT_SECRET`, and `PAYPAL_WEBHOOK_ID`. Sandbox is used unless `NEXT_PUBLIC_PAYPAL_SANDBOX=false`. Used for storefront checkout with PayPal approval, capture, refunds, and verified webhooks. The POS payment screen has no PayPal tender.

### Manual (`pp_system_default`)

No credentials. Used for cash in the POS and for storefront orders paid outside the app. Storefront orders using it are created as `open` with a pending payment. The manual adapter does not support webhooks.

Onboarding creates these three records. Stripe and PayPal count as configured only when their environment variables are set.

## How the provider model works

<Steps>
<Step>
### Create or seed the provider record
Onboarding creates Stripe, PayPal, and Manual. You can add or toggle records on the Payment Providers page.
</Step>
<Step>
### The runtime resolves the adapter
`features/keystone/utils/paymentProviderAdapter.ts` reads the record and calls the named adapter function.
</Step>
<Step>
### Payments attach to orders
Each `Payment` links to a `RestaurantOrder` and its provider. Payments carry an idempotency key, status, method, tip, split index, and refunded amount.
</Step>
<Step>
### Webhooks are verified and logged
Providers post to `/api/integrations/payment/[providerCode]/webhook`, for example `/api/integrations/payment/pp_stripe_stripe/webhook`. The route passes the raw body and headers to `handlePaymentProviderWebhook`, which checks the signature through the adapter and stores the event as a `PaymentWebhookEvent`. Duplicate events are ignored. A verified payment event can also complete a pending storefront checkout.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Payment statuses

`pending`, `processing`, `authorized`, `unknown`, `succeeded`, `failed`, `cancelled`, `refunded`, `partially_refunded`. A check cannot close while any of its payments is `processing`, `authorized`, or `unknown`.

## Refunds and reconciliation

- `refundPayment` needs an approved `ManagerApproval` from a different user and an idempotency key. It writes a `Refund` and a refund `Receipt`.
- `reconcilePayment` and `reconcileRefund` ask the provider for the current state when a result was unclear.
- `retryVerifiedRefundWebhookEvent` re-processes a stored, verified refund webhook.
- `capturePayment` and `getPaymentStatus` are also exposed.

The refund and webhook recovery panels are in the POS "Checkout recovery" section and on the payment screen.

## Provider fields

- `name`, `code`, `isInstalled`
- `createPaymentFunction`, `capturePaymentFunction`, `refundPaymentFunction`, `getPaymentStatusFunction`, `generatePaymentLinkFunction`, `handleWebhookFunction`
- `credentials` and `metadata`

The only webhook route is `/api/integrations/payment/[providerCode]/webhook`.
