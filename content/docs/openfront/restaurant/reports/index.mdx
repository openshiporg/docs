Openfront Restaurant has a reports hub, an operations dashboard, and money screens for receipts and the daily cash close. They answer the questions an operator asks after a shift. They are not a data warehouse.

The hub at `/dashboard/platform/reports` links to Sales Overview, Menu Performance, and Labor & Staffing. It also lists Revenue Analytics, Customer Insights, Inventory Cost Tracking, and Comparative Period Reports as "Soon". Those four do not exist.

## Operations dashboard

`/dashboard/platform/operations` (also in the sidebar as "Operations") uses `GetOperationalMetrics`. It shows:

- open, in-progress, and ready orders
- occupied and total tables
- today's net food collections and order count
- recorded order voids and cancelled orders
- quick links to POS, KDS, orders, and the sales report

## Sales report

`/dashboard/platform/reports/sales` uses `GetSalesOverview` and `GetPaymentBreakdown`. It covers:

- net collected on completed checks, total orders, average check, and total guests
- tax, tips, and discounts
- comparison with the previous period
- daypart trends (breakfast, lunch, dinner, late night)
- order type mix (dine-in, takeout, delivery)
- payment method mix

## Menu performance

`/dashboard/platform/reports/menu-performance` uses `GetMenuItemPerformance` and `GetCategoryPerformance` on completed order items. It sorts items into Star, Plow Horse, Puzzle, and Dog groups and shows category revenue. `GetServerPerformance` is also defined in the report actions.

## Labor report

`/dashboard/platform/reports/labor` uses `GetLaborData`. It combines `restaurantAttendance`, calculated or distributed tip pools, and completed orders. See [Staff and labor](/docs/openfront/restaurant/staff).

## Receipts

`/dashboard/platform/receipts` lists `Receipt` records: sale, refund, and correction. Receipts are written by the server when a payment succeeds or a refund is made, and are not edited. You can filter by check ID, view a receipt, print it from the browser, and export the list as CSV. Amounts are in minor units of the recorded currency.

## Cash and business-day close

`/dashboard/platform/reconciliation` ("Cash & Close" in the sidebar) manages the business day. All actions need payment permissions:

- `openRestaurantBusinessDay` with an opening cash float and optional cutoff minutes. Only one day can be open.
- `recordRestaurantCashMovement` for cash in or out with a reason.
- `closeRestaurantBusinessDay` with counted cash and a reason. Closed totals cannot change.
- `recordRestaurantSettlement` to attach processor statement evidence (reference, method, gross, fees, net).
- `restaurantBusinessDays` and `restaurantBusinessDay` read the history.

Business days are stored as `AuditEvent` rows, not their own list. Payments processed after a close are carried into the next day.

## Where reporting is still early

- The four "Soon" reports on the hub are not built.
- Report pages export nothing except receipts, time entries, and tip ledgers (CSV).
- There is no forecasting, scheduled report, or accounting export.
- Recipe-based food cost only appears on the recipes page, not in reports.

<Callout type="info">
Sales figures come from completed orders. Open checks do not show up in sales until they are closed.
