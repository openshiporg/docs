Staff are `User` records with restaurant fields. There are screens for the staff list, the weekly schedule, the time clock, tip pools, and labor reporting.

## Staff data

A user record can store:

- staff role: server, bartender, host, cook, manager, admin, busser, chef
- employee ID, hire date, and hourly rate
- phone number and photo
- a PIN field (write-only; no screen in the app uses it)
- extra staff permissions as JSON
- onboarding status
- emergency contact name and phone
- certifications
- active or inactive status

What a user can do is set by their `Role`, not by `staffRole`. Inactive users lose access.

## Screens

| Route | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/dashboard/platform/users` | Staff list with role filter, create and edit drawers |
| `/dashboard/platform/staff/schedule` | Weekly roster ("Staff Roster"). Not in the sidebar |
| `/dashboard/platform/time-clock` | Clock in and out, breaks, attest, corrections, approval, CSV export |
| `/dashboard/platform/staff/tips` | Tip pools and ledger with CSV export. Not in the sidebar |
| `/dashboard/platform/reports/labor` | Labor report |

### Weekly schedule

Managers create and edit shifts with `upsertShift`: staff member, role (server, bartender, host, busser, cook, dishwasher, manager), start and end time, and hourly rate. `updateShiftStatus` takes `start`, `complete`, `cancel`, or `no_show`. Both need `canManageStaff`.

### Time clock

Each employee runs their own clock with `recordRestaurantAttendance`:

- `clock_in` links to the employee's scheduled shift if there is one, and marks it started
- `start_break` and `end_break`
- `clock_out` closes the entry and completes the shift
- `attest` lets the employee confirm a closed entry
- `approve_time` must come from a different user with staff management permission

Closed entries can only change through a correction. The employee asks with `requestRestaurantTimeCorrection` and a different manager approves with `approveRestaurantTimeCorrection`. `restaurantAttendance` lists entries for a date range.

### Tip pools

Tip pools use cash and credit tip amounts for one business day. Types are:

- **Individual**: all tips to one staff member
- **Pool by role**: weighted by role
- **House pool**: split by hours worked

`previewRestaurantTipPool` shows the split before saving. `createTipPoolLedger` saves it with a source note. `updateTipPoolStatus` with `distribute` records the payout with a reference. Every tip-eligible time entry for that day must be closed, attested, and manager-approved first.

### Labor report

The labor report combines approved attendance, completed-order sales, and calculated or distributed tip pools. It shows hours, payroll cost, labor percentage, sales per labor hour, tips, and role breakdowns. It can filter by role.

## How teams use it

<Steps>
<Step>
### Create staff records
Add the user, their role, hourly rate, and contact details.
</Step>
<Step>
### Build the schedule
Assign shifts for the week.
</Step>
<Step>
### Run the time clock
Staff clock in and out. They attest closed entries and a manager approves them.
</Step>
<Step>
### Pool tips and review labor
Preview and save the day's tip pool, mark it distributed, then review the labor report.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Current limitations

- There is no PIN login or shared-terminal clock-in. Staff clock in under their own signed-in session.
- There is no payroll integration. Time and tips export as CSV only.
- `staffRole` is a label. Permissions come only from the linked `Role`.

<Callout type="info">
Approvals for time, time corrections, and refunds or voids must come from a different user than the requester.
</Callout>
