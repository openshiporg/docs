Openfront Tutor is for one person teaching one-to-one, online or in person. Families book from the tutor's own page and pay by card or directly (bank transfer, a payment app, mobile money or cash). The tutor keeps families, students, sessions, notes, homework, packages of hours, invoices and earnings in one dashboard. No marketplace takes a cut.

The live site runs one fictional tutor, Daniel Reyes, who teaches maths and physics for grades 8 to 12, online and in North Austin. His page is `/book/daniel-reyes`. The dashboard is at `/dashboard`.

To install it, see [Getting started](/docs/openfront/tutor/getting-started).

## Concepts

| Concept | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Tutor profile | The business: page address (`slug`), name, photo, subjects, timezone, currency, booking rules, policies and payment instructions. |
| Session type | Something a family books: length, price, online, in person or either, and whether new families may book it. |
| Family and student | One guardian (parent or adult student) and one or more students. A family has a billing mode: pay as you book, monthly invoice, or a flat monthly fee. |
| Session | One lesson. Its **funding** is `free`, `package`, `card`, `invoice` or `flat`, decided by the server when it is booked. |
| Package and purchase | A prepaid number of hours (for example 10 hours at 10% off, valid 12 months). A purchase holds the family's balance. |
| Credit entry | One row in the package ledger: grant, hold, release, debit, reversal, expire, refund or adjustment. A balance is the sum of its rows. |
| Invoice | A monthly bill drafted from the month's lessons, sent to the family and paid by card or directly. |
| Reported payment | A family says they paid directly. Nothing changes until the tutor confirms it. |

## Ownership

The first account on a deployment is the tutor and gets the **Tutor** role. Every later account is a **Family** account with no dashboard and no generic list access. Families use the family account at `/account`, which only reaches their own records through named operations.

## Stack

Next.js 16 (App Router) with React 19, Keystone 6 with GraphQL at `/api/graphql`, Prisma with PostgreSQL, Tailwind CSS and shadcn/ui. Node.js 20 or later.

## Main workflows

1. **Set up.** Create the first account and fill in the setup form. **Start with a typical setup** adds five session types, three packages, weekly hours and a written policy. See [Settings](/docs/openfront/tutor/settings).
2. **Take a first booking.** A new family books a free consultation or a first lesson on `/book/<address>`, and gets a private booking link. See [Storefront](/docs/openfront/tutor/storefront).
3. **Teach.** **Today** lists the day's sessions. Complete one with a note and homework in one form, or mark a no-show. See [Today and Calendar](/docs/openfront/tutor/today-calendar).
4. **Charge.** Per session by card, prepaid packages, or a monthly invoice. Families can also pay directly and report it. See [Billing](/docs/openfront/tutor/billing).
5. **Keep the books.** Earnings, expenses and CSV exports of sessions and payments. See [Earnings](/docs/openfront/tutor/earnings).
