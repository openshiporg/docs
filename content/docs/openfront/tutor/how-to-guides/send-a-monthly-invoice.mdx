---
title: Send a monthly invoice
description: Draft an invoice from the month's lessons, check the lines, send it and confirm the payment.
icon: FileText
---

import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

<Steps>
<Step>

### Put the family on invoice billing

On the family page, set the billing mode to `invoice`. Their sessions are then funded by invoice.

</Step>
<Step>

### Draft

Open **Billing**. **Ready to invoice** lists the month's lessons per family. Draft the invoice and add or remove lines.

</Step>
<Step>

### Send

Send it. It gets a number and a due date, and the family gets a private link to pay by card or see your payment instructions.

</Step>
<Step>

### Confirm the payment

A card payment settles by itself. A direct payment the family reports appears on Today and Billing for you to confirm.

</Step>
</Steps>
