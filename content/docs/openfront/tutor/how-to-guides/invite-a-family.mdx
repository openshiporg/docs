---
title: Invite a family
description: Add a family, add a student and send the claim link so they can book from their own account.
icon: UserPlus
---

import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

<Steps>
<Step>

### Add the family

Go to **Families** and add the guardian's name, email or phone, and timezone. Choose a billing mode.

</Step>
<Step>

### Add a student

On the family page, add the student's name, level and goals.

</Step>
<Step>

### Send the invite

Invite the family from their page and send them the link to `/account/claim`.

</Step>
<Step>

### They claim it

The guardian chooses a password and is signed in to `/account`, where they can book, pay and see notes.

</Step>
</Steps>
