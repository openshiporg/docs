Route: `/dashboard/platform/earnings`. Needs `canViewEarnings`.

`myEarnings(from, to)` reads confirmed payments in the range and returns:

| Figure | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Money in | Payments less refunds (`inCents`, `refundsCents`) |
| By method and by month | The same, split by payment method and by month in the tutor's timezone |
| Sessions and hours taught | Completed sessions that started in the range |
| Packages sold | Count and value of packages bought in the range |
| Prepaid | Minutes left on active packages and their unearned value |
| Outstanding | What sent invoices still owe |
| Expenses and net | Expenses in the range by category, and money in less expenses |

Expenses are recorded on the same page with a date, category (`travel`, `materials`, `software`, `room`, `other`), amount and note.

`exportCsv(kind, from, to)` exports `sessions.csv` (kind `sessions`) or `payments.csv` (any other kind) for your accountant.
