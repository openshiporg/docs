Route: `/dashboard/platform/setup`. Code: `features/platform/setup`.

## First-run setup

Until the signed-in user has a business, the page shows the setup form. `setUpMyBusiness` creates the `BarberProfile`, seven `WeeklyHours` rows (working Tuesday to Saturday, 10:00 to 18:00), the **In person** and **Card online** payment providers, and, with **Start with a typical menu**, these services and products:

| Service | Price | Length | Deposit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Haircut | $35 | 30 min | $10 |
| Skin fade | $40 | 45 min | $10 |
| Cut and beard | $55 | 60 min | $15 |
| Beard trim | $20 | 20 min | none |
| Hot towel shave | $45 | 45 min | $15 |

Products: Matte clay ($22, 12 in stock), Beard oil ($18, 10), Sea salt spray ($16, 8).

A booking address that is already taken is refused. One user can set up only one business.

## Settings form

After setup the same route edits the profile:

| Field | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Business name, location, phone, email, bio | Business name is required; email must be valid. |
| Booking open | Clients can book from your page. |
| Hold unpaid bookings for | 1 to 72 hours (default 6). |
| Clients can cancel up to | 0 to 720 hours before (default 24). |
| Shortest notice | 0 to 720 hours (default 2). |
| Book up to | 1 to 365 days ahead (default 60). |
| Clean-up time | 0 to 120 minutes after each client (default 10). |

The same limits are enforced again on the `BarberProfile` fields. Slot spacing (`slotMinutes`, default 15), currency, timezone, work style and the no-show fee are edited under **Models → Barber profiles**.
