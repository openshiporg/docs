---
title: Check out a client
description: Take the balance, a tip and a product sale in one step from Today.
icon: Receipt
---

import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open Today

Go to `/dashboard/platform/today` and find the appointment.

</Step>
<Step>

### Fill in checkout

Change the price if the service changed, enter the tip, pick a product and quantity if one was sold, and choose how the client paid: cash, card, or Zelle/Venmo/Cash App.

</Step>
<Step>

### Save

The appointment becomes `completed` and `paid`. The payment is the price less any deposit already paid, plus the tip and products, and appears in **Earnings**.

</Step>
</Steps>
