To create a custom Barber dashboard, copy the skill and give it to your LLM. The skill explains how `features/platform` implements Today, Earnings and Settings, and which named operations they call.

Skill file: [`/skills/openfront-barber-custom-dashboard/SKILL.md`](/skills/openfront-barber-custom-dashboard/SKILL.md)

## Built-in routes

| Route | Page | Operations |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/dashboard/platform/today` | Open appointments, checkout, no-show, cancel | `checkOutAppointment`, `markAppointmentNoShow`, `cancelAppointmentAsBarber` |
| `/dashboard/platform/earnings` | Earnings for 7, 30 or 365 days | `myEarnings` |
| `/dashboard/platform/setup` | First-run setup, then Settings | `setUpMyBusiness`, `updateBarberProfile` |
| `/dashboard/<list>` | Generic list pages | Keystone CRUD with ownership filters |

Route files are thin wrappers in `app/dashboard/(admin)/platform/`. The sidebar comes from `features/platform/lib/navigation.ts`.

A separate client must keep checkout, refunds and earnings on the server. Call the named mutations; do not write `Payment` rows, which are read-only through the API.
